**Slave Farm** is a town in [Heng](/heng-zone) controlled by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). This location relies heavily on [Slavery](/slavery). Slaves here work on over ten XL [Cactus Farms](/cactus-farm). [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) lives here in a Noble House.

It can be found by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Extended Territories)**.

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Heft](/heft) and [Stoat](/stoat), and some of the [Samurai Army in the desert](/samurai-rogue).

There is also another slave farm, it's called [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) controlled by [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) located in [The Hook](/the-hook).

## Town override

_Main article: [Slave Farm/Destroyed](/slave-farm/destroyed)_

If **Slave Mistress Ren** is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), **Slave Farm** will fall to ruins.

## Trivia

-   This labor camp can be considered to be the 2nd most important/influential out of all 6 labor camps in northern UC, as the destruction of it is necessary for [malnourished rogues](/samurai-rogue) to spawn, tied with decreased [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) and [Manhunters](/manhunters) and increased [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) presence in [Heng](/heng-zone), and can cause a [malnourishment of Heft](/heft/malnourished), as well as being necessary for [malnourishment of Stoat](/stoat/malnourished).
