The **Fabric Chest** is a [storage](/storage) container which exclusively contains [Fabrics](/fabrics).

This container can hold a total of 50 items.

Players can construct this building after researching one of the following technologies:

-   [Fabric Manufacture (Tech)](/fabric-manufacture-tech)
-   [Hemp-Based Fabric (Tech)](/hemp-based-fabric-tech)
-   [Storage Boxes: Crafting (Tech)](/storage-boxes-crafting-tech).
