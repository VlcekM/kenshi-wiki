Outpost s-I is a seemingly older variant of [Outpost s-III](/outpost-s-iii). Asides from its more dilapidated texture, the structure of the building is identical. This building cannot be constructed by the player.

The only confirmed instance of this building is the [Lost Armour Store](/lost-armour-store).

### Building stats

-   Door condition: 40
-   Building condition: 40
