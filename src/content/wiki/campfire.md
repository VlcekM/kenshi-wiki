**Campfires** are [camping](/camping) [buildings](/buildings-list) which the player can construct anywhere. They require very few materials to construct (none actually used in game terms) and do not result in the creation of a [player town](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). Campfires can be used to cook [raw meat](/raw-meat) into [dried meat](/dried-meat) from the very beginning of the game.
