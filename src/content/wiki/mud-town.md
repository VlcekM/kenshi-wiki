**Mud Town** is a [Swampers](/swampers) village located north of [Shark](/shark) within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). It can be discovered by trudging through the swamp or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Swamps**. [Twinblades](/twinblades) leader, [Big Fang](/big-fang), resides within the village. The local [bar](/bar) is run by the [Blackshifters](/blackshifters).

Mud Town has the potential to spawn the most unique recruits than any other swamp village, or even Shark. [Doctor Chung](/doctor-chung), [Green](/green) and [Stubs Momuso](/stubs-momuso) can be recruited here if they are not in Shark. [Silvershade](/silvershade) has the highest chance to spawn here. [Hamut](/hamut) might be found here as well.

The [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) pacifier can be found here.
