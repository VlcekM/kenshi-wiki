**Flotsam Refugees** are people who have escaped the oppression of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and taken refuge under [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) protection. They can be found around [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) tending to farms, as well as running the [Flotsam Bar](/flotsam-bar) and [Flotsam Armory](/flotsam-armory).

If they have the Fearful [Personality](/personality), they will refuse to trade with non-humans, begging them to turn away and leave. After speaking to them again however you will be able to trade.

They have a 2% chance to have a bounty of c.500. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).

-   Greenlander
