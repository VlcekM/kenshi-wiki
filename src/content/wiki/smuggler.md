**Smugglers** are members of the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) faction. Eight can be found in the [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar).

They have a 5% chance of spawning with a bounty ranging from 1,500 to 2,500 cats. They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive Prince
