There are many **Races** in Kenshi, including races for animal characters. Animal races are not listed on this page.

## Playable Races

These races can be selected during the start of the game. Some [Game Starts](/game-starts) have further restrictions on which races can be chosen, but many allow players to choose from all seven options on this list.

Players cannot choose these races at the start of a game, but members of these races can become [Playable Characters](/playable-characters) in certain circumstances.
