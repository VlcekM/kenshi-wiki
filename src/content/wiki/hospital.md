**Hospital** is a vendor found in [Town Overrides](/town-overrides). Hospitals sell [Medical Items](/medical-items). By talking to the surgeon, players can open the [Character Customization](/character-customization) screen for their selected character for some amount of money. These vendors are essentially the exact same as [Holy Hospitals](/holy-hospital).

### Possible Locations

Depending on [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield)
-   [Rebirth](/rebirth)
