**The Old Prison** is the base of [The Gorrillo Bandits](/the-gorrillo-bandits). It is located in the Southeastern corner of the [Bonefields](/bonefields), almost directly South of [Mourn](/mourn). Inside you will find the leader of the faction, [Gorrillo](/gorrillo), and his [Gorillo Bandits](/gorillo-bandit).

The Old Prison complex consists of the main tower resembling an [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab), fairly sophisticated walls and a destroyed [Watchtower](/watchtower), suggesting several prior uses before becoming a home of the bulky brawlers.
