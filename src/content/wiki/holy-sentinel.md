**Holy Sentinels** are guards and middle-ranking foot soldiers for [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), charged with protecting towns and assets such as mines. Sometimes they patrol, usually led by a [Paladin](/paladin).

They frequently stand at the gates of Holy Nation towns, checking outsiders for contraband. They are also responsible for executing heretics via burning in the lore.

Holy Sentinels are distinguished from Paladins in that they use [Combat Cleavers](/combat-cleaver) instead of a [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross), and a sleaveless [Leather Shirt](/leather-shirt) as well as having slightly weaker stats. In terms of appearance, Sentinels have reddish armor with a white emblem, while Paladins have more grayish armor with a yellow emblem.
