The **Stormgap Coast** is part of the territory of the [United Cities](/united-cities).

The wilds of this place is frequented groups of [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates) and some [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers). This is the frontier of United Cities that separates them from the wild lands such as [Reavers](/reavers) of The Outlands and the rest of the "Forbidden Zone" of the Southeast.

## Inhabitants

There are no Ancient Locations in the Stormgap Coast. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
