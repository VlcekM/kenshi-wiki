**Cooking** determines how quickly a character cooks [food](/food) while working on cooking equipment. The [unique](/unique-recruits) recruit [Jewel](/jewel) comes with level 40 cooking. There are other [recruits](/generic-recruits) which come with good cooking and have randomized names.

## Training

Characters can train their **cooking skill** by cooking food, or by brewing alcohol.

## Research

The related technologies to research for cooking are: Cooking, Baking, Advanced Cooking, and Desert Cooking.
