An outpost owned by the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). Its location can be revealed by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Shek Kingdom**.

It may have been a former great fortress that was destroyed and only now partially restored during their conflict with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), similar to the [Shek Ruins](/shek-ruins) nearby.

Even though it has the word 'great' in its name, it certainly doesn't look that way with its many destroyed buildings. The only operational buildings here are one Police Station and one Faction HQ.

Since it's located near [New Kralia](/new-kralia) it is a great spot to turn in [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), the leader of [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), to the police.
