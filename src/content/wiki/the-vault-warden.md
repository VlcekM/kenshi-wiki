**The Vault Warden** is a high-level character located at [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) wielding a [Meitou](/meitou) grade [Jitte](/jitte). He can often be seen taunting prisoners and new arrivals with sarcastic comments.

If this character is killed a [Samurai Captain](/samurai-captain) will replace Warden's position.

They wear \[High\] armour (10% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.
