**Slaves** is a very minor faction. This faction will not appear on the factions list. Slaves can refer to 'escaped slaves' fleeing the [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). Slaves can also refer to characters [enslaved](/slavery) by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

Slaves is a similar faction to [Rebirth Slaves](/rebirth-slaves) and [Outlaw](/outlaw), both of which are factions which primarily spawn enslaved characters.

-   [Lower Servant](/lower-servant)
