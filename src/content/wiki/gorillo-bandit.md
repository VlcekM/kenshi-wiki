**Gorillo Bandits** are members of [The Gorrillo Bandits](/the-gorrillo-bandits). Unlike their leader, [Gorrillo](/gorrillo), they carry no weapons and wear nearly very little armor. They can be seen in packs harassing settlements and wanderers within the [Bonefields](/bonefields) and [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon). A defining feature of a Gorillo Bandit are their unnaturally bulky arms and hunchbacked appearance. Gorillo Bandits can spawn as Sheks, Greenlanders and Scorchlander, however due to having a unique body assigned to them the Scorchlanders will use a Greenlander skin. (Similar to some other Scorchlanders like [Jewel](/jewel) or [Stubs Momuso](/stubs-momuso))

There is a 3% chance that a Gorillo Bandit will have a bounty of c.5,000 from the [United Cities](/united-cities) and the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (30% chance of \[Standard\]).
