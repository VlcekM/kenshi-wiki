**Reaver Blooded** is the main force of the [Reavers](/reavers). They can be found patrolling [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [The Crags](/the-crags), [The Outlands](/the-outlands), and [The Pits East](/the-pits-east). They can also be found in [Ark](/ark) and in [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).

Reaver Bloodeds will enslave any nearby unconscious characters.

These characters have a 25% chance of spawning with a crossbow. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (20% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) (200), [Unknown](/unknown) (50), or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) (50).
