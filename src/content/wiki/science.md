Science is the skill which governs the rate of researching new [technologies](/technology).

Characters can gain experience in Science by using [Research Bench](/research-bench), [Electrical Workbench](/electrical-workbench) or [Hemp Processor](/hemp-processor).

The rate at which you research at is 0.5+0.009(Science).
