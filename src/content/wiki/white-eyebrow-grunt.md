**White Eyebrow Grunt** is a member of the mythical [White Eyebrow Clan](/white-eyebrow-clan). They are not set to spawn anywhere in game.

These characters, if they existed, would be almost identical to [White Eyebrow](/white-eyebrow) himself. The primary differences between them are that Grunts have no bounty and have a 50% chance of being female.
