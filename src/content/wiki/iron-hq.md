The **Iron HQ** is an outpost located in [The Crags](/the-crags). This is the [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) and only settlement of the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits), housing their leader [Elder](/elder).

This outpost is made up of [Wallhouses](/metal-wallhouse) (presumably acting as barracks) and a [Faction HQ](/faction-hq). Two of the Wallhouses are for sale.

[Artifacts](/artifacts) which spawn in this town aren't only in the main building, they can also appear in the [Metal Wallhouses](/metal-wallhouse).

It is possible to ally with the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) by speaking with [Elder](/elder), with him being located within the central lab, although this can be difficult for [Humans](/human) since the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) in the HQ are hostile. If the player is a [Skeleton](/skeleton), this is easier and will grant unique dialogue with [Elder](/elder).

## Town overrides

**Iron HQ** can be affected by multiple [World States](/world-states) to produce the following [Town Overrides](/town-overrides).

-   [Destroyed](/iron-hq/destroyed)
-   [Southern Hive](/iron-hq/southern-hive)

  

## Trivia

-   If you are allied with the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) then be careful staying in the HQ for too long, since the stationed [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandit) there can bodyguard you, which may also include [Elder](/elder). This can inadvertently lead to [Elder](/elder)'s unintentional demise, and for the HQ to switch to the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive).
-   Even though [Elder](/elder) will become hostile to you if you trespass as a [Human](/human), he will still allow you to talk to him and immediately join, making the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) your allies.
