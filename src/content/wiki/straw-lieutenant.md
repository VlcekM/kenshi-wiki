**Straw Lieutenants** are members of the [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates). They can be found leading squads of 5 to 12 [Grass Pirate](/grass-pirate) characters on patrols of [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

Straw Lieutenants have a 10% chance of spawning with a [bounty](/bounty) ranging from c.1,000 to c.3,000.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown) or [Ancient](/ancient).

They only have a 25% chance to spawn with a weapon. The other 75% of the time, they will be unarmed.

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
