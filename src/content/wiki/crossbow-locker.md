The **Crossbow Locker** is as [Storage](/storage) container visually identical to the [Weapon Cabinet](/weapon-cabinet). The crossbow locker can only be used to store [Crossbows](/crossbows).

## Details

This container has a storage space of **18x18** squares.

## Notes

-   As of patch 1.0.19, a bug exists with the crossbow locker allowing any item to be placed inside. Additionally, removes the stolen tag from any item placed inside.
