Vagrant Camps are camps belonging to [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) that are seen in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng-zone). Squads of squatters can be seen around here, and more rarely [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) too.
