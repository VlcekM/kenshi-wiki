Holy Farms are a series of similar [minor outposts](/minor-outposts) of [Greenlanders](/human) located in territory owned by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and are the presumed source of [food](/trade-goods) and population base for the faction. These places are mostly occupied by farmers alongside having some military presence. Their locations can be learned by reading the **Glorious Holy [Map](/maps).**

There are in total 6 Holy Farms, 4 of them are located in [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) while the other 2 are located in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley). The northernmost is the only one growing Cactus whilst the others grow Greenfruit, Cotton and Wheat.

## Behavior

While [Holy Farmers](/holy-farmer), [Holy Farmer Wives](/holy-farmer-wife) and domesticated animals make up the vast majority of the population, the only NPC with dialogue options is the [Holy Farm Leader](/holy-farm-leader) who will trade farm based [trade goods](/trade-goods) and domesticated animals with the player.

Every farm has at least one field of crops and is typically used for growing either [cotton](/cotton), [greenfruit](/greenfruit) or [wheat](/wheatstraw) (the northernmost farm has [cactus](/cactus)) that is tended to by the Holy Farmers during the day.

Holy Farmer Wives do not seem to have a labor schedule, offer dialogue options or even spawn with a weapon. They remain inside with the Holy Farm Leader the majority of the time and flee during combat.

## World States

Although not a town override, if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is dead and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive, the Holy Farmers (and their leaders and wives) will lament Phoenix's demise and complain about sheks hounding them for [tributes](/tribute-round), implying that Shek raiders have been demanding tributes from them on threats of violence.

These include 2 different dialogues:

-   \*sigh\* Okran has forsaken us... The Holy Lord Phoenix himself is gone... And the barbarian devils hound us for tributes we can't afford.

And also:

-   Farmer 1: The warlord devils are back already?!
-   Farmer 2: No, it's okay, that's not them...
-   Farmer 1: How can we afford to give away all our food like this? \*sigh\* The devils and their tribute... they're relentless
-   Farmer 2: At least we're alive and well, sister...
-   Farmer 1: ... Okran have mercy on us.

Despite the dialogue, since there are no overrides for Holy Farms in this scenario, the food levels will remain the exact same in-game.

(As farms are clearly still under The Holy Nation control in this scenario, and these lines can happen even when [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is still alive and clearly still blocking large portions of Shek Warriors from invading [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, it would suggest it's less a case of Shek Kingdom control and occupation of Holy Farms, and more of [periodic raids](/return-for-blood) [and demands](/return-for-tribute) from their roaming shek forces that targets certain parts of farming settlements.

Shek Kingdom's entire nation could [go completely Kamikaze](/kamikaze) and they would still fail to destroy the farms even if [all](/high-inquisitor-seta) [the](/high-inquisitor-valtena) [main](/holy-lord-phoenix) Holy Nation leaders are incapacitated, suggesting that the population base and military presence of the farms are simply too large for Sheks to wipe out even if the holy army is at its [most disorganized state](/strayed-paladin).)

**Note:** When this particular dialogue is achieved, farmers are notably vulnerable to being attacked and slaughtered by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) (and potentially [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)) forces as the [World State](/world-states) for achieving this dialogue directly ties with their patrol spawns inside regions with Holy Farms. Players should either be prepared to fight Shek Kingdom to protect the farmers, or risk losing the ability to trade with [Holy Farm Leaders](/holy-farm-leader).

## Town overrides

_Main article: [Holy Farm/Slave Traders](/holy-farm/slave-traders)_

Holy Farms will be [overridden](/town-overrides) by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and either [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) _and_/_or_ [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) (if both are killed, it will switch to the below overrides shortly after achieving this one) have been killed/kidnapped, as long as [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are still alive and free.

Tengu and Longen's status wouldn't matter _after_ this override is achieved, however, it could change into overrides below in the following circumstances:

If _both_ [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) after achieving this override are killed/captured the overriding faction can then become (it is assigned the same override as [Holy Mines](/holy-mines)):

-   [Holy Mines/Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive and free (only possible if she is captured and released after achieving this override).
-   [Holy Mines/Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is dead and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive.
-   [Holy Mines/Destroyed](/holy-mines/destroyed) if both [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are dead.
