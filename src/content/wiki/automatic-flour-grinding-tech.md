**Automatic Flour Grinding** is [Farming Tech](/farming-tech) which allows the player to construct an [Automatic Grain Silo](/grain-silo).

This research makes turning [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) into [Strawflour](/strawflour) more convenient. Players who plan on [Cooking](/cooking) [Bread](/bread) often should consider completing this research.
