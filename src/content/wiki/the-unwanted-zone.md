**The Unwanted Zone** fully lives up to its name, unless players have very skilled fighters and are in need of [Animal Skins](/animal-skin) or [Raw Meat](/raw-meat). It is densely populated by [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Crabs](/crab), and they often come into conflicts with one another. There are many Nests in this Zone, making it a good source of [Crab Eggs](/crab-egg) and [Beak Thing Eggs](/beak-thing-egg).

Players hoping for a good path from [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) (home to the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)) to [The Outlands](/the-outlands) (home of Anti-Slaver recruit [Bo](/bo)) are recommended to go around this Zone or have a member capable of using a [Falling Sun](/falling-sun) or other anti-Beak Thing weaponry to deal with the abundant and fast-running [Gutters](/gutters) in these areas.

Alternatively, the players could venture through the neighboring [Venge](/venge), albeit they are recommended to wear [Dustcoat](/dustcoat) or travel at night to not be burnt.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or Ancient Locations in The Unwanted Zone. This zone does not spawn Camps.

### Nests

[_Nests_](/nest) _are temporary locations which can spawn randomly and typically belong to [Animals](/fauna). The zone has a max number nest of 22._
