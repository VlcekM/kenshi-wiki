**Headshot** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is a slave hunter who specializes in [Turrets](/turrets) and [Crossbows](/crossbows). She costs c.7,500 to recruit and can be found in either [Eyesocket](/eyesocket) or [Slave Markets](/slave-markets) in a Slaver Bar.

She has a Traitorous personality.
