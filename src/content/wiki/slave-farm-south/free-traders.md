Slave Farm South, Free Traders is a farm town in [The Hook](/the-hook) under the influence of [Free Traders](/free-traders). It belonged to [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) until [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) and [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) are both killed.

Despite appearing as a town icon, the place has in fact shrinked in size with more destroyed buildings than before.

This town appears to be a semi-rebuilt slave camp into a farm town by Free Traders after the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) influence have completely been removed from the area.

Since the place is ruled by Free Traders, [Free Traders Trade Culture](/free-traders-trade-culture) is applied. Be aware that [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture) disappears from this location when the override occurs.
