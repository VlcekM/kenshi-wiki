**Ancient Samurai Legplates** are Heavy Armour equipped in the Legwear slot. Due to its age, it has less resistances compared to its [modern version](/samurai-legplates) while having the same penalties, making this armour an inferior choice.

It is worn as a part of a set together with [Ancient Samurai Armour](/ancient-samurai-armour). [Ancient Samurai Helmet](/ancient-samurai-helmet) and [Ancient Samurai Boots](/ancient-samurai-boots) could have been part of this set, but they do not spawn in game.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x5**.

## Appearance

**Ancient Samurai Legplates** look like normal [Samurai Legplates](/samurai-legplates) that are covered in rust.
