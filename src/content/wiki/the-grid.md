**The Grid** is an area northeast of [the great crater](/the-crater) and southwest of [the swamp](/the-swamp). It contains a [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex) with two workshops, so it's a great place to find high grade weapons, [engineering research](/engineering-research) and [ancient research books](/ancient-science-book). This place is surrounded by spikey split in a grid-like manner.

## Lore

According to the pre-alpha description within the [CPU of Cat-Lon](/cpu-of-cat-lon), "Science and archaeology flourished in the empire until an explosion in the Grid caused a famine". This may explain the presence of a huge crater on the zones southwestern extremes though it is unclear what exactly these large rock formations originally were.

Though the CPU is a pre-alpha item, the mention of a famine is still supported by the [CPU of the Head of Agriculture](/cpu-of-the-head-of-agriculture), which states that "He could never have forseen the events that happened, but he still blamed himself for the famine, and the subsequent downfall of the empire". This makes it clear that a famine did happen, though whether the explosion in the Grid was the cause of it is yet to be confirmed.

### Possible Theories

Using the pre-alpha description of the CPU of Cat-Lon, fans theorize The Grid was an agricultural center with irrigation around farms built on the high ground of each grid square. Further evidence of this is the fertility of the land between each high rock, eventually showing evidence of drainage into the modern day Swamp with a puddle to the northeast. This indicates that the soil, even the top soil, was a good host for life. However, this fan theory does not suggest any further details about The Grid and its relationship to [The Crater](/the-crater) with regards to the spiky rock protrusions as seen most abundantly on the outside rim of The Crater, and without pattern among the grid squares. It is unclear as to whether this is an effect of the explosion that created The Crater, or a natural feature exaggerated by it.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in The Grid. This zone does not spawn Camps or have any Homeless Spawns.
