The **Second Empire** was formed by [Skeletons](/skeletons) after the collapse of the [Old Empire](/old-empire), located in the modern-day [Ashlands](/ashlands).

They had a prominent military order known as the [Hydraulic Knights](/hydraulic-knight), led by [General Jang](/general-jang). They also had a highly skilled [police](/old-empire-police-captain) force.

Around a few hundred years before the events of Kenshi, the Second Empire collapsed, with the [United Cities](/united-cities) becoming the next iteration of the Empire (implied to be through a rebellion). The remnants of the Second Empire became known as the [Second Empire Exiles](/second-empire-exile), led by one of the fallen Empire's founders, [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon), whose sanity was severely compromised over the years.
