Located next to the [Deadlands](/deadlands) and [Iron Valleys](/iron-valleys), **The Black Desert** gets its name from dark moving poison clouds and dark sand in the region. Poison clouds will hurt your chest or stomach at a rapid rate.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or Ancient Locations in The Black Desert. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest (**0.33x** multiplier)._

-   [Black Desert Ninjas](/black-desert-ninjas)
    -   Black Desert Ninjas (3 ~ 9 [Cloud Ninjas](/cloud-ninja)) {1}

## Tips

-   Avoid walking into the gas clouds unless wearing gas protection gear. Characters will cough, heave, gasp, hack, wheeze, and say my lungs... when entering.
-   Make sure you're wearing [shoes](/footwear) or characters will complain about their feet cooking while barefooted and will ask you to leave the area.
