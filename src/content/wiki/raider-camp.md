**Raider Camps** are randomly spawning camps. They can spawn in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng-zone).

This location spawns [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) which the player can use for free, however since [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) tend to be aggressive without allying with them it is not advised to use the beds before taking care of the threat.

However, due to their relatively high population, they can be an easy way to quickly level up [Stealth](/stealth) if you are not allied to them.

As a result of the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)' hostility towards neutral factions and their poor combat abilities, their camps are often wiped out by roaming [Garru](/garru) herds.

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this camp. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### Rebel Farmers Patrol

-   x2
    -   3-10 [Outlaw Farmer](/outlaw-farmer)
    -   1-6 [Outlaw Swordsman](/outlaw-swordsman)

## Item Spawns

These items will spawn randomly with a min of 0 and a max of 10 total in each Raider camp, with the following relative chance. Data is from FCS.

-   [Hashish](/hashish) (22)
-   [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum) (5)
-   [Water Jug](/water-jug) (200)
-   [Chewsticks](/chewsticks) (50)
-   [Cup](/cup) (300)
-   [Damaged Book](/damaged-book) (100)
-   [Dried Meat](/dried-meat) (50)
-   [Greenfruit](/greenfruit) (50)
-   [Grog](/grog) (5)
-   [Wooden Bowl](/wooden-bowl) (200)
-   [Book](/book) (50)
-   [Empty Grog Can](/empty-grog-can) (100)

It can also spawn up to 1 [Trade Good](/trade-goods).

## World States

If [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed/imprisoned, **Raider Camps** will become under the control of [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) instead of [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers).
