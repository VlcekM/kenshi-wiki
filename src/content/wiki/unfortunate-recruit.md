> Me? I'd prefer to bury my past and make a new story actually, brother.
> 
> —**Unfortunate Recruit**

**Unfortunate Recruit** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits) available in the [United Cities](/united-cities). They are characters who have had some sort of unfortunate or dark past and are looking to get out of town. You can hire them for c.3,000.

They have a Random [Personality](/personality) and after recruited will have the 'Npc TOUGH Merc Aggro 2' and 'Player TOUGH Merc' Dialogue Package. They can spawn as [Greenlanders](/greenlander), [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander), or [Hive Soldier Drones](/hive-soldier-drone). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
