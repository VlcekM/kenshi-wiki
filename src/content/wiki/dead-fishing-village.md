The **Dead Fishing Village** is a location inhabited by over 30 [ErrorCode 0x000000](/errorcode-0x000000) skeletons. These skeletons are of the [Old Machines](/old-machines) faction and will attempt to "arrest" any characters which enter this location. The village consists of two Watchtowers and four Old Storm Houses filled with these robots. An attack on any of these houses will prompt reinforcements to arrive from all the others.

## Tips

-   Beware that the acid rain can occur in this area. Bring acid protection or materials to construct [tents](/camping) to wait out the rain.
-   [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) that belong to the same faction often roam these parts in large groups. Look out for enemy reinforcements in this area because groups of enemies can rush in one after another and overwhelm an unprepared group.

## Trivia

-   Once the reinforcements are called, the "Police" can be seen on the map with red dots.
-   Although the town is owned by the Fisher faction, no character in game exists who is a member of it.
