The **Cult Village** is home to [The Preacher](/the-preacher) and its followers. [Farmers](/farmer-preacher-cult) farm outside the town walls and if the guards have taken prisoners, will claim that "blood in the soil brings harvest" and other cultist nonsense.

Extreme caution must be taken as any squad members that are knocked unconscious will be locked in a prisoner pole and if all of the poles are in use the cultists will begin to beat prisoners to death.

This location is one of the [two](/tower-of-goats) in the game for procurement of the "most valuable" [Half-Eaten Ancient Science Book](/half-eaten-ancient-science-book) (spoiler warning: it's not valuable at all, but somewhat memorable).

## Town overrides

_Main article: [Cult Village/Slave Traders](/cult-village/slave-traders)_

If [Tinfist](/tinfist) is killed or imprisoned and both [Longen](/longen) and [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande) are alive and free, this place is taken over by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).
