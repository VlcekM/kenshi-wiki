The **Bughouse** is one of the Medium sized buildings and can be [constructed](/construction) by player. Players can construct this building after researching [Medium Building Shells](/medium-building-shells-tech). Buildings can be placed on the interior of the building as well as the roof.

## Bughouse Building Info

-   Building Condition - 35
-   Door Condition - 16
-   Door Lock - lvl15
