The Holy Empire were a neutral [faction](/factions-alpha) (or hostile depending on what character you start with) that owned the towns of [Shark](/shark-alpha), [EyeSocket](/eyesocket-alpha), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch-alpha), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam-alpha) and [Fort Simion](/fort-simion-alpha) in the Oldworld. You could often find 6-8 men patrols of the Holy Empire roaming the desert. Soldiers of the Holy Nation used Horse Choppers and Straight Cleavers. 

If you start with the Retired Captain or Holy Sword character start, they will be hostile toward you.

They evolved into [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) in the final game.
