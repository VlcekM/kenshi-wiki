**Robots** are forms of mechanical or synthetic life found in Kenshi. They are often relics of the [Old Empire](/old-empire) and the Second Empire. A majority of humanoid robots are [Skeleton](/skeleton) subraces, but not all.

There are specific weapons which have damage modifiers which cause that weapon to do extra damage towards robots. Robotic Humanoids (Robotic Animals are unaffected by this) and Robotic limbs on any race take _wear damage_ equal to 4% of damage taken. As an example, if a Skeleton is struck for 100 damage, their new max health is 196. Wear damage taken needs to be healed using a [Skeleton Repair bed](/skeleton-repair-bed) in order to reach max 200 health again.
