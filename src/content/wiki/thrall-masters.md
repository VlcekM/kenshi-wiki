**Thrall Masters** is a bandit faction composed of reprogrammed ([enthralled](/skeleton-no-head-mkii)) [Skeletons](/skeleton) found in [Venge](/venge), and occasionally in [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) and [The Eye](/the-eye). Their HQ is located in the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse).
