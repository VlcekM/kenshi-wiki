The **Tower of Ninjas** is the home of the [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas). It is located in the northeast region of the [Border Zone](/border-zone). This location can be discovered by reading a **Tech Hunters [Map](/maps)**.

Upon entering the tower, the Black Dragon Ninja faction becomes hostile and attacks; they will, however, also attack mobs like [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), so you can lead a group in to help. The layout of the tower is very similar to that of a [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) tower, with [Black Dragon Genin](/black-dragon-genin) manning four crossbow turrets on the roof.

The location is home to [Dimak](/dimak) and [Buzan](/buzan), the leaders of the faction, both of whom are wanted by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) with a 20,000 cat price on each. Dimak wields a [Meitou](/meitou) quality [Ninja Blade](/ninja-blade).
