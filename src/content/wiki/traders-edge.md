**Trader's Edge** is a settlement and probable [special economic zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_economic_zone) in [Heng](/heng-zone), territory of the [United Cities](/united-cities). Although it's run by [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), the [United Cities](/united-cities) protects its entrance. It is located on a plateau next to the city [Heng](/heng) and connected by a metal bridge.

This is also the home of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) HQ. Inside the HQ is their leader [Longen](/longen), as well as a potential recruit [Yamdu](/yamdu).

The economy of this town relies on supplies from [Stone Camp](/stone-camp), [South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp) and [Eyesocket](/eyesocket), the same as Heng.

The player can often encounter [Harmothoe](/harmothoe)'s trade caravan in Trader's Edge or its neighboring city, Heng.

It is the location of the "Wandering Trader" [game start](/game-starts).
