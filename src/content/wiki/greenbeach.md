**Greenbeach** is a zone primarily controlled by the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders), with occasional skirmishes with [Reavers](/reavers).

It is notable for its abundance of [Crabs](/crabs), with occasional sights of [Beak Things](/beak-thing).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or Ancient Locations in Greenbeach. This zone does not spawn Camps.

### Minor Outposts

_These locations are typically small towns, villages, or faction bases._

-   [Crab Village](/crab-village)

### Nests

[_Nests_](/nest) _are temporary locations which can spawn randomly and typically belong to [Animals](/fauna). This zone has a max number nest of 6._

-   [Crab Nest](/crab-nest)

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. (**1.2x** multiplier) This list is in order of likelihood._

-   [Crabs](/crab)
    -   Crab Wander Solo {10}
    -   Crab Wander Group {10}
    -   Massive Crab Wander {1}
-   [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders)
    -   Crab Raiders Patrol {10} ([Crab Queen](/crab-queen) alive)
    -   Crab Raiders Broken {3} ([Crab Queen](/crab-queen) dead)
-   [Reavers](/reavers)
    -   Reaver Patrol {5} ([Valamon](/valamon) alive)
-   [Beak Things](/beak-thing)
    -   Beak Squad Small {4}
