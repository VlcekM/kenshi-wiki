**Forbidden Sympathy** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to Flotsam HQ in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in the inventories of [Holy Citizens](/holy-citizen).

This item itself is also considered an illegal item by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). If held in the player's inventory during a bag search at a Holy Nation town, that character will receive a [bounty](/bounty) for holding illegal goods and subsequently attacked and/or arrested.
