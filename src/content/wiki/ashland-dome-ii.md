**Ashland Dome II** is a large, multi-floored, dome-like structure located in the [Ashlands](/ashlands). The dome has two entrances and one winding staircase in the middle, leading to the higher floors. It is located south of [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i) and can be marked by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Ashlands**.

It houses [Rhinobot](/rhinobot) and several [Screamer Units](/screamer-unit).
