**Beep** is a Hiveless [Western Worker Drone](/Hive_Worker_Drone) who can be found in [Mongrel](/Mongrel). He is a [Unique Recruit](/Unique_Recruits) and can be recruited at no cost as he seems to have no understanding of money. He will follow around the player faction when he sees them until the dialogue is initiated.

He beeps occasionally and has many lines of unique dialogue, proclaiming himself the "STRONGEST WARRIOR" and "Cyber-beep" after limb replacements, as well as some extra lines of dialogue with [Agnu](/Agnu).

Beep has a Brave/Dumb/Honorable [Personality](/Personality).

## Backstory

Beep was exiled from the [Hive](/Western_Hive) for "being defective," and ever since then, he has had to run away from enemies. He's tired of running, and he wants to join your faction.

## Trivia

-   Beep can transform into a being known (to himself) as Cyber Beep. When he is Cyber Beep, he receives more unique dialogue and will often talk to skeletons with dialogue such as "Beep is also a skeleton". Humorously, this can be used to interrupt [Cat-Lon's](/Mad_Cat-Lon) speech or prevent you from trading with a trader if they are a Skeleton until the dialogue finishes.
-   Beep is featured in a background available for unlocking through [Steam Trading Cards](/Steam_Trading_Cards).
-   Beep is one of the few characters whose dialogue is written by Chris Hunt.
-   Beep will almost always beep when waking up, whether from a KO or from sleeping to recover wounds, sometimes when fighting he may spam the dialogue box with hundreds of Beeps.
-   Beep may also beep when reloading saves.
-   Beep's name is strange due to the fact that Hivers choose their names based on the first thing they see.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
-   Beep is the most important character in the game, according to many players.
-   If you were to dismiss Beep (Provided he is not too powerful to recruit after being dismissed) you would be able to speak with him again to recruit him again.
-   When placed into a peeler machine instead of saying the usual dialogue Beep will repeat the phrase "Beep!" Until removed
-   Beep!
