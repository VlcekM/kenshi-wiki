**Slave Farm South** in [The Hook](/the-hook) is where characters are taken after they are enslaved by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) or [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters). Slaves can also come from the characters who break the law in [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) and are then given to the Slavers.

There is also another slave farm, called simply the [Slave Farm](/slave-farm). It is controlled by [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and is located in [Heng](/heng-zone).

Slave Farm South is the home of [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace).

## Town Overrides

-   If **Slave Mistress Grace** is killed or imprisoned, then **Slave Farm South** will fall to ruins.
-   [Slave Farm South, Free Traders](/slave-farm-south-free-traders) – if **Slave Mistress Grace** and [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) are both either killed or imprisoned, **Slave Farm South** will be taken over by [Free Traders](/free-traders).

## Tips

-   If you're being brought to the Slave Farm from [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last), the slavers will have to swim to get there. While in the water, they can't attack you for escaping. They will not chase you if you swim away from them.
-   The easiest way to escape from inside the Slave Farm is to wait until your character is being ordered to farm in one of the farms outside of the walls. Remove the "obedient slave" job and start running to the [Waystation](/waystation-the-hook). Before this, you should already pick the lock on your shackles so that you can remove them while running away.
