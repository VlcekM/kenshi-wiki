They expand Kenshi's arsenal with new weapon types and unique variants. This page lists all weapons introduced by UWE and their stats.

## Overview

UWE Weapons diversify combat options by introducing:

-   New weapons for various functios from early to late game
-   Rebalanced damage values
-   Faction-exclusive gear
-   Unique models and textures

## Acquisition

UWE Weapons can be obtained through:

-   Crafting at weapon benches (once research is completed).
-   Purchasing from certain vendors (varies by faction).
-   Looting from enemies, ruins, and unique NPCs.

## Notable Changes from Vanilla

-   Expanded weapon variety for NPCs and factions.
-   Adjusted weapon balance for immersion and difficulty.
-   Significantly reduced weapon value for a more challenging experience, especially in early game.
-   Enhanced visuals and naming conventions for better distinction.

## Notes

-   UWE continues to update, and weapon stats may change between versions.
