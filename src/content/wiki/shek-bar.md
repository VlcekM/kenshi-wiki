**Shek Bar** is a [bar](/bar) found in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). This variant is used in all Shek Kingdom towns which have a bar. The most distinct difference between this bar and ones found outside Shek Kingdom is that this bar's residents are exclusively [Shek](/shek) races.

### Possible Locations

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Last Stand](/last-stand)
-   [Squin](/squin)

Depending on World States, there may be a Independent Bar found in these locations:

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)
-   [Squin](/squin)
-   [Stack](/stack)
