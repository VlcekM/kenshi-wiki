The **Great White Claw** is an item obtained after killing the [Great White Gorillo](/great-white-gorillo) trapped in the locked headquarters in [Mourn](/mourn)**.**

If you talk to a bartender while the Great White Claw is in your inventory, he will congratulate you and give you free [Sake](/sake) and [Grog](/grog).
