This **nasty machine** is something the player should avoid. Owned by the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits), they will use it to skin alive [Humans](/human) that they have captured and taken to one of their [Skinhouses](/skinhouse).

Aside from damaging the character inside of it, the Peeler Machine functions exactly like a [Prisoner Cage](/prisoner-cage). This means that placing any characters into them will count as imprisoning them, and any effects for imprisoning a character in a [Prisoner Cage](/prisoner-cage) (such as faction relationship changes) will also occur. However, if a character of any [faction](/factions) dies in a peeler machine, faction relations will not be affected any further. The Peeler Machine also produces [Animal Skins](/animal-skin) (somehow even from skeletons).

The Peeler Machine works through 3 stages of its victim before it becomes inactive, if there is any clothing worn on the character, they will be destroyed by the peeler over time, after which the peeler will begin to deal cut damage to the victim's limbs. Once all limbs have fallen below 0 health it begins to deal damage to the characters vitals. It does not require the victim to be alive for this machine to work. Occasionally a peeler machine may bug and not damage a character's vitals even with all limbs below 0 health, and will stop damaging the vitals of a character if they are low enough.

As of update 0.99.5 (experimental) it is possible to acquire the blueprint of this from [Savant](/savant).

## Trivia

-   Running into a player owned peeler machine will sometimes cause your character to take damage as if they were inside the machine itself. This can cause your armour to be destroyed if you react too slowly. This continues until your legs are crippled.
-   (Bug) Rarely sometimes characters are able to attack others while inside a peeler.
-   (Bug) In extremely rare cases a character may have their model changed to appear flayed, while still alive.
-   If a character has their leg health drop below their KO point (And their arms are above 0 health) they are able to bandage/repair themselves while inside of a peeler machine.
-   Characters can still be damaged by attacks while inside a Peeler machine. If you attack another character and your attack connects with the unit inside the peeler machine they will be hit as well.
-   This machine does not require any power to use.
-   In the final stage of peeling, when a character has reached a point that their texture is altered to show only muscular tissue, peeled characters may no longer be able to be picked up or looted. This is a minor bug, and these bodies will eventually despawn. The peeler will show their body within itself but continue to function normally for subsequent characters.
-   Skeletons have a different dialogue pack when they are being peeled. Instead of screaming, they will just mope on about how their parts are being damaged with lines such as "Nooo...", "No, don't-- \*crack\*" and "No, that's my-- \*snap\*"
-   Characters put into a peeler machine will normally scream/yell even when knocked unconscious/in recovery coma.
