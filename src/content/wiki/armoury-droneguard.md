**Armoury Droneguards** are members of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). They can be found in [Hiver-Claimed Armoury](/hiver-claimed-armoury) and in some locations after they have been [overtaken](/town-overrides) by the Southern Hive.

Given the increased weapon quality these type of Droneguards have, it's likely that the weapon they have is looted from the armoury that they have taken over.
