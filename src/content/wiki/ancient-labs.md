**Ancient Labs** are the remnants of a time long past. Once a functional part of advanced civilizations, they now lay in shambles. Treasure seekers should be wary, as the ruins have not gone altogether silent. The mechanical sentries of the past still roam the rubble, and those who wish to salvage the lost tech from these labs must face down many [Security Spiders](/security-spider). [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) have scouted these locations and [mapped](/maps) them for brave treasure seekers.

## Enemies

-   [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider)
-   [Security Spiders](/security-spider)
-   [Broken Skeletons](/broken-skeleton)

## Notable Loot

-   [AI Core](/ai-core)
-   [Ancient Science Book](/ancient-science-book)
-   [CPU Unit](/cpu-unit)
-   [Generator Core](/generator-core)

## Locations

Currently in the world, there are fourteen Ancient Labs.

### Burning Forest Lab

There is an Ancient Lab in the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest). It is occupied by the Security Spiders and therefore still contains loot from ancient times.

### The Crater Labs

Inside of [The Crater](/the-crater), there are many [Empty Labs](/empty-lab) and one Ancient Lab to the north.

### Crumbling Labs

In [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) east of [Spring](/spring), there is [Crumbling Labs](/crumbling-labs) inside of an acid lake. This location has two Ancient Labs buildings, but one is ruined and contains [King Gorillo](/king-gorillo). The other Ancient Lab will likely contain many Security Spiders.

### The Floodland Lab

In the center of the [Floodlands](/floodlands), there is an Ancient Lab. Security Spiders control this lab.

### The Forbidden Lab

Far to the east of the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and in the northernmost corner of the [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle), there is an Ancient Lab.

### The Infested Lab

The [Infested Lab](/infested-lab), is a lab in the southeastern part of [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

### The Iron Lab

This [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab) is on the westernmost section of [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail). After passing numerous [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) to reach the lab, it will likely be inhabited by six [Security Spiders](/security-spider) and a singular [Broken Skeleton](/broken-skeleton).

### Island Lab

The [Island Lab](/island-lab) is an Ancient Lab located on the southwest tip of [Fishman Island](/fishman-island). It is the home of many [Fishmen](/fishmen) as well as their leader, [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler).

### Narko's Trap

This [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab) can be found in the eastern edge of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s territory. The Holy Nation is keeping [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap) under heavy guard so that no one can "sin" by studying the technology inside. Two [Security Spiders](/security-spider) are inside of the lab itself.

### Obedience

There is an Ancient Lab in [Obedience](/obedience). It possibly contains no [AI Cores](/ai-core).

### Tower of Abuse

[Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse) is a location in [Venge](/venge). This [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab) is the home of the leaders of the [Thrall Masters](/thrall-masters) and their prisoner [Agnu](/agnu).

### The Shun Lab

In the center of the northwestern peninsula of the region of [Shun](/shun), there is an [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab). This lab will likely contain many [Security Spiders](/security-spider).

### Suspicious Lab

The [Suspicious Lab](/suspicious-lab) is an Ancient Lab which was overrun by [Cannibals](/cannibals). The Cannibals are much easier to sneak past than Security Spiders are, however, this Lab does not contain an [AI Core](/ai-core). Only [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) can be found on the floor of the Lab and inside the boxes upstairs.

### Swamped Lab

The [Swamped Lab](/swamped-lab) is an Ancient Lab which was lost to [The Swamp](/the-swamp). It contains many [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) inside which swarm outside when the gate is unlocked.

## For sale

If the player kills or imprisons [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Longen](/longen) and [Tinfist](/tinfist), the Ancient Lab in in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge). will be put for sale at c.320,000. It becomes the most expensive investment in the game, as Trader’s Edge turns to the Empire Peasants override.

## Trivia

-   Given the Ancient Labs proximity to multiple anomalies inside Kenshi, it is probable that they are responsible for various experiments that may've caused lasting (and sometimes devastating) consequences to the lands of Kenshi. These are some possible speculations.
    -   Machine-Manufacturing or Modification:
        -   The Ancient Lab of [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle) (specifically the [Spider Foreman](/spider-foreman) inside it) are directly tied to the presence of [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) in the region, which implies that it may still be functioning and operated by Spider Foreman.
        -   The one in Venge is directly linked to presence of [Weak Thralls](/weak-thrall) (specifically by [Ponk](/ponk) and [Screamer](/screamer-the-false) who are inside it) and may still be functional and operated by them.
        -   The one in [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail) coincides with the large amount of Iron Spiders in the region.
        -   The one in [Floodlands](/floodlands) and [Obedience](/obedience) coincides with the machine presence in the region.
    -   Biological-Modification and Experiments
        -   The one in [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) are theorized by some to cause the [Cannibals](/cannibals) to be present (see [The Cannibal Plains: De-evolution of Man?](/the-cannibal-plains-de-evolution-of-man)).
        -   The one in [Fishman Island](/fishman-island) coincides with the large presence of [Fishmen](/fishmen) in the region.
        -   The one in [Shun](/shun) coincides with the large presence of [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) (which may have originated from the adjacent [Arach](/arach) but may have also caused their presence there too).
        -   The one in [The Burning Forest](/burning-forest) coincide with the significant presence of [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) (which may have originated from the adjacent [Swamp](/the-swamp) but may have also caused their presence there too).
        -   The one in [The Crater](/the-crater) coincides with the large presence of [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) and [Beak Things](/beak-thing) in the region.
        -   The one in [Obedience](/obedience) are very close to the neighboring [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) and [Fogmen](/fogmen) (although they are at 2 separate zones, they share similar themes and are adjacent with one another).
    -   Environmental Degradation or Modification
        -   The one in [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) coincides with the Acidic rain presence in the zone it is located, and many adjacent and connected zones ([Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast) and [Sinkuun](/sinkuun)), The [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone) and [Dreg](/dreg) which also have acidic presence are not directly adjacent and somewhat distant, but are close enough to may have possibly been affected.
        -   The one in [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle) coincides with the Acidic rain presence in the zone it is located, and many adjacent, close and connected zones ([None](/none-coast), [Howler Maze](/howler-maze), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and very close but not directly connected [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone)).
        -   The one in [Burning Forest](/burning-forest) coincides with the Acidic rain presence in the zone it is located, which is notably, not spread to, or from, any other adjacent zones.
        -   The one in [Floodlands](/floodlands) coincides with the heavy presence of rain and water located in the region.
        -   The one in [Obedience](/obedience) coincides with a permanent foggy atmosphere, shared with the neighboring [Fog Islands](/fog-islands).
        -   The one in [Venge](/venge) coincides with the Burning weather found in that region, which may have something to do with the control tower (broken or functioning).
