The **Chainmail Sheet Fabrication Bench** is a crafting building type introduced in patch 0.50. This crafting station allows players to craft [Chainmail Sheets](/chainmail-sheet) by hand. These sheets can then be fashioned into armour at a [Chain Armour Crafting Bench](/chain-armour-crafting-bench), training the [Armour Smithing](/armour-smith) skill.
