The **Skeleton Legion** are a faction of [Skeletons](/skeleton) found in [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark) and the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

## Background

The Skeleton Legion has its origins in the [Second Empire](/second-empire). Not much is known about this shadowy faction, but they are presumably the remnants of the Second Empire's skeletal military. Much of the Legion is made up of [thralls](/skeleton-no-head-mkii), likely victims of the [Emperor's](/mad-cat-lon) crazed mass-thrallings.

The Legion works with remnants of the Second Empire, its thralls guarding the [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture) in [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i).

Scouts and Legionnaires of the faction are not enthralled ([headless](/skeleton-no-head-mkii)), whereas its main forces, Legion Thralls, are numerous and make the bulk of the group. Their emperor is now [exiled](/second-empire-exile), and the Legion has no especial love or hatred for him. It's possible they are enforcing his exile - perhaps to stop him from enthralling _all_ the Second Empire remnants. Still, if the player defeats and kills their emperor, the army will apparently go berserk, as the Legion's patrols will increase in size after Cat-Lon's death.
