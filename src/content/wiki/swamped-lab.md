The **Swamped Lab** is an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) which is located in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) to the southwest of [Shark](/shark). This location is surrounded by [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider). Inside of the lab, there are inactive [Security Spiders](/security-spider), making this lab preferable to other labs.

[Red Sabre](/red-sabres) (and [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas)) squads can often be found attacking the blood spiders in this area, distracting them from guarding the lab. This possible encounter can be used to get inside the lab. As the gang will almost certainly lose the fight, the looting and return voyage must be made with caution and speed.
