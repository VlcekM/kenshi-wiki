**Lost Prince** is a member of the [Western Hive](/western-hive) faction. They can spawn after [The Queen](/the-queen) has been killed/kidnapped. They have retained their "smart" [Personality](/personality) type and they have a Hiver Ronin dialogue package.

They will spawn in [Dreg](/dreg), [The Eye](/the-eye), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [The Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [The Swamp](/the-swamp). 

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by Unknown.
