**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Clownsteady is the farthest northeastern town and is owned by [Dead Cat](/dead-cat). Nearby towns include [Brink](/brink-alpha), [Shark](/shark-alpha), and [Teblooze](/telbooze).

As many of its neighbors [Clownsteady](/clownsteady) made it into the final release of the game.

## Important Buildings in Clownsteady

-   Two Bars
-   An Armour Trader
-   A Weapon Trader
-   A Trade Building
-   A Clothing Trader
