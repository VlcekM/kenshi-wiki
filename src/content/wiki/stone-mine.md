Stone Mines are resource production buildings that generate the raw material [Raw Stone](/raw-stone) and directly [Building Materials](/building-materials) at higher upgrade levels. Stone mines use and level up the [Labouring](/labouring) skill of miners. The Hybrid Stone Mine II, at the end of the tech tree, completely removes the need for manual labour.

The amount and rate of stone gathered will be dependent on where the mine is built. A character should use [Prospecting](/prospecting) to find a suitable location.
