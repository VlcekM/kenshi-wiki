**Slave Mistress Grace** is a noble member of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). She can be found at [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) in [The Hook](/the-hook).

As with other Traders Guild nobles, she is guarded by 4 [Empire Noble Guard Hooded](/empire-noble-guard-hooded) and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

She has a 90% chance of carrying money items and can be holding between c.1,000 and c.5,000. They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).She also has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Grace is killed, it will cause Slave Farm South to become destroyed, or to be handed over to [Free Traders](/free-traders) if the [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) is also dead or imprisoned.

Death of Grace will also cause [Clownsteady](/clownsteady) to become malnourished. But if the Slave Market Master is also dead or imprisoned then **Clownsteady** instead becomes controlled by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

**Slave Mistress Grace** being dead or imprisoned is also a condition for [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) to be handed over to **Empire Peasants** if [Lady Merin](/lady-merin) has also been killed or imprisoned.

If **Slave Mistress Grace** and **Slave Market Master** are both killed/imprisoned, [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base) will fall under the control of [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild).

If **Slave Mistress Grace** and **Slave Market Master** are both killed/imprisoned, [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) patrols will cease to spawn in [The Hook](/the-hook)
