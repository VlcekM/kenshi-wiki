If the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) is alive and [Elder](/elder) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), the Iron HQ becomes [overrun](/town-overrides) by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). The [Skeleton Bandit](/skeleton-bandit) patrols will become made up of 1-5 [Broken Skeleton Bandits](/broken-skeleton-bandit) and spawn less often.
