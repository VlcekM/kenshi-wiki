**Letter Of Parting** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in the [United Cities](/united-cities). This item can only be found in residential buildings inside of [United Cities](/united-cities) locations.

This letter was written by a member of the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) to a peasant. It has an interesting mention of a willow tree; something not typically found in the desert regions which the United Cities control.
