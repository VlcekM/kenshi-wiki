**Ex-Servant** is a character that can be found squatting in [The Hub](/the-hub).

As their name implies, they are likely to be a previously [Escaped Servant](/escaped-servant) that became an "Ex-Servant" after having been able to live the life as an escapee for a prolonged period of time (similar to how "Escaped Slave" status turns to "Ex-Slave" for characters that escaped [slavery](/slavery)).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
