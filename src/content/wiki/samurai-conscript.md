**Samurai Conscripts** are low-ranking foot soldiers that make up a decent portion of the armies for the [United Cities](/united-cities). The [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) rarely uses conscription as well.

They are typically led by [Samurai Sergeants](/samurai-sergeant) on patrols throughout the wastelands. Sometimes they can also spawn as guards of [Empire General Storage](/empire-general-storage) and UC barracks.

[Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba), the noble governor of Stoat, is in charge of organizing conscript patrols in the [Bast](/bast-zone) region - the patrols in question will cease to spawn if he is killed or captured.

They sometimes spawn without Headgear.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   **Samurai Conscript's** name could be put in contrast with [Holy Chosen](/holy-chosen) of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), as the latter would indicate a sense of voluntary service and being granted the chance to serve in the frontlines while conscription is mandated and forced. It also suggests that [United Cities](/united-cities) is comparatively conscripting a larger portion of their populace for combat.
