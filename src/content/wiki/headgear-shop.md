**Headgear Shop** is a shop which sells [Headgear](/headgear) and related blueprints.

### Possible Locations

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)
-   [Deadcat](/deadcat)
-   [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon)
-   [World's End](/worlds-end)

### Residents

-   [Ronin Hat Trader](/ronin-hat-trader)
-   [Ninja Guard](/ninja-guard)

This location has a gear [Artifacts](/artifacts) value of 50,000 Cats, meaning it will spawn armour and weapons from the Artifacts system.
