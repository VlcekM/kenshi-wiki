**Blood Bandit Camps**, or **Bloodraider Camps**, are randomly spawning camps belonging to the [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders). They can only spawn in [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon).

This location spawns [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) which the player can use for free. Looting items from the camp after its occupants have been defeated will wipe it off the map.

They are the only static locations belonging to the [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders), which imply that these temporary camps act as their headquarters.

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this camp. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

Bloodraider Patrol

-   x 3
    -   3 - 10 [Blood Raider](/blood-raider)

## Item Spawns

These items will spawn randomly with a min of 2 and a max of 7 total in each bandit camp, with the following relative chance. Data is from FCS.

-   Book (50)
-   Cactus Rum (5)
-   Chewsticks (50)
-   Cup (300)
-   Damaged Book (100)
-   Dried Meat (50)
-   Empty Grog Can (100)
-   Greenfruit (50)
-   Grog (5)
-   Hashish (22)
-   Water Jug (200)
-   Wooden Bowl (200)

## Trivia

-   Despite the fact that the Bloodraider Camp can spawn in the far north of [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon), [Bloodraider](/bloodraiders) patrols do not spawn in either [Skimsands](/skimsands) or [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert); instead they only spawn in [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) and [The Eye](/the-eye). This makes any camps quite isolated from their area of most activity, which is unusual compared to other camped factions like [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) or the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers).
