The **Rebel Swordsman Veteran** is a member of the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) faction. They appear as guards in towns controlled by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) in the northeastern territories.

They have a 10% chance to spawn with a bounty ranging from c.300 to c.1,300. They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Hive Soldier Drone
    
-   Hive Worker Drone
