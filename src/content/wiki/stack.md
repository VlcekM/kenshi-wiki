**Stack** is a large town on the edge of [the Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) territory. It has a decent variety of shops, and its proximity to the [Western Hive](/western-hive) and [the Hub](/the-hub) make it a good location for aspiring traders and thieves alike.

The surrounding areas often have [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits), [Bonedogs](/bonedog), and [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), all of which are relatively weak enemies, making this a decent training area for newer characters.

The [Game Start](/game-starts) "Holy Nation Citizen" can begin at this location, the other possible choice is in [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth). If you are not starting out in this town, you can place it on your map after travelling here or by reading the **Glorious Holy [Map](/maps)**.

This is the place of residence of [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta). The mother of [Knife](/knife), a [unique recruit](/unique-recruits), supposedly lives here. [Bard](/bard) and [Griffin](/griffin) could spawn here to be recruited in a bar.

## Town Overrides

Stack, on the brink of Holy Nation territory, can be affected by [world states](/world-states) to produce [town overrides](/town-overrides). Most danger comes from the Shek Kingdom or other Sheks.
