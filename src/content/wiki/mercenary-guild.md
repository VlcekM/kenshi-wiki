The **Mercenary Guild** can be found in various cities and travelling mostly between [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) territories.

In HN/UC consist of many [Human Mercenaries](/human-mercenary)/[Mercenaries](/mercenary) and [Mercenary Heavies](/mercenary-heavy) following a [Mercenary Captain](/mercenary-captain) who will loan themselves out as bodyguards (c.2,000 per day) or outpost protectors (c.1,000 per day). They are competent fighters but have extremely low-quality gear. That being said, they can still be useful to hire for a low skilled squad needing some protection. Contracts can be renewed by simply talking to the leader of their squad again.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Despite its name, and the claim that it has become standard amongst their kind, no members of the **Mercenary Guild** wear [Mercenary Plate](/mercenary-plate).
    -   Manhunters could by extension be considered similar to a form of Mercenary work, and they have similar overlaps as seen in [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base) and [their override](/manhunter-base/mercenary-guild).
-   Mercenary Guild can be considered to be a very small organization, as their numbers in homeless spawns tends to get outnumbered drastically by [bandit](/bandits) groups.
