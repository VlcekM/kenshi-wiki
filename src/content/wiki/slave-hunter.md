**Slave Hunters** are members of the faction of [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters). They patrol the areas around [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) in groups of 4 ~ 10. and will attack lone individuals and small groups in an attempt to pass them off as escaped slaves. They will not do this if there are witnesses around, however. In many ways, they are essentially a weaker and less numerous [Manhunter](/manhunter).

They will not target the player if the player is allied with either [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), if there are any police nearby, if they are inside an NPC town, or if they are lesser in number than the player's squad.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
