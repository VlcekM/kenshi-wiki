**New Kralia** is the HQ of the hostile faction [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) and is located south of [Admag](/admag).

The outpost is inhabited by around 15-20 [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen-character) characters, who will turn hostile when you approach their gates. Their leader [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) can also be found here, he has a bounty of c.30,000.
