**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Shark is the northernmost town in Kenshi. It is characterized by its odd layout and lack of completed walls. Nearby towns include [Brink](/brink-alpha), [Catan](/catun-alpha), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady-alpha), as well as an outpost to the southeast. Shark is a part of [The Holy Empire](/the-holy-empire).

[Shark](/shark) made it into the final release of the game as a city in the Swamps.

## Important Buildings in Shark

-   Two Bars
-   A Trade Building
-   A Clothing Trader
-   A Recruitment Building
-   A Police Station (note: The Police station is marked with a trade sign and has no cells, it appears as the last possible building within the complete section of the walls.)
