**Okran's Fist** is a fortified garrison belonging to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). The garrison is set into the side of the East [Arm of Okran](/arm-of-okran) where it guards the Northeastern approach into Holy Nation territory. [Paladins](/paladin) and [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel) man the defenses.

Given its approximation to [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Bast](/bast-zone), and the buildings status tied with the leaders of the army in those regions<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, it is likely that this is a military garrison meant to station the patrol troops throughout the area.
