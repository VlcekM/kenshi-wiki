**Waystation Ruins** is type of a [Waystation](/waystation) location found in the southeast. It can be the possible place of the Freedom Seekers [Game Start](/game-starts). There is a Waystation Ruins in [Venge](/venge) as well as one in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden). Most of the loot here is junk, however items such as [Skeleton Repair Kits](/skeleton-repair-kit) also have a chance of spawning.

No Artifacts system spawning here.
