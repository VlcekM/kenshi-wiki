**Mukai The Mountain** is an influential [Shek](/shek) warrior, and potentially the leader of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) if [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) is killed or captured.

The overwhelming vast majority<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> of [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) went [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) after [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) was defeated, [completely depopulating Admag](/admag/berserkers).

Mukai takes over the Shek Kingdom, becoming the next [Invincible](/the-five-invincibles) in Esata's place, the headquarters are then relocated to the much smaller town of [Squin](/squin), where he holds influence over the token Kamikaze squad inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

If he is killed, [Squin](/squin) will be taken over by [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) (if [Flying Bull's](/flying-bull) alive) or [Berserkers](/berserkers) otherwise.

He carries a [Meitou](/meitou)\-level [Fragment Axe](/fragment-axe).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Mukai The Mountain is killed/imprisoned there are some World States that would trigger.

[Squin](/squin) will fall under [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) rule if [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive and free, if **Flying Bull** is killed/imprisoned as well, Squin will fall under [Berserkers](/berserkers) rule.

[Kamikaze](/kamikaze) patrols will cease to spawn in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) provided that **Esata** is also killed/imprisoned (which would automatically be the case since Mukai only spawns when that condition is achieved).

## Trivia

-   In Japanese, his name could be interpreted as「向い」_(mukai)_, which means "opposing", "facing", or even "confront" or "defy". This could represent how he wants to confront [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) forces by kamikazeing into them as tensions flare up from a cold war stalemate to the previous total war [similar to under Shager](/king-shagers-reign).
    -   「向井」_(mukai)_ is also a common Japanese surname.
-   The town override with Mukai has a few weird instances and could be considered to be poorly done, these include:
    -   When handing in [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), they will still refer you to [Bayan](/bayan), even though Bayan should be dead or missing in this scenario.
