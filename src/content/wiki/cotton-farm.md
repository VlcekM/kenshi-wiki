A Cotton Farm produces [cotton](/cotton), which can be used to make [fabric](/fabrics). It can be farmed most efficiently in areas with the Green [Environment](/environment).

Contrary to the description, cotton can also be grown inside an Arid or Swamp environment, as long as there is more than 0% of Green environment there as well.

## Research Requirements

-   Tech Level 1
-   2 cotton and 2 books

## Farming Plots

Cotton Farm S

-   Requires 10 crops to build
-   Produces 30 crops
-   Takes 30 in game hours to fully grow at 100% fertility
-   Must be harvested within 20 in-game hours
-   Uses 6 buckets of water per day, assuming no rain.
