**The Crabinator** is a Crab member of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) that will accompany the fourth [Crab Tournament](/crab-tournament) sent to the player's outpost. He can spawn with an age skill multiplier of 0.90x to 1.30x, towering over the rest of the crabs and crab raiders in the attack, and will commonly be their strongest fighter.

## Strategy

The best means of attack would be to man harpoons in front of your outposts main gate while having other characters repair the gate during conflict, unfortunately raiders may group too tightly in front of your gate and be forced through the gate so having trained soldiers behind your gate is also advised.
