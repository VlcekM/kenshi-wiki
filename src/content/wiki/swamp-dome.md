A **Swamp Dome** is a large structure in Kenshi that cannot be constructed by the player, though it can be purchased in specific locations such as [Mud Town](/mud-town).

Usually found throughout the [The Swamp](/the-swamp), it is utilized by several factions as [Bars](/bars) and even sometimes as a [Faction HQ](/faction-hq).

[The Dancing Skeleton](/the-dancing-skeleton) in [Shark](/shark) is a Swamp Dome.
