**Thief Bosses** are characters in charge of the local [Thieves Guild](/thieves-guild) Tower for the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves). This character is usually stationed outside the tower and must be talked to in order to join the Thieves.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Hive Prince
    
-   Hive Soldier Drone
    
-   Hive Worker Drone
