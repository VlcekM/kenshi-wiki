**Crossbow Parts** are [items](/items) used in the construction of [Crossbows](/crossbows). They are made out of [Hinges](/hinge) and [Spring Steel](/spring-steel).

The ability to craft this item is learned through researching [Crossbow Crafting](/crossbow-crafting-tech). They are made on the [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench).
