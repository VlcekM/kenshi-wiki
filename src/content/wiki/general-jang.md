**General Jang** is the leader of the Hydraulic Knights of the [Second Empire](/second-empire). He can be found in [Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii).

He is known for fighting 'pirates' and [cannibals](/cannibals), the latter coined him the title of "The Inedible One".

Removing either his AI Core or his CPU from his body will instantly kill him.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (5% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).
