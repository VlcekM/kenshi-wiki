**Clownsteady, Empire Peasants** is a town controlled by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). It belonged to [United Cities](/united-cities) until [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) and [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) were both killed. Be aware that Empire Peasants and United Cities share the same legal system.

[Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture) has been removed when the override occurred and since Empire Peasants have the [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) the value modifier of [Hashish](/hashish) is 1,200% instead of 500%.
