Killing or capturing the [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) at the nearby [Slave Markets](/slave-markets) will cripple [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)'s economy, destroying half of the buildings and making the inhabitants starve. The same will happen if [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) is also killed or kidnapped.

If both Slave Masters are killed, this town will switch its override to "Empire Peasants".
