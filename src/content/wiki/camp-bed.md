**Camp Bed** is a building that can be placed (via the "Build Menu") anywhere outside the borders of towns or even in [player owned buildings](/player-owned-buildings-in-town) in town. It is used in the same way as a normal [bed](/bed). Once you've rested, it can be deconstructed to be used over and over again. Like other [camping](/camping) buildings, its "construction" does not create a [player outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). When slept in your recover organic health 4x faster and blood/oil twice as fast as normal.

**Camp Bed** is essentially an unfolded [Sleeping Bag](/sleeping-bag) (an item).
