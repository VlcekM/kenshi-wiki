65953-Dialogue.mod**Crab Shop** is a vendor for the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) found in [Crab Town](/crab-town) and [Crab Village](/crab-village). Players can purchase Crabs at this location.

This store only exists as long as the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) has not been killed or imprisoned.

### Residents

-   [Crab Man](/crab-man)
-   [Crab](/crab) (4 to 8) at 0.1x to 0.3x age when spawned
