The **Slave Markets** is a location in [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands) at which slavers sell their slaves. It can be found by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Extended Territories)**. [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) is in charge of the **Slave Markets**.

Unlike other slave camps, this place has zero industrial facilities as slaves neither farm nor mine here. It is therefore likely that this is a place that organizes the capturing (such as the likes of [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) and [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) going by the zone's spawns), organizing, trading and transferring of slaves to the neighboring [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) with the necessary personnel to utilize plantations effectively.

There are a few [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) in this location, as well as many [Slave Recruits](/slave-recruits). The unique recruits that can spawn here are: [Headshot](/headshot), [Ray](/ray), named [Scarred Adventurer](/scarred-adventurer), named [Soldier Drone Swordslinger](/soldier-drone-swordslinger) and [Wall Man](/wall-man). The unique slave trader [Garret](/garret) can also spawn here, though he is not a recruit. All of these characters can also spawn in [Eyesocket](/eyesocket), except Wall Man who can also spawn in many other locations.

Players can use the prisoner cages here to collect bounties for characters wanted by [United Cities](/united-cities).
