The **Kusari Zukin** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be sold by Ronin, United Cities, and Holy Nation hat vendors. It cannot be worn by [Deadhive Workers](/deadhive-worker), [Hive Worker Drones](/hive-worker-drone), and [Southern Hive Worker Drones](/southern-hive-worker-drone).

It has exactly the same stats as [Karuta Zukin](/karuta-zukin), and worth slightly less.

It can be randomly equipped by [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter), [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/empire-noble-guard-hooded), [Moon Guardians](/moon-guardian), and [The Preacher](/the-preacher).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3.**

## Appearance

A simple chainmail hood with leather pieces for trim.
