A compact town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction, located in the Southwest region of the map known as [The Hook](/the-hook). It can be found by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Extended Territories)**.

The economy of this town relies on supplies from [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) and [Slave Markets](/slave-markets).

Despite its close proximity to [the Swamp](/the-swamp), merchants in Clownsteady will still pay a hefty premium for [sake](/sake) and the bar sells the [rum](/cactus-rum) desired by Swampers, making it a fair choice for an alcohol trade route. Members of the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) can also flog [less-legal substances](/hashish) here, albeit at less of a markup than in the Great Desert cities.

## Tips

-   The [Police Station](/police-station) can be a good place to train up [toughness](/toughness) and [lockpicking](/thievery) as they will quickly beat you up for using their [training dummies](/training-dummy), carry your unconscious character to a cage, and then heal them. This is preferable to the way which the [Hounds](/hounds) respond to unauthorized training dummy usage (beating you up and dropping you off outside for the [blood spiders](/blood-spider)) and to the other city in [The Hook](/the-hook) ([Drifter's Last](/drifters-last)), where characters are enslaved and sent to [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south). Although _some_ captured criminals will be enslaved.
