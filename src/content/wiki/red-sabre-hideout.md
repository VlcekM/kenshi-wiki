**Red Sabre Hideout** is the [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) of the [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) faction and their leader [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss). It is located in the southwestern part of [the Swamp](/the-swamp). Despite its name, it can be discovered by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Swamps**.

The building contains about 22 [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) and, of course, the [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss). The item of most value in the hideout is the [Meitou](/meitou) [Horse Chopper](/horse-chopper) wielded by the [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss).

This location has a gear [Artifacts](/artifacts) value of c.10,000, meaning it will spawn armour and weapons from the Artifacts system. This location also has a total item count of 30 items, and it refreshes every 24 hours.
