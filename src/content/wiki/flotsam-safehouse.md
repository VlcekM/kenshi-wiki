**Flotsam Safehouse** is a single building located to the north of [Rebirth](/rebirth). It was established as a way point for escaped [Rebirth Slaves](/rebirth-slaves) to rest and recover their strength. The purpose behind the location is explained in the [Flotsam Safehouse Note](/flotsam-safehouse-note). The contents of the shack include some low-level gear, a decent amount of food, and 2 [beds](/bed).

Despite what is stated in the Flotsam Safehouse Note, the player suffers no penalties with the Flotsam Ninjas should they loot the place, even if they are not an escapee from Rebirth. Although the items may be tagged as stolen, they can be sold to the Flotsam Ninjas without the risk of getting in trouble.
