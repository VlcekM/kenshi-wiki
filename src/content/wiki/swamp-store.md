A very small shack. They can be found in [Bark](/bark) for c6,400 alongside the [Swamp House](/swamp-house) and [Swamp Shack](/swamp-shack).

Like the [Swamp House](/swamp-house), buildings can only be placed inside and not on the porch.

This is probably the worst building in the game - it is more expensive than either a [Small Shack](/small-shack) or a [Swamp Shack](/swamp-shack), while being smaller than both of them.
