[Kidnapping or killing](/world-states) [Longen](/longen) or [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) results in [Heng](/heng) being [half-destroyed](/town-overrides) and in a civil war.

3 squads of [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) each composed of (6 ~ 10 [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer), 2 ~ 4 [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsman) and 0 ~ 1 [Bonedog](/bonedog)) will spawn in the town and engage in combat with townsfolk.
