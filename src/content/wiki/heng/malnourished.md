Heng will [become malnourished](/town-overrides) if [Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Haga](/slave-master-haga), and [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) are all [killed or kidnapped](/world-states).

The town is plagued by rampant famine and poverty after all the slave camps of [Stone Camp](/stone-camp), [South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp) and [Eyesocket](/eyesocket) supplying this town have been destroyed and supply lines cut off, as a result the town is in a state of civil war, and the ensuing chaos has destroyed and killed a part of the town and its inhabitants.
