If [Longen](/longen) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are both [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Heng](/heng) will be [overtaken](/town-overrides) by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), with two different overrides depending on whether [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive or not. The town is set to be roughly 1/3rd destroyed<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving either of the version of this override.
