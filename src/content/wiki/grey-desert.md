The **Grey Desert** is a relatively small zone which borders a handful of the most polluted areas in the game. The single [Tech Hunter](/tech-hunters) [Waystation](/waystation), when coming from the east, is the only place for resupply before heading to [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) in the [Deadlands](/deadlands).

This place is mostly empty with a few [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [Nomads](/nomads) and [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders).

There are occasionally squads of [Weak Thralls](/weak-thrall) that could be found in this area, likely migrated from the neighbouring [Venge](/venge). Alongside [Grayflayer](/grayflayers) smugglers that may have travelled here from the nearby [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or Ancient Locations in the Grey Desert. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
