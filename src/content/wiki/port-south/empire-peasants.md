If [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada), [Longen](/longen) and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are all [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Port South](/port-south) will [fall under the control](/town-overrides) of [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

This place appears to be turned into a semi-rebuilt fish port and farm town that ostensibly runs the supply lines through [Heft](/heft) to [Heng](/heng).
