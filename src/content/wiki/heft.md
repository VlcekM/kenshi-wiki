**Heft** is the current Capital of the [United Cities](/united-cities) and is located in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) region. The economy of this town is relied upon the supplies from both [Port South](/port-south) and the stable economy of [Heng](/heng-zone). It is located directly east of [Stoat](/stoat), Southeast of [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), and Northeast of [Heng](/heng).

**Heft** is home to [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), leader of the United Cities, as well as [Lord Ohta](/lord-ohta), one of the members of the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle). [Koin](/koin), who is in charge of the United Cities' diplomatic affairs, can also be found here.

Next to the Faction HQ is a 'yard' separated from the rest of the town by makeshift walls and gate alongside the [Samurai Gate Sergeant](/samurai-gate-sergeant) and the [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard) guarding the gate. Inside this area a building alongside several camp beds can be found, the latter being most likely slums for the homeless to sleep constructed by the government or [peasants](/empire-peasants) themselves.

This town is noteworthy for having a unique [Heft-variant Armory Shop](/heft-armory) which sells the best armor in the entire UC, alongside a Thieves Guild where the player could buy sneaky equipments from, and a Robotics Workshop where characters in need of robotic repairs or replacements can go to.

The player can find it by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

[Player owned buildings](/player-owned-buildings-in-town) in Heft are not safe from [Town Overrides](/town-overrides).
