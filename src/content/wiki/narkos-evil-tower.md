**Narko's Evil Tower** is a walled in [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab) guarded by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). It can be found in [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap). Inside the tower, there will be 2 [Security Spiders](/security-spider), which aspiring tech hunters will have to fight or sneak past. At least 3 [AI Cores](/ai-core), 2 [Engineering Research](/engineering-research) and 13 [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) can be found at this location. Two of the AI cores are found in the 73 Lock Complexity Ancient Safe at the top. The last one is usually inside another of the Ancient Safes.
