The **L-House** is one of the medium sized buildings and can be [constructed](/construction) by the player.

To be able to build the L-House you need first to research the [Medium Building Shells](/medium-building-shells-tech).

Besides the interior of the building, you have access to a roof as well.

## L-House Building Info

-   Building Condition - 35
-   Door condition - 16
-   Door Lock - lvl15
