The **Worker Hut** is a shack that can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town) and [Crab Village](/crab-village). It has workers inside that are randomly named.

The player faction is allowed to use the [Beds](/bed) inside if they are allied to the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders).
