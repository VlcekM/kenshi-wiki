The **Tricorn Hat** is a Light Armour equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be sold by Ronin Hat Vendors.

it is randomly equipped by [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin), [Anti-Slavers (unarmed)](/anti-slaver-unarmed), [Drifters](/drifter), [Hounds](/hound), [Human Mercenaries](/human-mercenary), [Outlaws](/outlaw), [Smugglers](/smuggler), [Swamper Gate Guards](/swamper-gate-guard), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), and [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

Headgear of a bygone era, the Tricorn Hat is a three-sided hat made out of brown leather. It covers the head and two of the three corners hang over the character's shoulders while the final corner juts out of the face. The leather has a sunbleached appearance, showing signs of wear from the harsh desert climate.
