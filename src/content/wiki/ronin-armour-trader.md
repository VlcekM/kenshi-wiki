**Ronin Armour Traders** are unnamed shopkeepers of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction), [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), or [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), depending on who owns the city their [armour shop](/ronin-armor-shop) is located.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

## Statistics

-   Humans
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive
    
-   Skeletons
    

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
