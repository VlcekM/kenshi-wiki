**Legion Scouts** are members of the [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion) faction. They can be found in the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

They can usually be found in a pair, either alone or integrated into a force of 7 [Legion](/legion) skeletons.

If [Mad Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) has been killed, the force they are with is increased to a dozen Legions.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
