**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Bark is the easternmost town in Kenshi, and is located near the base of a large mountain. Nearby towns include [Heft](/heft-alpha) and [Black Scratch](/black-scratch-alpha). It is apparently owned by the [Imperial Lords](/imperial-lords). Bark has multiple unmarked buildings with beds and other amenities.

A town named [Bark](/bark) made it into the final release of the game. It is controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), a further development of Imperial Lords faction.

-   A [Police](/police-alpha) Station
-   An Item Trader
