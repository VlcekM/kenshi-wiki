**Maps** are [items](/category/map-items) in [Kenshi](/kenshi) which can be used in order to reveal unknown locations on the player's [Map](/world-of-kenshi). After use, these items disappear from whichever inventory they were in. Players cannot use a map item if they already know all the [locations](/category/locations) which that map can reveal.

All maps are either obtained through purchasing them, or through looting them from [Ancient Locations](/ancient-locations), such as ruins. All of the obtainable maps can be found in the lists below, or in the [Map Items category page](/category/map-items).

## Purchasable Maps

These maps can be purchased in various shops, including the [Travel Gear](/travel-gear) shop. They unlock a number of locations on the world map. **Purchasable Maps** generally deal with locations of the "new world", settlements that are known to the general public.

SPOILERS AHEAD!

**[Glorious Holy Map](/glorious-holy-map)** - shows locations of The Holy Nation - [Holy Farms](/holy-farms), both [Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base), [Holy Mines](/holy-mines), and the cities of [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth), [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) and [Stack](/stack).

**[Map of the Border Zone](/map-of-the-border-zone)** - unlocks locations of [The Hub](/the-hub), a [Waystation](/waystation) (not necessarily in the Border Zone) and a [Tiny Settlement](/tiny-settlement).

**[Map of the Shek Kingdom](/map-of-the-shek-kingdom)** - unlocks all of their locations: [Admag](/admag), [The Great Fortress](/the-great-fortress), [Last Stand](/last-stand) and [Squin](/squin).

**[Map of the Swamps](/map-of-the-swamps)** - unlocks the locations of [Shark](/shark) and the swamp villages: [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village), [Mud Town](/mud-town), [Red Sabre Hideout](/red-sabre-hideout), [Rot](/rot), [Stone Rat Village](/stone-rat-village), two [Swamp Villages](/swamp-village).

**[Map of the United Cities (Central Territories)](/map-of-the-united-cities-central-territories)** - unlocks many of the UC locations in the Eastern side of the map: [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), [Eyesocket](/eyesocket), [Port North](/port-north), [Port South](/port-south), [Stone Camp](/stone-camp), [Brink](/brink), [Farming Village](/farming-village), [Fishing Village](/fishing-village) (of [Deadcat](/deadcat) faction), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Bark](/bark), [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), [Stoat](/stoat), [Drin](/drin), [Bast](/bast), [Endin](/endin).

**[Map of the United Cities (Extended Territories)](/map-of-the-united-cities-extended-territories)** - unlocks the outer UC territories, located mostly in the South: [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady), [Slave Farm](/slave-farm), [Slave Markets](/slave-markets), [Catun](/catun), [Mourn](/mourn) (formerly of UC).

**[Map of Vain](/map-of-vain)** - unlocks the [Hive Villages](/hive-village).

**[Map to Mongrel](/map-to-mongrel)** - shows the location of [Mongrel](/mongrel).

**[Map to World's End](/map-to-worlds-end)** - shows where [World's End](/worlds-end) is.

**[Tech Hunters Library Map](/tech-hunter-library-map)** - unlocks a location of a [Lost Library](/lost-library).

**[Tech Hunters Map](/tech-hunters-map)** - unlocks a location of any of the [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs), any [Lab Ruins](/lab-ruin), [Tower of Goats](/tower-of-goats), [Tower of Spiders](/tower-of-spiders), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Tower Of Ninjas](/tower-of-ninjas).

## Looted Maps

These maps can be found in many ancient locations themselves and they unlock sites of a number of other ancient locations (to be visible on player's map).

SPOILERS AHEAD!

**[Ancient Military Documents](/ancient-military-documents)** - can unlock a location of any [Lost Armoury](/lost-armoury).

**[Bugmaster's Map](/bugmasters-map)** - unlocks the location of his eternal enemy, [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile).

**[Engineers Map](/engineers-map)** - unlocks locations of any of the workshops - [Dead Workshop](/dead-workshop), [Deadland Workshop](/deadland-workshop), [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop) and [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex)**.**

**[Map of the Ashlands](/map-of-the-ashlands)** - unlocks any of the [Ashland](/ashlands) Domes and [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile).

**[Old Treasure Map](/old-treasure-map)** - can unlock an item from a variety of these locations: an [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab), an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs), the [Spider Factory](/spider-factory), the [Reprogramming Workshop](/reprogramming-workshop), [Ashland Domes I](/ashland-dome-i) and [III](/ashland-dome-iii), [Ashland Dome Ruin](/ashland-dome-ruin), [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) and [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location) (Location).

**[Secret Holy Nation Documents](/secret-holy-nation-documents)** - unlocks the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s military secret - [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap).
