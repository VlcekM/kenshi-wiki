**Dyed Robes** are Clothing that are equipped in the Body Armour slot. As the name suggests, this item can be [dyed](/colour-scheme). An already coloured version of this item is given to the newly instituted slaves in [Rebirth](/rebirth).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

Dyed Robes appear to be puffy, long-sleeved, collared robes tied at the bottom by sash.

Orange-colored versions are equipped by [United Cities](/united-cities) nobles - [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi), [Longen](/longen), [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata), and [Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro). Redder versions are equipped by [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) slaves - [Lower Servants](/lower-servant) and [Rebirth Slaves](/rebirth-slaves). Blue versions are equipped by [Slaver Bosses](/slaver-boss).
