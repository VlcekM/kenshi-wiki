**Electrical Workbench** is a [Crafting](/crafting) station which converts [Copper](/copper) into [Electrical Components](/electrical-components), at a one-to-one ratio. This station uses and trains the [Science](/science) stat of its worker.

Players can construct this building after researching [Electrical Crafting](/electrical-crafting-tech).
