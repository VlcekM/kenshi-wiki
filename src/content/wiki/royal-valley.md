The **Royal Valley** is the home of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive).

## Inhabitants

There are no Ancient Locations in the Royal Valley. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

### Major Towns

-   [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location)

### Minor Outposts

_These locations are typically small towns, villages, or faction bases._

-   [Hiver-Claimed Armoury](/hiver-claimed-armoury)
-   [Southern Hive Village](/southern-hive-village)

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. This list is in order of likelihood. (1x multiplier)_

-   [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) (90%)
    -   South Hive Scouts (4 [Droneguards](/droneguard)) {5} ([South Queen](/queen-of-the-south) alive)
    -   South Hive Patrol (1 [Prince](/prince), 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard)) {4} ([South Queen](/queen-of-the-south) alive)
-   [Goat](/goat) (10%)
    -   Wild Goats {1}
