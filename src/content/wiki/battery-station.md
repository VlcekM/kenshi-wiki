**Battery Stations** are [small buildings](/small-shack) in towns that house most, if not all, of the towns battery banks. They are easily accessed by simply unlocking the door through [Lockpicking](/lockpicking) and can even have small trinkets in them such as small statues or rarely bread. Otherwise they are purely aesthetic.
