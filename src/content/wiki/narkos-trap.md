**Narko's Trap** is a [Minor Outpost](/minor-outposts) controlled by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). Due to their hatred and fear of ancient technologies, the Holy Nation have set up a small military outpost around the only known ancient laboratory in their territory, to prevent would-be thieves and tech hunters from purloining the forbidden knowledge within.

This location can be discovered by travelling here or through reading the **[Secret Holy Nation Documents](/maps)**.

The location itself consists of two parts: the Holy Nation military base manned by [unique paladins](/paladin-guardian), who will immediately attack and remove any intruders and/or Agents of Narko from the premises, and, more importantly, an [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab) called [Narko's Evil Tower](/narkos-evil-tower)**.**

The lab itself is walled in and is home to hostile [Security Spiders](/security-spider), which can be defeated, snuck past, or left for any paladins in pursuit. Aside from the normal ancient lab loot, the tower has a high amount of possible [Research Artifacts](/research-artifacts), including [AI Cores](/ai-core), as well as a guaranteed [Map of the Ashlands](/map-of-the-ashlands).

Note that due to the proximity of this location to the [Deadlands](/deadlands), characters can get hit by acid rain if they accidentally move too far.

## Tips

-   If the player is allied with The Holy Nation (50+ relations), the players squad can get inside **Narko's Evil Tower** without repercussions.
-   This place is one of the few places (like [Watchtower](/watchtower-town)) of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) that has no overrides, the inhabitants will stay the same regardless of the status [of](/high-inquisitor-seta) [their](/high-inquisitor-valtena) [leaders](/holy-lord-phoenix).
-   You can bypass the wall and avoid fighting the Paladins by placing a **Camp Bed** on the other side of the wall, as close to it as possible. Then lie down in the bed to pass through the wall. A good place to do this is near the ramp at **Narko’s Evil Tower**.
