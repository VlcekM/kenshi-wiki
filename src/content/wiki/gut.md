Neighboring the Desert Regions to the east, **Gut** is known for its sky-blue sands and the Iron Trees that dot the region and the large amount of [Gutters](/gutters) for which this zone might be named after (or vice versa). The primary fauna are [Beak Things](/beak-thing), making this region extremely dangerous, but also highly profitable. It's a good location to farm [Animal Skins](/animal-skin) and [Beak Thing Eggs](/beak-thing-egg).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or Ancient Locations in Gut. This zone does not spawn Camps or have any Homeless Spawns.
