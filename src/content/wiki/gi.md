**Gi** is a type of [clothing](/clothing) that is wearable in the [body armour](/body-armour) slot. It can be worn together with white [Gi Pants](/gi-pants).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

There is a black version of this item named [Ninja Gi](/ninja-gi).

## Appearance

Looks like a real life Gi.

## Crafting

It can be crafted at the [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench) after researching [Simple Clothing Types](/simple-clothing-types-tech).
