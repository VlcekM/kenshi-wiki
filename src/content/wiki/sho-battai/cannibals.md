If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Nagata](/lord-nagata) and [Simion](/boss-simion) are all either killed or imprisoned, [Cannibals](/cannibals) will take control of Sho-Battai.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

Following the complete collapse of the [Empire's](/united-cities) authority as a result of their important leaders and governors taken out, the town descended into death and ruin from all the chaos resulting from [civil war](/sho-battai/civil-war) and [in-fighting](/sho-battai/half-destroyed).

The [Cannibals](/cannibals) originating from [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) have migrated here through [Bast](/bast-zone) to take over the town and expand their base of operations across [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).
