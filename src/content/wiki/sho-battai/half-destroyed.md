**Sho-Battai** will become Half-Destroyed if [Nagata](/lord-nagata) is killed/imprisoned while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive and free.

This override can switch to [malnourished + half-destroyed](/sho-battai/civil-war), [Empire Peasants](/sho-battai/empire-peasants) or [Cannibal](/sho-battai/cannibals) variants.

If [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) is killed/imprisoned as well, it will trigger the Half-Destroyed and malnourished version instead, causing squads of [Peasant Farmers](/peasant-farmer) to spawn and fight with the [Samurai](/samurai), both of whom are malnourished.
