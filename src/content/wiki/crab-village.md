The **Crab Villages** are two [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) controlled by the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders). They are located in [Greenbeach](/greenbeach) and [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) – the latter can be found on an elevated plateau next to what looks like an overturned and decayed [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) platform.

Players can purchase [Crabs](/crab) in this location.

The beds in this village are all free to use. However, the town's population, often use the beds.

[Reavers](/reavers) can take over this town if [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) is kidnapped or dead.
