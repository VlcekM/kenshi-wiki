**Samurai Gate Guards** are well-equipped static sentries for the [United Cities](/united-cities) that protect the entrances to their towns, including small villages. Their [sergeants](/samurai-gate-sergeant) sometimes stop travelers to check for contraband.

It is likely that they are veteran [Samurai](/samurai) that earned the rank to be a gate guard given their title and similar but slightly stronger stats.

Gate guards will usually accompany a sergeant in groups of 6 to 8 in major towns, or up to 2 in the case of villages. There will always be 8 of them guarding [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault). Malnourished towns will only have 2 to 4 guards.

They wear \[High\] armour (5% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
