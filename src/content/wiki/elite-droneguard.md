**Elite Droneguards** are the personal guards of the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) and can be found in [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location). There can be 10 to 13 Elite Droneguards protecting the Queen.

They are far more skilled than the standard [Droneguard](/droneguard) and the [Armoury Droneguards](/armoury-droneguard). They will attack the player for trespassing.
