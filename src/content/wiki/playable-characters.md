Players start Kenshi with a set number of player characters to customize depending on their selected [Game Start](/game-starts). Across the world of Kenshi, there are many characters who can become player characters after certain interactions. After recruiting any number of these characters, players can control each squad member as well as all of them at once.

The maximum number of characters you can control is 30. This can be increased with [mods](/modifications) fairly easily. Once players have reached 30 characters total, dialogue options will no longer appear for recruitable characters. Note that having more characters is more computationally demanding and can affect game performance.

## 'Is Recruitable' Tag

Most characters that can join the player must also be "Recruitable". Which means that they must have some core stats below a certain threshold, and cannot be certain NPC classes. Though there are a few exceptions to these rules, due to dialog loopholes.

To be considered recruitable by the _DC\_IS\_RECRUITABLE_ tag, a character must **not** be one of the following classes:

-   [Diplomat](/diplomatic-status)
-   Law Enforcer
-   Military
-   Trader

Furthermore, no combination of these three stat pairs can be 75 or higher:

([Strength](/strength) or [Dexterity](/dexterity)) + ([Melee Attack](/melee-attack) or [Martial Arts](/martial-arts)) + ([Melee Defense](/melee-defense) or [Dodge](/dodge))

In each pair, it will use whichever one is higher. If the total from all three is 75 or higher, the character is not recruitable.

But it will also use only one stat in each of those pairs. So, for instance, a character with 70 Strength and 70 Dexterity would still be considered recruitable, as long as their other pairs sum up to less than 5. Also remember that while a character's stats might display 25, they could actually be 25.99, which would make the math off by 2 or 3.

## Hiring Characters

Most Recruits join the [Player Faction](/nameless) through dialogue options found in towns. Recruits for Hire can be found patrolling town or hanging out in a bar. These recruits have a set fee for joining and become Player Characters immediately after receiving that money. This fee is generally between c.3,000 to c.7,500.

Characters for Hire can be randomized [Generic Recruits](/generic-recruits) or [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits). Most randomized recruits start with very low stats, rag clothing, a simple weapon, and hunger. Unique recruits often have a few elevated stats (10-15) depending on their specialty (i.e. Medic) but are still relatively weak.

## Rescuing Characters

[Slavery](/slavery) is prevalent across the world of Kenshi. Some Slaves are happy to serve their masters, but most are very appreciative to be freed by the Player. After freeing Slaves and Prisoners, they will become allied to the Player. They will appear green on the Map and will follow around the character who freed them. If they are kept safe for a while, they can potentially become Player Characters. Otherwise, they will run off into the wilderness to join back up with their faction.

In some locations such as [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) and the [Dust King Tower](/dust-king-tower), [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) are being kept prisoner. They will join the Player Faction after being freed.

Some [Shek](/shek) characters have a loophole that does not check the character's recruitable status when rescued, if they have an Honourable or Maniacal [Personality](/personality). This can allow the player to recruit certain characters that are usually considered invalid, like [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), [Ghost](/ghost), [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian), or even [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles).

## Purchasing Characters

[Slaves](/slave-recruits) and Animal Player Characters can be purchased from their owners. After Slaves are purchased from their [Slaver Boss](/slaver-boss), they will be Allies. If they are kept safe for a while, they can potentially become Player Characters. The [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) [Ray](/ray) is an exception to this. After purchasing Ray from his [master](/bar-fly), he is automatically a Player Character despite still wearing [Hive Prisoner Shackles](/hive-prisoner-shackles).

Animals purchased from [Nomads](/nomads), [Farm Shops](/farm-shop), or [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) automatically become Animal Player Characters and do count towards your character limit. More information on purchasable animals can be found on [Guide to Animals](/guide-to-animals).

## Saving a Character's life

You can recruit a character by triggering a "You saved my life!"(FCS name) dialog. This is a special dialog that is intended to trigger after saving a character's life, then letting them run off alone and running into them later on.

Conditions:

-   "LT\_MY\_LIFESAVER" or "LT\_FREED\_ME" tag on your character; The latter of which seems to be related to rescuing from slavery/imprisonment, unclear how to get the "LT\_MY\_LIFESAVER" flag (may be related to healing, or rescuing from cannibals/fogmen/etc after being sentenced to death). You also must have never "kidnapped" the character, a single instance of kidnapping even if undetected seems to permanently block these flags. The only way to manually transport them is if it is done through the "pickup" option instead, which is not considered kidnapping, but is generally only available to allies.
-   "DC\_TARGET\_LAST\_SEEN\_X\_HOURS\_AGO" > 24; This means 24 in-game hours have passed since the saved character last spoke to your character.
-   Saved character must not have the traitorous [Personality](/personality).
-   Mercenary/tough characters may trigger it on sight. Civilians may trigger it only on being healed by the player. [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard), [Drifters](/drifter), and [Skeleton Drifters](/skeleton-drifter) may do both.

Once the dialog triggers there are a couple different possibilities.

1.  If in combat when they spot you, they may attempt to help you fight, instead of the normal dialog.
2.  As long as you are in good health, they will have a dialog available to them if you speak to them.
    1.  If they have an honourable [Personality](/personality) and are Recruitable, they can be asked to bodyguard you for a while, or to join you permanently.
    2.  If they have a brave [Personality](/personality), have the "Npc TOUGH Merc Basic 1" dialogue package, and are NOT Recruitable, they can be asked to bodyguard you for a while, or to join you permanently.
    3.  In any other circumstance, they will trigger a dialog where you can ask them for money (which will only ever get you a couple hundred cats at most, sometimes nothing), ask them to bodyguard you which may or may not succeed (if refused it can be rerouted into asking for money), or ask them to join you permanently which has a few possibilities:
        1.  If they are not Recruitable, or the other routes are invalid, they will simply refuse. Which can be rerouted into the asking for money mentioned above.
        2.  If they are Recruitable, and you have killed or captured the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), or [Longen](/longen), they may accept immediately.
        3.  If they are Recruitable and have one of the below packages, they will be skeptical if they should but can be convinced. Saying it's for labour always succeeds. Saying there is danger may fail but can be attempted to coerce them into joining again.
            1.  civilian normal
            2.  civilian tough
            3.  Fresh Ronin
            4.  Wandering citizen
            5.  wandering trader (civilian combo)

  
Note: Due to the brave personality path conditions incorrectly checking if a character is not recruitable instead of recruitable, it is possible to recruit characters with very high skill and attribute levels, such as named [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin) (~80 stats in almost all skills and attributes (not counting racial bonuses which can boost them up to 101+ and break the exp cap)). Conversely a character that is normally recruitable and has a brave personality would not be able to use that dialog path. \[game version 1.0.68\]
