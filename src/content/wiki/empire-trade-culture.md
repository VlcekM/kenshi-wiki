**Empire Trade Culture** is a [trade culture](/traders) which removes cultural items from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and as well as other food items, this culture applies mostly to [United Cities](/united-cities) locations. It's not to be confused with the similar, yet distinct, [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture).

### Locations

-   [Raider Camp](/raider-camp)
-   [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge)
-   [Waystation (Border Zone)](/waystation-border-zone)

All locations controlled by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), and [United Cities](/united-cities).

## Trivia

-   Although the Empire Trade Culture forbids [Greenfruit](/greenfruit)\-related items, many nobles appear to have the privilege of eating [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables) (as can be seen in their inventory) and [Emperor Tengu's](/emperor-tengu) "[Seal](/mystery-sack)" is also a Greenfruit. Some soldiers also appear to be able to give the player [Greenfruit](/greenfruit) after they took pity/have been bribed.
