**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Heft is a town located in the southeast of the map. It sits on top of a cliff face and is characterized by a large metal cage near its center. Nearby towns include [Bark](/bark-alpha) and [Black Scratch](/black-scratch-alpha). Heft is controlled by [Dead Cat](/dead-cat).

[Heft](/heft) made it into the final release of the game as a capital of the [United Cities](/united-cities).

## Important Buildings in Heft

-   Two Bars
-   A Trade Building
-   An Item Trader
-   A Weapon Trader
-   A Clothing Trader
-   A [Police](/police-alpha) Station
-   [A Flophouse](/flophouse)
