**Light Post** is a [Lighting](/lighting) building which can be constructed by the player. It is unlocked through research of [Light Post](/light-post-tech).

There are Light Posts type I and III. Since they are found in a list BUILD menu, similar to Walls, Type III might be easy to miss as a viable [construction](/construction) option.

### Details

-   Power Usage -4
-   Efficiency 100%
