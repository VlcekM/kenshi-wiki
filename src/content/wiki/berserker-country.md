**Berserker Country** is a harsh zone named for its [Berserker](/berserkers) inhabitants. The entire area is themed with rocky terrain with crimson red background (and some white powder-like substance) in contrast to the greenery in [The Shrieking Forest](/the-shrieking-forest). The coastline seems to be decorated with lots of inactive [spiders](/iron-spider) and spiders can be seen along the coast all the way passing through [Purple Sands](/purple-sands) and to [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail) in the further north.

Occasionally, Berserkers and Cannibals will come into clashes with one another here.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or Ancient Locations in Berserker Country. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

## Trivia

-   On the western part of the zone, there's an area you cannot traverse over, despite it being relatively flat.
