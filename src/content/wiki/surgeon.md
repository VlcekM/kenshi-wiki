**Surgeons** are shopkeepers of the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) that can be found in hospitals at the southern [Holy Military Base](/holy-military-base) and [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield), working alongside [Doctors](/doctor).

They sell medical supplies and offer facelifts for c.500.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
