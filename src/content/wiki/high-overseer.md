**High Overseers** are the leading authority figures of a [Holy Mine](/holy-mines). Killing a High Overseer will not destroy the Holy Mine, however.

They are charged with overseeing day-to-day operations and making sure the [Lower Servants](/lower-servant) are kept in line. They command a Holy Mine's garrison of [Slavers](/slaver), [Paladins](/paladin) and [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel).

High Overseers are similar in appearance to [High Paladins](/high-paladin), but they do not carry a [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross) or wear a helmet. Despite wearing [High Paladin](/high-paladin) colors, they are no stronger than a regular Paladin.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.
