If both [Big Gray](/big-gray) and [Big Grim](/big-grim) are [killed or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless) is alive and free, then the [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village) is [taken over](/town-overrides) by the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones). It may then become their new headquarters.
