Not to be confused with [Crimper (Faction)](/crimper-faction).

The **Crimper** is a massive maintenance skeleton that resembles a [Gorillo](/gorillo). They use the same "weapon" that Gorillos use along with attack animations. There is only one Crimper in game, and it's the Southern Hive [King](/king).

## Trivia

-   [King](/king) and the [Hive Queens](/hive-queen) are the only robotic units with a hunger rate. If placed into a player owned cage they will eventually starve to death. The only way to tell they are starving it by looking at their stats.
-   Crimpers (Along with Gorillo variants and [Cleanser Units](/cleanser-unit)) do not have self healing. This means unlike other animals while conscious they are not immune to wound degeneration. However, the only Crimper which spawns in game has an average Toughness of 147 (Not counting the "raid" version of King) which means his wound degeneration value is in the negatives. This means he will actually heal cut damage faster than an animal with self healing even while awake.
