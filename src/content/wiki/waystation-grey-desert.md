This [Waystation](/waystation) is a little [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) Outpost located in the [Grey Desert](/grey-desert). This location functions well as a base of operations when raiding the nearby zone, the [Deadlands](/deadlands).

As with all other non-ruined Waystations, this place has a 25% chance to spawn pacifiers from [United Cities](/united-cities), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

It could be one of the starting locations for the [The Freedom Seekers](/game-starts) game start.

## Buildings

-   [Bar](/adventurer-bar)
-   [Mechanical Shop](/adventurer-mechanical-shop)
-   [Watchtower](/watchtower) (ruined), Price: c.21,000

## Tips

-   The tower, once rebuilt, can function as a good early-mid game base for a small group.
-   Additional Building Materials and Iron Plates are required to rebuild. These can be obtained from the Mechanical Shop (the exact number of materials required is randomized).
