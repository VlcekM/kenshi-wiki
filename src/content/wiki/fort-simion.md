**Fort Simion** is a location in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) controlled by the faction [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers). Rebel Farmers leader, [Boss Simion](/boss-simion), can be found in the Faction HQ.

This town defaults to "Red" icon even if the player isn't hostile towards the Rebel Farmers.

Some of the roaming squads here may attack the player if they come too close and the player isn't allied to them. The town guards will not attack unprovoked, but they will join the fight if they are in the vicinity of the roaming squads.

This settlement is defended by 3 [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer) acting as gate guards, alongside a number of garrisons inside the town.

## Buildings

-   [Swamp Dome](/swamp-dome) (Faction HQ) (1 [Boss Simion](/boss-simion), 8 [Outlaw Swordsman Captains](/outlaw-swordsman-captain), 4 ~ 6 [Outlaw Heavies](/outlaw-heavy), 0 ~ 2 [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman))
-   Swamp Dome (Barracks) (5 ~ 6 [Outlaw Heavies](/outlaw-heavy), 4 ~ 6 [Outlaw Swordsman](/outlaw-swordsman))
-   Swamp Shack x3 (Hideout) (4 ~ 7 [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer))

## Tips and Tricks

-   When raiding, doing so at night is often better, as many rebels are inside the barracks sleeping and will not be aggroed outside of the buildings (except for the swamp huts).
-   Make sure that you have a decent number of men and weaponry before attacking, as what the rebels lack in skill and gear they make up with numbers.
-   Bring enough first aids kits for both your men, and any prisoners you chose to capture, as [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) has a c.20,000 bounty, and each of his captains are worth around c.3,000 3k.
-   You are able to steal from the Barracks (Not the Faction HQ) without getting in trouble. You can constantly steal from the containers by putting stuff inside and taking it out (Stacking items work best along with shift-clicking to try to steal the entire stack back) and putting it back in. Rinse and repeat.

## Trivia
