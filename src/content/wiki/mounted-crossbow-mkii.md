**Mounted Crossbow mkII** is a Defence building. Players can construct this building after researching [Mounted Crossbows II](/mounted-crossbows-tech). They can be built on the ground or on top of [Defensive Walls](/defensive-walls).

The range, accuracy, and speed of a turret is modified by the operator's related skills as well as the height difference between the turret and its target. The damage is also affected by height difference. Turrets will be more effective if fired from above, and less effective when fired from below.

Mounted Crossbow mkII can be upgraded to Mounted Crossbow mkII \[light\] after researching [Crossbow Turret Spotlight Fitting](/crossbow-turret-spotlight-fitting-tech). The stats for an unlighted Mounted Crossbow mkII are the same as a lighted one, disregarding the negative effects of working in darkness.

This crossbow uses 0 power, except when upgraded with the light, which requires 4 power.
