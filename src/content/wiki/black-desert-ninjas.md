**Black Desert Ninjas** is the faction of the [Cloud Ninjas](/cloud-ninja). They can be encountered all over the Deadlands and its nearby regions. Despite this, they're still (mostly) humans, and therefore highly vulnerable to the region's lethal weather. As they have no protection against acid, they tend to be killed by attrition.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Characters
