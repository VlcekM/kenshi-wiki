**Equipment Shops** are [stores](/traders) which trade exclusive equipment types, like [Armour](/armour), [Clothing](/clothing), [Footwear](/footwear) or [Weapons](/weapons). Such traders can be found in most towns. Specific stock varies by location or faction.

Players should be aware that these locations may not be the only vendors selling their specific type. Through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system, vendors can spawn items randomly even if they are not a part of their set vendor lists.

## Weapon Shops

Weapons Shops are [merchants](/traders) who trade exclusively in [arms](/weapons). Weapons Traders can be found in most towns. Stock may vary by faction or location. Shops which have a gear artifact value can also spawn weapons randomly through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system. Another shop which sells weapons is the [Black Market Bar](/black-market-bar).
