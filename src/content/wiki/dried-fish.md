**Dried Fish** is a [food](/food) item similar to [Thinfish](/thinfish) and [Grand Fish](/grand-fish). This item cannot be created through [cooking](/cooking) and must be purchased or looted. Fish are typically found near the coast or in [The Swamp](/the-swamp).
