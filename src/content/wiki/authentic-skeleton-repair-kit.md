**Authentic Skeleton Repair Kits** are [medical](/medical-items) [items](/items) used to heal cutting [damage](/damage) on [robotic](/robots) body parts. These kits can be stored in [Storage: Robotics](/storage-robotics). The name is somewhat misleading as the standard [Skeleton Repair Kit](/skeleton-repair-kit) is superior both in weight and charges. This likely implies that the [Western Hivers](/western-hive) gave their inferior product a fancy name in order to boost sales.

Generic members of the [Anti-slavers](/anti-slavers) can be found carrying these. This might suggest some trade relation between them and the Western Hive.
