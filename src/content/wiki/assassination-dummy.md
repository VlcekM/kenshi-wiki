**Assassination Dummy** is a [training](/training) dummy for use in training [Assassination](/assassination) stat. It is definitely suggested to use this dummy before attempting [stealth](/stealth) KOs, as failure to knock out a character undetected can result in a [bounty](/bounty) and potential jail time. Players can construct this building after researching [Assassin Training](/assassin-training-tech).
