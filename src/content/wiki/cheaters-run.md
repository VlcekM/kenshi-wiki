**Cheaters Run** is the coastal area just south of the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley). Running through this zone can be an easy route to - and from - the [Ashlands](/ashlands), without having to encounter the nearby [Southern Hive](/southern-hive).

Squads of Fishmen and Iron Spiders can be seen alongside the coast in this region.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in Cheater's Run. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

## Trivia

-   The name of this zone could be aptly named for adventurers wishing to cross to [Ashlands](/ashlands) without going through the risks of through the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley) nor the [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley), as this zone is relatively safe and easy to traverse compared to other routes and methods to get to the Ashlands.
