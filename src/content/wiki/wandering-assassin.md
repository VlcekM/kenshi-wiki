**Wandering Assassins** are [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) who wander the world of Kenshi. They will heal player characters and help in a battle if the player is allied with the Shinobi Thieves.

Though their primary job is "Wandering Trader", players cannot trade with them. In daylight hours, they will stop to trade with local merchants.

Wandering Assassins always spawn in a squad alone. They have a chance of spawning in the following zones: [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Spider Plains](/spider-plains), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [The Hook](/the-hook), [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
