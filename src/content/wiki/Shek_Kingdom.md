Homeland of the [Shek](/Shek) and headed by queen [Esata "the Stone Golem"](/Stone_Golem), the Shek Kingdom is a very sparsely populated and tribalistic [faction](/Factions) that resides almost entirely within the ranges of [Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert), centered in their capital, [Admag](/Admag).

They are a nation composed of battlehungry and stubborn tribal raiders who were unified under [Kral](/Way_Of_The_Warrior:_The_Book_Of_Kral), a legendary Shek warrior who consolidated the warring Shek tribes into a centralized nation hundreds of years ago, and have continued their raids and wars against their neighbors ever since.

Their entire culture revolves around the teachings of Kral, which emphasizes sacrificing their own life for the prospect of glory. This pursuit of glory almost led to the destruction of the Kingdom under the reign of Shager, who had extensively depopulated his own kingdom in total war against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) in large scale futile assaults that were defeated due to being far outnumbered in the field.

Because of these strong beliefs in ‘honor through death in battle’ etc, the Shek make a formidable enemy, persistent and fearless in combat. But their warrior attitude also disadvantages the Shek, as their suicidal mindset means that their numbers are diminishing and weakening them as a nation.

They aim to assert their supremacy above all other nations and races (i.e. [Humans](/Human) and [Hivers](/Hive)) that they consider to be weaker. In pursuit of this ideal, they have taken an aggressive stance with regards to the outside nations, and they are no strangers to warring, raiding, killing and [demanding tribute](/Tribute_Round) from their neighbours to assert their dominance.

However, their recent assaults and total wars against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) (and to a lesser extent [United Cities](/United_Cities)) has crippled their population demographics and driven their country to the brink of extinction, and they were forced to relent on their aggressive policies or face certain early destruction.

As a result, they had to sign a truce with UC, and also forbade the attacking of outsiders in their cities to focus their efforts on plaguing The Holy Nation. They also opened up their cities to outside trade until they manage to recover themselves to a sufficient degree. Their society has suffered a great fracture in cohesion, and a significant portion of of their Sheks have already deserted the main kingdom itself to join [Kral's Chosen](/Kral's_Chosen) under [Flying Bull](/Flying_Bull), with the rest on the smaller ones like [Berserkers](/Berserkers) and [Band of Bones](/Band_of_Bones).

They love to hate [Okranites](/The_Holy_Nation), and will often start unprovoked assaults in an attempt to kill any that they see. Their nation often conducts raids to terrorize their nation. The goal of their leadership revolves entirely around [weakening The Holy Nation's military command to gain foothold in their territory](/Shek_Kingdom_Route).

Fighting prowess are valued above all else in their society, to such a degree that they have sidelined all intellectual pursuits, and vast majority of Sheks are illiterate as a result.

## History

While the exact date that the Shek Kingdom was founded is unclear, it ranges anywhere between 100 ~ 1000 years ago, some time after they started sprouting horns, and some time before [United Cities](/United_Cities) and [Traders Guild](/Traders_Guild) were founded.

For most of history that anyone could remember, the Shek were scattered, primitive tribes living in the wilderness, divided and warring regularly amongst each other, and their neighbors. This all changed after a great warrior named Kral unified the tribes together into a powerful kingdom, and led them into a march of conquest. Eventually he was slain in battle by forces that is rumored to have outnumbered him a hundredfold in a last stand and attained a godlike status amongst his followers, who seek to follow in his heroic example. As a result, they have been engaged in raiding, pillaging, and other barbaric practices with their neighbors like [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) and [United Cities](/United_Cities) ever since.

The frequent raids of their neighbors have contributed to [The Red Rebellion](/The_Red_Rebellion) of [United Cities](/United_Cities), as their raiders have blockaded the routes and took the supplies for themselves.

In more recent history however, they have suffered from significant military drawbacks against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) (who by contrast, has [now militarized themselves substantially and start beating back](/The_Holy_Crusade)), and their kingdom's population demographics crippled under the sustained losses in simultaneous wars against both [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) and [United Cities](/United_Cities).

In the final stages of their conflict against The Holy Nation where it became very clear that they were losing, Shager in his fury proposed a [suicide-attack](/Kamikaze) with the remaining against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) which [almost caused an utter annihilation of their country](/King_Shager's_Reign) if it weren't for [Bayan's](/Bayan) objection that was supported by [Esata](/Esata). Shager and Esata dueled with each other, to which the latter won and executed Shager on the spot.

Under Esata now, their kingdom was forced to relent, and they have called off their main [assaults](/Kamikaze) against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) and resorted to low-intensity raids instead. They also signed a truce with the [United Cities](/United_Cities), forbade the attacking of outsiders and opened up their cities for trade (albeit begrudgingly). This has caused an internal strife within the society, with many Sheks denouncing Esata and breaking off from the Kingdom, such as the [Kral's Chosen](/Kral's_Chosen).

For these reasons, the present-day Shek Kingdom finds itself in a state of instability, with discontent rising as many warmongers grow impatient yearning for the day of bloodshed.

## Society

<table><tbody><tr><td>“</td><td>Around here, a Shek's standing is measured in bravery and battle skill, as in the same footsteps of Kral. Foreigners are only tolerated in this town for their trade, under the Stone Golem's orders. Every Shek in this city is itching for a fight since she came to power, especially against an honourless foreigner like you. I suggest you keep your head down if you want to leave the city in one piece.</td><td>”</td></tr><tr><td colspan="3"><p><cite>–<a href="/Shek_Warrior">Shek Warrior</a></cite></p></td></tr></tbody></table>

Intellect and advanced technology have no place in Shek society. The Shek earn their respect amongst each other through strength and bravery alone. The outsiders are only tolerated for the trade they bring by Esata's orders, and only for now.

Kral taught them that dying in battle is the only way to find prosperity and honor, and by dying in battle they can pass to Gateway, a spiritual realm where all their great heroes reside. This suicidal mindset makes for some of the strongest warriors on the continent, but greatly decreases their population.

Shek society is divided into two castes. The upper class of warriors, and the lower class of servants. They base this upon the belief that the weak deserve to be subjugated by the strong, where the weak make, and the strong take.

The title of the warriors are based upon their rough measure of fighting skills, the most standard of them are the [Shek Warriors](/Shek_Warrior) and [Shek Scouts](/Shek_Scout). The Shek frequently train in combat and duel with each other to determine their rank in society, with [Hundred Guardians](/Hundred_Guardian) being the top 100 elites of their society below only the strongest of [The Five Invincibles](/The_Five_Invincibles), one of the 5 being [Esata](/Esata_the_Stone_Golem) herself.

The servants of the nation are composed of deserters, cowards and those that are knocked out in combat and branded as such these individuals have their horns cut, are denied from combat, and are subjected to a life similar to indentured servitude where they owe their life to the lord they serve. This is implied to be insufferable for many, causing the formation of [Band of Bones](/Band_of_Bones) where most of their members (most notably their leader [Tora](/Tora_the_Fearless)) have deserted the kingdom to find freedom to fight for themselves.

The Shek love to have an excuse to fight, and have a tendency to impulsively duel and kill others. They are also known for being very demanding and antagonistic towards others. Should their victim resist, they'd often frame it as an act of aggression on the victim's part and seek 'vengeance' for resisting their demands and assaults.

Their society is currently suffering from an ever increasingly impatient populace who wish to launch assaults upon other nations to sate their thirst for bloodshed and battle, and a growing number of them are deserting the kingdom to join [Berserkers](/Berserkers) or [Kral's Chosen](/Kral's_Chosen) as a result. It's likely only a matter of time before the kingdom completely crumbles from the pent-up anger and frustration.

The Shek loathe the races that they consider weaker and often target and harass Humans and Hivers that are travelling through their cities as a result.

[Hashish](/Hashish) is outlawed in the Shek Kingdom and characters will be searched by the gate guards to stop its contraband to the Shek cities.

Being the only faction to live in a barren land with neither any noticeable farms nor mines to support themselves, it could be inferred that their source of income comes from the tributes from the neighboring [Western Hive](/Western_Hive) given their [practice of demanding tributes from neighbors](/Tribute_Round) and the presence of Hive Trader Caravans that occasionally travel through [Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert).

## Military

The Shek Kingdom has an extremely small but skilled force that functions less as disciplined military but more as roaming warbands grouped together based on their rough measure of skills.

The patrols of Shek Kingdom include:

-   Shek Patrol ([Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert), [Spider Plains](/Spider_Plains)) (pot. [Border Zone](/Border_Zone), [Okran's Gulf](/Okran's_Gulf), [Okran's Pride](/Okran's_Pride))
    -   1 ~ 2 Hundred Guardians
    -   4 ~ 8 Shek Warriors

-   Shek Scout Squad ([Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert), [Spider Plains](/Spider_Plains))
    -   4 ~ 7 Shek Scouts

-   Shek Wandering Duelist ([Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert))
    -   1 Shek Fighter

-   Shek Warrior Bar ([Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert))
    -   1 Shek Warrior

When settling in the vicinity of Shek Kingdom's territory, they will send squads to [demand all the food from your outpost as tribute](/Tribute_Round) or [face consequences](/Return_For_Blood).

## Disposition

Shek Kingdom has a list of relations with other factions both inside and outside their country.

### [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation)

Shek Kingdom is presently at conflict with [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation), but the frontlines have remained in a fairly cold stalemate as their forces were called off from assaulting Holy Nation under [Esata](/Esata_the_Stone_Golem) since their assaults would be blocked off by the forces of [Seta](/High_Inquisitor_Seta) and [Phoenix](/Holy_Lord_Phoenix).

The Shek Kingdom have raided their neighbors for food and tribute for as long as they've existed, and continue to do so till this day. The Holy Nation has long been terrorized by them for centuries.

In more recent times, however, the current [Phoenix](/Holy_Lord_Phoenix) has called for [a Holy Crusade](/The_Holy_Crusade) aimed at eradicating the Shek raids to bring peace to their lands once and for all. The Shek leader Shager, enraged on having being forced to held back from their raids responded to this action by mobilizing and sending in a large portion of their population in an attempt to destroy Holy Nation towns and slaughter their populace. Their all-out assaults earned them little success in the end, as even the Sheks themselves admit they were overwhelmed by the forces of Holy Nation, who far outnumbered them.

Shager intended to throw all his remaining weakened warriors away in a [suicide-attack](/Kamikaze) against The Holy Nation, which almost caused a complete destruction of their kingdom, but this was opposed by [Bayan](/Bayan), who was supported by [Esata](/Esata_the_Stone_Golem). Esata dueled Shager to the death and usurped his position. Now under Esata, the main Shek Kingdom force is retained inside her country until an opportunity presents itself.

The Shek Kingdom wishes to crush The Holy Nation, conquer their towns, slaughter their people, and then raid and dominate the rest of the farmers as servants for handing in all their food as tributes.

### [United Cities](/United_Cities)

Much like with [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation), the [United Cities](/United_Cities) were targets for harassment, wars, and raids by the **Shek Kingdom** which earned their wariness and ire.

This has recently changed under Esata, out of a necessity to sign truce with United Cities to focus their war efforts against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation). Although they move on to target and harass United Cities immediately after [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation) is weakened.

### [Kral's Chosen](/Kral's_Chosen)

The Kral's Chosen are a group of rogue Sheks lead by [Flying Bull](/Flying_Bull), who is ostensibly a Shager loyalist that have broken off from the kingdom. Their leader holds considerable respect among the general Shek populace. Despite being similar to a rebel faction, they often live in coexistence with those of the Shek Kingdom. Some of their squads can even spawn inside the territory they own. Their roaming patrols will often harass and attack outsiders.

### [Berserkers](/Berserkers)

Rogue Sheks who have deserted from the kingdom to find excitement in seeking fights and challenges with others, they will often attack outsiders unprovoked.

The Shek Kingdom leadership doesn't really care in eradicating them, stating that they should just be allowed to "Do what they will".

### [Band of Bones](/Band_of_Bones)

Generally composed of deserted Shek Retainers and servants who are fed up with the life of servantry and have refused to give up the sword and resort to banditry. They are generally found in the areas including and south of [The Swamp](/The_Swamp). Members tend to be hornless and malnourished.

### [Western Hive](/Western_Hive)

Neighbors and tributary state to to the **Shek Kingdom** given the [Shek's practice of demanding tribute from their neighbors](/Tribute_Round), with some commodities as evidence.

Caravans of this faction will often spawn inside [Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert).

### [Traders Guild](/Traders_Guild)

Traders that engage in various trading activities such as weapon-sales of [Catun](/Catun_Scrapmaster_\(Manufacturer\)) quality.

Caravans of this faction will often spawn inside [Stenn Desert](/Stenn_Desert).

## Tips

-   [Shek Warriors](/Shek_Warrior) and [Hundred Guardians](/Hundred_Guardian) homeless spawn squads are rare examples of squads that will start unprovoked fights if you are allied with a faction ([Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation)), so one should expect combat when coming close to their territory even if peaceful and neutral.
