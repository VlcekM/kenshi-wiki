Traders Guild Route is a [World State Route](/world-state-routes) where the player has initiated dialogue with [Yamdu](/yamdu) and asked about matters of warfare. To do this, the player needs only not to be an enemy of Traders Guild and Yamdu.

The TG route is diametrically opposed to Anti-Slavers Route, [Shek Kingdom Route](/shek-kingdom-route) and [The Holy Nation Route](/the-holy-nation-route).

The goal of this questline mainly revolves around ridding of [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) and destroy both [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) by pitting them against each other.

## Main Targets

(This part includes the targets that are directly referenced by the questline)

To open up this questline, the player simply has to say to Yamdu "Let's talk about war." and choose the various options.

### [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)

Ah yes, sir. Quite troublesome for production... The main leaders are hiding in the Forbidden Zone, south of the desert, we don’t know where exactly. The Forbidden Zone is out of bounds, it became expensive sending soldiers there... as thrall feed imaginably. But in order for us to continue expanding, Tinfist’s operation needs to be shut down. Once he is killed, his faction will crumble. You would be rewarded well for his eradication.

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

Ah yes, sir. Profits in the weapons trade... \[He pulls out a clip board\]... have plummeted 45% since the Stone Golem took the throne in Stenn. Starting a war with the Shek would be very beneficial to kickstart a few more sales.

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

Good call, sir. The Holy Nation wastes many resources, it’s a shame to leave all of that iron ore unharvested when we can claim it for ourselves and put some of those uneducated civilians to good use.
