**Skeleton Repair Kits** are [medical](/medical-items) [items](/items) used to heal cutting [damage](/damage) on [robotic](/robots) body parts. Players can learn how to craft this item by researching [Robotics](/robotics-tech). After researching, these kits can be crafted on a [Robotics Bench](/robotics-bench). These kits can be stored in [Storage: Robotics](/storage-robotics).

They can be found on some skeleton characters (such as [Tinfist](/tinfist)) and [Police Chiefs](/police-chief) (United Cities Police), as well as on [pretend skeletons](/skeleton-bandits). They can also be found in many ancient ruins.

They can also be found at some shops. Typically, they are sold in Adventurer shops.

Its id in the save editor is 18020-gamedata.base.
