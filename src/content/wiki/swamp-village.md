**Swamp Villages** are two small villages which contain a few buildings and resources within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). Both can be discovered by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Swamps**. Both have Big Shops and [hashish](/hashish) dealers. [Swampers](/swampers) run and protect both villages so they serve as helpful rest stops in [the Swamp](/the-swamp) for players to heal in relative safety.

## Northwest Village

The northwest **Swamp Village** is situated at the northwestern part of The Swamp bordering [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). This village contains a multipurpose supplies shop and is fenced in to help protect against the unforgiving residents.

### Buildings

-   [Makeshift gate](/defensive-gates)
-   Residential building x5 for sale (x4 c.4,000) (x1 c.16,000)
-   Lookout tower
-   Big Shop
-   [Riceweed](/riceweed) farms x8

## Southwest Village

This **Swamp Village** is southwest of [Shark](/shark). Unlike its northern counterpart it lacks a fence and gate and also has some building situated on top of water. It is the midway point between [Shark](/shark) and the [Swamped Lab](/swamped-lab).

### Buildings

-   Residential building x2 for sale (x1 c.4,000) (x1 16,000)
-   Residential Small building
-   Big Shop
-   Lookout Tower
-   [Riceweed](/riceweed) farm x9
