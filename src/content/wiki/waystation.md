**Waystations** are [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) controlled by the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters). There are four in total and two [Waystation Ruins](/waystation-ruins). All Waystation (of the top 4) locations have a chance of being the initial town of the Freedom Seekers [Game Start](/game-starts). An additional Waystation building can be found in Black Scratch. 4 Can be found in the city of [World's End](/worlds-end).

### Waystations

-   In [Border Zone](/waystation-border-zone)
-   In [Grey Desert](/waystation-grey-desert)
-   In [Stenn Desert](/waystation-stenn-desert)
-   In [The Hook](/waystation-the-hook)

### [Waystation Ruins](/waystation-ruins)

-   In [Venge](/venge)
-   In [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden)

### Inside towns

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch) Waystation
-   [World's End](/worlds-end) has 4 Waystation buildings
