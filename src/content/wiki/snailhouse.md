**Snailhouse** is a building in Kenshi which can be constructed or [purchased](/player-owned-buildings-in-town) by the player. It can be constructed after researching [Large Building Shells](/large-building-shells-tech). It is a considerably large structure, costing the same resources to build as the [Stationhouse](/stationhouse).

[Black Dog HQ](/black-dog-hq) is a Snailhouse.

## Unique characteristics

The Snailhouse's curvy shape makes it less arrangeable than the Stationhouse, while also being smaller in general. However, it offers a mostly roofed second floor, which has both advantages and drawbacks:

-   It offers a lot of interior floor space protected from the environment while having a relatively small footprint (e.g. compared to Stationhouse), making it a potentially good choice for compact bases.
-   Conversely, it is a bad choice for town living due to the severely limited area for placing [Small Wind Generators](/small-wind-generator) and [Rain Collectors](/rain-collector).
-   The small exposed portion on the second floor can have turrets. However they can only cover the area near the entrance, which makes this building a much worse option if the player wants to utilize buildings as a second layer of defense (first one being [Defensive Walls](/defensive-walls)).
