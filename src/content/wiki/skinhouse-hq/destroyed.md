If [Savant](/savant) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), the [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq) falls into [ruins](/town-overrides). Any valuable loot left behind will then be removed.

## Trivia

-   The "destroyed" overrides of [Skinhouses](/skinhouse) and the [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq) are the only [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) items of type "Town" in use which are not assigned to any faction. In practice, though, the game treats them as if they still belonged to the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).
