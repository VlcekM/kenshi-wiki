## Overview

****Universal Wasteland Expansion**** (often shortened to **UWE**) is a singularly developed Kenshi mod created by TreadLightly77. It is a _total world overhaul_ designed to expand nearly every aspect of Kenshi’s base game while remaining faithful to its lore and mechanics.

Instead of overwriting or discarding Kenshi’s vanilla design, UWE carefully expands upon existing systems. Every aspect of the game—worldstates, factions, animals, stealth, theft, quests, dialogue, and more—has been fleshed out to make the world feel deeper, harsher, and more dynamic.

The mod also integrates and updates all of the author’s earlier overhaul/content mods into a single package.

## Features

##### Expanded World & Lore

-   Vanilla lore references are fully realized (e.g., the mythical _Grievewraiths_ appear as monsters, and Hamut’s wife Lena can be found at a slave town).
-   More towns, villages, and points of interest to discover.
-   New quests, dialogue, and races that integrate seamlessly with existing lore.

##### Reworked Mechanics

-   Theft, looting, and stealth are overhauled to provide a greater challenge.
-   Shopkeepers and guards now react realistically to players sneaking in shops.
-   Animals made more detailed and impactful.

**Dynamic Factions & Worldstates**

-   65+ factions with expanded rosters and equipment.
-   More varied armor and weapons across enemy groups.
-   Over 500 new town overrides—killing or kidnapping a leader can trigger full power struggles and ownership flips.
-   Chain reactions allow towns to change hands based on world events.

##### Gameplay Experience

-   Early-game difficulty stretched into mid/late-game for a desperate, survivalist tone.
-   Constant threat of danger until players are truly established.
-   New game starts, gear, and assets to expand replayability.

## Installation & Load Order

-   A **new game start** is strongly recommended when first using UWE.
-   An import is possible, but it is advised to back up saves before importing.
-   **Load Order:** UWE should always be placed at the very bottom of your mod load order with the exception of UWE patches and specific load order instructions by other mods.
