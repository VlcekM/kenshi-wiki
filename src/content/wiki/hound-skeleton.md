**Hound Skeleton** is an unused character in Kenshi. They can only be accessed through the [Forgotten Construction Set](/forgotten-construction-set).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
