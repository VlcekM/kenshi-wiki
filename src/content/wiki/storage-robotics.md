**Storage: Robotics** is a [Storage](/storage) container mainly for storing [Robotics](/items#robotics) and [Technology](/items#technology) items. Players can construct this building after researching [Robotics (Tech)](/robotics-tech).

## Details

This chest has a storage space of **12x12** squares and a stacking multiplier of 5, which is the same as [General Storage Chest](/general-storage-chest). As such, the main use case for this storage box is for robotics crafting jobs.

## Compatible items

-   [Skeleton Eye](/skeleton-eye)
-   [Skeleton Muscle](/skeleton-muscle)
-   [Skeleton Repair Kit](/skeleton-repair-kit)
-   [Robotics Components](/robotics-components)
-   [Motor](/motor)
-   [Press](/press)
-   [Gears](/gears)
-   [Capacitor](/capacitor)
-   [Tools](/tools)
-   [Hinge](/hinge)
-   [Authentic Skeleton Repair Kit](/authentic-skeleton-repair-kit)

Notably, robotic trade junk like [CPU Units](/cpu-unit) or [Power Cores](/power-core) are not compatible.
