**Swamp Ninja Hideout** is the base of the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) and is located in the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands). Many [Swamp Ninja Genin](/swamp-ninja-genin), [Swamp Ninja Chunin](/swamp-ninja-chunin), [Swamp Ninja Jonin](/swamp-ninja-jonin), and [Shade](/shade), leader of the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas), reside here.
