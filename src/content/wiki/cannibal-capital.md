Once the town of [Deadcat](/deadcat), it has fallen to [Cannibals](/cannibals). It will appear in the map initially as the neutral town of Deadcat, presumably to lure players there, thinking it is a safe haven from the cannibal hordes following them.

There is a large number of [Cannibals](/cannibal) and [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal) in the area. In addition, there are at least 4 [Cannibal Chiefs](/cannibal-chief), with two guarding the Cannibal leader, the [Cannibal Grand Wizard](/cannibal-grand-wizard).

The [Captive's Journal Series](/captives-journal-series) can be found in this location, inside the chests in the Grand Wizard's Hut.
