_This article is about the farm located in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley). For the farm of a similar name in [Burning Forest](/burning-forest), see [Farm Ruin](/farm-ruin)._

**Farm Ruins** is a location at the northernmost tip of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley). It is comprised of destroyed buildings containing no notable loot.

The close proximity this farm is to [Ravaged Hamlets](/ravaged-hamlet) would suggest that this area was razed and abandoned after [United Cities](/united-cities)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> pushed into [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) territory, but the conflict frontlines has since been pushed back by The Holy Nation forces to [Bast](/bast-zone) and stayed there till this day.

Four [Camp Beds](/camp-bed), which are free to use, can be found here.
