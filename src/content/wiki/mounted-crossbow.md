The **Mounted Crossbow** is a type of defensive turret. It can be built on the ground or on top of [Defensive Walls](/defensive-walls). By researching [Crossbow Turret Strings](/crossbow-turret-strings-tech) and [Composite Runners](/composite-runners) you can increase the damage of your Mounted Crossbows.

The accuracy, and speed of reloading a turret is modified by the operator's related skills and the height difference between the turret and its target impacts the accuracy, range and damage. Turrets will be more effective if fired from above, less effective if fired from below a target.

This building cannot be upgraded to [Mounted Crossbow MkII](/mounted-crossbow-mkii). In order to switch a mounted crossbow to an improved mounted crossbow, players must dismantle their existing mounted crossbow, then build the desired one.

This crossbow uses 0 power, except when upgraded with the light, which requires 4 more power.
