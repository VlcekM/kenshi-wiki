**Reaver Ironclads** are the toughest members of the [Reavers](/reavers), aside from [Valamon](/valamon) himself. They can be found leading groups of [Reaver Bloodeds](/reaver-blooded) in patrols in [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [The Crags](/the-crags), [The Outlands](/the-outlands), and [The Pits East](/the-pits-east). They can also be found in [Ark](/ark) and in [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).

Reaver Ironclads will enslave any nearby unconscious characters.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (20% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
