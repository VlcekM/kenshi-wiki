**Independent Bar** is a [bar](/bar) found in [Mongrel](/mongrel). There are typically three instances of this vendor in Mongrel.

### Possible Locations

-   [Mongrel](/mongrel)

Depending on World States, there may be a Independent Bar found in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)
-   [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last)
-   [Heng](/heng)

### Residents

-   [Barman](/barman)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard)

The items found in these bars might vary due to trade culture. As the Mongrel faction does not appear to have a trade culture, these bars might even sell [Bloodrum](/bloodrum).
