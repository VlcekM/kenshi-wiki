_This article is about the [Old World](/old-world) faction. For the faction found in the current game, see [Deadcat](/deadcat)._

Dead Cat is a neutral [faction](/factions-alpha) that own the towns of [Catan](/catun-alpha), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady-alpha), [Telbooze](/telbooze), [Bark](/bark-alpha) and [Heft](/heft-alpha). Dead Cat is protected by Mercenaries and Police. Currently this faction does not have any patrols associated with it but on occasion the Police in town will wander off. Since these towns do have Police, they are a good place turn in [bounties](/bounty). 

Dead Cat starts out with a negative relation with The Holy Nation. If you start a game with a [Holy Sword](/game-starts#the-holy-sword) character you will spawn in Catan.
