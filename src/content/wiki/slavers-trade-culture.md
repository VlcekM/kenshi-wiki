**Slavers Trade Culture** is a [trade culture](/traders) which removes cultural items from [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) and some food items. Forbidden items are identical to the [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture), [Empire Lawless Trade Culture](/empire-lawless-trade-culture) and [Southeast Trade Culture.](/southeast-trade-culture)

**List of factions concerned by this trade culture:**

[Slavers](/slavers)  

**Forbidden items (unspawnable items, things you won't find):**

-   [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame)
-   [A Holy Purpose](/a-holy-purpose)
-   [Holy Edict](/holy-edict)
-   [Bloodrum](/bloodrum)
-   [Hashish](/hashish)
-   [Hemp](/hemp)
-   [Rice Bowl](/rice-bowl)
-   [Riceweed](/riceweed)
-   [Gohan](/gohan)
-   [Dried Fish](/dried-fish)
-   [Grand Fish](/grand-fish)
-   [Thinfish](/thinfish)
-   [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables)
-   [Small Emperor Statue](/small-emperor-statue)

  

**Illegal buildings (builds that are considered illegal and will trigger the dialogue event):**

**Illegal goods (contraband):**
