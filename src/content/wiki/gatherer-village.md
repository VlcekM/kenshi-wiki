There are two settlements of this type in [Kenshi](/kenshi) world.

## Eastern Gatherer Village

**Gatherer Village** is a small settlement in [Dreg](/dreg) where [Deadcat Fishermen](/deadcat-fisherman), a Unique Character called [Suspicious Settler](/suspicious-settler), and his two pets [Beloved Goat](/beloved-goat) and [Settler's Pup](/settlers-pup) live. The place lacks any shops, but the [Suspicious Settler](/suspicious-settler) will offer you some fish to purchase if you choose not to attack him. The place is often swarmed by the local [Fogmen](/fogmen) and [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws).

### Buildings

-   [Residential buildings](/metal-wallhouse) x3

## Western Gatherer Village

Further west there is another **Gatherer Village** where a few [Deadcat Fishermen](/deadcat-fisherman) managed to survive. It is completely fenced in and protected by a locked [Makeshift gate](/defensive-gates) to shelter it from the outside world. [Fogmen](/fogmen) and [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) can be seen often patrolling outside its walls.

### Buildings

-   Residential buildings x2
-   Makeshift gate
