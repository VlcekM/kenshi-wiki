A **Fogman** is a [Hive](/hive) worker drone from the [Fogmen](/fogmen) faction.

Fogmen are deformed Hive drones. They lurk in the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) in large groups and are hostile towards anyone they come across. They will kidnap nearby unconscious characters.

Fogmen can also be found in the [Tower of Fog](/tower-of-fog) and [Deadhive Overrun](/deadhive-overrun).

They will start appearing in Hive territories if their respective queens have died, implying the original Hivers turned.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
