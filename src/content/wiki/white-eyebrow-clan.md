**White Eyebrow Clan** is a minor faction. It is not implemented in the game and is mentioned only in a [Wanted Poster](/wanted-posters) on White Eyebrow, the leader of the clan. This faction would likely have been made up of only [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander). According to the wanted poster, they could have likely been found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). According to their stats they are an enemy of the [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas).
