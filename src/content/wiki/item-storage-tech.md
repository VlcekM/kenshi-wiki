**Item Storage** is a [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) which gives players the ability to construct general [Storage](/storage) containers as well as [Food](/food)\-specific containers.

This research is free and quick and should be done by all players. General Storage Chest and Boxes are convenient for use when players only have a couple of a specific item and don't want to construct a specific storage container. Food Store and Animal Feeders hold all types of food and characters will run to take from them whenever they're hungry enough to eat.
