**Large Backpack** is an unwieldy [backpack](/backpacks) that will hamper your fighting ability. Of all the backpacks available to humanoids, it has the largest grid, which is **12x20**. Only the [Garru Backpack](/garru-backpack) and [Bull Backpack](/bull-backpack) are larger. They can also stack [Trade Goods](/trade-goods), which this bag can't. Due to this bag being tied with [Traders Backpack Large](/traders-backpack-large) as the backpacks with the largest combat penalties, it can be used when training combat skills to drastically increase the experience gained.

It can be bought from many general [Item Traders](/item-trader) for c.3,000.
