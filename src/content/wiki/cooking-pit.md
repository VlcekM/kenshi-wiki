**Cooking Pit** usually found in [Cannibals](/cannibals) camps, also with some of the Prisoner Pole beside it. The function of Cooking Pit is to cook the prisoner or the victim of cannibals, but it also functions as [Corpse Furnace](/corpse-furnace).

## Trivia

-   When disposing a corpse using the Cooking Pit, there will be a floating "Cooking" line as opposed to showing "Incinerating" when using a typical Corpse Furnace.
