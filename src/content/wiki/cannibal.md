**Cannibals** are members of the [Cannibals](/cannibals) faction. They roam the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) and [Darkfinger](/darkfinger) with several other members looking to capture people and return to their outposts in order to consume their prisoners as food.

They usually roam in squads numbering 6 ~ 25 Cannibals, or 16 of them on average.

Besides the obvious anatomical differences, a cannibal's gender is made clear by the pattern of their war paint/tribal markings. Males wear darker paint around their legs and have a rib cage like bone marking on their upper torso. A female's paint is all done in a blood red pattern with a symbol at their stomach that looks somewhat similar to the symbol of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They wear no other clothing besides this paint/markings.

Despite living in harsh conditions, Cannibals are equipped with simple [Basic First Aid Kits](/basic-first-aid-kit), which means that they are aware of the dangers of having open wounds or passing out in their unsafe neighborhood and/or they want to bandage up the victims they captured for them to be alive and keep their 'meat fresh'.
