**Flotsam Jonin** are veteran soldiers of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), they have better skills than the typical [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja).

3 of them can be found in Flotsam's HQ, acting similar to [Moll's](/moll) bodyguards. 1 or 2 of them will spawn in one or both [Scout Posts](/scout-post) depending on if [Mani](/mani) is killed or not.

They have a 5% chance to spawn with a bounty ranging from c.1,000 to c.3,000 from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They wear \[Standard\] armour (20% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Tips

-   Their high attack skill makes them great opponents for training [Melee Defense](/melee-defense) and [Dodge](/dodge) should one manage to capture them.
    -   It is advised that you replace their weapon with something other than Ninja Blade, with the worst quality possible such as an [unknown](/unknown-manufacturer) grade [wakizashi](/wakizashi) or [iron stick](/iron-stick).
    -   It is also advised to equip a member with full masterwork heavy armor.
    -   Done correctly, it could raise a character whose defense level is similar to a [Holy Sentinel](/holy-sentinel) (40) to the level of a [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant) (60) in a single round before they are KO'd.
