**Outpost s-V** is a building shell similar to [Outpost s-III](/outpost-s-iii). It cannot be constructed by the player. They can be found in [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) [Police Barracks](/police-barracks) and in the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city), which might mean they originated in the [Second Empire](/second-empire).
