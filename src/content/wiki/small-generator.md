**Small Generator** is a [building](/buildings-list) which turns [Fuel](/fuel) into [power](/power). It can be constructed after researching [Fuel Generator Theory](/fuel-generator-theory-tech) and upgraded through research into [Upgraded Small Generators](/upgraded-small-generators-tech). This upgrade can be repeated for a total output of 70.

Fuel can be made with a [Biofuel Distillery](/biofuel-distillery) (using Wheatstraw, Greenfruit, or Hemp) or found in some Construction Shops.

### Details

-   Power output: 40
-   Efficiency: 100%
