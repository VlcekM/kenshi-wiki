**Holy Nation Outlaws** are a small and insignificant [Faction](/factions) of bandits and criminals who are on the run from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They are typically found in small groups of gangs wandering out in the wilds. [The Hub](/the-hub) and [Rebel Base](/rebel-base) are nominally controlled by them.

While not inherently hostile to the player, they can attack out of a need for food like the starvers, or for other reasons. One could potentially recruit them if they are injured in the wild. They will often have the same dialogue reactions as the Holy Nation when spotting skeleton player characters, but do not attack on sight unless the player is already their enemy.

They are generally found in areas in and around The Holy Nation territory, albeit usually in a much lesser frequency and numbers compared to the more numerous [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) in regions where they overlap<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, making them even more insignificant than starvers.

Compared to the [starvers](/starving-bandits), they are also rarely found in [Vain](/vain), [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) but _not_ in [Shem](/shem) or [Northern Coast](/northern-coast).
