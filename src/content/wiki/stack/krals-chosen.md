If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are kidnapped or killed, this location will be overtaken by [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), assuming [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive and free.

This town was slaughtered and conquered by the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) after the combined pressure of [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) and [Flying Bull's](/flying-bull) forces overwhelmed The Holy Nation's southern defense after their fracture in the chain of command after Seta was defeated.

Alternatively, the town may have also already been conquered by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) prior and merely changed its ownership to Kral's Chosen after Esata was defeated and imprisoned/killed.

Some of the squads found in this town are still affiliated with Shek Kingdom.

Much like the [Shek Kingdom override](/stack/shek-kingdom), it appears to be a town where most buildings and original inhabitants are destroyed and massacred in the midst of the conflict, with a few of the buildings rebuilt and Shek's HQ relocated in this area.

This override can switch

-   To [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) if [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is imprisoned or dead while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is free.
-   To [Destroyed](/stack/destroyed) if [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are all imprisoned/dead.
