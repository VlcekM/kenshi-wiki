_This article is a [stub](/category/article-stubs). You can help the Kenshi Wiki by [expanding it](/stack/destroyed)._

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) have all been killed or kidnapped, [Stack](/stack) will fall into ruins under the control of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

It appears that this place is (depending on previous overrides) nominally reclaimed<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> by The Holy Nation after Shek Kingdom/Kral's Chosen have been defeated or vacated from this area, or otherwise evacuated and abandoned by the military after the high command governing this settlement is incapacitated.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

All buildings are abandoned and destroyed in this override.
