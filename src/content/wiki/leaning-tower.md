**Leaning Tower** is a location in the [Floodlands](/floodlands), and as its name suggests, the location is a leaning tower. This location is controlled by the faction [Floodland Ruins](/floodland-ruins).

  

## Dangers

-   There are no enemy spawns inside the Leaning Tower, however plenty of [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) can be found in the Floodlands around.
