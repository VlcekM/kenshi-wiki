Inhabited by Old Empire Police Robots or [ErrorCode 0x000000](/errorcode-0x000000), these locations contain little loot.

The Police Barracks located in [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) is empty.

When entering Police Barracks located in the Pits, the Police Robots instantly shout "POLICE!" and will attack. If you run away, they will pursue you indefinitely. Their position(s) will be shown as a red blip on the map. Only death will stop them.
