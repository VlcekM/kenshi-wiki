**Outpost Items** are all the building blocks used in the creation of items and buildings. They are the materials you must buy, gather, and produce to expand your empire. Raw materials will need to be processed through [Crafting](/crafting).

## Raw Materials

These items are often refined in [Industry Buildings](/industry-buildings) before being used for [Construction](/construction).

-   [Animal Skin](/animal-skin)
-   [Raw Copper](/raw-copper)
-   [Raw Iron](/raw-iron)
-   [Raw Meat](/raw-meat)
-   [Raw Stone](/raw-stone)
-   [Water](/water)
