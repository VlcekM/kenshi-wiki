If the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Moll](/moll) are all [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Rebirth](/rebirth) gets [overtaken](/town-overrides) by [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws).

All of the buildings are destroyed in this override, with only a few squatters inside some of the ruins.
