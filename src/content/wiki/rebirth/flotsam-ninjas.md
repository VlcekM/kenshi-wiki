If [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), and [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Rebirth](/rebirth) is [overtaken](/town-overrides) by the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

Most of the areas are destroyed after the takeover, with few buildings changed into residence and shops. It is mostly patrolled by Flotsam Ninjas with no signs of servants, presumably having escaped or died within the midst of chaos following the death of the above important individuals.

## Buildings

3 patrols of 4 ~ 10 [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) spawn in this area.

### FCS values (Priority)

-   2x Flotsam Guards (3)
-   2x Flotsam Refuge (2)
-   1x Flotsam Armory (2)
-   1x Hospital (2)
-   10x Destroyed Buildings

#### Gate Guards

-   3x Flotsam Gate Guards (4)

## Trivia

-   This override will change the Rebirth icon on the map from a "labour camp" (pickaxe and shovel) to one similar to an "Outpost" (cross-swords with a banner).
