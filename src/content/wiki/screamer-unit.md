**Screamer Units** are members of the, now dead, [Second Empire](/second-empire). They can be found in [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii), protecting [Rhinobot](/rhinobot).

They can also spawn as the members of [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion), patrolling the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

They wear \[High\] armour (15% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Screamer Units have an unarmed stat set of 10, which overrides the Dexterity level they would otherwise have.
    -   If they did not have this unarmed stat value set, their Dexterity would be 60, unarmed 6 and dodge 4.8.
