This [Waystation](/waystation) is a small outpost in the center of the landmass. It is found in the easternmost area of the Borderlands. Waystation is controlled by [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters). Its residents include [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild) members. This is the only waystation with a robotics workshop.

It can be discovered by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Border Zone.**

This location is a good place to rest, especially after being harassed by a group of [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) or [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits). It is a good place to sell items looted from the nearby ruins such as [Tiny Settlement](/tiny-settlement) and [Ruined Holy Outpost](/ruined-holy-outpost).

As with all other non-ruined Waystations, this place has a 25% chance to spawn pacifiers from [United Cities](/united-cities), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).
