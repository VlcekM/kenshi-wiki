**Skinner's Roam** is a fairly large rocky desert territory belonging to the [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They control multiple [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) in the area, with the ones closest to [Deadlands](/deadlands) abandoned, though still "claimed" by The Holy Nation. A valley between ridges in the South of the zone corresponds to the geographic center of the Kenshi [World map](/world-of-kenshi).

This region is one of the 2 frontiers between [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, with the other (and main) one being [Border Zone](/border-zone).

The lack of patrols in this area allows huge groups of hungry bandits to harass travelers. Wild Bulls deem it safe enough to roam the zone in large herds.

Skinner's Roam is also known for the huge dust wall that might have ruined one of the Holy Mines.

## Trivia

-   [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam-alpha) used to be a town in the [Alpha version](/old-world) of the game.
-   If no world states have been triggered Starving Bandits are extremely common, taking up a third of the homeless spawn pool.
    -   Once certain world states have been triggered, they become much rarer with [Hive Bandits](/hive-bandit), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) appearing relatively more often.
        -   For frequency, [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) in [Border Zone](/border-zone) are 2x/5x/3.33x more frequent than [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)/[Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)/[Western Hive](/western-hive) but is only 1x/2x/0.66x as frequent in this zone.
        -   For individuals, it is 8.16x/4.16x for [Western Hive](/western-hive), 6.53x/6.66x for [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), 0.7x/0.8x for [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
            -   The size would indicate that there are roughly half as much starving bandits, with HN and SK also sending out half as much of their forces here as compared to [Border Zone](/border-zone).
-   Since skinning [River Raptors](/river-raptor) is relatively profitable for the lower classes, it's possible that the region gained its name from raptor huntsmen going to sell their [Animal Skins](/animal-skin) in either [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) or [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth), and taking the namesake desert as a shortcut instead of following the winding [river](/wend) of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), sacrificing safety for speed.
