**Mongrel Clothes Shop** is a shop which sells [Clothing](/clothing) and some [Armour](/armour) in [Mongrel](/mongrel), it is also possible to buy some blueprints from this area.

### Possible Locations

-   [Mongrel](/mongrel)

### Residents

-   [Ronin Armour Trader](/ronin-armour-trader)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (2)

This shop is capable of spawning [Artifacts](/artifacts), as such it may spawn items not in the list. These items, however, will not respawn when the shop restocks provided that they are purchased.
