**Berserkers** are rogue Shek fighters that deserted the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) to return to their old tribal nature to seek challenges and conflicts.

It appears that many berserkers were expelled or migrated northwards, far away from the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), ultimately forming [Berserker Country](/berserker-country). However, a significant number of them stayed behind, along with their most important leader, [Ghost](/ghost). Their Combat levels are in the 40+ ranges.   

While talking to [Bayan](/bayan) about alliances, the player can suggest taking care of the Berserker problem plaguing their lands, Bayan responds to that choice by stating that they should let Berserkers "do what they will"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The berserker Sheks are recognized by their [maroon](/colour-scheme) clothes and armour.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
