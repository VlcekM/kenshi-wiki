**Vigilante Chiefs** are members of the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) faction. They act as the Police Chiefs for towns controlled by the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) in the northeastern territories.

The Vigilante Chiefs use the dialogue of standard Police Chiefs. This means they can potentially have unique dialogues for turning in many same bounties as under the [United Cities](/united-cities) and use the same legal system as they do.

They spawn with a bounty ranging from c.2,500 to c.3,500. They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Interaction

**Vigilante Chiefs** act as [Police Chiefs](/police-chief) for Empire Peasant controlled towns. As such, bounties can be turned over to them.
