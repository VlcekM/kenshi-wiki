**Tower of Spiders** is a [watchtower](/watchtower) in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) with 30-40 Elder (1.6x to 2.0x age multiplier) [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) and several containers of miscellaneous loot, including skeleton repair kits and [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book). It can be discovered by reading a **Tech Hunters [Map](/maps)**.

Total item count: 20

Refresh time: 24 hours

No Artifacts system spawning here. There will be exactly 6 Ancient Science Books at this location.
