A **Small Shack** is the smallest and cheapest building players can [construct](/construction). Small shacks require no research in order to build. They take very little time to build and can contain only the [Small Research Bench](/small-research-bench) level I, making this building a perfect starting platform for early-game research.

Purchasable **Small Shack** can be located almost around the entirety of maps, usually appearing in big or major cities.

##  Small House Building Info

-   Building Condition - 5
-   Door Condition - 12
-   Door Lock - lvl15

## Trivia

For purchasable Small Shacks located in towns, before player bought them, they are usually private and host actual NPC residents. Entering these private shacks will trigger dialogues, and if the player remains in these private shacks, they will be attacked by the original residents. Also, after purchasing these Small Shack, the original interior will disappear.
