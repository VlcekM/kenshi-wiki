**Outpost s-III** are large building models that require appropriate [technologies](/outpost-type-tech) to be researched in order to be built. As the in-game description suggests, these building types construct well on awkward or uneven terrain.

The outside ramp prevents characters from walking under them, therefore avoiding an issue present in exterior stairs of buildings like [Storm Houses](/storm-house) and [Stephouses](/stephouse) where characters get stuck underneath when the building protrudes from the ground too much.

Outpost s-III can be purchased in the following cities and towns:

-   [Catun](/catun)
-   [Mourn](/mourn)

## Interior limitations

-   Due to a low ceiling, not every [interior building](/buildings-list) can fit inside. For example, a [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy) is impossible to fit on the first floor due to its height.
-   Ceiling lamps on the first floor will hang low to the floor, limiting their light cone.
-   Interior buildings cannot be constructed on the exterior "ring" walkway of the Outpost s-III tower because that space is qualified as "outside".
-   This building has no mount points for turrets_\[clarify\]_. However, they can be built as freely standing on the outer ring_\[clarify\]_.

The similarly shaped and sized [Outpost s-IV](/outpost-s-iv) gives the player more flexibility in furnishing.

## Outpost s-III Building Info

-   Building Condition - 40
-   Door Condition - 40
-   Door Lock - lvl15

## Trivia

-   The **Outpost s-III** types can technically be placed on top of [Stationhouses](/stationhouse). This could have been an amazing opportunity to create spacious palace-like structures. It is however impossible to regulate the height of the new construction, leaving the outpost uselessly hanging above the Stationhouse structure. Using the FCS might help you to construct one building on top of another correctly.
-   This type of building has visual variants which cannot be built by the player: [Outpost s-I](/outpost-s-i) and [Outpost s-V](/outpost-s-v).
