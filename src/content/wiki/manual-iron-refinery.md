**Manual Iron Refinery** is an [Industry Building](/industry-buildings) where players can convert [Raw Iron](/raw-iron) into [Iron Plates](/iron-plates). This is the first of many buildings which can perform this task. It requires one to three workers to operate. Players can construct this building after researching [Iron Plates](/iron-plates-tech).

The **Manual Iron Refinery** cannot be upgraded into the [Iron Refinery](/iron-refinery) for some reason. This building is still desirable, as it does not require electricity to operate.
