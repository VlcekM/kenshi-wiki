**Slave Master 'Sly' Ruben** is a noble member of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). They can be found at [South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp) in [Heng](/heng-zone).

This character has a Traitorous [Personality](/personality).

As with the other TG Nobles, Ruben lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house) and is guarded by 4 [Hooded Empire Noble Guard](/hooded-empire-noble-guard) characters and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## [World States](/world-states)

[Killing or imprisoning](/world-states) Slave Master Ruben results in South Stone Camp falling to ruins.

If [Haga](/slave-master-haga) and [Grande](/slave-master-grande) are all also killed, [Heng](/heng) will fall into a malnourished state if still being under [United Cities](/united-cities) rule.

If [Wada](/slave-master-wada) is also killed, [Heft](/heft) will become malnourished if still being under [United Cities](/united-cities) rule.

If [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) is also killed while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive and free. Patrols of malnourished [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) ranging from 4-13 individuals to start spawning in [Heng](/heng), [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Eye](/the-eye).

If [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) is also killed while [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive and free, patrols of [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slaver-jonin) will spawn in [Heng](/heng-zone).

[Slave Trader](/slave-traders) and [Manhunter](/manhunter) patrol will decrease in frequency moderately at [Heng](/the-great-desert), provided [Ren](/slave-master-haga) wasn't already killed beforehand (which provides the same effect).

**Slave Master Ruben** is considered to be one of the Northern Slave Masters/Mistresses alongside with [Longen](/longen), [Lady Kana](/lady-kana), [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada), [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande). Killing or imprisoning them all will completely remove [Manhunter](/manhunter) and [Slave Hunter](/slave-hunter) patrols.

## Trivia

-   Out of all TG Nobles, **Slave Master Ruben** is the only character with a chance to spawn as both male or female.
-   In the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set), they are referred to as "noble stone boss/ Sly" in reference to their squad.
