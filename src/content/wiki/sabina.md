Sabina was an author that wrote the books [Mass Extinction II](/mass-extinction-series), [The Lost Ancients II](/the-lost-ancients-series), and [Okranite Theories In Relation To Findings I](/okranite-theories-in-relation-to-findings-series). Little is known about Sabina since Sabina doesn't exist as an NPC that players can interact with, however, it's likely that Sabina was a [Machinist](/machinists) and that Sabina is possibly dead (hence the lack of an NPC). Sabina's is cited by [Finch](/finch) in [The Lost Ancients I](/the-lost-ancients-series).
