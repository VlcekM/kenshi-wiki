**Rebel Fighters** are members of the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) faction. They appear as patrols and guards of towns controlled by the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) in the northeastern territories.

They have a 20% chance to spawn with a crossbow. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (25% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient). They have a 10% chance to spawn with a bounty ranging from c.2500 to c.3500.

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Hive Soldier Drone
    
-   Hive Worker Drone
