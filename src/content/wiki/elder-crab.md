**Elder Crabs** are larger and stronger variants of the normal [Crab](/crab). They can be found rarely wandering certain eastern regions of the map.

Though they are significantly stronger than a standard crab, they are still quite outclassed by their larger siblings such as the [Megacrab](/megacrab) and the ones found in the [Crab Tournament](/crab-tournament). They spawn at an age of 1.9 to 2.0 meaning their base stats are multiplied by that amount.
