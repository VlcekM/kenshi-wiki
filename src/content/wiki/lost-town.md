The **Lost Town** is a large ruin located within the [Howler Maze](/howler-maze). It is currently home to multiple [crab](/crab) nests and a [Megacrab](/megacrab).

The ruined town consists of multiple buildings with one containing a library for higher end loot as well as multiple areas where you might find mid-low level loot.

## Tips

-   The Megacrab isn't very good at detecting characters in stealth mode and sticks to a very small patrol area, making it very easy to sneak past.
-   Nests of crabs spawn within the buildings. When these buildings are opened, the crabs will swarm into the middle area, making it more difficult to fight the [Megacrab](/megacrab).
-   If you wait long enough in-game, it is possible for the baby crabs nesting in the buildings to age to "Elder". These are much more dangerous than the adult crabs that patrol the maze.
-   [Acid rain](/acid-rain) and continuous crab patrols can make this area difficult.
