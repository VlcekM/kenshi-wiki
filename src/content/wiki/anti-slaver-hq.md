62175-rebirth.mod**Anti-Slaver HQ** is the headquarters of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) faction, located in [Spring](/spring). The headquarters is in a [swamp dome](/swamp-dome) and is located in the center of town.

## Residents

-   [Tinfist](/tinfist)
-   [Grey](/grey)
-   [Anti-Slaver Jonin](/anti-slaver-jonin) (HQ Variant)
-   [Anti-Slaver](/anti-slaver) (HQ Variant)

## Interior

The interior has a sleeping area, training area with 6 [Training Dummies](/training-dummy), a kitchen, cages, a storage area, and a main sitting area.

## Item Spawns

There are several container types, including wood barrels, metal chests, wood chests, baskets and [weapon cabinets](/weapon-cabinet). The containers mostly hold stealth gear and [Katanas](/katanas).
