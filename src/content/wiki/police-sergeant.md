**Police Sergeant** is a higher-leveled version of the [Samurai Police](/samurai-police), generally residing in police stations in the [United Cities](/united-cities).

Has EXTREMELY high detection of characters with any bounty, on par with bounty hunters. This character wears a [Faction Uniform](/faction-uniform), meaning any looted armour will be tagged as a [Disguise](/disguise).
