The **Turtleneck** is worn in the [shirt](/shirts) armour slot. It does not provide any armour or stat buffs/debuffs.

There is also a short-sleeved and no-sleeved version of this, which are [Cloth Shirt](/cloth-shirt) and [White Vest](/white-vest), respectively. A [differently named version of this armour](/dyed-turtleneck) can be dyed.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

## Appearance

A plain, fully sleeved white shirt.

## Crafting

Turtlenecks can be crafted on the [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench). A Blueprint is not needed.

## Trivia

-   The description is probably a reference to the show [Archer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer_\(2009_TV_series\)), where there are reoccurring references to the fact the titular character, a spy, wears turtlenecks on stealth missions.
