**Monk Pants** are clothing that are wearable as pants. They are also widely available at Clothing Traders throughout the game. They neither improve or decrease protection or stats for the character wearing them.

Monk Pants are most notably worn by [Finch](/finch) and [Iyo](/iyo) as well as [Holy Priest](/holy-priest), [Holy Citizen](/holy-citizen), and [Holy Farmer Wife](/holy-farmer-wife) characters. When worn by [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) characters they are part of a set with the [Monk Robe](/monk-robe) (though they often substitute these pants with [Stout Hessian](/stout-hessian)).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x5**.

## Appearance

Monk Pants are orange in color and fit loose on the characters legs.
