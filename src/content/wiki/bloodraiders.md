The **Bloodraiders** are a faction of hostile bandits found primarily within [The Eye](/the-eye), as well as the adjacent [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) and [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached, called 'blood bandits', which happens to be the same scheme as the one used for the [Reavers](/reavers). The rank of 'Blooded' in the Reavers is also a similar motif.

##  Characters

-   [Blood Raider](/blood-raider)

Member of this faction have a chance to spawn as this race (relative chance)

-   Greenlander (100)
-   Shek (35)
