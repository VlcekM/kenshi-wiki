**Outlaw Heavies** are members of the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) faction. These are the closest the rebel farmers have to heavy infantry, forming the core of heavy squads roaming [Bast](/bast-zone), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), and [Sinkuun](/sinkuun). They can be found in groups of 3-7, sometimes being tagged along by 1-3 [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman) but are always led by an [Outlaw Swordsman Captain](/outlaw-swordsman-captain).

With [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) dead, an extra heavy squad will spawn (with a higher maximum of 11 Heavies) in Bast, Heng, and [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

Heavies can also be found in groups of 5-6 in Barracks in [Fort Simion](/fort-simion), as well 4-6 serving as bodyguards for [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) himself.

They will also populate the Barracks in peasant-controlled [Port North](/port-north), [Port South](/port-south), and [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south).

A heavy squad will also help repel attacks by the [United Cities](/united-cities) on the player base should the player be an ally of the Rebel Farmers.

All these squads stop spawning when [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed, having presumably either laying down the sword, or joining the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen). 

They have a 10% chance to have a bounty ranging from c.2,500 to c.3,500. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (25% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown) or [Ancient](/ancient).
