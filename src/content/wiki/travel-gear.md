**Travel Gear** is a [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) vendor found in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and some [United Cities](/united-cities) locations. It is quite similar to the [Tech Hunter Shop](/tech-hunter-shop) and [Travel and Repairs Shop](/travel-and-repairs-shop) found in other [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) locations.

### Possible Locations

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Farming Village](/farming-village)
-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Last Stand](/last-stand)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Squin](/squin)

Depending on World States, there may be a Independent Bar found in these locations:

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)
-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Squin](/squin)
-   [Stack](/stack)

### Residents

-   [Tech Hunter Shopkeeper](/tech-hunter-shopkeeper)
-   [Mercenary](/mercenary) (3)

This location has an item [Artifacts](/artifacts) value of c.100,000, so it should spawn some additional items through the artifacts system.
