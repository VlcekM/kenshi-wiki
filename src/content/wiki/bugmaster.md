The **Bugmaster** is a dangerous criminal who resides in the [Throne of the Bugmaster](/throne-of-the-bugmaster), surrounded by a giant army of [Skin](/skin-spider) and [Blood](/blood-spider) [Spiders](/spiders). He's a member of the Spiders faction, and due to his name, the presumed leader.

It is highly recommended that players avoid encountering Bugmaster until they have a group of mid to high level [stat](/statistics) characters and/or the best gear. Alternatively, players with high [Stealth](/stealth) and [Assassination](/assassination) skills player can attempt to sneak into his tower to knock him out, although the knockout will likely have a low chance of success due to his high toughness.

Due to the [map](/maps) which spawns in his chest it's safe to assume that the Bugmaster is currently preparing himself to fight an enemy in the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

## Claiming Bounty

If the Bugmaster is defeated by the player, they can turn in his c.100,000 bounty to the HQ of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), in [Admag](/admag). Carrying the Bugmaster in proximity of Shek Warriors or civilians will prompt them to tell you to bring Him to the [Stone Golem](/stone-golem), the faction leader. The Stone Golem is located in [Admag](/admag).

Turning him in will also give you +100 Reputation with the Shek Kingdom. You will gain +70 Rep by turning him in to the [Stone Golem's](/stone-golem) aid, and the rest for talking to her directly after (if the player wants to acquire **[Seto](/seto)** in their party then talking to the Stone Golem should be slightly delayed to allow Seto to join the party). If directly placed inside of a cage no relations are initially gained. However, you can start the dialogue chain granting the normal relationship modifiers by speaking to Boyan after doing so.

An alliance with the [United Cities](/united-cities)/[The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)/[Traders Guild](/traders-guild) prior to turning in the Bugmaster to [Bayan](/bayan) the [Stone Golem's](/stone-golem) aid will remove the Reputation gain entirely with the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and you will not be able to talk with Esata the Stone Golem at all. This removes the possibility of a unique recruit, her daughter [Seto](/seto). On the plus side, you will also not have a -10 reputation with HN for turning in Bugmaster to SK in this case.

**WARNING: By turning in the Bugmaster to the Shek Kingdom, you will first gain a -10 rep with HN for turning in Bugmaster, and then a -70 rep with The Holy Nation if accepting alliance to join in with their faction after, effectively making them hostile unless your initial reputation with HN is already 51, which is not possible to be the case as it makes HN your ally and will trigger the above case instead.**

The Bugmaster can also be turned in to the [United Cities](/united-cities), however there is no extra benefits besides the reward money and a very small (+2) reputation boost. On the plus side, there aren't any penalties of reputation from HN either.

## [World states](/world-states)

Defeating the Bugmaster prompts Berserkers to attack the player's outpost, in a '[Challenger of the Undefeated](/challenger-of-the-undefeated)' event consisting of at least 2 squads of Berserkers.

The [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) will be so impressed by the [player](/nameless) that they no longer [Raid](/raid) [their base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost).

## Tips/Trivia

-   The Bugmaster stands in one spot and usually will not notice any injured spiders on other floors. If you sneak up to the top floor where only one spider guards, you can slowly eliminate his pets without attracting his attention.
-   You are able to place down campbeds inside of his building. Right behind the cages is a relatively safe spot to place some.
-   If multiple spiders might come up the stairs at once and notice you/your squad can activate HOLD for your units and run behind the cages. This will slow down/help prevent off some of the Skin Spiders from surrounding you/your squad.
-   The Bugmaster will sometimes pickup unconscious intruders and place them into cages. Be sure to have at least a moderate lockpicking level on a character in case you are defeated! (Or have a pair of tools/hacksaw on hand)
