**Empire Bakery** is essentially a [bar](/bar) found in a few [United Cities](/united-cities) locations. This is a different vendor from the Holy Nation equivalent, [Holy Bakery](/holy-bakery).

### Possible Locations

-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

### Residents

-   [Baker](/baker)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (1 to 2)
