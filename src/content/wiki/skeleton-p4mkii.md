The **P4 Unit MkII** is a subrace variant of [Skeleton](/skeleton). A supposedly 'improved' variant of the [P4 Units](/p4-unit), this race is used for the most dangerous and high leveled characters in Kenshi: [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12), [General Jang](/general-jang), the [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture), [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon), and [Ponk](/ponk).

Mark II units are more delicate models with only 150 HP in their body parts, while most other skeleton models are more durable. They also have different skill learning capability when compared to the standard P4 Unit, most notably replacing most of the crafting related skills to combat skills.

There are no [Playable Characters](/playable-characters) available which belong to this race.

## Trivia

-   Cat-Lon may have downgraded from a MkI to a MkII, since he was presumably not a MkII before the Second Empire began producing its own versions of skeleton units.
-   Similar to the Mark I P4 Units, Mark IIs take up high-ranking command roles, while built frailer than the average skeleton. They were clearly destined for command far away from danger. Due to the surviving Mark II members roster, many in the community speculate that the Mark II line was a product of the Second Empire's development of [P4 Mark I](/p4-unit). This is however unconfirmed in-game.
