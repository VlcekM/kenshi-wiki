[Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) was a [Major Town](/major-towns) controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction. It became half destroyed when [Lady Merin](/lady-merin), its former governor, was killed, but [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) of the nearby [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) remained alive. As a consequence, Drifter's Last went into a decline, while still under control of the United Cities.

It uses the [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture)**.**
