**Drifter's Last, Free Traders** is a town under the influence of [Free Traders](/free-traders). It [belonged](/drifters-last) to [United Cities](/united-cities) until [Lady Merin](/lady-merin) and [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) were both killed. The city is well defended with [skilled gate guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard) and a police station. Be aware that Free Traders and United Cities share the same legal system.

Since Free Traders rule the place, the [Free Traders Trade Culture](/free-traders-trade-culture) applies as well as [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture).
