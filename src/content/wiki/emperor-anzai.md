**Emperor Anzai** was the previous leader of the [United Cities](/united-cities), preceding the current ruler [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu). He met his demise in battle during the events of [the Red Rebellion](/the-red-rebellion). His death and the Red Rebellion are believed to have been orchestrated chiefly by [Longen](/longen), the leader of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), with possible conspiring aid by the Nobles.

Little of him is mentioned in the game, the main document of his existence is the unsigned letter titled "[Puppet](/puppet)".
