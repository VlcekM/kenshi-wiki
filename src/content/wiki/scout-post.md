There are two **Scout Posts.** Both are on the border of [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest) and [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) regions. One of them is led by [Mani](/mani) who will trade you anything inside if you talk him into it. Both Posts are occupied by a few [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninja) who are capable of stopping some groups of [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal), but are likely to falter if met with larger forces or their stronger brothers [Cannibals](/cannibal), as well as other bigger threats such as [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits), [Berserkers](/berserkers) and especially a group of [Beak Things](/beak-thing) that wander around this zone from time to time.

Residents

-   [Mani](/mani) (only in 1 of the Scout Posts)
-   [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja) (5 to 8)
