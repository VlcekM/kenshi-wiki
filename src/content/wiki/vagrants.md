**Vagrants** are a faction of poor and/or starving characters, somewhat concentrated in the [United Cities](/united-cities). Some hang out in bars, but many resort to banditry in the northwestern [United Cities](/united-cities)' territory. They are similar to [Drifters](/drifters) in that they are more of a general category of similar types of people than an actual faction. This faction is not shown on the faction list as it is "not real".
