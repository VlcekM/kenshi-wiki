**Abandoned Towns** are [Ancient Locations](/ancient-locations) in the southmost part of [The Hook](/the-hook) and [Shun](/shun). Officially, they are controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities).

These locations have no inhabitants and spawns no loot.
