**Sho-Battai Headgear Shop** is a shop which sells [Headgear](/headgear) in [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai). This independent vendor is not affected by the death of [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata), but will not stick around if [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) is harmed. This vendor will reappear, however, if the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) [overtake](/town-overrides) Sho-Battai.
