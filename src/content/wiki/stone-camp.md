**Stone Camp** is a town in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) which is controlled by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). It lies directly on the path and nearly half-way between [Stoat](/stoat) and [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai). Stone Camp is essentially a slave worker camp where slaves toil in many [Stone Mines](/stone-mine). This location is home to [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga). It can be discovered by reading the [Map of the United Cities (Central Territories)](/map-of-the-united-cities-central-territories).

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Stoat](/stoat), [Heng](/heng), [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), and ostensibly some of the [northern Samurai Army](/samurai-rogue)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Despite featuring buildings labeled "[Slaver Bar](/slaver-bar)", there is no vendor in these 'bars' and therefore [slave recruits](/slave-recruits) do not spawn.

## Town Overrides

If **Slave Master Haga** is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), the **Stone Camp** falls to ruins. All inhabitants are dead or missing in this override.
