The **Small Thieves' Backpack** is a small [backpack](/backpacks) that reduces encumbrance by 60% and does not hinder your stealth or combat abilities. It is a smaller version of the [Thieves Backpack](/thieves-backpack).

Small Thieves Backpacks have an inventory size of **4x4**, making it slightly larger than a [Small Backpack](/small-backpack). When equipped in the BACKPACK slot, this backpack adds a backpack inventory with a size of **8x8**.

It can be purchased from General [Item Trader](/item-trader) at [World's End](/worlds-end) settlement, many backpack stores such as the one in [Heft](/heft) and [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) for c. 3,000, as well as [Shinobi Thief towers](/shinobi-thieves) for c. 1,500.

## Trivia

-   Small Thieves Backpack is the only Backpack which has no penalty for wearing.
-   If carrying just an Advanced First Aid Kit or Skeleton Repair Kit (both at 2 kg) you will have less encumbrance wearing this backpack as it only weighs 1 kg and reduces the weight of items held inside by 60%.
-   If you wish to sneak but are wearing Heavy Armour you can fit your Armour, Pants and Shirt inside of the Small Thieves Backpack.
