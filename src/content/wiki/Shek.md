**Shek** are a [Race](/Races) of bony, horned humanoids. It is the only race which does not have a related subrace. They have a very low population relative to [Humans](/Human) and are noted to be prone to violence and aggression<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are generally only found in professions that require combat and strength.

The Shek have a [culture](/Shek/Lore) that values pride, honor, and physical strength—so much so that many would prefer to die in a hopeless battle than continue living as a disgraced survivor. The Shek are known for their steadfast stoicism and considerable absence of humor when compared to the more jovial races, such as [Humans](/Human). However, they are much more enthusiastic when it comes to battling matters or surviving bleak situations.

Shek's bodies are covered from head to toe in bony plates which serve as an excellent "natural armor", granting them 25 extra HP (but no special in-game damage resistance, unlike what is stated in the race's description). The Shek are also remarkable for their long bony protrusion/horns, which are a source of pride for many Shek. They are renowned for their exceptional strength and incredible toughness. The Shek legend, [Kral](/Way_Of_The_Warrior_The_Book_Of_Kral), is a testament to the immense toughness of the Shek—or is at least indicative of how much the Shek value toughness, strength, and courage.

Sheks are visually significantly larger than most other races, and thus require more food than any other race to survive<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It is important to consider this before recruiting a Shek.

## Distribution

While a large portion of Sheks belong to or originated from the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, they are a small but globally dispersed racial group found across the [entire continent of Kenshi](/World_of_Kenshi). The population proportion they occupy relative to [Humans](/Human) is very small, being usually a low-single-digit-percentage share of the population<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as their aggressive and rather suicidal nature drives them towards depopulation<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Nonetheless, despite their extraordinarily low population, their natural combat prowess<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and a general lack of intelligence for skilled labor<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> or other dexterious pursuits<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> means that they are very often times found as bandits<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, outlaws<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, guards<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and military<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> occupations, usually being overrepresented by a factor of 10 to 50 times compared to their population size. As a result of their aggressive nature, their criminality rates (specifically on violent crimes), are also noted to be exceptionally high<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and in the wilds it is common to see many Sheks that have joined to be part of outlaws than those that didn't<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## History

<table class="wikitable"><tbody><tr><td>“</td><td>The Horned devil was created human, as brother of the Okranite. But the Horned Devil possessed a sinful mind, it rejected Okran's light in it's heart in favor of Narko's temptations.</td><td>”</td></tr><tr><td colspan="3"><p><cite>–The Guiding Light IV</cite></p></td></tr></tbody></table>

<table class="wikitable"><tbody><tr><td>“</td><td>What is that? An Enforcer? The Enforcers were always spiky in character... How humourous they now sport real spikes to match</td><td>”</td></tr><tr><td colspan="3"><p><cite>–Black Desert City Skeletons directed towards a Shek player</cite></p></td></tr></tbody></table>

The origins of the Shek are shrouded, but there are hints as to their origin story. One theory is that the Shek were descendants of a class of augmented Humans who were called the "Enforcers". This theory is supported by the comments made by Black Desert City skeletons whenever they meet a Shek within their walls. This could further explain why the Holy Nation have such a hatred towards the Shek race, seeing as the Enforcers (may have) aided the Skeletons of the Second Empire in cracking down on the progenitor cult of the Holy Nation. Alternatively, it could just simply be the case of Shek's culture of raiding, killing and tributizing their neighbors have caused a lasting grudge.

By the time of the fall of the Second Empire, the Shek were said to have lived in countless warring tribes until a great warrior by the name of Kral united the tribes and founded the Shek Kingdom. According to a book called "Kral's Last Stand", the mighty Shek warlord was ambushed by a great many enemies and was cut down. His warrior philosophy and the story of his demise became a core principle within Shek society, where it is expected for all Shek to die in glorious battle and to not do so would bring great shame upon the individual.

## Trivia

-   Sheks don't have ears, so their hearing sensors must be located somewhere else.
-   They are the slowest swimmers in Kenshi, maxing out at 5.5925mph at level 100 swimming which is slower than the speed that robotics walk under water.
-   Their heal rate is 0.8, however due to the fact that all of their natural body parts have 125 HP, 0.8 x 1.25 = 1 which makes them heal the same amount of HP in a given time when compared to a Greenlander. However, their lower heal rate results in robotic limbs taking 20% longer to heal when compared to a Greenlander. This also impacts the speed at which they recover their blood. Although they have an impressive racial bleed rate of 0.9x, on average they will take longer to recover their blood back to full when compared to even a Greenlander.
-   When Sheks are [enslaved](/Slavery), unlike humans (i.e Greenlanders and Scorchlanders) where they will have their hair shaved off, Sheks will have their horns cut off which results into a stubby horn look.
-   Female Sheks breast cloths appear to be darker compared to that of Humans.
-   As Dexterity experience uses [Stronger Opponent Logic](/Stronger_Opponent_Logic) based off your attack and the opponents defense/martial arts (If unarmed), their bonus to attack xp and reduction to Dexterity xp results in a much lower Dexterity level than other races when training.
-   The [Holy Nation](/Holy_Nation) has a racial distrust for the Shek due to their experiences and religious beliefs and will attack Sheks that are approaching their cities without a human posing as their master.
-   Because of the conflict between [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) as well as [Berserkers](/Berserkers) and [Kral's Chosen](/Kral's_Chosen) against [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation), a lot of captured Sheks can be found serving in [Rebirth](/Rebirth), occupying 73% of all [Rebirth Slaves](/Rebirth_Slaves).
-   Many Sheks, even those outside of the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom), share at least one or more traits that those in the nation often do.
    -   Gluttony: [Inaba](/Lord_Inaba) (appearance), [Ells](/Ells) (appearance), [Shiro](/Lord_Shiro) (excessive food in inventory)
    -   Dumb: [Sanda](/Lady_Sanda) (Personality), [Ells](/Ells) (Personality)
    -   Bloodlust: [Headshot](/Headshot) (dialogue)
    -   War-like: [Horse](/Horse) (war paint)
    -   Heavy Weapon: [Sanda](/Lady_Sanda) ([Long Cleaver](/Long_Cleaver))
-   Sheks could be considered to be the least populated race inside [United Cities](/United_Cities), only more populated than [Skeletons](/Skeletons).
    -   They are never found in [Slave Traders](/Slave_Traders), [Shinobi Thieves](/Shinobi_Thieves), [United Heroes League](/United_Heroes_League), [Rebel Farmers](/Rebel_Farmers), [Rebel Swordsmen](/Rebel_Swordsmen) factions, and neither as [Starving Vagrants](/Starving_Vagrant).
