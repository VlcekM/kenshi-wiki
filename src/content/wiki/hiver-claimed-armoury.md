**Hiver-Claimed Armoury** is a location controlled by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). There are three of these locations in [Kenshi](/kenshi) - two in [Greyshelf](/greyshelf) and one in the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley).

Hiver-Claimed Armoury can contain these characters: [Armoury Droneguard](/armoury-droneguard), [Prince](/prince), [Droneguard](/droneguard), [Drone](/drone).

If [Valamon](/valamon) is killed or kidnapped, [Reaver Camp](/reaver-camp) locations can be taken over by the Southern Hive and turned into a location similar to a Hiver-Claimed Armoury.
