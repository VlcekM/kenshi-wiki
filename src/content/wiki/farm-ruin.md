_This article is about location in the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest). For the location of similar name in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), see [Farm Ruins](/farm-ruins)._

Farm Ruin is a location in the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest). It consists of two destroyed Storm Houses and three destroyed Small Shacks originally belonging to the [United Cities](/united-cities). Presumably this site was constructed either before [Acid Rain](/weather-effects#acid-rain) started to occur in the Burning Forest or by people who were not aware of this hazard at all. But without any direct lore indications, it is unclear what exactly resulted in its abandonment.

There is no loot at this location.

## Trivia

-   Despite being a ruined settlement, this location can spawn the United Cities' [Elite Hunter Assault](/elite-hunter-assault).
