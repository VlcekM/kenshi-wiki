**Holy Citizens** are [Greenlanders](/greenlander) that live in [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) cities, though some of them are squatters in the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws).

Along with the [farmers](/holy-farmer), they make up the free populace of the Holy Nation, ranking above the slave castes of [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) and [Lower Servants](/lower-servant).

They will make surprised remarks when they see "unshackled beasts" such as [Shek](/shek) members of the player's party.

Both Holy Citizen character types have an [NPC Shopping Behavior](/npc-shopping-behavior) of purchasing [Rice Bowl](/rice-bowl) and [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum).
