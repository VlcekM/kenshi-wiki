**Jaegar** is a member of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) and can be found wandering in the city [Spring](/spring) located in the southwest region of [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble).

He has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (5% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
