**Bark** is a Town on the north-eastern coast of [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) region. It resembles a former port, built around a number of natural lagoons. Its economy relies on supplies from both [Port North](/port-north) and [Port South](/port-south). [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda) lives in and governs from a [Noble House](/noble-house).

As with many other northern cities, it can be discovered by travelling there or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

Somewhere close to the center of this town, a 'yard' of camp beds can be found that are most likely slums for the homeless to sleep constructed by the government or [peasants](/empire-peasants) themselves.

Due to the lack of Police Station presence in this town, if the player or any other NPC gets arrested, they will directly get imprisoned in the cages of the [Slave Shop](/slave-shop) and get enslaved there.

Players should beware as it is possible to be pick-pocketed by [Red](/red), a unique resident of Bark.

## Town overrides

Bark can change depending on [world states](/world-states), controlled by which nobles are killed or imprisoned.

In essence, as long as [Sanda](/lady-sanda) is alive, the town can only become malnourished if [Kana](/lady-kana) and [Wada](/slave-master-wada) are dead, and switch to [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) control if she is killed after that.

If [Sanda](/lady-sanda) is dead, then it will become Half-Destroyed if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive, or switch to [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) control if he is also dead, or if [Kana](/lady-kana) and [Wada](/slave-master-wada) are also killed, as mentioned earlier.

## Trivia

-   This town [existed](/bark-alpha) in the [Alpha version](/old-world) of the game.
-   Random NPCs can also be named Bark, including possible recruits.
-   The towns name 'Bark' could be a reference to the wood logs that may occasionally flow by the port.
-   Bark can be considered to be the smallest of all 6 northern UC towns given the overall patrol spawn value of [Sanda](/lady-sanda)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
-   Bark is the only town in northern UC that doesn't have an override to become half-destroyed when [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed.
