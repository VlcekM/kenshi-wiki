**Precision Shooting** is a [stat](/statistics) which is used during ranged combat. Using [Crossbows](/crossbows) and [Turrets](/turrets) can train this skill.

## Values

Affects friendly fire avoidance at 1.5% per one point of Precision Shooting. It's affected by armor that modifies the "crossbows" stat.

Keep in mind, Precision Shooting is reduced during [Duststorms](/weather-effects#duststorms), with head injuries and while firing in the dark.
