The **Heart Protector** is a Light Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot. It can be sold from Armour Shops.

It is commonly seen among bandits and rebels, most notably [Blood Raiders](/blood-raider), [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandit), [Dust Bandit Bowmen](/dust-bandit-bowman), the [Dust King](/dust-king), [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman), [Outlaw Swordsman Captains](/outlaw-swordsman-captain), [Reaver Neophytes](/reaver-neophyte), and [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsman). It can be randomly equipped by [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones-character), [Drifters](/drifter), [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue), [Stone Rats](/stone-rat), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins), and [Territorial Thugs](/territorial-thug).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x4**.

## Appearance

The Heart Protectors is depicted as two pieces of sheet metal riveted and shaped to cover the left shoulder and heart. The armor is held in place with two thick leather belts that run across the chest and over and under the right shoulder.
