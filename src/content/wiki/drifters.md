**Drifters** (AKA "Outlanders") are independent characters that wander and live off the [land](/world-of-kenshi). Rather than being a single organized [faction](/factions), they are more of a general category of various independent individuals and groups who subsist off the land. Despite this, they all appear to have a very high opinion of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), and the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves).

There are groups and individuals of experienced Drifters that can be seen adventuring and seeking treasure in the Forbidden Zone. Some of them are hostile groups who are willing to attack strangers on sight, while the others are neutral groups who usually mind their own business and will defend themselves when attacked by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive), [Old Machines](/old-machines), aggressive [Fauna](/fauna) or the pit gangs. Some of these groups contain criminals who are [wanted](/bounty) by the [United Cities](/united-cities). The [ones](/karate-drifter) traveling individually are experienced [martial artists](/martial-arts) and can rarely be found wandering in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble).

Weaker and inexperienced Drifters can be found traveling alone in the territories of the United Cites and the [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). Unlike the ones in the Forbidden Zone, these Drifters are unarmed and inexperienced due to their civilian lifestyle, making them easy pickings to aggressive Fauna and bandits. The player is able to talk to and possibly hire some Drifters. A few are generally stronger than hirable NPCs in towns but usually cannot be recruited.
