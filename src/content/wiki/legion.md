**Legions** are members of the [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion) faction. They can often be found leading large patrols of [Legion Thralls](/legion-thrall) throughout the [Ashlands](/ashlands). These patrols can have 18 to 35 Thralls.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) and [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

Interestingly, the Legionnaires are not [Soldierbots](/soldierbot), but rather (lower quality) [Log-Head MKIIs](/skeleton-log-head-mkii).
