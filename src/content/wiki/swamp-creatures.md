**Swamp Creatures** is an animal faction. This faction will not appear on the factions list.

## Characters

This faction consists of the [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) and the [Megaraptor](/megaraptor). Their smaller cousins, [River Raptors](/river-raptor), spawn as part of the [Raptors](/raptors) faction instead.

Some [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) may also spawn as part of this faction, particularly the members of the squads of those in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands) and [Burning Forest](/burning-forest). Although not all Blood Spiders spawn as part of the Swamp Creatures (those that spawn at [Lost Libraries](/lost-library) for instance, are part of [Spiders](/spiders) faction).

Despite their name, [Swamp Turtles](/swamp-turtle) spawn as part of the [Wildlife](/wildlife) factions instead.

[Swamp Raptor Nests](/swamp-raptor-nest) are also controlled by this faction.
