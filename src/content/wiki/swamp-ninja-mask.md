The **Swamp Ninja Mask** is a Light Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot.

It is equipped by [Shinobi Guards](/shinobi-guard) (randomly), and, true to the description, it is also used by [Shade](/shade), [Swamp Ninja Chunins](/swamp-ninja-chunin) and [Swamp Ninja Genins](/swamp-ninja-genin).

Despite similar appearance, it is different from and should not be confused with a [Fog Mask](/fog-mask).

This armour has inventory dimensions of 3x3.

## Appearance

It looks like a rusty gas mask with a funky long tube coming out the mouth-area that looks similar to the trunk of a Swamp Turtle.

## Crafting

The Blueprint can be found in [The Great Library](/the-great-library) in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch). Despite being tagged as light armor and leather, the mask needs to be crafted at a [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy).
