**The Bastion** is the base of the [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) faction. This outpost is on the border of [Darkfinger](/darkfinger) and [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) and very close to [Bast](/bast-zone). [Cannibal Hunter Robun](/cannibal-hunter-robun) can be found in the Bughouse.

-   [Bughouse](/bughouse) (Faction HQ)
-   [Watchtower](/watchtower)
-   Watchtower (partially in ruins)
