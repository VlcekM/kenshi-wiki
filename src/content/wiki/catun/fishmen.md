If [Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) is killed or imprisoned, [Catun](/catun) gets overtaken by [Fishmen](/fishmen). Every building is destroyed in this override.

## Buildings

### FCS values (Priority)

-   3x Broken Gates (3)
-   15x Gurgler Town Squad (2) (1 [Alpha Gurgler](/alpha-gurgler), 3 ~ 6 [Gurglers](/gurgler))

## Trivia

-   Despite being heavily hinted that [Fishmen's](/fishmen) population amount are tied with [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) (which seemingly disappears entirely if he is killed). This override can happen regardless of if he is alive or not.
