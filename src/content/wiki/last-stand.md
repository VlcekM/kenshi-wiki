**Last Stand** is a [Shek](/shek-kingdom) Fortress split up into 2 walled areas. The first area has two [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) buildings, a [Bar](/shek-bar), a [Police Station](/police-station) and a couple of ruined buildings. Its location can be revealed by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Shek Kingdom**.

The other walled area has 4 [Police Stations](/police-station), a [Shek Weapon Shop](/shek-weapon-shop), a [Shek Armoury](/armour-clothing-shops), a [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) and a couple of ruined buildings.

It is a place where older [Shek](/shek) go to prepare themselves for an honorable death against the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) (in the neighboring region [Arach](/arach)), this offers some unique dialog options with shek NPC's inside the bar. If the Player returns to the bar after having defeated the Bugmaster the bartender will give the player a celebratory statement and a free grog.

There is also a chance for a player to encounter some [unique recruits](/unique-recruits) inside the bar here such as [Oron](/oron), [Ruka](/ruka), [Rane the Giant](/rane-the-giant) and [Kang](/kang).

Last Stand and [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap) are the only two settlements with multiple walled areas.

Be careful if you are allied with opposing factions as entering the walled in area that contains the armoury will have guards that attack you unprovoked due to your affiliation with factions such as the Holy Nation. Going passive and blocking only will still give you a bounty as if you attacked. If you still intend to buy from the armoury you should prepare a disguise.
