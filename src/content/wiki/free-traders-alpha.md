Free Traders are a neutral [faction](/factions-alpha) that don't own any towns but do have outposts northeast of [Black Scratch](/black-scratch-alpha) and south of [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha). Free Traders' outpost are in a Outpost s-III and are part [Flophouse](/flophouse), medical station and Trader in one.

Free Traders start with no negative relationships with anyone.

Unlike other Flophouses in version 0.50, all the beds at a Free Traders outpost will function properly and characters will be able to sleep in them to recover.
