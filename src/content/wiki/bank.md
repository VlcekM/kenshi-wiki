**Bank** is a [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) location found in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge). This location has a copy of the life-changing lore book [Money is Freedom](/money-is-freedom). Despite storing some valuable items such as [Leviathan Pearls](/leviathan-pearl), this institution does not provide any banking services to the player.

### Residents

-   [Traders Guard](/traders-guard) (5)

This location has an item artifacts value of c.25,000, meaning it will spawn additional items through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system.
