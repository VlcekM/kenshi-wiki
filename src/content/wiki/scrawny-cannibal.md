**Scrawny Cannibals** are the weakest members of the [Cannibal](/cannibals) faction. Scrawny Cannibals can be found roaming the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) and [Darkfinger](/darkfinger) in large groups.

Scrawny Cannibals are visually distinct from the stronger [Cannibal](/cannibal) character because they have green body paint instead of red. Similarly to their stronger counterparts, the genders of Scrawny Cannibals are differentiated by the pattern of their war paint/tribal markings. Males wear darker paint around their legs and have a rib cage-like bone marking on their upper torso. A female's paint is all done in a green pattern, with a pattern on their stomach that looks somewhat suspiciously similar to [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) symbol.

They are also significantly smaller when it comes to body size, almost giving them the appearance of teenagers.

If they are near a Cannibal Camp, Scrawny Cannibals will carry unconscious characters to be imprisoned in the camp. If they are not close enough to a camp, they will leave the unconscious character on the ground.

Despite living in harsh conditions, Scrawny Cannibals are equipped with simple [Basic First Aid Kits](/basic-first-aid-kit), which means that they are aware of the dangers of having open wounds or passing out in their unsafe neighborhood and/or they want to bandage up the victims they captured for them to be alive and keep their meat 'fresh'.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

-   They are known as "Cannibal Skavs" in the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
-   Scrawny Cannibals are quite often used as a substitute for the standard [Cannibals](/cannibal) in regions where Cannibal presence is fairly minimal and outstretched, such as [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Dreg](/dreg).
