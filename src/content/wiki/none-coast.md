The None Coast is a region in the world of [Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) that does not fall into any other zone in the world. Though the None Coast is surrounded by multiple dangerous zones, it is relatively safe.

There is a similar, larger zone north of [Dreg](/dreg) and west of [The Shrieking Forest](/the-shrieking-forest).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in None Coast. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
