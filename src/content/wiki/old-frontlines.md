**Old Frontlines** is a ruin located near the border of the [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) and [Arach](/arach). This location has many damaged buildings surrounded by a Wall IV.

This may have been a former military outpost of the Shek Kingdom, stationed as a garrison and assault point against the southern part of [United Cities](/united-cities)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but their wars against United Cities has caused their kingdom to crumble from sustained losses of life<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Alternatively, this may have been an old outpost used in the Shek conflict with the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) getting it's name from it's location on the border of Arach, although it's comparatively unlikely as [Last Stand](/last-stand) already fits this role. In either case, it was likely abandoned as a result of no longer having the necessary manpower to properly maintain this area due to the amount of wars the Kingdom was fighting.
