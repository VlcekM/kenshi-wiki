**Swamp Raptor Nests** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest), [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone), [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands), [The Swamp](/the-swamp), and [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone).

## Squads

This squad can contain 3 to 12 [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor). They spawn with an age multiplier between 0.7x to 0.9x. These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

## Item Spawns

No items spawn in Swamp Raptor Nests.

## Trivia

Unlike the similar [River Nest](/river-nest), the Swamp Raptor Nest's faction is the [Swamp Creatures](/swamp-creatures) faction.
