**Lord Shiro** is one of the diplomatic leaders of the United Cities. He can be found in the city of [Catun](/catun) with 4 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard). He lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house).

He has Fearful [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

If **Lord Shiro** is killed or imprisoned, then **Catun** is taken over by [Fishmen](/fishmen).

## Trivia

-   Given his high archer skill, it is likely that this noble has a hobby of shooting [Crossbows](/crossbows), a relatively common pastime among the nobility of the Empire.
-   He may be a bit of a gourmet, since he has Dried Meat, Chewsticks and a Meatwrap in his inventory... or he's possibly hungry because he also does Hashish.
-   **Lord Shiro** and [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) are the only nobles in UC to have 4 **Hooded Empire Noble Guards** instead of 5.
