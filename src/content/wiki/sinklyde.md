**Sinklyde** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) imprisoned within [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault).

He has a c.25,000 [bounty](/bounty).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality) and after recruitment will have the "Player THUG" dialogue package. Despite being a unique recruit, Sinklyde does not seem to have any unique dialogue.
