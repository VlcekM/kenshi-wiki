**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Black Scratch was a town located in a small valley near the south of the map. Nearby towns included [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha), Fisher (later renamed to [Fort Simion](/fort-simion-alpha)), and [Heft](/heft-alpha). Black Scratch is a part of [The Holy Empire](/the-holy-empire). The [town](/black-scratch) made it into the final release of the game.
