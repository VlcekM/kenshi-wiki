If either [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) or [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states), a few of the [Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) buildings [become](/town-overrides) ruins. [Bakery](/holy-bakery) is no longer available. The garrison accommodates fewer soldiers. There are no more patrol spawns around the location.

It appears that a portion of the military may have deserted or fallen into disarray around the region after one of the high command governing over this territory was taken out, leaving this area undermanned and in a state of disrepair.
