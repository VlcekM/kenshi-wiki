If both [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states), [Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) is [overrun](/town-overrides) by the [Cannibals](/cannibals).

This zone has 15 ~ 38 [Cannibals](/cannibals), 10 ~ 35 [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal) and 2 [Cannibal Chiefs](/cannibal-chief) as bar squads.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

## Buildings

### (FCS values) Priority

-   2x Cannibal Raid Sho Battai (4) (6 ~ 25 [Cannibals](/cannibals))
-   11x Cannibal Sho Battai Ruin (3) (1 [Cannibal Chief](/cannibal-chief), 4 ~ 6 [Cannibals](/cannibals))
-   7x Cannibal Sho Battai Scrawny (3) (10 ~ 35 [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal))
-   1x Broken Gates (2)
-   2x Power Station (0)
