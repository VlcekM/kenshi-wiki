**Yabuta Outlaws** is a minor [Hostile Faction](/hostile-factions). The Yabuta Outlaws are created after the character [Yabuta of the Sands](/yabuta-of-the-sands) is released from [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault). Despite ostensibly created under the leadership of Yabuta, he is nowhere to be found in this faction.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

-   [Yabuta Chief](/yabuta-chief)
-   [Yabuta Outlaw](/yabuta-outlaw)

### Races

-   Shek (100)
-   Hive Soldier Drone (30)

## Relations

###  Faction Relations

This faction has no special relations with any factions. Their default relation is -100.

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   This faction is hostile to player no matter what.
