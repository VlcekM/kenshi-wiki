**Lost Hiver** is a member of the [Western Hive](/western-hive) faction. They can spawn after [The Queen](/the-queen) has been [killed/kidnapped](/world-states). This character always has a Dumb [Personality](/personality).

These hivers should not be confused with the [Lost Drone](/lost-drone) who can spawn regardless of **The Queen's** status.

They would also sometimes attack the player if seen by them.

This character can be found in a Homeless Spawn squad of 1 to 5 Lost Hivers. They can be found in these zones:

-   [Border Zone](/border-zone)
-   [Dreg](/dreg)
-   [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon)
-   [Purple Sands](/purple-sands)
-   [Sinkuun](/sinkuun)
-   [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)
-   [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)
-   [The Eye](/the-eye)
-   [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert)
-   [The Swamp](/the-swamp)
