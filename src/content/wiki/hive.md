The **Hive**, also known as Hivers, are a [Race](/races) of stick-like humanoids who are meant to collaborate under a collective mind, ruled by a [Hive Queen](/hive-queen) and her princes. Hive characters are separated into many subspecies with their own unique appearance. The majority of the Hivers care for little else other than the Hive's happiness and survival and are willing to give their lives for the Hive as a whole. Hivers hatch from eggs, the only member of society able to reproduce is the queen via parthenogenesis. New queens can be made by having the queen’s eggs fertilized by another hiver.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

The longer a Hiver is separated from their Hive, the more their bond with it will weaken, giving way to an increased sense of will and a lack of purpose, although many of them will instead die ([sometimes in peculiar ways](/deadhive-worker)). Once the bond breaks completely, Hiver characters cannot return and are considered "Hiveless" or "Lost Ones", as they are no longer connected to their Queen's pheromones.

The Hiveless have complete free will and individuality, although they will feel purposeless and depressed, now that they have independence. Many would still wish to find a master to give meaning and guidance to their life. As a result, they are very often times found as serfs<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Those Hiveless who have separated from the Hive and joined in the societies such as United Cities are known to frequently commit crimes, such as the crimes of scamming and theft<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Princes that are still connected to their Hive will become distressed at the presence of a Hiveless in their vicinity, and if possible, alert any nearby Hive Drone if the Lost One refuses to leave.

There are currently two existing hive minds: [Western Hive](/western-hive) and [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). Both of these hives are led by a Hive Queen.

## Distribution

The vast majority of Hivers that other civilization come into contact with is overwhelmingly (>~99%)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> part of the [Western Hive](/western-hive) (as opposed to [Southern Hive](/southern-hive)), due to the fact that they have much more contact with outside world<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and are also a much larger hive civilization<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Of these [Western Hivers](/western-hive), it is estimated that the overwhelming (likely ~95%) of them are located at the heartlands of [Vain](/vain) and [Dreg](/dreg)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, while a few of them have settled up in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).

## [Western Hive](/western-hive)

The Western Hive is the largest and most 'friendly' Hive [faction](/factions). Western Hive can be found in many zones of Kenshi, inhabiting mainly in the regions of [Vain](/vain) and [Dreg](/dreg) and a few of them at Sinkuun<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are also often found in Trade Caravans. This faction is home to hivers who are of these races:

-   [Hive Prince](/hive-prince)
-   [Hive Soldier Drone](/hive-soldier-drone)
-   [Hive Worker Drone](/hive-worker-drone)
-   [Hive Queen](/hive-queen)

## [Fogmen](/fogmen)

Also known as **Deadhive**.

When hivers are separated from their hive, they can become corrupted and eventually turn into Fogmen. Fogmen are insane, cannibalistic, and numerous, but also have extremely low survivability<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are most frequently seen in the region of Fog Islands, with the proximity close to [Vain](/vain) and [Dreg](/dreg) suggesting that they may have been once an ecosystem similar to or part of the [Western Hive](/western-hive), they are located at the western edge of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) border. Players should be careful when Fogmen are nearby.

Although the name would imply that they originated from [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), it is possible for Fogmen to spawn outside of those regions (e.g. [Vain](/vain), [Royal Valley](/royal-valley), [Tower of Fog](/tower-of-fog)), indicating that the "Fog" is not necessarily the condition for them to turn. Many Western Hive and Southern Hivers are heavily hinted to turn into Fogmen if their respective queens are taken out<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, it is likely the case for many of the ronins too.

This faction is home to hivers who are of these races:

-   [Deadhive Prince](/deadhive-prince)
-   [Deadhive Soldier](/deadhive-soldier)
-   [Deadhive Worker](/deadhive-worker)

## Play Style

Hivers can be bandaged using [First Aid Kits](/medical-items) through the [Field Medic](/field-medic) skill like all organic races. They can use [Beds](/bed) and [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) to quickly recover their organic health. Hivers typically have lower limb health than characters of other races, meaning that loss of limbs is highly likely. On top of that, when you lose a limb, between 6 to 30 blood (The amount is random and not influenced by stats) is lost instantly along with a large amount of bleeding. It is important to take care as losing multiple limbs in a short period of time could result in a bloodloss KO leading to death via bloodloss if you do not have a replacement on hand or bandage fast enough. Lost limbs will need to be replaced with [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs), which are healed with [Skeleton Repair Kits](/skeleton-repair-kit) like [Skeleton](/skeleton) characters.

All playable Hive characters gain experience at a higher rate for [Toughness](/toughness). The subraces have experience multipliers for other stats as well, meaning that some combat styles will train faster than others.

Players should ensure their Hiver characters have adequate armour protection, primarily because of their low health bars compared to other races. [Soldier Drones](/hive-soldier-drone) cannot wear any equipment in the "[HEAD](/headgear)" slot, and the other subraces have specific limitations on that slot as well. All Hive subraces are restricted regarding the "[SHIRT](/shirts)" slot since they are unable to wear regular shirts. Hiver shirts are inferior to normal ones as they lack arm protection, and do not have their own variant of [Dark Leather Shirt](/dark-leather-shirt). Hive characters are also unable to wear equipment in the "[BOOTS](/footwear)" slot since they have no feet; this has resulted in [Hive Prisoner Shackles](/hive-prisoner-shackles) existing to be equipped in the "[ARMOR"](/body-armour) slot.

All playable Hivers are capable of consuming [Raw Meat](/raw-meat) and all but [Hive Princes](/hive-prince) can eat [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat). This means that they can easily find food as long as there is [fauna](/fauna) nearby. Hivers are immune to Acid, meaning they do not need to worry about [Acid Ground](/acid-ground), acidic bodies of water, and [Acid Rain](/weather-effects#acid-rain). They are still harmed or hindered by other [Weather Effects](/weather-effects), and players should be aware that there is no way to fully protect [Soldier Drones](/hive-soldier-drone) from Gas or burning.

Hive merchants in Hive Villages dislike Hiveless characters and will refuse to sell them goods. Players should use a character of another race to trade with these merchants. This does not apply for [Hive Caravans](/hive-caravan-boss), which wander many regions, as they might even follow and harass Hiver player characters in order to trade.

Due to religious and other reasons, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) views Hivers as an impure race. Holy Nation military will attack and then arrest hivers that approach their cities alone but can usually be pacified with a [Human](/human) escort. Any captured hivers will generally be sent to [Rebirth](/rebirth) where they will toil away for the rest of their life under their newfound masters (similar to their life in the Hive). It is possible to ally

[Workers](/hive-worker-drone) and [Princes](/hive-prince) gain experience at a higher rate for [Stealth](/stealth) and [Thievery](/thievery) (as well as [Lockpicking](/lockpicking) for Princes). This likely means they have a tendency for committing crimes of thefts as evidenced by the [United Weekly Series](/united-weekly-series), where they tend to be seen as impoverished and criminal elements.

Hivers all have a 60 relative chance to be hit in their stomach, and 140 relative chance for their chest which results in most KOs coming from chest injuries.

## Lore

The sixth volume in [The Guiding Light Series](/the-guiding-light-series) refers to Hivers. This book shows how hivers are viewed by Okranites, who refer to them as "insect fiends."

The first and fifth volume in [United Weekly Series](/united-weekly-series) refers to Hivers. The first one is an empire perspective on an increase of Hiver citizens in the [United Cities](/united-cities) which views them negatively as criminal and impoverished elements. The fifth volume is a story of empire traders visiting [Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village), and getting harassed.

Disdain against Hivers is commonplace in Kenshi, as a result of shunning the Hivers' organization, status, tendencies and race. The justification and policies to deal with them differs from faction to factions.

[The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) considers Hivers to be darkened, beastly and dangerous influences born to a life of misery that will bring down their society. In order to purge their influence and enforce the holy doctrine, only **Hivers** with [Human](/human) escorts are tolerated, those without an escort found in holy lands are systematically imprisoned and sent to [Rebirth](/rebirth) for further judgement. Needless to say, they do not trade with them either<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) detests Hivers for their feeble physical build and lack of martial prowess, and often times harass them for this. For this reason, some haughty Shek warriors will refuse to be recruited by a Hiver, and it is likely as a result of this that no Hivers are found as citizens in their country, as Sheks there in general probably do not sell housing to them. Despite their inhospitality towards Hivers, the [Hiver Trade Caravans](/hiver-trader-caravan) are allowed to come inside their cities to pay [tribute](/tribute-round).

The [United Cities](/united-cities) in general, but especially the [Nobles](/noble-circle), loath Hiver immigrants for being impoverished, low-class, and ostensibly a contributor to increasing crime-rates inside UC. Although they do welcome them on [one condition](/slavery) as Hiver immigrants can be easily exploited to be made as decent slaves and servants, to the point that a [Worker Drone](/hive-worker-drone) is around 4x more prevalent than a [Human](/human) to be an [Empire Peasant](/empire-peasant). However, a xenophobic group who can be found inside UC called the [United Heroes League](/united-heroes-league) are unhappy with them, seeing them as both repulsive and competitors in the workforce, and often target them for harassment and violence. (Please note that the United Heroes League is a bandit faction and not actually affiliated with the United Cities. If attacked the guards will assist you in defeating the League.)

Lastly, [Western Hive](/western-hive) itself hates individualist Hivers, seeing them as traitors to the queen and defective. They absolutely refuse to have any deals with the "Hiveless".

Regardless of which group they belong to, a commonly held belief between the denizens of Kenshi and all (non-Hive) factions above is that the Hivers have a tendency to be dirty, greedy, stealing and scamming. Given that [Hiver Trade Caravans](/hiver-trader-caravan) have an event where they make a ridiculously high mark-up of [Lantern of Radiance](/lantern-of-radiance) (and claiming it's a discount), and the fact that the source for their 'cargo' are suspicious at best, the better experience gain in the stats with regards to [Thievery](/thievery) (which suggests they are good at doing and have a tendency of doing these stuff) alongside the fact that [there is an entire shady gambling group made up solely of Hivers in Swamp](/blackshifters), these beliefs are at least partially true.

Although this is not referenced by gameplay mechanics, western hive people are mostly vegetarian, mainly living on cacti and other plant based foods due to their lesser hunting skills and superior farming/gathering skills. Their high-in-cactus diet, means that they are able to produce a kind of super hemp by using their manure as both a fertilizer and additive during it’s fermentation process. Bug juice (known as ‘nutrients’ to the hivers) is an extremely potent and addictive substance known throughout the world.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

    -   [Thievery](/thievery) is likely a common practice by Hivers given their increased Thievery skills<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the suspiciously-specific denial of goods being stolen by [Hive Traders](/hive-trader-character)<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the large proportion of Hivers inside thief factions such as [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves)<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [Scavengers](/scavengers)<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as well as the mentioning of Hivers spiking crime rates in [United Cities](/united-cities)<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
        -   [Pearl Cup](/pearl-cup), [Pearl Urn](/pearl-urn), [Pearl Vase](/pearl-vase), [Ceramic Bowl](/ceramic-bowl) and [Cups](/cup) that occasionally spawn in [Hive Traders](/hive-trader) could quite easily be seen as stolen items as no shops would sell them.
    -   [Blackshifters](/blackshifters) are mentioned to still have "that greedy goddamn hiver mindset." and engage in gambling and loansharking (often times in a shady and disputable manner)<sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They also have a [Secret Drug Farm](/secret-drug-farm).
    -   [Yamdu](/yamdu) as a Hive Prince only cares about generating profits.
    -   It is hinted that Hivers at least do some level of smuggling given the presence of [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish) inside their [Hive Trader](/hive-trader) vendors.
    -   It could be the likely cause for xenophobia [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Weeklies](/united-weekly-series) who see them as unwelcome and dangerous elements of their society.
-   Deadhive could be a reference to how the Hivers "die" in their racial description. Although unlike the typical definition of dead (i.e. Not alive), Deadhive could be where Hivers become (un)dead due to their zombie-esque nature.
-   Hivers have no anatomical sex and in character creation have their gender setting locked to " - " but are generally referred to as male by members of other races. This has no mechanical effect in practice, as the [only faction](/the-holy-nation) that behaves differently based on characters' gender is also automatically hostile to Hivers<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It appears that many of them have little understanding of what sex is, with examples such as:
    -   [Beep](/beep) has dialogue suggesting that he at least does not consider himself female, but also that he has minimal understanding of what "female" is.
    -   [Yamdu](/yamdu) would be perplexed if a female character objects to being called "Sir".
-   Hivers screams seem to almost always include "G", such as "GAHH!" or "Greee!"
-   **Southern Hive** characters are not properly implemented for play and are frequently treated as being human.
-   Hivers in general, though especially the [Worker Drones](/hive-worker-drone), act exceptionally childish compared to other races.
    -   Upon seeing a foreigner (such as a [Human](/human)) entering their Hive, Worker Drones will follow around said individuals and giggle at them for "being floppy".
-   "No-Hive" is a term given by non-defected Hivers that can be applied to every race, including the defected Hivers themselves, but "Hiveless" refers more commonly to the Hivers that have defected from the Hive.
-   [Beep](/beep) is a Hiver!
