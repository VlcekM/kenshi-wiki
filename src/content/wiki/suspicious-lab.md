**Suspicious Lab** is an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) found in the [Floodlands](/floodlands). What was once apparently a research facility is now overrun by a horde of ravenous [cannibals](/cannibals).

[Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) can sometimes be found here, either laying on the floor or in one of the locked storage chests on the top level of the structure.

Funnily enough, the cannibals have laid out said [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book), as well as pieces of [bread](/bread), [dried meat](/dried-meat), and other misc. goodies in a bread crumb trail leading up to their camp on the second floor. This is likely to serve as a trap to lure players into investigating the free loot, only to trick them with a Cannibal ambush. On the third floor, there are several cages, as well as many containers of loot.

## Trivia/Tips

-   The "bread crumb trail" loot has a respawn time of 1 to 2 days meaning that it is the best source of getting replenishable Ancient Science Books. (Libraries are viable too however they have a MUCH lower Ancient Science Book spawn rate)
