The **United Heroes League** is a faction of self-proclaimed "[city heroes](/city-hero)" that operate around [United Cities](/united-cities) territory[.](/greenlander) Made up of [Greenlanders](/human), they are hostile vigilantes of the United Cities, opposed to foreigners and nonhuman races (although not foreign humans). [Lord Mirage](/lord-mirage) and many city heroes can be found at [Fort Mirage](/fort-mirage) in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

Patrols may follow and harass your non-human characters. If they still follow you and you leave the city with them, they could attack.

They are a guaranteed spawn in [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) at either of the bars or food stores and can easily be lured to guards in town.

They accuse foreign races of committing rape, stealing their jobs, making slaves, sexually harassing and assaulting women, damaging farms, technology, businesses, etc.

They spawn in a squad composed of 1 [Hero Boss](/hero-boss) carrying an [Unknown](/unknown-manufacturer) quality [Nodachi](/nodachi), and a few [City Heroes](/city-hero). The Nodachi as well as the armour they wear can be sold in any town for some easy early game cash without fear of getting into trouble.

[The Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will hire them as expendable troops for their [Base Assault](/base-assault).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
