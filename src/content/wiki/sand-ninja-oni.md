**Sand Ninja Oni** is the leader of the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) and resides at [Cactus Den](/cactus-den). Infiltrating their den makes the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) faction hostile towards you.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

Although their bounty is for c.10,000 in the FCS, it counts for both the Traders Guild and the United Cities, which both use the same legal system. This causes the bounty to apply twice making it c.20,000. They can be turned in for their bounty to [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).
