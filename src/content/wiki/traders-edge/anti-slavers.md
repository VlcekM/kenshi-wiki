If [Longen](/longen) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are both killed/imprisoned while [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive and free, Trader's Edge will fall under the control of [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers).

This override can switch to [Empire Peasants](/traders-edge/empire-peasants) if [Longen](/longen), [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Tinfist](/tinfist) are all killed or imprisoned.

Of the 6 buildings:

-   x1 Destroyed
-   x1 Weapon Shop Ronin
-   x1 Ranger Shop
-   x1 Storage
-   x1 Barrack
-   x1 Prison (similar to [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault))
