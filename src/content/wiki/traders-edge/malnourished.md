If [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) and [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande) are all killed/imprisoned Trader's Edge will become malnourished while still under Traders Guild rule. This override can also occur even if Trader's Edge is already destroyed or vice-versa.

Of the 6 buildings

-   x2 Destroyed
-   x2 Residential
-   x1 Barracks
-   x1 Faction HQ
