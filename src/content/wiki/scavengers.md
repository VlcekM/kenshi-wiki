The **Scavengers** are a bandit faction primarily found in the southeast quarter of the map and are led by [Gutterhead](/gutterhead). They will attack other characters nearby and loot those that are unconscious or dead.

In the Forbidden Zone, they can be seen skirmishing with the pit gangs, [Southern Hive](/southern-hive), [Old Machines](/old-machines), and hostile [Fauna](/fauna) which usually doesn't end well for the Scavengers due to their stats.

-   [Gutterhead](/gutterhead)
-   [Scavenger](/scavenger)
