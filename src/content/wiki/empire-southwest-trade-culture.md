**Empire Southwest Trade Culture** (named in the FCS _empire SW override_) is a [trade culture](/traders) which modifies the value of [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish). No one faction uses this trade culture, as it's only applied at certain locations, which need particular [World States](/world-states) to be active.

This trade culture could be complementary with a another one, like [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture). If it's happens the value of [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish) override the similar value of the other concern trade culture. This is the case for [Catun](/catun) ([Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) ([United Cities](/united-cities)) & **Empire South West Trade Culture**), [Mourn](/mourn) and [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) ([Empire Lawless Trade Culture](/empire-lawless-trade-culture) ([Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters)) & **Empire South West Trade Culture**).

The purpose of this trade culture is only modifying the current value of [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish) locally. That means it could be still illegal to sell it depending of the faction who controlled the location.
