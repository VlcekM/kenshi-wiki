The **Border Zone** is an arid no-mans-land between the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) territories. This zone is included in several of the [game starts](/game-starts), often making it the first area that the new players would see. It occupies the region of the map south of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and east of [Vain](/vain).

It is a relatively forgiving area, most notable for its very large quantity of [Iron](/raw-iron) and [Copper](/copper) veins of high quality near towns with merchants that make it easy for early-game [moneymaking](/money-making-strategies). However, the lack of regular patrols in this region allowed the rise of various bandit factions that inhabit the region will be the downfall of any fledgling outpost. The zone is occupied by groups of wandering [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) and [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) who constantly harass each other, as well as the player. Both the Dust Bandits and the [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas) have their fortified centres of banditry in this area.

The zone is also teeming with [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) (and even has a Tech Hunter settlement, known as [Waystation](/waystation-border-zone), in the southeast). This zone was previously under [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) control, but their settlements are now in ruins.

There are only two major inhabited settlements in the region. The Shek town of Squin acts as the main bastion standing between the Shek Kingdom and bandits of the Border Zone and the many factions of [the Swamp](/the-swamp).

The former major city of the Holy Nation, The Hub, is now in ruins and is partially controlled by various petty outlaw factions that cannot yet hope to rebuild it fully.

Aside from the occasional bandit menace and fairly balanced growth stats for crops, the Border Zone is host to a lot of [Bonedog](/bonedog) packs. Rarely you may encounter a pack of [goats](/goat) or [garru](/garru) wandering through your land, especially in the mountainous south or the canyons. This region is also home to the enigmatic [Urchins](/urchin), which are strewn across the landscape. Notable settlements in the Border Zone are Squin, the Hub, and a Tech Hunter Waystation. With all of the different factions and cities in close proximity to the area, the Border Zone is one of the most diverse regions in the world as far as faction selection and patrols go.

The only other noteworthy attribute of The Border Zone is its abundance of ore in the region. [Iron](/raw-iron) veins of 100% quality can be found almost anywhere, but predominantly in the southern canyons or the western edge of the region near Vain. Waystation has access to a lot of iron veins and 40% [copper](/copper) nodes as well, and satellite wreckage can be salvaged for iron closer to the southern edge of the Border Zone. The same can be said about the Hub and Squin that have iron and copper nodes in near proximity.

## Tips

-   Being the default starting zone for the most game starts, this zone is arguably the most mild in dangers in all of Kenshi for a [neutral, unaligned loner/group of vagabonds](/nameless) to survive and learn the initial hardships of Kenshi for several reasons.
    -   The [Weather Effects](/weather-effects) are not life threatening (unlike [Deadlands](/deadlands), [Black Desert](/the-black-desert) or even [Venge](/venge)).
    -   The roaming [Dust Bandit](/dust-bandit) groups aren't skilled, (besides [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) (and [the](/rebel-farmers) [like](/starving-vagrant)) [all](/sand-ninjas) [other](/bloodraiders) [roaming](/swamp-ninjas) [bandit](/grass-pirates) [groups](/black-desert-ninjas) [are](/berserkers) [at](/krals-chosen) [least](/band-of-bones) [slightly](/scavengers) [more](/red-sabres) [skilled](/crab-raiders) [than](/skin-bandits) [them](/skeleton-bandits)) and there aren't hordes of mobs to overrun your group to [beat you](/fishman-island), [rob you](/skinners-roam), or better yet, [eat](/fog-islands) [you](/cannibal-plains) [up](/darkfinger) [entirely](/royal-valley).
    -   You won't be the subject of [opportunistic](/manhunters) [enslavement](/slave-hunters), and if you do get beaten down and are about to die from it, chances are you will be saved, and slaved by some [passers-by](/slavemonger) to give you a [second chance at life](/slavery).
    -   The [Fauna](/fauna) are non-challenging, both easy to defeat and easy to run away from, and they do not come in large numbers (or hide in the sand for ambush like [some](/skimmer)). Set the foot slightly outside this zone to the western [Vain](/vain) or the southern [Swamp](/the-swamp) and the threat increases significantly, with [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) able to incapacitate any non-skilled combatant with ease and feast upon them when they are unconscious (with the former being so fast in speed that running away is hardly a possibility). And even then, these zones are nothing compared to the sheer amount of Fauna seen in (based on roughly escalating order of threats) [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone), [Gut](/gut), [Bonefields](/bonefields) and [Arach](/arach).
    -   Being a desolated no-mans-land, you won't really find yourself caught in conflict with any of the major factions with their dogma and way of life. Which can range anywhere from [racial, technological and theological monolithic purity](/the-holy-nation) - [decadent and corrupted dystopian hierarchy](/united-cities) to [jingoistic reckless warmongering tribalism](/shek-kingdom) each bringing [their](/prayer-day) [own](/tax-collector) [agendas](/tribute-round) to shove it down your throat. The only instance of interacting with them is when you settle down in the middle of the zone, which causes both HN and SK to come, but they usually beat each other up first due to their [own](/the-holy-crusade) [issues](/king-shagers-reign) with one another before they get to the player.
    -   Outside of being knocked out dead, players will not really lose characters as a result of getting into places they are not supposed to be in without sufficient preparations. [Dust King Tower](/dust-king-tower) for one, will simply throw the player out once they get knocked out, giving them a chance to live another day. Mean whilst, places like [Deadcat](/cannibal-capital), [Nice Ruin](/horrible-ruin) and [Preacher Cult](/preacher-cult) simply kill off the character for good (and generally, most types of [ancient ruins](/ancient-ruins) have dangers that can kill off an ill-prepared group).
    -   There are a reasonable number of settlements to trade here, Squin, Waystation, The Hub and [the bar not far from there](/rebel-base) are all good places to trade provided you aren't enemies with any of them. Scavenging bodies, stealing or mining ores are all good ways to start off the budget.
