**Way Of The Warrior: The Book Of Kral** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). This item is 'unique' to Shek HQ in [Admag](/admag), as well as a few Shek HQ overrides. However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in a couple locations.

Places which might stock this item:

-   [The Great Library](/the-great-library)
-   Shek residential
-   Shek storage

Another book on Kral is called "[Kral's Last Stand](/krals-last-stand)".

## Trivia

-   The name Kral translates to "King" in Turkish and various West Slavic languages. Representing his unification of the Shek tribes, and his status as the first King of the Shek Kingdom.
