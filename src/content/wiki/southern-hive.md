_This article is about the faction. For the location, see [Southern Hive (Location)](/southern-hive-location)._

  

  
The **Southern Hive** is a [hostile faction](/hostile-factions) of [hivers](/hive) located in the regions of the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley) and [Greyshelf](/greyshelf), south of [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) and [Mourn](/mourn). Those who enter their land will be met with swarms of [Droneguard](/droneguard) and, upon reaching the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location) itself, will encounter [Elite Droneguard](/elite-droneguard) and the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south).

Southern Hivers are mostly identical to [Western Hivers](/western-hive), except for the pink coloration of their skin and white line patterns covering their bodies (and slight differentiation in skill and bleeding rate). Compared to their western cousins though, Southern Hivers are far more aggressive. They are obsessed with devouring and/or sacrificing any intruder or Hiveless to their [King](/king). Because of their intense xenophobia towards the peoples of "the Faraway", as they call the lands outside their territory, Hiveless/Ronin Southerners are extremely rare.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Given their hostility towards Western Hive, and that they start to invade immediately to the regions where [Hive Bandits](/hive-bandit) and [Fogmen](/fogmen) appear after the [Western Hive Queen](/the-queen) is killed/imprisoned, it's likely that they are launching an assault unto them once they are sufficiently weakened, possibly to feed their [King](/king).
-   Southern Hive is considerably expansionistic, often times taking over locations and patrolling in places in and around [The Pits](/the-pits) when [Valamon](/valamon), [Elder](/elder) and [Savant](/savant) are killed.
-   Southern Hive specifically avoids targeting [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers), [Wolves](/wolves) and [Wildlife](/wildlife), although they could still come into conflict with them if attacked by them.
-   All Southern Hivers are considered human to other factions. They still act as their western counter parts in clothing but are treated as if they are human.
-   Southern Hive will not attack Skeletons if the player faction is neutral towards them, as Southern Hive does not see Skeletons as viable targets for the [King](/king) to eat.
