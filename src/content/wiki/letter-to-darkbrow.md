**Letter To Darkbrow** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). This item is 'unique' to [Blackshifter Casino Bars](/blackshifter-casino-bar). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow)'s Hideout.

This letter gives information on how the Hounds came to be the gang in charge of the swamp.
