**Lady Merin** is a [United Cities](/united-cities) noble, a member of the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) and the ruler of [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last). She is accompanied by a squad of 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard). She has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

The death or imprisonment of **Lady Merin** will cause **Drifter's Last** to become half-destroyed, or if [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) has also been killed or imprisoned then **Drifter's Last** will be turned over to [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

Her death/imprisonment will also cause [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) patrols to cease spawning and be replaced with [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) patrols in **The Hook**.

If Merin, [Market Master](/slave-market-master) and [Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) are all killed, [Samurai](/samurai) patrols will cease to spawn in [The Hook](/the-hook).

## Trivia

-   **Lady Merin** and [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) have the exact same base stats.
-   **Lady Merin** and [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda) have the exact same clothing outfit.
-   **Lady Merin** is the only noble of UC to have an alcoholic drink of [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum) instead of [Bloodrum](/bloodrum).
