**Southeast Trade Culture** is a [trade culture](/traders) identical to [Empire Lawless Trade Culture](/empire-lawless-trade-culture). It's removes cultural items from The [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) and several food items.

Since there is no illegal goods at locations ruled by this trade culture, [Hashish](/hashish) and [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) are sell withouth particular bonuses.
