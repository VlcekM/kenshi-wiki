Cannibal Infestation are Nests/Camps that start to appear in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) after [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is dead. They are composed of multiple [Scrawny Cannibal](/scrawny-cannibal) squads and have a nest resident population of 30.

They occupy roughly 30% of all nest spawns after this condition is achieved. The other 60% is divided evenly between [Skimmer Nest](/skimmer-nest) and [Raider Camps](/raider-camp).
