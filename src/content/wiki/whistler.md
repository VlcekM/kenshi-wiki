Whistler is a cut content race possibly referred to in the [Legend of the Headless Ancient](/legends-series) book series.

## Trivia

-   The [Whistler's](https://www.moddb.com/members/methelina/images/whistler-v006) model was created by Linda "Methelina" McGill, who also created the Leviathan's model.
-   There is a mod that adds a fan-speculated use of the model via [modding](https://www.nexusmods.com/kenshi/mods/404/?tab=description&topic_id=7718733), although it's unlikely the orginal was ever throught to be a player-race, nor the size it has in the mod potrayal.
