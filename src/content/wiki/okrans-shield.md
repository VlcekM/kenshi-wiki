**Okran's Shield** is a heavily fortified settlement meant to guard the eastern approach to Holy Nation territory. It is bisected by a fortified chokepoint that cuts the town into two parts. It's possible to sneak past the settlement through a narrow mountain pass to the north, or by traveling through the dangerous Iron Valleys to the south.

Holy Nation patrols often pass through the Okran's Shield in both directions. [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) can be found in the Barracks at this location. Killing or kidnapping him results in a [Town Override](/town-overrides) of this location.

In the 'middle' of this military post, a 'bridge' can be seen mounted with several Crossbows, likely built as a defensive position to prevent enemy forces (predominantly [United Cities](/united-cities)) to capture this military hardpoint with ease.

There are at least 3 Holy Army Patrols found as bar squads in this area.

At the right side of this military headquarters, (when looking towards the Eastern direction) a [Holy Hospital](/holy-hospital) can be seen, presumably built to tend to and heal the wounded and/or sick soldiers. Several incinerators can be seen on both sides, likely built for the purpose of incinerating corpses of fallen soldiers from either side.

## Town overrides

**Okran's Shield** can be affected by multiple [World States](/world-states) to produce the following [Town Overrides](/town-overrides). If all override condition can be achieved at the same time, it goes from Empire > Destroyed > Shek Kingdom > Reavers.

-   [Destroyed](/okrans-shield/destroyed)
-   [United Cities](/okrans-shield/united-cities)
-   [Shek Kingdom](/okrans-shield/shek-kingdom)
-   [Reavers](/okrans-shield/reavers)

  

## Trivia

-   Prospecting reveals that this town is built in 'NONE' zone.
-   If the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) takes over Okran's Shield (being allied to the player) and then [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed or imprisoned, the location switches to the [Destroyed](/okrans-shield/destroyed) override since it is not tied [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem)'s [world state](/world-states).
    -   This could be reasonably explained that after [United Cities](/united-cities) is destroyed, the Shek don't need to prevent the Empire's infiltration into the grasslands anymore or have no one else to properly raid, and abandoned this region as a result.
-   The Shek Kingdom override likely forgot to check about the state of either [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), since SK forces shouldn't be able to capture Okran's Shield without first at least penetrating HN's Southern border.
    -   It is possible to do some limited level of penetration in cases of [Seto](/seto) or [Esata's](/esata-the-stone-golem) death, but it would require them to significantly strain their population going to the point of [depopulation](/admag/weakened) or the [brink of extinction](/admag/berserkers).
