1120-gamedata.base**Police Station Towers** are buildings found in most major faction cities. Find them by looking for signs posted near the door, or by clicking on the building and checking the name.

In Police Stations, players are able to turn in [bounties](/bounty) by imprisoning the bounty yourself in one of the [cages](/prisoner-cage), talking to the [Police Chief](/police-chief), [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian), or [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) usually standing behind a table. If you talk and give bounties to the chief, they will take them to the second floor and place them in one of the many cells, with the prisoner's weapon stored in one of the nearby containers. They will be available to buy from the chief with a huge 75% discount (better to take their equipment and sell it separately before you turn them in). However, dead bounties will only net you half the reward.

Dead or alive, most bounties will raise the relation with the [faction](/factions) by 2 points, but be warned, this will only happen if you take them to the chief directly, not imprison the bounty yourself.

If the [cells](/prisoner-cage) are full, you can still turn in bounties to the chief. They'll take them from you if they aren't carrying someone else, but you'll still receive the bounty and increased relations even if their hands are occupied. Typically, if all the cells are full, the chiefs would normally throw the body outside. This can be exploited, allowing a player to repeatedly turn in the body for a bounty.

There are [training dummies](/training-dummy) here that can be used. The residents (unless allied) will however not approve and berate the player for doing so.
