**Metal Purity** was a weapon manufacturer company in the Alpha version of the game.

## Overview

Metal Purity is an average-grade mass manufacturer of [weapons](/weapons). Their quality is average and tend to be cheaper and weaker than other manufacturers. Though their weapons perform better than rusted weapons from [Unknown](/unknown) manufacturers. Metal Purity does not produce [Flesh Cleaver](/flesh-cleaver), [Fragment Axe](/fragment-axe), [Heavy Iron Club](/heavy-iron-club), or [Iron Club](/iron-club) type weapons.
