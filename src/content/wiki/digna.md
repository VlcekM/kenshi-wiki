**Digna** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is a former resident of [Stack](/stack). Her father caught her trying to escape so she killed him and fled to [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village).

She has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

She has the "Player FLOTSAM" dialogue package, which is shared with all recruits from the Flotsam Ninjas. She also has a one-time unique dialogue about nightmares. It triggers when entering certain biomes "away from civilization", such as the neighboring Arm of Okran. If Griffin is in the party, Digna (or Reva) will use the one-time event to criticize him instead.
