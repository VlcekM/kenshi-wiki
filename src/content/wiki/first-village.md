**First Village** is a secluded [Cannibal Village](/cannibal-village) found in the far north of [Darkfinger](/darkfinger). It is the home of [Meat Lord](/meat-lord), his [lieutenants](/cannibal-chief) and many other [Cannibals](/cannibal).

It is comprised of various metal shacks and tents surrounding a central collection of cages. One shack is home to [Meat Lord](/meat-lord) and several [Cannibal Chiefs](/cannibal-chief). They will stand in a static position, while the rest of the village's population will continuously roam around the center.

It is the largest of the [Darkfinger](/darkfinger) villages, with it being a relatively dangerous location due to how many [Cannibals](/cannibal) swarm the village center and the abundance of roaming squads that use it as a base.

However, unless you wish to claim [Meat Lord](/meat-lord)'s bounty, there is not too much reason to visit the village due it having relatively poor loot for the danger that it poses.

## Trivia

-   Its name could imply that it was the original village of the [Cannibals](/cannibals), although this is contradicted by some dialogue.
