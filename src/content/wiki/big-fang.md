**Big Fang** is leader of the [Twinblades](/twinblades) faction within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). Despite being a leader, he does not have a [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). He can be found in [Mud Town](/mud-town), located north of the city [Shark](/shark), or in **Shark** itself if the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) take it over.

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

-   If the player's faction is allied to the [Hounds](/hounds) and Big Fang is kidnapped and brought to the [Hound's](/hounds) [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) in [Shark](/shark), [Big Grim](/big-grim) will deride him by referring to him as "Li'l Fang" and then request that the player take a break from delivering other gang leaders for some time.
