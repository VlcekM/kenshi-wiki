**Zed** is an unused character in Kenshi found in the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set). Per the [Tech Hunter's Notes Series](/tech-hunters-notes-series), it appears that Zed was meant to be one of the more prominent [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) that could be [recruited](/recruit). Zed's appearance is randomized in-game.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
