**Nomad Animal Traders** are members of the [Nomads](/nomads) faction. They can be found in a squad of [Nomads](/nomad) and various animals called the [Beast Trader](/beast-trader). The animals in this squad are up for sale.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
