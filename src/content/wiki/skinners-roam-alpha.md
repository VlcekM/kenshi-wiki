**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Skinner's Roam is a town located in the southern half of the map and is controlled by [The Holy Empire](/the-holy-empire). It sits in a valley on a cliff edge, and nearby towns include [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha) and Fisher (later renamed [Fort Simion](/fort-simion-alpha)).

The name "[Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)" appears in the released game, this time referring to a rocky desert zone, located to the South-West of the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

## Important Buildings in Skinner's Roam

-   A Police Station
-   A Trade Building
-   A Hat Vendor
-   An Item Vendor
-   A Boot Trader
-   A Clothing Trader
-   A Bar
-   An Armour Trader
-   A Weapon Trader
