**Drin** is a semi-ruined barrack settlement belonging to the [United Cities](/united-cities). There is a sizable garrison of UC samurai here, as well as a Bar. It can be discovered by travelling or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

The town was likely destroyed during [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) initial invasion<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> into Bast or the ensuing stalemate, however it since been partially recovered by the [United Cities](/united-cities), now acting as the largest military outpost in [Bast](/bast-zone).

It may have once been one of [Bast's](/bast-zone) trade towns,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> however it has since been repurposed into a military outpost by [United Cities](/united-cities) [Samurai](/samurai), though it still keeps its old bar.

This appears to be a place where the United Cities station and organize much of their patrols throughout [Bast](/bast-zone), as the status of Drin is tied with [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), directly correlating with homeless patrol spawns throughout the region.
