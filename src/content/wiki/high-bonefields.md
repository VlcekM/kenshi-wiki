The **High Bonefields** is a mountain range located north of [Catun](/catun).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or [Ancient Locations](/ancient-locations) in the High Bonefields. This zone does not spawn Camps.

### Nests

[_Nests_](/nest) _are temporary locations which can spawn randomly and typically belong to [Animals](/fauna). This zone has a max number nest of 3._

-   [Nest of Beak Things](/nest-of-beak-things) {100}
-   [Boneyard Wolf Den](/boneyard-wolf-den) {50}

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. This list is in order of likelihood. (1x multiplier)_

-   [Skin Spider](/skin-spider)
    -   Spider Squad Small 1 - 3 (1 ~ 3 [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) aged 60) {7}
    -   Spider Squad (1 ~ 10 [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) aged 90) {7}
-   [Beak Thing](/beak-thing)
    -   Beak Squad Small {10}
    -   Beak Elder {2}
-   [Wolves](/wolves)
    -   Mountain Wolf Pack {10}
    -   Wolf Swarm {10}
-   [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones)
    -   Band of Bones Squad (8)
    -   Band of Bones Squad (broken) (4)
-   [Garru](/garru)
    -   Wild Pack Beast Herd {1}
