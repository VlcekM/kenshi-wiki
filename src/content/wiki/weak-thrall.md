**Weak Thralls** are the weakest members of the faction [Thrall Masters](/thrall-masters). There are ten Weak Thralls in the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse). They can also be found in squads roaming [The Eye](/the-eye), [Venge](/venge) and the [Grey Desert](/grey-desert). These squads can contain 2 to 22 Weak Thralls.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## Trivia

While most of Skeletons possess the sturdy 200 HP body model, Weak Thralls are among the few models that have 120HPs. Since all of the Weak Thralls use the 120 HP model and [very](/skeleton-p4mkii) [few](/skeleton-mkii-screamer) other models that use the 150HP model body, it can be assumed that the weaker, perhaps service-oriented or creative-minded, skeleton models where enthralled en-masse by their stronger 200HP cousins after the fall of the ancient empires.
