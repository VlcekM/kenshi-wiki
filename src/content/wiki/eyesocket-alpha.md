**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Eyesocket is a town located near the center of the map. Nearby towns include [Telbooze](/telbooze), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch-alpha), and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam-alpha). Eyesocket is a part of [The Holy Empire](/the-holy-empire).

[Eyesocket](/eyesocket) made it into the final release, but changed sides and is now a [United Cities](/united-cities) location.

## Important Buildings in Eyesocket

-   A Bar
-   A Trade Building
-   A Footwear Trader
-   An Item Trader
-   A Hat Trader
