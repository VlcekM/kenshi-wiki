A Flat Topper is a [Hacker Class](/hacker) weapon. It has a nice balance between cutting and blunt damage. Hackers are considered a thug's weapon due to the fact that they require brute force rather than any skill, however this does make them easy to use.

Flat Toppers were removed from Kenshi when the map overhaul was released, and their niche was replaced with [Longswords](/longsword).

Although the weapon has been removed from the game world and can no longer be found, the weapon's model still exists in the game files and can be restored through a mod.

## Related skill

The [Hacker](/hacker) skill determines damage with weapons classified as Hackers.
