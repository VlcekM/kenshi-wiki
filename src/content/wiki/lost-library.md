**Lost Libraries** are ancient repositories of knowledge left to mold and decay, not to be confused with [Library Ruins](/library-ruins). Intrepid adventurers can occasionally find [ancient science books](/ancient-science-book) in these forgotten treasure troves. Unless discovered by exploration, they can be found by reading a **Tech Hunters Library [Map](/maps)**.

These locations often contain maps revealing other [Ancient locations](/ancient-locations), such as ancient military bases. The second floor often harbours an empty [Medical Workbench](/medical-workbench) belonging to an [old organisation](/second-empire).

They are sometimes protected by large groups of [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider).

## Locations

Lost Libraries can be found in:

-   [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast)
-   [Shun](/shun) x6
-   [The Hook](/the-hook)
-   [The Swamp](/the-swamp) (labeled "Lost Library Outpost")
-   [Vain](/vain)
-   [Wend](/wend)
-   [Howler Maze](/howler-maze)
-   [Darkfinger](/darkfinger)
-   [Lost Town](/lost-town)
