**Lore Books** are various books and notes found throughout the world of [Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi). They can range from historical research to ancient legends, from military reports to suicide letters. They are there to give the player information about the world and its denizens, and even in some cases tips for how to survive in certain biomes. The Books **category** can be found [here](/category/books).
