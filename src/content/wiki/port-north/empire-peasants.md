If [Kana](/lady-kana), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are all killed while [Simion](/boss-simion) is alive, [Port North](/port-north) gets [overtaken](/town-overrides) by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

After [moving into Sho-Battai](/sho-battai/empire-peasants), Simion's people have partially repaired this destroyed slave camp that was [laid waste](/port-south/destroyed) after [Kana](/lady-kana) was killed.

This place appears to be semi-repaired and converted into a fish port and farm town that ostensibly runs the supply lines through [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) to [Heft](/heft).
