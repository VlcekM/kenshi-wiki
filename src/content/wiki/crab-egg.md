**Crab Egg** is an [item](/items) which can be obtained from [Crab Nests](/crab-nest) in the [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), [Howler Maze](/howler-maze), and [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone). While not worth as much compared to other eggs, it is very easy to steal due to the Crabs low speed, and easier to carry due to its light weight. Presently no crabs can spawn from these eggs.
