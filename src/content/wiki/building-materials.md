While also used for trade, the main function of **Building Materials** is the [construction](/construction) of buildings and objects, such as outposts or interior items.

Building Materials can be purchased from Construction Traders in towns or produced on your own at a [Stone Processor](/stone-processor) from [Raw Stone](/raw-stone). A [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine), a late-game building, can create Building Materials directly without the need to process them.

They can be stored in [Storage: Building Materials](/storage-building-materials).

### Location

Building materials can be bought at these vendors: Adventurer Mechanical Shops, Blackmarket Bars, Empire Construction Shops, [Hive Traders](/hive-trader), Holy Construction Shops, Ronin Construction Shops, and Trade Goods Shops.

They can be looted from these abandoned locations: [Holy Mine Ruin](/holy-mine-ruin), [Tiny Settlement](/tiny-settlement).
