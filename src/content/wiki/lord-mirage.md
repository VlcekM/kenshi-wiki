**Lord Mirage** is the leader of the [United Heroes League](/united-heroes-league). They can be found at [Fort Mirage](/fort-mirage), surrounded by multiple [City Heroes](/city-hero). Given their title, it is unknown if they are a minor UC noble who has started their own militia force, or simply a common thug who has taken up the mantle of "Lord".

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (40% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
