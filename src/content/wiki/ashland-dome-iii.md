**Ashland Dome III** is a large, multi-floored, dome-like structure located in the [Ashlands](/ashlands). The dome has two entrances and one winding staircase in the middle, leading to the higher floors. The location can be marked by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Ashlands** or an **Old Treasure Map**.

it is located northeast of [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii). It houses the [General Jang](/general-jang) and 5 [Hydraulic Knights](/hydraulic-knight).
