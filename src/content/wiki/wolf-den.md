**Wolf Dens** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in [Bast](/bast-zone), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), [Heng](/heng-zone), the [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), and [The Eye](/the-eye). These nests contain a lot of bones of various shapes and wolf droppings. They can contain various traces of (un)consumed civilisation (see below).

  

  

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set). This area has a nest resident population of 10.

### Wolf Pack nocturnal (100)

This squad can contain 1 to 6 Bonedogs. They spawn with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.7x.

### Wolf Pack Den (200)

This squad can contain from 1 to 7 Bonedogs. One or two of these will spawn with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.8x, with the rest will be spawning with age multipliers between 0.1x to 0.3x.
