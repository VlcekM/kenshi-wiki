**Landbats** are aggressive bat-like beasts with light fur and curled snouts. As the name suggests, they are land-based creatures who use their wing-like forelegs not to fly but to walk on the ground. They travel in packs in the Southeast corner of the continent and have medium statistics.

As with most animals, they are more dangerous than their stats might imply, as they are impervious to being staggered. They are hostile to people who cross their path but they primarily patrol far from civilization and are not particularly fast runners, making them relatively easy to avoid.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Landbats can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which Landbats have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.77961008 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.83928 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.89894992 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
