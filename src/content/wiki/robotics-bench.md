The **Robotics Bench** is a [crafting](/crafting) [building](/buildings-list) which makes use of a character's [Robotics](/robotics) stat in order to create robotics materials from [Electrical Components](/electrical-components), [Iron Plates](/iron-plates), and [Steel Bars](/steel-bars). It can be built after researching [Robotics](/robotics-tech).

## Production

The bench can be used to produce the following items:

-   [Hinge](/hinge)
-   [Motor](/motor)
-   [Press](/press)
-   [Robotics Components](/robotics-components)
-   [Skeleton Muscle](/skeleton-muscle)
-   [Skeleton Repair Kit](/skeleton-repair-kit)

## Power

-   Power Consumption: -5
-   Efficiency: 100%
