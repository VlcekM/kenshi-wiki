In Kenshi, you will come across many different **items** by either looting or buying from [traders](/traders). Items serve many purposes in Kenshi and drastically affect the playstyle you choose. They can range from simply being trade goods bought and sold for profit, to [Weapons](/weapons), [Armour](/armour) and [Clothing](/clothing). Some of these item types and usages are listed below.

  

## Equipment

One of the main aspects of Kenshi is to outfit your characters with armour, weapons, and clothing. Your characters have 9 equipment slots, each with a designated purpose that can be filled with one item. These slots are:

-   Weapon 1 - The primary weapon slot, [Weapons](/weapons) Only
-   Weapon 2 - The secondary weapon slot, [Weapons](/weapons) Only
-   Belt - The slot for equipment held on the belt, [Lanterns](/lanterns) Only
-   Head - The slot for head based apparel, [Headgear](/headgear) Only
-   Chest - The slot for chest based apparel, [Shirts](/shirts) Only
-   Armour - The slot for chest based apparel, [Body Armour](/body-armour) Only
-   Legs - The slot for leg based apparel, [Legwear](/legwear) Only
-   Feet - The slot for footwear, [Footwear](/footwear) Only
-   Backpack - The slot for a backpack, [Backpacks](/backpacks) Only
-   Limbs - Slots for all four limbs, [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs) Only
