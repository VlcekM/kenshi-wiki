Scout Leg (right) is a [Robot Limb](/robot-limbs), and a replacement for a lost right leg.

It's noteworthy that out of all Robotic Limbs, the Scout Legs have the lowest penalty for [Swimming](/swimming) skill, along with the largest Athletics bonus.
