The **Thieves' Backpack** is a medium [backpack](/backpacks) that reduces encumbrance by 60% and does not hinder your stealth. It slightly hinders combat skills (Attack and Defense) by -2 points, which is equal to the ordinary small backpack, and less than any of the larger standard issue backpacks, but still worse than the [Small Thieves Backpack](/small-thieves-backpack).

This backpack has a storage space of 10x14 tiles, which is the same as the [Medium Backpack](/medium-backpack). It also shares an inventory size of 5x4 tiles.

The Thieves Backpack can be purchased from the General [Item Trader](/item-trader) at [Black Scratch](/black-scratch) or [World's End](/worlds-end) settlement for c.5,000 as well as any of the [Shinobi Thieves traders](/shinobi-trader) for c.2,500. Additionally, the backpack can be bought at adventure trader stores, though they appear to be sold only occasionally.

It should be noted that the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves)' trader always sells items at a 50% discount, though one is required to be a member of this thieves guild in order to trade with the merchant in the first place.
