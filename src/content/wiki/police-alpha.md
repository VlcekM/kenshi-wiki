**Police** were guards and patrol units of United Cities and [Dead Cat](/dead-cat) towns in the [Old World](/old-world). They operate out of Police Stations if they are in a town. Police will normally patrol in small groups of 2-3, but on occasion a lone Policeman may be encountered.

## Trivia

-   They have been replaced by [Samurai Police](/samurai-police) in United Cities towns, and Mercenary Police in Deadcat towns.
