**Gi Pants** are a [clothing](/clothing) item that is equipped as [legwear](/legwear). It can be bought from a vendor or crafted at a Clothing Bench. They form a part of a white Gi set together with the [Gi](/gi) top.

There's a black version of this item called the [Ninja Pants](/ninja-pants).

Gi Pants are most notably worn by [Crumblejon](/crumblejon).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x5**.

## Appearance

A White pair of loose-fitting pants.

## Crafting

Gi Pants can be crafted on a [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench) after researching [Simple Clothing Types](/simple-clothing-types-tech).
