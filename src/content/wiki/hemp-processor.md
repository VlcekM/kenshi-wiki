The **Hemp Processor** turns [Hemp](/hemp) into [Hashish](/hashish). It can be constructed after researching [Hashish Production](/hashish-production-tech). It has a power consumption of 20 and it requires five bundles of hemp and one barrel of [Water](/water) to produce one unit of hashish. Using the Hemp Processor trains the [Science](/science) stat, and higher levels might result in faster production times.

Many Hemp Processors can be found in the [Secret Drug Farm](/secret-drug-farm).
