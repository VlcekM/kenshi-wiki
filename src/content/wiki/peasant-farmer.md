**Peasant Farmers** are members of the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) faction. They can be found in farm huts and farmer storehouses. Peasant Farmers can also be found as squatters in [United Cities](/united-cities) locations.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

## Statistics

-   Humans
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive
    
-   Skeletons
    

-   Greenlander
