**Free Settlement** is a small deserted town located south of [Brink](/brink). It belongs to the [United Cities](/united-cities) but there are no residents nor roaming guards. There are a couple of ruined buildings and several of them have [Building Materials](/building-materials), [Iron Plates](/iron-plates), [Fuel](/fuel) and some less useful items.

It is unclear what exactly is the cause of this town's devastation, but given the location where it is built, it was probably abandoned due to the hostile fauna or [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) in the region.

**The Free City** is a [Town Override](/town-overrides) which can occur at **Free Settlement** if [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive while [Longen](/longen) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are dead, it is controlled by the [Anti Slavers](/anti-slavers). The Free City will remain under the control of the Anti-slavers even if Tinfist is subsequently imprisoned or killed.

Although this settlement appears to be a "Town" type in the icon, it resembles more of a small [Waystation](/waystation) due to its low population and tech hunter shops.

Since there are no power generators that work here, Hydroponics and Power Stations don't actually work in this location unless the player deliberately installs some.

Since this override condition directly coincides with another override at [Trader's Edge (Anti-Slaver takeover)](/traders-edge/anti-slavers), it could be inferred that this is a jumping point for Anti-Slavers to expand their operations [into](/stormgap-coast) [the](/the-great-desert) [desert](/heng-zone).

A significant portion of buildings are completely empty.
