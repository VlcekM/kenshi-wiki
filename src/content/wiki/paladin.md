**Paladins** are soldiers for [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), typically acting as veteran troops and occasionally as squad leaders. They often go on patrols alongside [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) and are commanded by [High Paladins](/high-paladin). They can also instead act as leaders commanding [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel), or more rarely, serving under an elite formation of [Inquisitors](/inquisitor).

Paladins can be seen manning [Crossbow Turrets](/turrets) on walls and rooftops in Holy Nation towns. However, most of the day-to-day guarding of towns is handled by [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel), who are slightly weaker than Paladins. Sentinels are distinguished by their reddish armor with a white emblem, while Paladins have more grayish armor with a yellow emblem as well as having a Paladin's Cross instead of a Combat Cleaver.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.

## Player Interactions

Paladins may offer friendly conversation to male human characters, where they can be asked about [Okran](/okran) and the Holy Nation. The player can also prompt them to attack by espousing the word of [Narko](/narko).

They are leading both the [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor) and the [Holy Assault](/holy-assault) against a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) as War Leaders.
