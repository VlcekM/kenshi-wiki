**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it existed only in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Trader's Edge is the closest town to [Brink](/brink-alpha), the starting town. The town is currently owned by the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction. Nearby towns include [Brink](/brink-alpha), [Catun](/catun-alpha), and [Telbooze](/telbooze).

[Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) made it into the final release of the game.

## Important Buildings in Trader's Edge

-   A Trade Building
-   A Clothing Trader
-   An Armour Trader
-   A Bar
