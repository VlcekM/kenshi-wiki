**Human Teeth** is a [unique item](/unique-items) which can only be found at the [Throne of the Bugmaster](/throne-of-the-bugmaster) and (very rarely) in [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) [Temples](/temple). They can be eaten by Hive Worker Drones, Hive Soldier Drones and animals.
