**Mercenary Heavies** are members of the [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild). They can be found following [Mercenary Captains](/mercenary-captain) in cities and many parts of the world. They are slightly stronger than their normal counterparts and have heavier armour.  

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

-   Greenlander
