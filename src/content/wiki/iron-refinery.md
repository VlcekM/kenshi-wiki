**Iron refineries** are necessary for the processing of [Raw Iron](/raw-iron) into [Iron Plates](/iron-plates). A non-upgradable, unpowered [manual version](/manual-iron-refinery) can be constructed after researching [Iron Plates.](/iron-plates-tech) All of the **Iron Refinery** buildings require electricity and can be constructed after research in Iron Plates II, Iron Plates III, etc.

**Iron Refinery III,** **Iron Refinery IV** buildings are not fully automated as you will still require at least one worker to keep load them with ore.
