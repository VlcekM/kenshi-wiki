**Oldworld Bow MkI** is a [Crossbow](/crossbow-class) and uses [Bolts \[Regulars\]](/bolts-regulars). A more advanced version of this design is the [Oldworld Bow MkII](/oldworld-bow-mkii).

The Oldworld Bow MkI is a great choice for an early-game bow, as it an upgrade is most respects to the [Ranger](/ranger) (the Ranger has a only marginally faster rate of fire; the only noticeable advantage of the Ranger is its lesser weight), while having a much faster rate of fire than the [Oldworld Bow MkII](/oldworld-bow-mkii) at lower skill levels, and being much more convenient and economical than the [Eagle's Cross](/eagles-cross) due to using [regular bolts](/bolts-regulars) rather than [long bolts](/bolts-longs).

## Crafting

The blueprint for this weapon can be found at the [Scraphouse](/scraphouse).

After unlocking the ability to craft through use of a blueprint, players can craft this crossbow at a [Crossbow Crafting Bench](/crossbow-crafting-bench). A single Oldworld MkI is made up of 6 [Steel Bars](/steel-bars) and 4 [Crossbow Parts](/crossbow-parts).
