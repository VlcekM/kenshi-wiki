**Holy Mine Ruin** is an [Ancient Location](/ancient-locations) controlled by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This location has no inhabitants and spawns low-level loot, mostly useful for [construction](/construction). There are two Holy Mine Ruins, both located on the fringes of [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

[Tiny Settlement](/tiny-settlement) and [Ruined Holy Outpost](/ruined-holy-outpost) are similar sites, located nearby, cold relics, marking a former extension of the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) territory into the [Border Zone](/border-zone) and Skinner's Roam.
