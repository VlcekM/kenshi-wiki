**Outpost s-IV** are large building models that require appropriate [technologies](/outpost-type-tech) to be researched in order to be built. This building type constructs well on awkward or uneven terrain.

The outside ramp prevents characters from walking under them, therefore avoiding an issue present in exterior stairs of buildings like [Storm Houses](/storm-house) and [Stephouses](/stephouse) where characters get stuck underneath when the building protrudes from the ground too much.

The second floor's exterior walkway is technically considered to be an "interior" zone, allowing the placement of interior-type buildings such as [beds](/bed) or even [small research benches](/small-research-bench) in that area. Unlike with furnishing of the [Outpost s-III](/outpost-s-iii), this allows for placement of [mounted turrets](/mounted-crossbow) on that walkway, greatly contributing to defensibility of the building.

Outpost s-IV can be purchased in the following cities and towns:

-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Mourn](/mourn)

##  Outpost s-IV Building Info

-   Building Condition - 60
-   Door Condition - 50
-   Door Lock - lvl 100

## Trivia

-   The **Outpost s-IV** types can technically be placed on top of [Stationhouses](/stationhouse). This could have been an amazing opportunity to create spacious palace-like structures. It is however impossible to regulate the height of the new construction, leaving the outpost uselessly hanging above the Stationhouse structure. Using the FCS might help you to construct one building on top of another correctly.
-   The description of the structure states "The ultimate building. Large, spacious, and tough." The durability of structures is not worth mention due to it having no actual impact on the game. It is likely referring to the Door Condition, of which is the highest of all buildings in the game.
-   This type of building has a visual variant which cannot be built by the player: [Outpost s-II](/outpost-s-ii).
