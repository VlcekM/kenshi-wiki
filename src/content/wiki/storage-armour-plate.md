The **Storage: Armour Plate** is a [storage](/storage) container which exclusively contains [Armour Plating](/armour-plating). Players can construct this building after researching [Storage Boxes: Crafting (Tech)](/storage-boxes-crafting-tech).

This container can hold a total of 25 plates.

Having Storage: Armour Plate is recommended for outposts with [Armour Smiths](/armour-smith).
