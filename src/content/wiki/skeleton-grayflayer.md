**Skeleton Grayflayer** is a member of the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers). They can be found in the [Grayflayer Village](/swamp-village).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
