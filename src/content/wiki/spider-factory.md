The **Spider Factory** is a complex located on the [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle) and contains both valuable loot and powerful hostiles. Presumably built long ago during the war, now this factory stands as the only notable thing left on the [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle).

As can be inferred from its name, the factory houses roughly 15 [Security Spiders](/security-spider) and [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider). They are led by a dangerous skeleton named the [Spider Foreman](/spider-foreman), who uses the [Meitou](/meitou) [Staff](/staff).

If one can sneak by or defeat the spiders and the skeleton in charge, they can possibly find plenty of [Research Artifacts](/research-artifacts), with at least 4 [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) and 2 [Engineering Research](/engineering-research) littered in the locked metal boxes.
