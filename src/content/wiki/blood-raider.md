**Blood Raiders** are the sole members of the [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders). They can be found in Blood Raider Camps in the [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon) or in patrols of 3 to 11 in the areas of the [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) and [The Eye](/the-eye).
