The **Spider Foreman** is a dangerous character of the [Old Machines](/old-machines) faction found in the [Spider Factory](/spider-factory) on the [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle).

This enemy can be easily identified by his uncommon head and silvery [Meitou](/meitou) grade [staff](/staff). Despite wielding only a staff, this enemy can be easily lethal. The large range of the staff allows him to hit many foes in one swing.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Spider Foreman** is killed or imprisoned, the large groups of [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) will cease to spawn in the [Forbidden Isle](/forbidden-isle), while herds of [Goats](/goat) and [Garrus](/garru), as well as lone static [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) that do not move outside of combat will start spawning there.
