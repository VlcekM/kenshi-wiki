**The Forgotten Throne** is content found in the game files which was never added to the game. The devs likely replaced it with [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile).

The Forgotten Throne is controlled by the [Second Empire](/second-empire). It is the home of the [Immortal King](/immortal-king).
