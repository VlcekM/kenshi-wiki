A **Slaver Boss** is the shopkeeper of a Slave Shop. They will purchase slaves which the player or slave hunting NPCs are carrying. They will also sell slaves to the player, including enslaved player characters.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is also separately attached to this character, although it is the same colour as the faction's colour.
