**Slaver Barracks** is a barracks specifically found in slave farms and camps. This variant is part of the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) faction. Despite being called a 'Slaver Bar' in the game, it is not one, functioning more of a Barracks where slavers relax in town. There is a similarly named [Slaver Bar](/slaver-bar) that functions as a real bar.

-   [Port South](/port-south)
-   [Slave Farm](/slave-farm)
-   [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south)
-   [South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp)
-   [Stone Camp](/stone-camp)

Depending on [World States](/world-states), there may be a Slaver Bar found in these locations:

-   [Cult Village](/cult-village)
