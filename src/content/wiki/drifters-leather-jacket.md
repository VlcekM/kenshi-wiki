**Drifter's Leather Jacket** is a Light Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **5x4**.

## Appearance

Drifter's Leather Jacket is a black leather jacket with the right sleeve cut off. The jacket also has a brown leather sash tied around the abdomen that is held in place by a leather belt.
