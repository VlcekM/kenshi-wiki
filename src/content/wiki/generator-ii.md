A Generator produces [power](/power) by burning [Fuel](/fuel). Power output can be increased by 15 by researching [Amplified Generators](/amplified-generators-tech). This can be done twice for a full power output of 130.

With the existence of [Wind Generators](/wind-generator), some of which produce similar or greater power than Fuel-based Generators, one might question the need for them. The answer is simple: Some regions of Kenshi have very low wind speeds or are wind-dead. Because of this the only functional or reliable energy source would be Fuel-based Generators.

Comparably, wind farms will eventually require gargantuan amounts of space to power well-developed mid/late game bases and will need numerous [Battery Banks](/battery-bank) to ensure reliability during drops in wind-speed. On the other hand, all a vast array of Generators requires is a meager infrastructure of [Biofuel Distilleries](/biofuel-tech), which can be drawn from food-crops or Hemp which is more preferable due to the massive production-speed increase.

If space is a concern yet more power required, 4 [Small Generators](/small-generator) can be used in the same space as the Generator II. Fully upgraded, the 4 Small Generators will massively outclass the Generator II, yet will yield HORRENDOUS Fuel-efficiency as a result.

### Details

-   Power Output: 100
-   Generator II will burn 1 fuel in 220 in game minutes at 100% load, or ~6.55 Fuel per day, regardless of anything else.
