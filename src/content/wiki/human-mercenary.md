**Human Mercenaries** are members of the [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild). They can be found within [the Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) looking for work. They are potentially the weakest mercenary variant.

Speaking to their leader allows you to hire them as bodyguards (c.2,000 per day) or outpost protectors (c.1,000 per day).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
