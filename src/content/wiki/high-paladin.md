**High Paladins** are elite fighters that serve as squad leaders in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) army, as well as bodyguards for the [Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

They can often be found leading Holy Nation patrol groups, with [Paladins](/paladin) and [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) under their command. Rarely, they can also be found as an elite squad serving under an [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) patrolling HN heartlands.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.

High Paladins are senior ranking members of the Holy Nation's society, who do not have to show deference to the priestly caste<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They do, however, report to their own commanders, inquisitors, and presumably the 'high priests'.

## Player Interactions

Sometimes a High Paladin will approach male human characters and give them an item, such as a spare [Ration Pack](/ration-pack).

If you have non-human characters (such as [Shek](/shek)) in your squad, and are travelling in Holy Nation lands, you may be stopped by a High Paladin, questioning why you have "beasts" in your party.

If the High Paladin initiates dialog with a Shek/Hiver member of your squad, they will be given a chance to surrender themselves as slaves. If refused, the High Paladin and his men will become hostile. If accepted, the human members of your squad will be free to go, but the Shek/Hiver will become slaves.

Alternatively, if the High Paladin speaks with a human member of your squad first, a dialog option allows the human to claim the Shek/Hiver are already slaves, which will make the High Paladin leave you alone.

It helps to have a human with a copy of [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame) to convince the High Paladin.
