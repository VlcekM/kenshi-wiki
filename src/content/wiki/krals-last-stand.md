**Kral's Last Stand** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). This item is 'unique' to Shek HQ in [Admag](/admag), as well as a few Shek HQ overrides. However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in a couple locations, most of which are inside SK.

Places which might stock this item:

-   [The Great Library](/the-great-library)
-   Shek residential
-   Shek storage

Another book on Kral is called "[Way Of The Warrior: The Book Of Kral](/way-of-the-warrior-the-book-of-kral)".
