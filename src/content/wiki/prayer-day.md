**Prayer Day** is an [Event](/events) which occurs when the player outpost is within 2,000 meters of a [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) location, and not within a no-go zone. Its initiating event will have up to a 50% chance each day of triggering, if you are based 1,500 meters or closer to a Holy Nation owned location. The chance will scale down with distance, reaching 0% chance at 2,000 meters or more.

It consists of a squad of 5 [High Paladins](/high-paladin) and 4 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) led by a [Holy Priest](/holy-priest), the last of which will require an audience to pray with you.

The conditions for fulfilling the prayer day are the following:

-   Have at least 1 male in the squad.
-   Have [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame) in inventory.
-   Interact with the Priest as a Human.

In case of lacking [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame), the priest will provide a copy the very first time for free. This limit is per game, not character.

If the player fails the condition, a [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor) will be later launched aimed at evicting the player from their lands.

-   Interacting as a non-human counts as an insult, it will be launched in 10 ~ 20 hours.
-   If the player attacks the priest, it will be launched in 12 ~ 24 hours.
-   If all characters are female, it will be launched in 12 ~ 36 hours (this specific variant can be pacified by recruiting a male character).
-   If ignored for way too long, or the interaction goes poorly, it will be launched is 36 ~ 48 hours.

As soon as the Prayer Day is done another one should appear on the "Events" page and will launch in 100 to 160 hours. This event will stop happening if any of the following is true:

-   An enemy of the Holy Nation,
-   Allied with either the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) or the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)
-   [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) has been killed or imprisoned.

Arguably, out of all of the major factions, the Prayer Day is the least damaging of "Tax" Events to a player, as the Holy Nation only takes to show a token of loyalty and a little bit of player's time. The [Shek Kingdom](/tribute-round) and the [United Cities](/tax-man-visit) hurt the player in much more substantial ways.

Like most tax Events, if the campaign is defeated by a third-party faction like bandits or wild animals on the way to the player's outpost, it will prevent any future tax visits from the faction until the player's next import. This is due to the long repeat limit timer, and the fact that it doesn't consider being defeated by a third party as the player killing them. This can be exploited by purposefully luring hostiles to the event's forces while they en route to the player's base.

## Prayer

-   Oh Okran, Lord of Light...
-   Let thine flame burneth bright...
-   With this blessed body most pure...
-   Let us not be tempted by the Shadow's lure
-   Let us not yearn for fleshly pleasure...
-   But yearn only for love from the Creator...
-   Let us honour our sacred brothers...
-   Let us abstain from intoxications...
-   For darkness preys on the idle mind...
-   Let us pray for the wellbeing of all mankind.
-   In faith.

## Trivia

-   Due to the fact that the dialogue check doesn't actually account whether the male character inside the squad is a human or not, one could potentially fulfill the condition of prayer day even if all their male squad members are [Sheks](/shek), [Hivers](/hive), or even [Skeletons](/skeletons).
