## [World States](/world-states)

If **Big Darkbrow** is killed or kidnapped alone will not cause any changes. If [Big Grim](/big-grim) is killed or kidnapped while he is okay, the [Blackshifters](/blackshifters) will rule over [Shark](/shark), else if he is also not okay, the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) will win. Furthermore, if Grim, Darkbrow and [Big Gray](/big-gray) are not alive, Spiders will overrun [Rot](/rot).
