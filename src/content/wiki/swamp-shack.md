A small, irregularly shaped shack. They can be found in [Bark](/bark) for c4,000 alongside the [Swamp House](/swamp-house) and [Swamp Store](/swamp-store).

They are smaller than a [Small Shack](/small-shack) and cost the same, so it's better to just opt for the [Small Shack](/small-shack) instead.
