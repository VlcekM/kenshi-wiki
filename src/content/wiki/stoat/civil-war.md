If [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga) and [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) or [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are still alive, a [civil war](/town-overrides) breaks out in [Stoat](/stoat) between the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) (mostly comprised of [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer)) and the United Cities troops.

This override can switch over to [Tech Hunters](/stoat/tech-hunters) and [Yabuta Outlaws](/stoat/yabuta-outlaws).
