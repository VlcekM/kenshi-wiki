If Tengu and Inaba are both dead/imprisoned and if [Yabuta](/yabuta-of-the-sands) wasn't freed from [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) (or died after), Stoat is taken over by [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters).

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

The town appears to be almost completely destroyed and depopulated after the [Empire](/united-cities) completely lost its hold over this town from instability and civil wars, with squatters and displaced peasants littered all over the area, most of them being malnourished. This place has since become a lawless outlaw haven, with a few [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas) and [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) setting up small shops making this place into a waystation settlement similar to [Mourn](/mourn) or [The Hub](/the-hub).

As a direct consequence of having no more forces stationed here to deal with [Cannibals](/cannibals), they begin to start spilling into [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Oddly, the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) pacifier still can be found in the bar as well as [Hobbs](/hobbs).

All the gates are broken and incapable of being repaired. Due to a lack of gate security, it's easy for [Skimmers](/skimmer) and [Cannibals](/cannibals) to quickly run in the center of the town and start butchering everyone, although the large presence of Tech Hunters may prevent this. Notably, [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) and Tech Hunters aren't allies so they won't take care of each other making survival for them more difficult.

You should be aware that Tech Hunters rules imply the town is running by [Empire Lawless Trade Culture](/empire-lawless-trade-culture) and no longer by [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture). Slavery is now a crime which eventually leads to fights against [Manhunters](/manhunters) or [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) if they are still wandering from town to town in this zone.
