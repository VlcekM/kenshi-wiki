If Tengu or Inaba is dead/imprisoned, Stoat is half-destroyed.

This override can flip towards [malnourished + half-destroyed](/stoat/civil-war), [Tech Hunters](/stoat/tech-hunters), or [Yabuta Outlaws](/stoat/yabuta-outlaws).

Of all the 21 buildings:

-   9x Destroyed
-   5x Residential
-   2x Barracks
-   1x Weapon Shop
-   1x Bar
-   1x Bakery
-   1x Slave Shop
-   1x Cages
