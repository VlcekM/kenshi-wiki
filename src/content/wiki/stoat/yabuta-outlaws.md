If Tengu and Inaba are both dead/imprisoned and [Yabuta](/yabuta-of-the-sands) escaped from [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) while still being alive, Yabuta's outlaw will overrun the city with his cronies and set it up as his base of operations.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

It's difficult for Yabuta Outlaws to survive because they have to deal with [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunters), [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) and others factions depending on certain [World States](/world-states) like [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters).

-   6x Trashed Building (4)

-   3x Yabuta Barracks (3) (9 ~ 12 [Yabuta Outlaws](/yabuta-outlaws))
