**Ears** is the person in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) who can help characters 'get noticed'. Ears is a member of the [Hounds](/hounds) and has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). They can be found in either Bar in [Shark](/shark) and will refuse to interact with you if you are allied with the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), preventing you from allying with the [Hounds](/hounds) (but not vice-versa). The same is also true if you are allied with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [United Cities](/united-cities), or [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). If you break the alliance with any of said factions, you will be able to speak to him again.
