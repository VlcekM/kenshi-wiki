**Parchment** is some useless [junk](/junk) found in [Lost Libraries](/lost-library) along with [Damaged Books](/damaged-book) and [Old Maps](/old-map).

## Tips

-   Parchment, while functionally useless, stacks pretty well and sells for good value relative to its size and abundance. When you loot Lost Libraries, it's good to grab all the useless items (i.e. [Old Maps](/old-map), [Damaged Books](/damaged-book)) and utilize a large storage device (such as a [Trader's Backpack](/traders-wooden-backpack)) to carry them to the nearest city to sell for a large amount of [cats](/cats).
