Located in the middle of [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon), just northwest of [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault), this [watchtower](/watchtower) is the home of the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas). It is also here their leader [Sand Ninja Oni](/sand-ninja-oni) resides. Entering the tower will make the Sand Ninja faction hostile towards you.
