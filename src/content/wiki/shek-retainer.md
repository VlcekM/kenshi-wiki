**Shek Retainers** are lower-class members of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). They are generally farmers, craftsmen, and general workers and are ranked below other Sheks of the warrior caste such as [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) and [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian). Since they are not warriors, their horns are cut off.

Shek Retainers manage some of the businesses in Shek towns. Retainers are also servants; hornless [Shek recruits](/shek-recruit) will mention that their master must be paid a fee before they can be freed, suggesting some kind of indentured servitude.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
