**Ashland Dome IV** is a large, multi-floored, dome-like structure located in the [Ashlands](/ashlands). The dome has two entrances and one winding staircase in the middle, leading to the higher floors. It is located south of [Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii). The location can be marked by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Ashlands**.

It houses [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12) and several [Old Empire Police Captains](/old-empire-police-captain).
