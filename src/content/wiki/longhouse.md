The **Longhouse** is one of the medium sized buildings and can be [constructed](/construction) by the player. To be able to build the Longhouse you need first to research the [Medium Building Shells](/medium-building-shells-tech). Besides the interior of the building, the player has access to the roof, which is divided into lower and upper floors.

It is an unassuming structure, being in the same class as the [Bughouse](/bughouse) and [L-House](/l-house) but costing significantly less materials than both and surprisingly nearly matching their total floor space. Though its rival structures boast larger continuous open spaces, the Longhouse provides, unsurprisingly, a longer and by some measures larger yet thinner structure. This makes it an excellent cost-effective utilitarian structure.

To put into perspective: When all is tallied and accounted for, the Longhouse is nearly equal to its class competitors, yet for the cost of 2 of any other medium structures, 3 Longhouses can be built, which will provide far greater total useable interior and roof-access space.

It is a space-hungry structure though, creating small dead-zones around the building that make it difficult to build near it. Because of this, if space is tight, one should beware the spatial requirements of the structure.
