In game this structure is named "Weapon Smith" but for the purposes of this wiki we will refer it to as the "Weapon Smithing Bench" to avoid confusion with the [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith-skill) skill.

Each level of this building allows crafting of higher quality weapons at the expense of more valuable raw materials. As input the first level uses [iron plates](/iron-plates), the second level uses iron plates and [fabric](/fabrics), and the third level uses [steel bars](/steel-bars) and fabric. Each level takes progressively more raw materials to build and requires more [research](/smithing-tech) for its construction.
