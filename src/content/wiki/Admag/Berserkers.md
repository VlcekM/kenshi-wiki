If Esata dies, [Admag](/Admag) is [overtaken](/Town_Overrides) by [Berserkers](/Berserkers). Every building is destroyed in this override.

The capital has completely depopulated themselves as all the warriors have gone off in a [Kamikaze](/Kamikaze) assault upon [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation), with only a few Berserker squatters scattered around the area, presumably being leftover Shek or moved in after [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) completely lost control over this area.
