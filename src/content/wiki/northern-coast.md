The **Northern Coast** is home to [Deadcat](/deadcat). This place is at the northernmost edge of [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) territory<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and because of such some [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) can be found in this area. There are two Deadcat villages in this region, although one has already [been emptied](/ghost-village).

Being a region that's in the middle of [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) and [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), this place has fairly frequent sights of [Cannibals](/cannibals) wandering in from other regions, as well as the [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) that come here to fight them. It is hinted that some Cannibals move further into [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) from this region.

Squads of [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) are also regularly seen, possibly originating from the nearby [World's End](/worlds-end), intending to move in and explore the nearby [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) and [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast).
