[Blister Hill](/blister-hill) becomes inhabited by [Holy Nation Outlaw](/holy-nation-outlaws) squatters if the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Moll](/moll), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are all killed or imprisoned at the time of the override.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

The [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) [pacifier](/pacifiers) can be found here. And potentially [Thief Groups](/shinobi-thieves), [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild), Bar Plastic Surgeon, and [Hobbs](/hobbs).
