**Blister Hill, Flotsam Ninjas** is a large town located towards the north of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) controlled by [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas). It belonged to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) until [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) were kidnapped or killed while [Moll](/moll) stayed alive and free at the time of the override.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

It appears to be a barely reconstructed town after [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) have moved their HQ and [refugees](/flotsam-refugee) in here. The town appears to be in a mostly destroyed state<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, with some [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) setting up shops here making it similar to a [Waystation](/waystation).

The [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) [pacifier](/pacifiers) can be found here.

Be aware that Flotsam Ninjas don't have any trade culture making [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) still purchasable from barmen.

The city is also under a permanent blackout because Flotsam Ninjas took out all of the power supply when they took control of this city. Players must build their own windmills/generators in order to light up Blister Hill once again.
