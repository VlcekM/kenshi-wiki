**Mongrel** is a faction who resides in the town of [Mongrel](/mongrel). They are a group of ninjas who have set up a base on one of the islands that give the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) biome its name. The [Mission Graeme: Status](/mission-graeme-status) and [Maril's Diary Series](/marils-diary-series) books detail the events that led to the founding of their town, though nothing is known about their group prior to them. They have settled in the fog thanks to it providing a natural obstacle for their enemies, in the form of the fog itself and the [Fogmen](/fogmen) who live in it.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached (see image).
