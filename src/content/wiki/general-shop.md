**General Shop** is a vendor found primarily in [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and (Southern) [United Cities](/united-cities) major settlements. It has a much smaller stock than the United Cities exclusive [Empire General Shop](/empire-general-shop).

### Possible Locations

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Bark](/bark)
-   [Catun](/catun)
-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)
-   [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last)
-   [Last Stand](/last-stand)
-   [Mongrel](/mongrel)
-   [Squin](/squin)

Depending on World States, there may be an Independent Bar found in these locations:

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)
-   [Bark](/bark)
-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)
-   [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last)
-   [Squin](/squin)
-   [Stack](/stack)

### Residents

-   [Trade Goods Shopkeeper](/trade-goods-shopkeeper)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (2 to 3)
