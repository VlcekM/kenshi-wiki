**The Great Desert** is a vast, arid, and mostly empty landscape located in the northeastern corner of the continent.

This sector belongs to the [United Cities](/united-cities) who have their capital [Heft](/heft) alongside the towns of [Stoat](/stoat), [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) and [Bark](/bark) dispersed throughout the entire region.

Some main towns belonging to the United Cities are also home to the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves), an asset to any aspiring thief. There are a few camps belonging to the Slavers, which act as work camps and markets of their trade.

The patrols of this region are generally composed of groups of [Skimmers](/skimmers), [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers), [Starving Vagrants](/starving-vagrant), [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas).

Although the sea of dunes is a rather poor choice for most farming and settlement construction, the region is plentiful with metal and stone deposits.

Southeast of [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) is the location of the popular "Rock Bottom" [game start](/game-starts).
