**Bounty Hunters** are adventurers who make their living collecting the [bounties](/bounty) of bandits, criminals, and other wanted persons. They are mostly made up of citizens from the [United Cities](/united-cities) and can often be found in their lands or nearby areas. If bounty hunters see a crime being committed, they may attack the perpetrator.

**Bounty Hunters** can be found in [Watcher's Rim](/watchers-rim), manning [Harpoon Turrets](/harpoon-turret) in a camp. Some can be talked to about the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), whom they are all hoping to fight. Roaming [Bounty Hunter](/bounty-hunter) squads can also be found in [Heng](/heng), [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Non-Player Characters

This faction only has 3 generic NPCs:

-   [Bounty Hunter](/bounty-hunter)

-   Low Bounty Hunter

-   Shek Bounty Hunter

## Relations

### Faction Relations

_The factions this faction has special relations towards. For all others, it will use this faction's default (0)._

-   [United Cities](/united-cities) (+60)

### Player Relations

-   By having a large enough bounty, this faction will take notice of you.
