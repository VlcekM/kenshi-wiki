**Wend** is a zone which spans part of the length of the eponymous river and both of its banks. It separates [Rebirth](/rebirth-zone) in the west from north-eastern Holy Nation territories. It contains [Drowned Ruins](/drowned-ruins) where in one of the partially drowned building the legendary [Armour King](/armour-king) can be found. There are sometimes a few wandering Skeletons that can be found patrolling this area. Some mysterious objects can be seen hanging across the mountains in this area.

The [Lost Library](/lost-library) that is located in Wend is actually impossible to get to from Wend and the player character must backtrack to [Rebirth](/rebirth-zone) to find the path that leads to it. There the player will have to get through a horde of [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) before making their way to the locked library door.

The river **Wend** runs through the entire Holy Nation Territory and is located east of [Rebirth](/rebirth) and south of [World's End](/worlds-end). It is elevated and classified as Arid.

There is a bridge located north of [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) which spans across the river.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in Wend. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
