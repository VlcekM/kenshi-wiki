If [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive and free at the time of this override, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will be [taken over](/town-overrides) by [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen).

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

These mines are turned into what looks akin to a food storage, similar to when it is taken by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

## Trivia

-   This override has the same [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) name as [Holy Mines/Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom).
