_This article is a [stub](/category/article-stubs). You can help the Kenshi Wiki by [expanding it](/holy-mines/destroyed)._

If the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states), the [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will [fall into ruins](/town-overrides).

It can switch to [Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom) or [Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) override if conditions are achieved.

This has the same override assigned as [Holy Farms](/holy-farm/slave-traders).
