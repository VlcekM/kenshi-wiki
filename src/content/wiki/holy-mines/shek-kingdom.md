If [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are [dead or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive and free, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will be [taken over](/town-overrides) by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version. It will retain this version even if Esata is subsequently killed or imprisoned, or if Phoenix and/or Seta is later set to be alive and free.

This location functions more similar to a food storage than a mine, as indicated by the amount of food these buildings possess and the fact that there are no miners. It is likely that this is a food storage for the tributes that Shek Kingdom has collected from the Holy Farms after The Holy Nation was defeated.
