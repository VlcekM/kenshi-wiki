The [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) HQ is the home of [Longen](/longen) and [Yamdu](/yamdu) in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge). It should not be confused with another building in the same location, named [Bank](/bank). The Bank is a vault or storehouse location, while the Traders Guild HQ is a [Faction HQ](/faction-hq).

If the player kills or imprisons Tengu and Longen while Tinfist is alive and free, the HQ will be turned into what looks like [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) 2.0 and named "Prison".

If the player kills or imprisons Tengu, Longen and Tinfist, the Ancient Lab will be put for sale at c.320,000. It becomes the most expensive building in the game, as Trader’s Edge turns to the Empire Peasants override.
