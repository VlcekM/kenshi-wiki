**Starving Vagrants** are members of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They are similar to the [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) territories in that they resort to banditry to get money and food to feed themselves.

They operate around the northern territory of the [United Cities](/united-cities) of [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) & [Heng](/heng-zone), most likely making them the bottom impoverished outcasts from the Empire<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) often times target and enslave these bandits for profit with ease, as the vagrants are weak and easily captured, and are found in sizable quantities throughout the desert.

The Vagrants have slightly better armor and weapons than [Hungry Bandit](/hungry-bandit) gangs and are slightly more dangerous as they can wield sharper weapons. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

## Trivia

-   Starving Vagrants could be estimated to be around ~0.5% of the northern [United Cities](/united-cities) total population by cross-referencing the [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai/empire-peasants) override's [Simion's](/boss-simion) forces required to take over that town (1655 value) and recover 80% of it.
-   They cease to exist after [Longen](/longen) is killed, which coincides directly with the devastation of [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) and much of their supply lines, causing all [Trade Caravans](/caravan-trader-boss) to despawn<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This would suggest that these vagrants wander in the wilds looking for traders to prey upon<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and cease to do so when robbery and scavenging are unfeasible.
    -   The alternative explanation would be vagrants achieved housing in cities like the more organized [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)/[Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This is, however, extremely unlikely as homelessness is still an issue (as seen in [vagrant camps](/vagrant-camp) that still persist). And Longen's death in of itself doesn't cause any towns to change ownership status<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and the towns themselves have no effect on the status of vagrants<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   Most inside [Vagrant Camps](/vagrant-camp) are [Hivers](/hive) who require less quality food for maintenance<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, suggesting the more numerous [Greenlanders](/greenlander) that make up the Starving Vagrants have begun to die off from starvation and other sources of attrition with the lack of means to get food supplies.
