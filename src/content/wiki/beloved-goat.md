One of the [Suspicious Settler](/suspicious-settler)'s pets, along with the [Settler's Pup](/settlers-pup). Can be found in a [Gatherer Village](/gatherer-village) west of the city [Mongrel](/mongrel). Will aid the [Settler](/suspicious-settler) if he is attacked.

Beyond the name, it is virtually identical to a normal [Goat](/goat).
