The **Beak Thing Egg** is an obscure Animal Product [item](/items). Eggs can only be found in the [nests](/nest) of [Beak Things](/beak-thing). They are decently valuable and can be sold to any [trader](/traders). Due to the eggs' weight and space, it is difficult to carry many of them by one character.

A particularly good area to acquire these items is from [Gut](/gut). [Beak Thing Nests](/nest-of-beak-things) are easy to spot and plentiful in this environment, plus one is in close proximity to numerous cities that these can be sold to, such as [Brink](/brink) and [Heng](/heng).

It is not possible to grow pets from the eggs, but during development the Devs have hinted that it may be implemented in [future](/kenshi-2).

## Trivia

Given the description of the egg, one could speculate that the nobles have a taste for this type of egg.
