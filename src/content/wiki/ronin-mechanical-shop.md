**Ronin Mechanical Shop** is a vendor found in some territories taken over by the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) and the [Free Traders](/free-traders). It replaces the [Empire Mechanical Shop](/empire-mechanical-shop), with the only difference being the lack of UC Books.

### Possible Locations

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady) (Empire Peasants)
-   [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) (Free Traders)

### Residents

-   [Ronin Construction Trader](/ronin-construction-trader)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard)
