The **Red Sabre** is a member of the [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) faction. They can typically be found wandering [the Swamp](/the-swamp) or in their base, the [Red Sabre Hideout](/red-sabre-hideout).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached to their faction. The colour is also separately attached to this character, although it is the same colour as the faction's colour.

-   Greenlander
