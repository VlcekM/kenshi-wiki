If [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) along with either of [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben), [Longen](/longen) or [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) are [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Heft](/heft) will become malnourished.

The town is plagued by rampant famine and poverty after all the slave camps of [Port South](/port-south) and the strained supply lines of [Heng](/heng-zone) as a result of [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge)/[South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp)/[Slave Farm](/slave-farm) supplying this town have been destroyed and supply lines cut off, as a result the ensuing chaos has destroyed and killed a part of the town and its inhabitants.

Technically, there are three different **Malnourished** [overrides](/town-overrides) for Heft — depending on which specific character is killed or imprisoned along with Wada. In practice, though, every single one of these overrides produce the same result.
