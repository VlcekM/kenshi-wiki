If [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed or imprisoned, [Heft](/heft) will be [taken over](/town-overrides) by the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

The town appears to be in the aftermath of the revolution of [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen), which has devastated and killed large parts of town and population in the midst of the chaos.

Most people are malnourished, and most buildings are destroyed in this override, while some others are trashed, such as the Capital HQ.

## Buildings

-   17x Destroyed
-   3x Residency (ruined)
-   2x Residency

-   1x Barrack ([Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen))
