The **Fogmen** (also known as the **Deadhive**) are a savage group of [Hivers](/hive) lurking within the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). Anyone who enters the Fog Islands is doomed to be attacked by the Fogmen, who are a constant threat to the people of the nearby town of [Mongrel](/mongrel).

## Overview

Upon attacking and incapacitating any non-Skeleton characters, nearby Fogmen will attempt to carry the injured person(s) back to their camps, where they will be tied to a pole and eaten alive by [Fog Princes](/fog-prince). If no Princes are nearby, Fog Heavies will engage in a ritualistic feast. It should be noted that even if all nearby Fogmen are killed, the injured person is still in immense danger as new Fogmen can always spawn in to take their place. They will often not attempt to fight after kidnapping their victim, merely running in to get the body and retreating back to camp. While it is possible to rescue captives, as their limbs will be eaten first, heavy caution is advised.

Their main weakness is that Fogmen are only barely able warriors and are individually very fragile. To make up for this weakness, they roam in large groups of [Fogmen](/fogman) and [Fog Heavies](/fog-heavy). When fighting a group of Fogmen it's not uncommon that others may take notice and join in as well, making it extremely easy to find yourself surrounded. With their vast manpower, they can eventually take down even a skilled warrior.

They are known to supposedly be controlled by a neurological parasite, but the details are unknown, and the reliability of these rumors is questionable. It appears that it is impossible for them to regain control of themselves by any means. Whatever causes their behavior, they are completely feral beyond their ritual cannibalization of humanoids. They have no voice lines besides incoherent screaming.

If both Hiver [Queens](/hive-queen) are dead, Fogmen will spread to all of the former Hiver locations, supporting a theory that Hivers cannot function without pheromones of their queens, even if separated from them.

### Deathyards

The Fog Islands are littered with so-called Deathyards, which are large camps of Fogmen that spawn groups of Fogmen. Within the center of these Fogmen nest are prison poles from which people captured by the Fogmen are eaten alive. Typically, Deathyards are spawned within one of the many large craters of the fog islands. Fogmen regularly patrol the roads which crisscross the Fog Islands, and it is possible to easily be overwhelmed by a horde of Fogmen due to the presence of several Deathyards located next to each other.

Deathyards, like animal nest and bandit camps, can be destroyed. It would require killing most of the Fogmen which were spawned by that Deathyard. However, also like animal nests, they are not permanently destroyed and will respawn after some time. This can be notoriously difficult if multiple Deathyards are close to one another, as you would have to also deal with the patrols of the nearby Deathyards before you can kill your current target.

## Trivia

-   Fogmen are the only Hive group with no Acid immunity. They are also unable to eat [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat), [Human Teeth](/human-teeth) or [Raw Meat](/raw-meat).
-   Fogmen are heavily implied to be turned Hivers, as the death of [Hive Queen](/hive-queen)/[South Hive Queen](/queen-of-the-south) increases their presence.
    -   It is also heavily implied that the Hivers that are separated from the Hive also turn as opposed to only the queen dying in their own description, where Hivers "die" and turn into Deadhive.
-   Fogmen appear to be a fairly commonly known anomaly in Kenshi, with mentions in the Swamp about them, priced Fog Prince Heads, and [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) directly bordering and [regularly defends against their infiltrations](/holy-military-base) at [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), but is never stated as something out of the ordinary.
