The **Cap** is Clothing that is equipped in the Headgear slot.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

The Cap is a basic brown baseball hat but made with several leather pieces crudely sewn together. There are no insignia, logos or any writing on the Cap.
