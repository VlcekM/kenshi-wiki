**Lady Sanda** is one of the diplomatic leaders of the [United Cities](/united-cities). She can be found in the city of [Bark](/bark) with between 5 to 8 [Empire noble guard---](/sandas-noble-guard) and 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/empire-noble-guard-hooded). She lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house).

She has a Dumb [Personality](/personality).

She appears to be the governor of **Bark**.

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Lady Sanda** is killed or imprisoned, then several possible world state changes can be triggered for Bark depending on the status of other characters.

If Lady Sanda is killed or imprisoned, then Bark will become half-destroyed. Alternatively, if Lady Sanda and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are both killed or imprisoned then [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) will take control of Bark. [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) will also take over Bark if Lady Sanda, [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) and [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) are all killed or imprisoned.

Samurai patrols will decrease their frequency in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) (50%).

If Lady Sanda is killed/imprisoned alongside with [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Lord Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) and [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) will cause patrols of 4 to 13 [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) to start spawning in the following regions: [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

## Trivia

-   Out of all nobles of UC, **Lady Sanda** is the only one who has a unique variant bodyguard (which is seemingly a cut content/bug).
-   Sanda, [Nagata](/lord-nagata) and [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) all use the same army squads.
-   **Lady Sanda** and [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) both use Long Cleaver as their choice of weapon.
-   **Lady Sanda** and [Lady Merin](/lady-merin) have the exact same clothing outfit.
