An unnamed member of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They are a variant of Bar Thugs which can be found in bars in [The Hub](/the-hub), [Waystation](/waystation), or in [Shark](/shark).

This thug is a bodyguard for hire. He charges c.2,000 per day for personal protection and c.1,000 per day for outpost guarding.

They have a 4% chance of having a bounty from c.500 to c.2,500. They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]).
