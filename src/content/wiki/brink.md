**Brink** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities). Its economy relies solely on the supplies from [Eyesocket](/eyesocket). Brink is locally governed by [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi). You can find it by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**. It is not uncommon to see [Reavers](/reavers) patrols near this town.

## Trivia

-   [Brink](/brink-alpha) was a town in the [alpha](/old-world) version of the game where the player would start out.
-   Brink can be considered to be the 5th largest town of all 6 northern UC towns given the overall patrol spawn value of [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
-   The town's name 'Brink' could be a reference that this town is "on the brink" of UC territory.
-   Alternatively, the name 'Brink' could come from the town being located right on the edge of the death lasers from the neighboring Venge.
