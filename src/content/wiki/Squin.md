**Squin** is a [Town](/Major_Towns) in the [Border Zone](/Border_Zone) housed in a narrow canyon. It is controlled by the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) and is located directly east of their capital, [Admag](/Admag). This town is much less populated than their capital. Its location can be revealed by exploring or by reading the **[Map](/Maps) of the Shek Kingdom**.

There are [Iron Resources](/Raw_Iron) and [Copper Resources](/Copper) that can be mined nearby. Also, [Dust Bandits](/Dust_Bandits) and [Hungry Bandits](/Hungry_Bandit) roam the region and will harass unsuspecting wanderers.

The entire town is powered entirely by [wind energy](/Power). With a lack of batteries in the city, power outages are to be expected fairly frequently as the wind speed naturally oscillates.

There is also a chance for a player to encounter some [Unique Recruits](/Unique_Recruits) inside the bar here, such as [Horse](/Horse), [Oron](/Oron), [Rane the Giant](/Rane_the_Giant), [Ruka](/Ruka) and [Kang](/Kang).

The [Flotsam Ninjas](/Flotsam_Ninjas) pacifier can be found here.
