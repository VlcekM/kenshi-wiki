**Squin** is a [Town](/major-towns) in the [Border Zone](/border-zone) housed in a narrow canyon. It is controlled by the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and is located directly east of their capital, [Admag](/admag). This town is much less populated than their capital<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Its location can be revealed by exploring or by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Shek Kingdom**.

There are [Iron Resources](/raw-iron) and [Copper Resources](/copper) that can be mined nearby. Also, [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) and [Hungry Bandits](/hungry-bandit) roam the region and will harass unsuspecting wanderers.

The entire town is powered entirely by [wind energy](/power). With a lack of batteries in the city, power outages are to be expected fairly frequently as the wind speed naturally oscillates.

There is also a chance for a player to encounter some [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) inside the bar here, such as [Horse](/horse), [Oron](/oron), [Rane the Giant](/rane-the-giant), [Ruka](/ruka) and [Kang](/kang).

The [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) pacifier can be found here.
