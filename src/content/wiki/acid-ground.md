**Acid Ground** is a trait of some [Zones](/category/zones) where there has been heavy pollution. This pollution has led to the ground becoming acidic. This acidity can harm the feet of characters, causing [Blunt Damage](/damage) to Legs. This trait has no effect on [Hive](/hive) or [Skeleton](/skeleton) characters, as they are immune to all forms of Acid. This also has no effect on [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs).

[Human](/human) and [Shek](/shek) characters can easily be protected from this by wearing any type of [Footwear](/footwear).
