**Goat Man** is located in [Settled Nomad Village](/settled-nomad-village) alongside his goat, [Cornelius](/cornelius). He is found in the Hideout building.

He has an Honorable [Personality](/personality) and will offer to sell Cornelius to the player if he deems you worthy. His price for Cornelius is half of what the nearby Nomad Animal Trader is offering.
