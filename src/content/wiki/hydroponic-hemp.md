**Hydroponic Hemp** is a farming building which grows [Hemp](/hemp). It can be constructed after researching [Hydroponic Hemp](/hydroponics-tech). This building uses and levels characters' [Farming](/farming) stat. It requires inputs of [Power](/power) (10 used) and [Water](/water) to grow.

Despite being an "indoor only" placement, [Hydroponics](/hydroponics) can be placed on the roofs of buildings.

Hydroponic Hemp farms can be found inside the buildings in [Secret Drug Farm](/secret-drug-farm).
