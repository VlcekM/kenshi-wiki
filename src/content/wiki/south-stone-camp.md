**South** **Stone Camp** is a town in [Heng](/heng-zone) controlled by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). This location heavily relies on the use of [slavery](/slavery). Slaves here work on the many [Stone Mines](/stone-mine). [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) lives in this location in a [Noble House](/noble-house).

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Heng](/heng), [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), and [Heft](/heft), and ostensibly some of the [southern Samurai Army](/samurai-rogue).

There is also another stone camp, called simply the [Stone Camp](/stone-camp). It is controlled by [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga) and is located in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

## World States

If **Slave Master Ruben** is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), **South Stone Camp** will fall to ruins. All inhabitants are dead or missing in this override.

## Trivia

-   This labor camp can be considered to be the 3rd most important/influential of all 6 camps in northern UC, as its destruction is necessary for [southern rogues](/samurai-rogue) to spawn, tied with decreased Slave Traders and Manhunters as well as increased Anti-Slavers presence in Heng, and potentially causes a malnourishment of Heft.
