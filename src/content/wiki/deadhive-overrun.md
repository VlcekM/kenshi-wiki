**Deadhive Overrun** is a ruin of a town which has been overtaken by [Fogmen](/fogmen). This location is at the edge of [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) zone, to the north near the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). Aside from potentially a [Fog Prince Head](/fog-prince-head), there is no major loot here.

Deadhive Overrun likely used to be a town controlled by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). It is unclear if this area was attacked and occupied by Fogmen, or was abandoned for other reasons, although the evidence available would suggest latter to be the case<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Considering the distance from other areas, this place may have been a minor frontier outpost that was abandoned for serving little purpose.

Hive villages can be turned into something similar to this if the faction's Hive Queen is harmed.
