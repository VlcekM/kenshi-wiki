**Free Traders Trade Culture** (named in the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) _empire but more worldy_) is a [trade culture](/traders) very similar to [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) but more food items can spawn like [Greenfruit](/greenfruit), [Gohan](/gohan) and [Riceweed](/riceweed).

**Factions:**

-   [Free Traders](/free-traders)

**Locations:**

-   All locations controlled by [Free Traders](/free-traders), potentially [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) and [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) depending on [World States](/world-states).
