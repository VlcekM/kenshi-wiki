_This article is about the Old Village under the control of Empire Peasants. For the one under Deadcat control, see [Old Village (Deadcat)](/old-village-deadcat)._

**Old Village** is a ruined town owned by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) in the eastern side of [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert). This location consists of a couple ravaged Old [Storm Houses](/storm-house), one ruin of a Storm House, three [Small Shack](/small-shack) ruins, two working [wells](/well), a [water tank](/water-tank) and six [cactus farms](/cactus-farm), all beautifully placed between two wave-like dunes.

Inside of the preserved buildings some low level loot can be found ([Fuel](/fuel), [Armour Plating](/armour-plating), [Iron Plates](/iron-plates), [Steel Bars](/steel-bars), [Building Materials](/building-materials), [Electronics](/electrical-components), [Fabrics](/fabrics), [Water Jugs](/water-jug), [Cups](/cup), [Wooden Bowls](/wooden-bowl), [Gears](/gears), [Lantern](/lantern), [Advanced Splint Kits](/advanced-splint-kit), [Authentic Skeleton Repair Kits](/authentic-skeleton-repair-kit), and [Sleeping Bags](/sleeping-bag)). Each house has at least one usable sitting box among the general furniture remains. The Old Village can be a good hiding location for a group hoping to wait out [United Cities](/united-cities) patrols or [Manhunter](/manhunters) squads, especially considering travel and medical loot that could be found here.
