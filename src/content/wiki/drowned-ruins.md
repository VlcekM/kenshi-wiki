The **Drowned Ruins** are housed in the crumbling remains of a long-forgotten settlement, the lost [Second Empire](/second-empire) town of Catch,<sup id="cite_ref-Armour_King_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Armour_King-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> located midway up the river that separates [Rebirth](/rebirth) from the [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest).

Though half sunken into the water, the ruins of Catch are not altogether abandoned. A sole skeleton merchant who refers to himself as the [Armor King](/armor-king) makes his home in the Drowned Ruins along with his [royal guard](/security-spider) and three of his own personal [Thralls](/armour-kings-thrall).

Details on the Armour King's merchandise can be found on [his shop page](/armour-kings-shop). Unlike all other Armour Shops, beds can be rented upstairs for free. The existence of regular beds instead of Skeleton Repair Beds suggests that non-Skeletons have come to this shop before.

## Tips

-   The only way to reach this location is by swimming up or down the river, entering either from the south side near [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) or the northwestern side near the [Floodlands](/floodlands).
-   If you don't have a moderate level of swimming, it is recommended to send a [Skeleton](/skeleton) to this shop as they do not need to swim.
-   It's not recommended to steal from this shop because all of the NPCs have combat stats past 90 (and you should respect the Armour King).

## Trivia

-   According to Armour King, his shop has been running for 937 years<sup id="cite_ref-Armour_King_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Armour_King-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Meaning that this ruin is likely to be older than that period.
