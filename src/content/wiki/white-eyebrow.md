**White Eyebrow** is the leader of the [White Eyebrow Clan](/white-eyebrow-clan). He is not set to spawn in game, but players might encounter his [Wanted Poster](/wanted-posters). The following information is from [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

He has a Brave [Personality](/personality). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
