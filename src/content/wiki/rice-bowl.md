**Rice Bowl** is a [food](/food) item which can be purchased from relevant traders or created in a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove). This food is easiest to find in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), where [Riceweed](/riceweed) is abundant.

Riceweed is the single most water-hungry crop in the game, with Hydroponics making it only slightly less bad. This doesn't matter so long as the crops are grown outdoors in rainy environments, but a whole barrel of [Water](/water) for a meager 25Nu makes Rice Bowls an absolute water-guzzling monster of food.

It is recommended to not eat Rice outright but to instead farm [Greenfruit](/greenfruit) as well to make [Gohan](/gohan) instead, which if grown in rainy environments, uses no Water at all.
