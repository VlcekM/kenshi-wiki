Different weapons deal [damage](/damage) in different ways.

#### [Blunt Weapons](/blunt-weapons)

> [Blunt Weapons](/blunt-weapons) primarily deal stun damage, which does not require a first aid kit to heal.

#### [Hackers](/hackers)

> [Hackers](/hackers) are weapons which balance cutting and stun damage. Hackers deal additional damage against [Robots](/robots), and have impressive armour penetration. Their drawback is that they are slightly less effective against [Animals](/category/animals).

#### [Heavy Weapons](/heavy-weapons)

> [Heavy Weapons](/heavy-weapons) are weapons which require large amounts of strength to properly wield, but reward the user with the ability to deal large amounts of damage in a wide arc.

#### [Katanas](/katanas)

> [Katanas](/katanas) are weapons which specialize cutting damage. They have a unique double attack animation which can give them an edge in 1v1 fights. They usually have bonus damage against humans at the cost of being weak against armour and robots.

#### [Polearms](/polearms)

> [Polearms](/polearms) are long weapons which are very light. They usually deal additional damage against animals. Their drawback is their penalties when used indoors.

#### [Sabres](/sabres)

> [Sabres](/sabres) are weapons which deal both types of damage, however they are more centered around cutting damage.
