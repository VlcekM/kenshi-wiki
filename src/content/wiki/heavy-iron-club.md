**Heavy Clubs** were weapons which could have been created by the player's faction. Blueprints were not required, they are craftable as soon as a [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith) is created, just like normal [Iron Clubs](/iron-club).

Heavy Iron Clubs were considered part of the [Blunt](/blunt) class, and were the heaviest weapons available, with the heaviest weighing in at a staggering 64 kilograms. They inflict massive blunt damage, but cause little bleeding and, unless their wielder is extremely strong, will be swung very slowly.

## Related Skill

The [Blunt](/blunt) skill determines the amount of damage a Heavy Club will inflict.
