**Wind Generator** is an energy production building that generates [Power](/power) based on the current wind speed, which can be seen in the [Prospecting](/prospecting) information screen or by clicking on the wind generator. Wind power is tied to weather and is not a consistent resource.

A **Wind Generator II** can produce +1 power for every 1% of wind in the area, meaning it can generate up to a maximum of 100 power.

If upgrading a Wind Generator instead of building a Wind Generator II from scratch, the building cost is only 8 [Electrical Components](/electrical-components) and the [Iron Plates](/iron-plate) are waived.
