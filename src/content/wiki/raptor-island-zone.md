**Raptor Island** is a large landmass on the Western coast of the continent. It is slightly to the north of the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) and appears sandy with a small mountain range in the center of the island.

Raptor Island is aptly named for its abundance of [Swamp Raptors.](/river-raptor) None of the raptors are aggressive, but they do get annoyed by standing near their nests. Occasionally, this zone can rain acid, so it may be a good idea to bring some acid-resistant items when exploring it.

In terms of natural scenery, it is also abundant in shrubbery and hosts a few sparse trees, which is fitting for its sandy appearance.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in Raptor Island. This zone does not spawn Camps or have any Homeless Spawns.

## Tips

-   A [Megaraptor](/megaraptor) with 50-57 stats resides inside an ancient citadel-turned-nest with a variety of mid-range [weapons](/weapons) and [armour](/armour).
-   It is a decent [Hashish](/hashish) production zone as long as you have the means to produce Hemp via [Hydroponics](/hydroponics). It is void of copper but very rich with iron and skins.
