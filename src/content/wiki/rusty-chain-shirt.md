The **Rusty Chain Shirt** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Shirt slot. It can be sold by Armour King and HN and Swamp Armour Traders, as well as from Crab Smithies.

There are [standard](/chain-shirt), [darkened](/blackened-chain-shirt) and [long-sleeve](/rusty-chainmail) versions of this clothing. Additionally, there is also a [Hiver-equivalent](/rusted-hive-shirt) version of this clothing.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

It is the shirt of choice of the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen), [Crab Barman](/crab-barman), [Crab Raiders](/crab-raider), [Crabsmith](/crabsmith), and [Gorrillo](/gorrillo). It can be randomly worn by [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter), [Gorillo Bandits](/gorillo-bandit), [Moon Guardians](/moon-guardian), [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue). and [Stone Rats](/stone-rat).

It can be crafted in the [Chain Armour Crafting Bench](/chain-armour-crafting-bench) after researching the blueprint.

## Appearance

It appears to be a normal chainmail shirt that has rusted away.
