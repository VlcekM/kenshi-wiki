**Clunknad Spanner** is the alleged skeleton wizard mentioned by [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) as a part of a joke. The emperor also gives the player a [Mystery Sack](/mystery-sack) in order to identify themselves to the wizard that should help the player to kill the notorious Grievewrath.

The dialogue with Tengu is satire, mocking traditional RPG quest structure and the use of magic. Both of these NPCs are not present in [Kenshi](/kenshi). The Mystery Sack is, however, very real.
