**Biofuel Distillery** is a type of [power](/power) [building](/buildings-list) which converts crops into [Fuel](/fuel). Fuel is used in [Generator II](/generator-ii) and [Small Generators](/small-generator).

Requires researching [Biofuel](/biofuel-tech) to build and comes in three forms:

-   [Biofuel Distillery (Greenfruit)](/biofuel-distillery-greenfruit)
-   [Biofuel Distillery (Wheatstraw)](/biofuel-distillery-wheatstraw)
-   [Biofuel Distillery (Hemp)](/biofuel-distillery-hemp) - Requires additional [Hemp Oil Biofuel](/hemp-oil-biofuel-tech) research

Hemp Biofuel Distillery is twice as effective as Greenfruit or Wheatstraw.
