**Wooden Bowl** is a [junk](/junk) [item](/items) with no purpose other than trading. It is only worth a small amount. This item can be found in Residential Buildings.

When starting out, stealing this item along with other junk can help players increase their [Thievery](/thievery) stat with little risk (if the building is empty) and reward.
