**Arm of Okran** is a pair of mountainous, green zones to the east and west of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley). It's likely given its name for its humongous and long area combined with the God of HN, [Okran](/okran), that gave it the figurative name of 'Arms'.

The western mountain is home to the capital of [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and the university of [Machinists](/machinists), [World's End](/worlds-end).

The eastern mountain contains military hardpoints of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

## Inhabitants

There are no Ancient Locations in either Arm of Okran. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps, or Homeless Spawns.
