The **Desert Sabre** is an excellent weapon for killing spiders. Because of this, the Bounty Hunters hunting the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) often carry these weapons.

In the item's stats, "Spider" and "Small Spider" refer to the races of [Skin Spider](/skin-spider) and [Blood Spider](/blood-spider), respectively.

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x8**, meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon.

The [Meitou](/meitou)\-version of this weapon can be found on [Eyegore](/eyegore), martial captain of [United Cities](/united-cities).
