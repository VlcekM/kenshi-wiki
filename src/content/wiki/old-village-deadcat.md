**Old Village** is a ruined location in the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains). Technically, it is assigned to the [Deadcat](/deadcat) faction.

It was most likely a village attached to the former city [Deadcat](/cannibal-capital), however it has long since been destroyed by the [Cannibals](/cannibals); with its inhabitants either devoured or driven to the [north](/northern-coast) or [south](/dreg).

It does not spawn any loot.
