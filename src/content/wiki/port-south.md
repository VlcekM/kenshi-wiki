**Port South** is a town controlled by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). Despite being called a "port," this town is essentially a [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) because it contains many [stone mines](/stone-mine) and is largely concentrated on producing raw stone and raw ore (alongside the fact that it doesn't even have a harbor). [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) lives here in a [Noble House](/noble-house). You can find this town by venturing to "middle" of the eastern seashore of [the Great Desert](/the-great-desert) or by acquiring and reading the [Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories).

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Bark](/bark) and [Heft](/heft).

Player can buy/free slaves from the multiple Slave Shops at this location. Players could also sell some captives to Slave Shops to earn a decent number of cats. [Skimmers](/skimmer) frequently attack this town.

There is also another "port" in The Great Desert**,** called [Port North](/port-north) and it is controlled by [Lady Kana](/lady-kana).

## Trivia

Despite being called a port, Port South is close to but does not have direct access to the sea.

Engine limitations preventing ships being in game despite existing in the lore leaves many unanswered questions about this settlement. The presence of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) could imply that slaves are being imported from the [Slave Markets](/slave-markets) via ship and then delivered via [Slaver Caravans](/slaver-caravan-boss), but it is also possible that the name serves a ceremonial purpose rather than a practical one inherited from the times of [Kenshi 2](/kenshi-2), when sea levels were increased and maritime technology was significantly more advanced, which would make it little more then a glorified [Stone Camp](/stone-camp).

[Port South](/port-south-alpha) was a United Cities location in the [Alpha version](/old-world) of the game.
