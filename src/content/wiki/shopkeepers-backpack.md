The **Shopkeepers Backpack** is a very large [Backpack](/backpacks). It is identical to the [Shopkeeper goods bag](/shopkeepers-goods-bag) except for the fact that it weighs half as much.

No characters spawn with this backpack. It used to spawn on clothing, item, and weapon traders.
