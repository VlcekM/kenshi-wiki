**Ponk** is one of the two leaders of the [Thrall Masters](/thrall-masters) faction (the other is [Screamer The False](/screamer-the-false)). Both are found in the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse) (called "old control tower," before the player reaches the building). Presumably, Ponk was a conservation scientist from the Second Empire, who later became a warlord alongside Screamer. According to Screamer, Ponk spends most of his time at a research bench.

Ponk has the [Meitou](/meitou) Grade [Holed Sabre](/holed-sabre). They wear \[High\] armour (30% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).
