**Razor** is a hiver with a [bounty](/bounty) of c.10.000 on his head from the [United Cities](/united-cities). He can be found in the bar in [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city), being notably the only non-Skeleton character inside the entire town.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
