**Fabric Loom (cotton)** is a [crafting](/crafting) building which converts [cotton](/cotton) into [fabrics](/fabrics). A [fabric loom](/fabric-looms) produces [fabrics](/fabrics), which can be used in the production of [armour](/armour), [clothing](/clothing), and [weapons](/weapons). Players can construct this building after researching [Fabric Manufacture](/fabric-manufacture-tech).

Using the Fabric Loom makes use of and improves the [labouring](/labouring) stat.
