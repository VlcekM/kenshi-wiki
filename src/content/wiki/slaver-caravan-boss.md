**Slaver Caravan Bosses** are leading members of the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). They are often found in drinking away their money in [Slaver Bars](/slaver-bar), or on the road between territories controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), and Slave Traders. 

They are usually found leading a caravan, followed by groups of 7 to 14 [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard). The caravan is sometimes accompanied by a [Slaver Heavy Guard](/slaver-heavy-guard).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
