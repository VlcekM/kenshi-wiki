**Doctors** of the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) can be found in hospitals at the southern [Holy Military Base](/holy-military-base) and [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield), working alongside [Surgeons](/surgeon). They tend to any wounded soldier of Holy Nation nearby.

Unlike the Surgeons, the Doctors do not appear to offer any services to the player.
