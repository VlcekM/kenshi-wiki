The **Tower of Goats** is a tower in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) which is full of [Goats](/goat). It can be discovered by reading a **Tech Hunters [Map](/maps)**. The goats are aggressive by default.  

One of the two [Half-Eaten Ancient Science Books](/half-eaten-ancient-science-book) can be found in this location, as well as an additional intact [Ancient Science Book](/ancient-science-book).

By default, entering the tower normally will trigger the goats and the NPC inside to become hostile immediately. However, it is possible to interact with him peacefully by crawling inside instead of walking. Approaching them by crawling prevents the hostile AI from triggering, allowing the player to safely initiate dialogue with the trader and purchase domestic goats.
