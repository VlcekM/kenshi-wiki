**Bakery** is essentially a [bar](/bar) found in a few [Town Overrides](/town-overrides). This is a different vendor from the other bakeries, [Holy Bakery](/holy-bakery) and [Empire Bakery](/empire-bakery).

Due to Trade Cultures, this vendor might have item restrictions depending on which location it is in.

### Possible Locations

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Port South](/port-south)
-   [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south)

### Residents

-   [Barman](/barman)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (1)
