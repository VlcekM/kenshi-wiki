The **Black Dragon Ninjas** are a [faction](/factions) of ninjas that engage in protection rackets, pillaging the outposts that do not pay them. They reside in a tower located in the [Border Zone](/border-zone). They are [hostile](/hostile-factions) and will attack if a player approaches the [Tower of Ninjas](/tower-of-ninjas). They also have roaming squads in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) with a very low chance of spawning.

The Black Dragon Ninjas are led by [Dimak](/dimak) and [Buzan](/buzan), both of which reside in the tower. The tower also contains at least 10 [Black Dragon Genin](/black-dragon-genin).

The Black Dragon Ninjas will [attack](/bandit-raid) the player's [outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) if it is within 4,500 meters from the Tower of Ninjas and Dimak is alive.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Originally, there was a faction that was called '[White Eyebrow Clan](/white-eyebrow-clan)" that existed before it was cut and it was hostile to **Black Dragon Ninjas**, so it is likely that these two factions were originally set to be warring rivals.
-   The Black Dragon Ninjas may be a reference to The Black Dragon Fighting Society, a real-world kung-fu dojo in Chicago in the 1970s. The Dojo boasts the Dim Mak kung-fu style and in the game the leader of the ninjas is named Dimak. The Dojo is infamous for its involvement in the Chicago Dojo Wars of the 1970s. It is also famous for its eccentric founder Count Raphael Dante.
