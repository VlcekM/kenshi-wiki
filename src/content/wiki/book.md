**Book** is an [item](/items) and the most common of the [Research Artifacts](/research-artifacts) in Kenshi. It is used at a [Research Bench](/research-bench) for researching various [Technologies](/technology).

Books can be purchased at Adventure Traders, Construction Traders, Hive Traders, Trade Goods Traders in nearly all settlements, as well as from Wandering Caravans.

They can be looted frequently from [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs), [Ancient Tech Lab](/ancient-tech-lab), [Burn's Tower](/burns-tower), [Dust King Tower](/dust-king-tower), [Lost Libraries](/lost-library), [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop), Spider Nests, and [World's End](/worlds-end), and infrequently from [Dust Bandit Camps](/dust-bandit-camp), [Holy Mine Ruin](/holy-mine-ruin), [Suspicious Lab](/suspicious-lab), [Tiny Settlement](/tiny-settlement). They can also often be found and stolen from inside houses throughout various towns. You need a total of 306 books to research everything.
