**Industry Buildings** are buildings which turn raw materials into refined items. These buildings do not require a crafting queue to be used by characters.

## Stone Processing

Early game, most [Construction](/construction) requires Building Materials. These can be purchased from a Trader but can easily be researched and crafted inside a Player Outpost.

### Stone Mines

[Raw Stone](/raw-stone) can be mined from a [Stone Mine](/stone-mine). Stone Mines can be upgraded with the research.

### Stone Processors

Stone can be processed into [Building Materials](/building-materials) with a [Stone Processor](/stone-processor).

## Iron Refining

[Raw Iron](/raw-iron) can be mined from an Iron Deposit in most Regions. These Deposits can be found through [Prospecting](/prospecting).

### Ore Drills

Characters can build an [Ore Drill](/ore-drill) after researching [Ore Drill](/ore-drill-tech). These Ore Drills can be built in areas away from Iron Deposits. After researching [Fully Automatic Ore Drill](/fully-automatic-ore-drill-tech), these Ore Drills no longer require workers.

### Iron Refineries

[Iron Plates](/iron-plate) can be created by refining Raw Iron. After researching [Iron Plates](/iron-plates-tech), characters can build an [Iron Refinery](/iron-refinery).

### Steel Refineries

[Steel Bars](/steel-bars) can be created by further refining Iron Plates. After researching [Steel Bars](/steel-bars-tech), characters can build a [Steel Refinery](/steel-refinery).

## Copper Refining

[Raw Copper](/raw-copper) can be mined from a Copper Deposit in most Regions. These Deposits can be found through [Prospecting](/prospecting).

###  Electrical Workbench

At an [Electrical Workbench](/electrical-workbench), characters can turn [Raw Copper](/raw-copper) into [Electrical Components](/electrical-components). This workstation trains the [Science](/science) skill while in use.

This workbench can be built after researching [Electrical Crafting](/electrical-crafting-tech).

### Copper Alloy Bench

At a [Copper Alloy Bench](/copper-alloy-bench), characters can turn [Raw Copper](/raw-copper) and [Iron Plates](/iron-plate) into [Copper Alloy Plates](/copper-alloy-plates). This workstation trains the [Engineer](/engineer) skill while in use.

This workbench can be built after researching [Copper Alloys](/copper-alloys-tech).
