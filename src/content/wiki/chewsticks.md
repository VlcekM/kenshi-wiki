**Chewsticks** are a [food](/food) item purchased from relevant [traders](/traders). Players can cook Chewsticks in a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove) after researching [Desert Cooking](/desert-cooking-tech).

If [Water](/water) is a rare commodity, Chewsticks are a very water-efficient food, with XL Cactus Fields producing 64 Cactus/24h for 2 Water +.25 per Chewstick = 8 per 4 water, 160nu/24h while [Rice Bowls](/rice-bowl) and [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables) require massively more water to produce equivalent nutrition.

A similar water-cost 1-ingredient food that provides similar nutrition would be Bread at 3.75 water per 150nu.

The lowest of all cultivated food is the [Dustwich](/dustwich), costing 1 water per 70nu.
