**Adventurer Mechanical Shop** is a shop which sells all crops except for [Riceweed](/riceweed) and construction materials. This shop is also capable of spawning [Artifacts](/artifacts), as such it may spawn items not in the list. These items, however, will not respawn when the shop restocks. Towns marked with an asterisk (\*) will have the shop spawn artifacts.

### Locations

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)
-   [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon)\*
-   [Waystations](/waystation)
-   [World's End](/worlds-end)\*

Depending on [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)
-   [Squin](/squin)
-   [Stack](/stack)
-   [The Free City](/the-free-city)

### Residents

-   [Ronin Construction Trader](/ronin-construction-trader)
-   [Ninja Guard](/ninja-guard) (5)
