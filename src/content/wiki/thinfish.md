**Thinfish** is a [food](/food) item which can only be obtained through trading or thieving. [Fish and Drugs Sellers](/fish-and-drugs-seller) sell Thinfish. Although [Dried Fish](/dried-fish) is described to be made from Thinfish, players cannot cook this item.

The name originates from the fact that it's simply thinner than the other type of fish, the [Grand Fish](/grand-fish).

## Trivia

-   Thinfish is the most expensive food value/nutrient wise. It costs an average of c.36 for 1 nutrition.
-   In the Fish Isles you can sometimes find clumps of fish called Camp Fish Pile. By putting a Thinfish inside of one it'll turn it into a Thinfish.
    -   This could be a bug/oversight. It might have been meant to turn into a [Dried Fish](/dried-fish). This would make sense as Dried Fish states that it is a Thinfish that's been dried and salted.
