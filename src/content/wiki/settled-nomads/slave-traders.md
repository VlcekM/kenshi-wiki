If [Tinfist](/tinfist) becomes [dead or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Longen](/longen) is alive, the [Settled Nomads](/settled-nomads) location gets [taken over](/town-overrides) by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

It appears that the Nomads have either been enslaved or evacuated<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> from this region after the increased slaver presence, and this place has become a trashed ruin with a few loot littered around this area.
