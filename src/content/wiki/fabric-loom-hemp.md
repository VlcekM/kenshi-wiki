**Fabric Loom (hemp)** is a [crafting](/crafting) building which converts [hemp](/hemp) into [fabrics](/fabrics). A [fabric loom](/fabric-looms) produces [fabrics](/fabrics), which can be used in the production of [armour](/armour), [clothing](/clothing), and [weapons](/weapons). Players can construct this building after researching [Hemp-Based Fabric](/hemp-based-fabric-tech).

Using the Fabric Loom improves the [labouring](/labouring) skill.
