**Ninja Guards** are generic characters which can spawn as a member of either the [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas) or [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) factions.

**Ninja Guards** are shop guards of the [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas). They can be seen guarding [bars](/bar) like the one in [Mourn](/mourn).

**Ninja Guards** can be found within and occasionally outside Thief watchtowers. They become allies who will defend you from being attacked after joining the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) by talking to the [Thief Boss](/thief-boss).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
