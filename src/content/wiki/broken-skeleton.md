**Broken Skeletons** are malfunctioning [Skeletons](/skeletons). They are usually found wandering alone in [Obedience](/obedience), [Floodlands](/floodlands), and the [Iron Valleys](/iron-valleys). They can also spawn in [Ancient Tech Labs](/ancient-tech-lab), and [Deadland Workshops](/deadland-workshop).
