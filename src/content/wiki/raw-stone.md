**Raw Stone** is the base ingredient used to create [Building Materials](/building-materials), the building blocks of construction.

Stone can be gathered through use of a [Stone Mine](/stone-mine) and stored in [Storage: Stones](/storage-stones) container. Both of those buildings become obsolete with the construction of a [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine). Small quantities of Raw Stone can be found in the ruins of various (ancient) locations around Kenshi.
