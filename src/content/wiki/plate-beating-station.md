The **Plate Beating Station** is a crafting building type introduced in patch 0.50. This crafting station allows players to craft [Armour Plating](/armour-plating) from [Raw Iron](/raw-iron). Armour Plating is later used to construct plate based armour at the [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy). The station requires no power to run, but does require manual labour of a worker. Using it improves the [Armour Smithing](/armour-smith) skill.

## Trivia

-   Some [worker drones](/hive-worker-drone) can be seen working at these stations in [Western Hive](/western-hive) [Villages](/hive-village).
-   Using a Plate beating station in a shop produces armor plates that are 'owned' by the shop. The output will have to be stolen.
