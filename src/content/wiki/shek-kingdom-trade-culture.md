**Shek Kingdom Trade Culture** is a [trade culture](/traders) which removes cultural items from [Holy Nation](/holy-nation), several technology items, food items and all alcohol except [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum). [Hashish](/hashish) is illegal but not buildings who are tied to hashish production, neither is [Hemp](/hemp).
