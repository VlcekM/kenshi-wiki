**Green Finger** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) who can be found in [Fishing Village](/fishing-village-northern-coast). He/She is a farmer who is tired of living so close to the [Cannibals](/cannibals) and can be recruited by the player for c.2,000. They are interested in moving to the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and farming the fertile land along the river [Wend](/wend). Green Finger has the Fearful [Personality](/personality).
