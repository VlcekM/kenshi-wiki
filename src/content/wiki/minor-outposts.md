**Minor Outposts** are the various small outposts that make up a large amount of the locations in the game. They can range from farms to mining outposts, to small villages to trading outposts or military outposts. Often times they will not have the amenities usually associated with [Major Towns](/major-towns), such as beds for rent and stores to buy and sell items at. Although some do.

## United Cities

These outposts are controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) or by their close allies the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) and the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

### [Drin](/drin)

-   A semi-ruined small garrison town in [Bast](/bast-zone).

### [Farming Village](/farming-village)

-   A small village in the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

### [Eyesocket](/eyesocket)

-   The slave markets of Eyesocket can be found in the western side of the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

### [Slave Markets](/slave-markets)

-   Slave Markets in the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands), east of the [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base).

## The Holy Nation

These outposts are controlled by the zealous [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), and thus non-human [Races](/races) will often be attacked on sight, unless they have a human escort.

### [Holy Farms](/holy-farms)

-   These farms are scattered across the fertile areas of [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) territory. The [Holy Farm Leader](/holy-farm-leader) is a trader that has a variety of food and crops. You can also buy [Bulls](/wild-bull) and [Dogs](/bonedog) from him, as well as the [Bull Backpack](/bull-backpack).

### [Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base)

-   These military outposts are often found on the edges of [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) lands. They are public outposts that contain several types of traders, including weapon, armour, and food traders. They however do not have any beds available for rent.

### [Holy Mines](/holy-mines)

-   These mines are scattered all around [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) territory. They usually have a [slave shop](/slave-shop) and a barracks surrounded by a wall. Human slaves known as [Lower Servants](/lower-servant) are caged at night and forced to mine ore during the day, supervised by a garrison of soldiers and [slavers](/slaver) commanded by a [High Overseer](/high-overseer). Anyone who attacks a Holy Mine and is knocked unconscious will be enslaved as well.

### [Rebirth](/rebirth)

-   This is a large slave camp in the northeastern mountains just east of the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) in the region of [Rebirth](/rebirth-zone). This where all non-human races are taken to perform labour for [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They appear to be constructing a massive statue of the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), or another Phoenix.

## Tech Hunters

These outposts are controlled by [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters). The Freedom Seekers [Game Start](/game-starts) can start at any one of these locations.

### [Waystation](/waystation-border-zone)

-   A small outpost in the [Border Zone](/border-zone) which contains a [Robotics Shop](/robotics-shop).

### [Waystation](/waystation-grey-desert)

-   A small outpost in the [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) which has a ruined tower for sale. (c.21,000)

### [Waystation](/waystation-the-hook)

-   A small outpost in [The Hook](/the-hook) which has a building for sale.

### [Waystation](/waystation-stenn-desert)

-   A small outpost in the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). This is the only waystation which only has two buildings: The [General Supplies](/item-trader) shop and the bar.

## Deadcat

These outposts are controlled by the dying faction [Deadcat](/deadcat). Since the growth of [Cannibal](/cannibals) tribes, their civilization has been in decline.

### [Fishing Village](/fishing-village-deadcat)

-   Mercenaries wander nearby as well as inside the [Bar](/bar). Besides residences, it only has a single shop and the aforementioned bar.

### [Gatherer Village](/gatherer-village)

-   A small group of outposts in the northwest, past the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). They are presumably mining outposts, though they do not appear to be complete.

### [Ghost Village](/ghost-village)

-   This village is implied to have been recently depopulated, as a result all items available are free to take and will not be marked [stolen](/thievery).
    -   If seen lockpicking containers, you may still be attacked by NPCs like Tech Hunters.

## Flotsam Ninjas

These outposts belong to the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas). The Flotsam Ninjas are the main resistance to The Holy Nation.

### [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village)

-   In the middle of the [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), the majority of the Flotsam Ninjas live here alongside their leader [Moll](/moll).

### [Flotsam Safehouse](/flotsam-safehouse)

-   Just north of Rebirth, the Flotsam Safehouse contains many supplies for recently escaped slaves of The Holy Nation.

### [Scout Post](/scout-post)

-   Both Scout Posts fight to keep the cannibals from entering the Hidden Forest. The north post is led by [Mani](/mani) who will trade players any items inside the post.

## Crab Raiders

These outposts belong to the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders), who are one of the three 'pit gangs', who inhabit the [The Pits East](/the-pits-east) and [Greenbeach](/greenbeach).

### [Crab Town](/crab-town)

-   The headquarters of the Crab Raider faction, where the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) [resides](/palace-of-the-crab-queen). It is one of the only settlements with vendors and beds in the region of [The Pits](/the-pits).

### [Crab Villages](/crab-village)

-   Two villages located in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) and [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), with them both having a [Crab Smithy](/crab-smithy) vendor.

## Other Factions

These outposts belong to small factions that tend to only have these small outposts.

### [Hive Villages](/hive-village)

-   These villages owned by the [Western Hive](/western-hive) are scattered around the map in [Vain](/vain), [Dreg](/dreg), and [Sinkuun](/sinkuun). They are populated by a lot of [Hive](/hive) worker and soldier drones, as well as a couple of [Hive Traders](/hive-trader). You can rent the beds in the worker's huts, and the traders sell a large variety of goods as well as buy stolen goods at full price.

### [Swamp Village](/swamp-village)

-   A small settlement within the northwest part of [The Swamp](/the-swamp). It has a swamper selling Hashish and fish. Also, there is a [Travel Supplies Shop](/deadcat-big-shop).
