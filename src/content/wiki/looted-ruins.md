**Looted Ruins** is a location from the [Second Empire](/second-empire) in the northern coast of the [Howler Maze](/howler-maze). This location consists of a couple ruined buildings except for one empty Outpost s-III. [Crabs](/crab) can be found patrolling here and there may be a Crab Nest nearby.
