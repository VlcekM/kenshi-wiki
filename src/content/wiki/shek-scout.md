**Shek Scouts** are warriors of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). They can be found roaming the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) and [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) in small groups of 4 to 7. They are equipped similarly to the [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) but are slightly stronger. True to their name, they are only found wandering outside Shek towns.

Unlike other patrols, these scouts will loot any unconcious bodies for food.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
