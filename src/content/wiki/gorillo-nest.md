**Gorillo Nests** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn absolutely nowhere.

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

-   blue gorillo nest
    -   This squad can contain 2 to 6 [Black Gorillos](/black-gorillo). One to two of these will spawn with an age multiplier between 0.75x to 1.0x. The rest will be between 0.2x to 0.45x age.
        -   This nest does not actually spawn in any zone in game.
-   gorillos blue
    -   This squad can contain 1 [Black Gorillo](/black-gorillo). This gorillo will spawn with an age multiplier between 0.7x to 0.8x.
        -   Attached to this nest, this squad does not spawn. However, this squad will spawn naturally in multiple zones in the more southeastern sections of the map.
