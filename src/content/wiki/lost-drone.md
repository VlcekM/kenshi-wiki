**Lost Drones** are [factionless](/factions) [hivers](/hive). They can be found wandering [Bast](/bast-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon-zone), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Hook](/the-hook).

These hivers were likely separated or exiled from the [Western Hive](/western-hive). They are not to be confused with the [Lost Hiver](/lost-hiver) drones. Given their abundance in both [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) areas, it's likely that these type of Hivers are the source for the majority of Hiver slaves in [Rebirth](/rebirth) for the HN and [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) for the UC.

If they get inside or too close to holy nation towns, the leading [Gate Sentinel](/holy-sentinel) will mark that their kind are not welcome there and arrest them to later send them to [Rebirth](/rebirth). They are also prime targets for [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunter) and [Noble Hunters](/noble-hunter) if they find themselves in United Cities territory.

Lost Drones can join the player faction if they encounter a Hive player character in a Player Outpost. Once recruited, they will have the "Player HIVER Ronin" Dialogue Package. It also seems like they have unique dialogue with [Вeep](/beep) specifically.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
