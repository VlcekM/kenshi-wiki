## Player Town Level Icons

A player town has a level from 0 to 3. The level sets the town's map icon and its town type.

## What decides the level?

-   **Buildings with Interiors**: buildings you can go inside, such as shacks, storm houses, outpost houses and other houses.
-   **Turrets**: any mounted crossbow or harpoon turret.
-   **Gates**: any gate, the tier doesn't matter.
-   **Research bench tier**: your best research bench. Only Research Bench II+ count toward a level.

### Level 0

-   Any building

### Level 1

Any **one** of:

-   A Gate
-   Research Bench II+
-   2+ Buildings
-   4 Turrets

### Level 2

-   A Gate

Plus any **one** of:

-   Research Bench IV+
-   5+ Buildings
-   5+ Turrets

### Level 3

**All** of:

-   A Gate
-   Research Bench IV+
-   5+ Buildings
-   7+ Turrets

A gate alone is enough for level 1. Levels 2 and 3 can't be reached without a gate.

### What does not count

-   Walls.
-   Gate tier.
-   Power, batteries, or generators.
-   Population.
-   Farms, storage, crafting stations or any other building type not listed above.

### Construction state

The game does not check whether a building is finished. A placed but unbuilt blueprint already counts toward the level.

## When the level updates

The level is not updated continuously. It is recalculated only:

-   when you leave the build menu (confirm, close or cancel), for the player town nearest the camera;
-   when two of your towns merge into one.

It is not recalculated when you load a save, when construction finishes, or when you dismantle or lose buildings.

-   Buildings you have just placed only count from the next time you leave the build menu, not the time you confirm them.
-   Removing buildings does not lower the level until you next open and close the build menu near that town.

The level also counts only buildings in the area around the town's centre. Buildings far out on a very spread-out base may not be counted.

## What the level affects

-   **The map icon and town type** (above).
-   **Shek Kingdom tribute**: the Shek tribute visit only comes to a town of level 1 or higher (and only once you have at least 5 characters).
-   **Dialogue**: some NPCs speak differently depending on your town level. The Empire tax collector calls a level 0-1 town's owner a peasant and a level 3 town's owner "sir", but asks the same amount.

The level is **not** the main driver of raids. Most raid events don't check it at all. They depend on distance to the raiding faction's towns and on how many characters you have in total.
