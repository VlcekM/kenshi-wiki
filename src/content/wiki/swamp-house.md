A small house similar in size to a [Small Shack](/small-shack). They can be found in [Bark](/bark) for c16,000 alongside the [Swamp Shack](/swamp-shack) and [Swamp Store](/swamp-store).

Like the [Swamp Store](/swamp-store), buildings can only be placed inside and not on the porch. Additionally, there is a slope in the middle of the house, where buildings cannot be placed.

They are significantly smaller than a [Storm House](/storm-house) along with having an inconvenient slope and being more expensive, so it's far better to just opt for a [Storm House](/storm-house) or even a [Small Shack](/small-shack) instead.
