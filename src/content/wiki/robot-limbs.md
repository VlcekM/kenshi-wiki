<div class="notice loading-tip"><span class="loading-tip-label">Loading screen tip:</span> If a limb is hit with a sharp weapon and it has less than -100 health then it will be severed. This is a permanent loss, you will have to find a robotic replacement.</div>

**\*The tip incorrectly mentions "sharp weapon". If you are hit by any attack whatsoever and your limb goes below -100% of its max health from that attack the limb will be severed if the limb severing frequency is set to anywhere more than "never"\***

**Robot Limbs** are replacements for [missing limbs](/severed-limbs). They come in many variants and give bonuses and penalties to certain skills. The boost can be so desirable that players may make their squad members lose limbs on purpose so robot limbs can be installed.

Because of how rare your chance of losing limbs is in usual combat<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, one way to intentionally lose limbs is to allow wounds to deteriorate on their own and set the limb severing frequency to "frequent"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the other way to lose limbs is through the use of the [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine) or a Fogpole.

Whenever a character is missing any natural limbs, the 'Limbs' button in the inventory window becomes usable and prosthetics can be installed the same way weapons and armor are equipped. (Once installed, robot limbs no longer count toward [Encumbrance](/encumbrance)). All characters, including [Skeletons](/skeleton), need to lose their natural limbs through trauma before Robot Limbs can be used. When a limb is lost a character receives 0.2 bleeding and 0.002 "steady bleeding" for every cut damage (and negative bandaged health) on the limb. Steady bleeding will not stop until bandaged or it can be stopped instantly by putting a robotic limb in the place of the lost limb. Animals cannot lose limbs or use prosthetics.

Robot Limbs need to be maintained in the same way Skeletons maintain themselves, which is notably expensive. Cut damage can be fixed on the spot with a [Robotics Repair Kit](/robotics-repair-kit). The "wear and tear damage" that builds up over time (4% of total damage taken is applied as wear damage) and decreases the max health of a limb can be fixed using a [Skeleton Repair Bed](/skeleton-repair-bed). These Repair Beds can be typically found in locations which sell limbs and usually require payment the same way a bar bed does (albeit significantly more expensive).

Characters can get in trouble in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) when having Robot Limbs. They will be seen as Skeletons and attacked unless the Holy Nation [faction reputation](/guide-to-faction-relations) is 50 or higher. Even if the reputation is 50 or higher, a character wearing robotic limbs will be unable to interact with [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and any other member of the Holy Nation including [Paladins](/paladin), [Holy Priests](/holy-priest) and Shopkeepers (taking off robotic limbs would nonetheless still allow). The Holy Nation trade caravans who patrol around can still be traded with even without allying with their faction.

## Crafting

Players can craft robotic limbs on a [Skeleton Limb Bench](/skeleton-limb-bench) and needed components on a [Robotics Bench](/robotics-bench). Unlike with weapon and armor, there are no [Blueprints](/blueprints) for limbs. New crafting options are unlocked by doing research in the [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) tree instead. Economy limb research becomes available at tech level 4, Skeleton limbs at tech level 5 and the rest at tech level 6. All robotic limbs are craftable except for the Steady and Thief's arms.

What quality a crafted limb will be is determined by the crafter's [Robotics](/robotics) skill. It's the same skill that is trained by using repair kits and by crafting any components at the Robotics Bench. There is also an element of chance, and the quality can suffer when the crafter is not physically well or working in low light conditions. Unlike a weapon or armor smith, a robotics crafter can attain a skill level at which all products will be max quality. The better limbs require a somewhat complicated crafting procedure when constructed from raw materials. Players crafting in a bought house may simply opt to buy the [Robotics Components](/robotics-components), greatly reducing the fuss.

## Locations

Robotic limbs are sold at the following vendors:

-   [Hive Robotics Shop](/hive-robotics-shop)
-   [Mongrel Robotics Workshop](/mongrel-robotics-workshop)
-   [Robotics Shop](/robotics-shop)
-   [Robotics Workshop](/robotics-workshop)
-   [Travel and Repairs Shop](/travel-and-repairs-shop)

[Hive](/western-hive) robotic shops specialize in cheap economy limbs while the others, usually run by [Skeletons](/skeleton), have higher quality merchandise. An exceptionally large selection of high-quality limbs can be found at the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) Robotics Shop.

Robotic limbs can also randomly spawn through the [Artifact](/artifacts) system in locations which have a gear artifacts value.

## Tips

-   Installing an artificial limb on a downed enemy will later open up an option to [heal](/field-medic) that enemy if they were to get hurt again. This option is normally not available for conscious enemies.
-   An exploit that can be abused to heal all damage (Including wear) on a robotic limb is to take off the limb, save your game and then load the save. When the limb is put back on it will be back at full health.

## Trivia

-   Wearing only one robotic leg with an organic leg on the other side will result in limping animation when in walking speed.
-   No characters are assigned to spawn with a Robotic Limb despite being capable of being assigned one, it is likely that they are usually reserved for small groups of [Skeletons](/skeletons) and [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) who have the money necessary to maintain it.
-   As of v1.0.68, the Unarmed Damage Bonus on robotic limbs still does nothing.
