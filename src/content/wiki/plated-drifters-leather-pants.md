**Plated Drifter's Leather Pants** are a pair of shoddily reinforced [Drifters Leather Pants](/drifters-leather-pants). They belong to the Medium Armour Class. This armour was introduced into the game with version 0.98.37.

Although being "plated", these pants are labeled "Chain."

These pants can be bought from [Hive](/western-hive), [Shek](/shek-kingdom), and [Tech Hunter](/tech-hunters) armour shops. They can normally be found worn by [Rebel Captains](/rebel-captain), [Rebel Swordsman Veterans](/rebel-swordsman-veteran), [Swamper Gate Guards](/swamper-gate-guard), and [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x4**.

## Appearance

**Plated Drifter's Leather Pants** look like [Drifters Leather Pants](/drifters-leather-pants) with two large pieces of armour strapped on the right leg, and chainmail and chain wrapped around the other. The female version sports an extra chainmail skirt.
