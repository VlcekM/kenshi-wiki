**Dreg** is a relatively vast and sparsely populated zone to the west of [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), north of [Vain](/vain), southwest of [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) and southeast of [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone). This place is inhabited mainly by [Western Hive](/western-hive), but it is also located at the frontiers of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

[Prospecting](/prospecting) reveals the area to be "dead", although it is populated by sparse trees and shrubs. Low quality [copper](/copper) can be mined here, although mid quality [iron](/iron-ore) in the form of giant structures are also found. The "Hive Exile" [game start](/game-starts) can spawn here.

This area is also inhabited by small factions such as [Nomads](/nomads) and [Deadcat](/deadcat).

Some groups of [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws), [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits), [Fogmen](/fogmen) and even [Cannibals](/cannibals) who drifted too far away can be occasionally found here.
