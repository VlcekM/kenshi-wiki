**Flats Lagoon** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) controlled by the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and therefore has been added to the **Tech Hunters [Map](/maps)**.

The town's bartender can be asked about the [Ashlands](/ashlands), though most of the information relayed more specifically relates to adjacent areas which players must travel through to reach the Ashlands.

As with all Tech Hunters locations, there are no [World States](/world-states) which can affect Flats Lagoon.

The [United Cities](/united-cities) pacifier can be found here. [Chad](/chad), the [unique recruit](/unique-recruits), will spawn at the local bar.

This location can spawn any of the following characters: [Recruit](/recruit), [Adventurer Recruit](/adventurer-recruit), [Fighter Recruit](/fighter-recruit), [Amateur Recruit](/amateur-recruit), [Bard](/bard), [Ex-Slave Recruit](/ex-slave-recruit), [Outlaw Recruit](/outlaw-recruit), [Tenacious Recruit](/tenacious-recruit), [Recruit Seeking Revenge](/recruit-seeking-revenge), [Silvershade](/silvershade), [Outlaw Medic](/outlaw-medic), [Engineer Recruit](/engineer-recruit), [Wall Man](/wall-man), [Medic Recruit](/medic-recruit), [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja), [Ninja](/ninja), [Sand Ninja](/sand-ninja), [Dust Bandit](/dust-bandit), and [Hungry Bandit](/hungry-bandit).

While Flats Lagoon is mostly safe from wildlife and bandit assaults, the harpooners have low Precision and can potentially harm Western Hive caravans, risking hostilities between both factions and leading to fights in the town.
