**Empire Weapon Shops** are [weapon shops](/weapons-shops) owned by the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) found in [United Cities](/united-cities).

This shop is the larger version of [Small Empire Weapon Shop](/small-empire-weapon-shop), and therefore also contains a larger variety and quantities than it's counterpart.

### Possible Locations

-   [Heng](/heng)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

### Residents

-   [UC Weapon Trader](/uc-weapon-trader)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (3 to 4)

In **Heng**, this shop is capable of spawning [Artifacts](/artifacts), as such it may spawn items not in the list. These items, however, will not respawn when the shop restocks.
