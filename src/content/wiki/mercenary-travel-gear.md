**Mercenary Travel Gear** is a [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) vendor unique to the Mercenary Base.

It is quite similar to the standard [Travel Gear](/travel-gear) found in other [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) locations. With the difference being that they sell more variety of food items.

### Possible Locations

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor may be found in these locations:

-   [Manhunter Base, Mercenary Guild](/manhunter-base-mercenary-guild)

### Residents

-   [Tech Hunter Shopkeeper](/tech-hunter-shopkeeper)
-   [Mercenary](/mercenary) (1 to 2)

This location has an item [Artifacts](/artifacts) value of c.100,000, so it should spawn some additional items through the artifacts system.
