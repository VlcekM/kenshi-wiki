**Lord Ohta** is a noble of the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction. He owns a Noble House in [Heft](/heft), and has a group of bodyguards consisting of 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard).

He has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Lord Ohta is killed/imprisoned it can cause a potential [event](/events) of Noble's Wrath, and some dialogue changes with Samurai Guards.

Aside from that, he has no other influences on the World States.

## Trivia

-   Out of all Nobles of UC, **Lord Ohta** is the only one who has no special World States aligned to him.
-   Lord Ohta despawns when Emperor Tengu is killed.
