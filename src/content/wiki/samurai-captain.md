**Samurai Captains** are elite officers in the [United Cities](/united-cities) armies. They can be found leading groups of [Samurai Heavies](/samurai-heavy) in patrolling towns or serving as barracks' leaders. They can also be found guarding important figures such as [Longen](/longen) and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), alongside squads of [Samurai Elites](/samurai-elite) and [Jonin Elites](/jonin-elite). One Samurai Captain accompanies [Eyegore](/eyegore) into battle.

There are in total 8 Samurai Captains guarding **Emperor Tengu** under default [Heft](/heft). If **Heft** is malnourished, there will be only 6 of them.

They wear \[High\] armour (10% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
