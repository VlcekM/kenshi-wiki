A **Power Core** is an [item](/items) which can be found in ancient locations. It serves no function outside of being worth a decent amount of money.

Found in:

-   [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs)
-   [Armoury Ruin](/armoury-ruin)
-   [Archive Town](/archive-town)
-   [Leaning Tower](/leaning-tower)
-   [Lost Town](/lost-town)
-   [Old Empire Watchtower](/old-empire-watchtower)
-   [Dead Workshop](/dead-workshop), [Deadland Workshop](/deadland-workshop), [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop), [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex)

## Tips

-   Picking up items off counters (Power Core included) in Ancient Labs may tag them as stolen. You can simply place the items tagged as stolen this way into a container in the lab to remove the tag.
