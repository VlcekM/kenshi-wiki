**Sniper Tower** has many [Upgraded Watchbot](/upgraded-watchbot) Skeletons manning MkII Harpoons at the top of the tower.

Interestingly, they are all facing northwards - preventing players from using the pits as an easy access point to the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

Be aware that like all turrets, these can fire in nearly 360 degrees. The first character or two reaching the roof will likely be hit with multiple harpoons. Heavy armor with 100% coverage is recommended. Oftentimes they will be able to hit you through the floors with their ranged weapons.

If you are able to clear the tower (or are particularly brave), the 3rd floor contains [Beds](/bed) with the usual 8x healing multiplier for organic characters. Unfortunately, only one bed can be used due to junk placement, but it's possible to clip characters in by picking them up.

Beware! The liquid surrounding this area is acidic.
