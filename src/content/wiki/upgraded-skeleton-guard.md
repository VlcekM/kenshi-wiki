**Upgraded Skeleton Guards** are elite members of the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) faction. Twenty can be found guarding [Elder](/elder) in the HQ building of [Iron HQ](/iron-hq), and an additional 24 to 36 can be found resting in their [Metal Wallhouse](/metal-wallhouse) residences.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (18% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
