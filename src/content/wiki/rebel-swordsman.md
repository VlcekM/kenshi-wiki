**Rebel Swordsmen** are members of the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) faction. They appear as guards and patrols of towns fully controlled by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) in the northeastern territories.

If the local noble of a town is killed but [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is still alive(conditions vary by town), then Rebel Swordsmen along with [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer) show up in force to assault the town and potentially occupy it if they overrun the defenders.

They have a 10% chance to spawn with a bounty ranging from 300 - 1300 Cats. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
