**Scavengers** are members of the [Scavengers](/scavengers) faction. They can be found roaming around the southeast portion of the map, such as [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Outlands](/the-outlands), [The Pits](/the-pits), [The Pits East](/the-pits-east), and [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley). They reside in the [Old Control Tower](/old-control-tower) in [Venge](/venge).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (40% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Unknown](/unknown).
