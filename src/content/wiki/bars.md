**Bars** are the gathering hubs of most cities. A shelter from the harshness of the wasteland to [recover](/bed), bars provide many amenities. The local [Barman](/barman) readily supplies alcohol, [bread](/bread) and [food](/food) (barring the [Skeleton Bar](/skeleton-bar)) and as such there is no shortage of adventurers gathered in these watering holes. This makes bars the ideal place to [recruit](/generic-recruits) new squad members. You may even come across some of the land's more eccentric denizens in the form of [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits).

One may also employ the services of [Mercenaries](/mercenary-guild) (or [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters)/[Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters)) or sell ill gotten goods to a [Thief Fence](/thief-fence) in these dens of iniquity.
