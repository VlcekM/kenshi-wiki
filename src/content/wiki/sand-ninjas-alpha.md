The Sand Ninjas is a [cult](/factions-alpha) of highly skilled highwaymen. They attack passerbys with skilled combat to slay the weak. Some ninjas must go through cult challenges to prove their worthy as lieutenants, such as surviving the badlands alone. Uncommonly found, the Sand Ninjas are typically more dangerous than the [Starving Bandits and Dust Bandits](/bandits).
