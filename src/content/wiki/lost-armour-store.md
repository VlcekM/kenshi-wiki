**Lost Armour Store** is an [Outpost s-I](/outpost-s-i) building in the north-east part of [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). Its door is locked and has level 72 security. Inside the armory are several armor crafting stations, armor component storage containers and generic containers.

The armory spawns [Artifacts](/artifacts), notably:

-   Masterwork [Black Plate Jacket](/black-plate-jacket)
-   Masterwork [Samurai Boots](/samurai-boots)
-   Edgewalker I [Polearm](/polearm)
-   Specialist [Armoured Rags](/armoured-rags)
-   Skeleton Smiths I [Horse Chopper](/horse-chopper)
