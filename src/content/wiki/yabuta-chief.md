The **Yabuta Chief** is a leader of the [Yabuta Outlaws](/yabuta-outlaws). He can be found in the HQ in [Fort Yabuta](/fort-yabuta) (formerly [Fort Simion](/fort-simion)) after [Yabuta of the Sands](/yabuta-of-the-sands) has been released from [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault) (as long as the player is not allied with [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)).

They have a 20% chance to have a bounty ranging from c.1,500 to c.2,500. They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
