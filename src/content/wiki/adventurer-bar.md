**Adventurer Bar** is a vendor found in [Tech Hunter](/tech-hunters) settlements. This bar has more high-tech and adventuring equipment than most other bars.

### Possible Locations

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)
-   [Spring](/spring)
-   [Waystations](/waystation)
-   [World's End](/worlds-end)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [The Free City](/the-free-city)
