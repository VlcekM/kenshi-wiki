The **Tech Hunter Bar** is the building HQ of the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) (according to the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set)).

### Location

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)

### Residents

-   [Shinobi Guard](/shinobi-guard) x9
-   [Tech Hunter Guard](/tech-hunter-guard) x9
-   [Undertaker](/undertaker) x3

## Trivia

-   While the FCS does show there should be a vendor at this location, none spawn. This may be due to several issues with the AI in the building.
-   It is possible for [Drifters](/drifter) and/or [Mercenaries](/mercenary) to spawn at this location.
