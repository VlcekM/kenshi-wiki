**Slave Master Haga** is a noble member of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). He can be found at [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

As with the other TG Nobles, Haga lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house) and is guarded by 4 [Hooded Empire Noble Guard](/hooded-empire-noble-guard) characters and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

He has Fearful [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Slave Master Haga is killed, then the Stone Camp will fall into ruins.

Haga's death alongside with [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) will cause [Stoat to become malnourished](/stoat/malnourished) (if under UC rule), as well as malnourished [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) to spawn in [Bast](/bast-zone) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) (if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive).

Haga's death alongside [Grande](/slave-master-grande) and [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) will cause [Heng](/heng) (under UC rule) and [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) (under TG rule) to become malnourished.

[Slave Trader](/slave-traders) and [Manhunter](/manhunter) patrol will decrease in frequency moderately at [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), provided [Kana](/lady-kana) wasn't already killed beforehand (which provides the same effect).

Slave Master Haga is considered to be one of the Northern slave Masters/Mistresses alongside with [Longen](/longen), [Lady Kana](/lady-kana), [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren), [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) and [Slave Master Ruben.](/slave-master-ruben) The death of all these characters will also stop [Manhunter](/manhunter) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng), [Skimsands](/skimsands), and [The Eye](/the-eye), as well as [Slave Hunter](/slave-hunter) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng).
