**Upgraded Watchbots** are members of the [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion) faction. They can be found in [Barrier Tower](/barrier-tower) and [Sniper Tower](/sniper-tower) manning the [Harpoon Turrets](/harpoon-turret).

Their ranged weapons can sometimes be of masterwork quality.

They wear \[High\] armour (20% chance of \[Specialist\]).
