**Big Al** is leader of the [Stone Rats](/stone-rats) faction and is found in [Stone Rat Village](/stone-rat-village) within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). Despite being their leader, he does not have [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status).

He has the Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Big Al** is killed or kidnapped, it will cause the Stone Rat Village to be taken over by the [Hounds](/hounds) or the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) depending on other world states. His death will also cease Stone Rat [raids](/stone-rat-assault) or [protection rackets](/stone-rat-visit) on a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost).

-   If the player's [faction](/factions) is allied to the [Hounds](/hounds) and they kidnap and bring Big Al to the [Hounds](/hounds)' [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) in [Shark](/shark), [Big Grim](/big-grim) will laugh at Big Al and tell him to let that be a warning. [Big Grim](/big-grim) will then warn the player not to bring her any more gang leaders because it's a "waste of her time" and that "He didn't do anything against us... Yet."
