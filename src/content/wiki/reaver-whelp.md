**Reaver Whelps** are indoctrinated slaves of the [Reavers](/reavers). They can be found in [Ark](/ark) and in [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Unknown](/unknown).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Shek
