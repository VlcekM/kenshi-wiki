_**This article is a Work in Progress. It may contain out of date or incomplete information.**_

The _vast_ majority of the player's Recruits will fall into these categories. They all have [random names](/random-names-list), along with semi-random backstories. Each kind can also (_often_) be found in multiple varieties, dependent on the area and/or faction who controls the bar the player is recruiting at. The different variations generally only affect dialog and [Race](/races), although in some cases it can affect [Personality](/personality) too.

Names, equipment, and bodies are generated based on a random **set** seed that does not change.

Most recruits are aligned with the faction "_Drifters_", so the factions listed here are typically just locations where these recruits can be found and nothing more.

Bar squads such as the recruits shown on this page will **not** respawn or change over time. If you have emptied a bar, or they got killed, do not expect it to refill at some point. An [import](/main-menu) must be performed for any of them to refill.

### Free Recruits

These recruits can join the player for free. They are either rare finds due to spawn rates or have a chance to join the player after being freed from imprisonment. Some of these recruits retain bandit AI behavior or target designations and may potentially antagonize neutral travelers on the road or get attacked by guards or major faction patrols until the game is saved and reloaded.

-   [Escaped Servant](/escaped-servant)
-   [Escaped Slave](/escaped-slave)

-   [Lost Drone](/lost-drone)

## Specialists

These Recruits have increased stats on a few specific skills. Players can save time on training characters with beginner stats if they spend slightly more money and recruit one of these characters.

### Cook

A recruit that specializes in cooking. They come with a high [Cooking](/cooking) skill.

-   [Cook Recruit](/cook-recruit)
-   [Outlaw Cook](/outlaw-cook)

### Engineer

The main appeal of this recruit is their very high [Engineer](/engineer) skill and decent [Labouring](/labouring) skill. They also have slightly increased [Strength](/strength) and [Athletics](/athletics), other than that they have beginner stats.

-   [Engineer Recruit](/engineer-recruit)
-   [Holy Nation Engineer](/holy-nation-engineer)
-   [Shek Engineer](/shek-engineer)

### Farmer

These recruits have decent [Farming](/farming). Aside from this, they have beginner stats.

-   [Farmer Recruit](/farmer-recruit)
-   [Hiveless Farmer](/hiveless-farmer)

### Gunner

These recruits have high [Crossbows](/crossbows) and [Turrets](/turrets) stats. Aside from this, they likely have beginner stats.

-   [Gunner Recruit](/gunner-recruit)
-   [Hiveless Gunner](/hiveless-gunner)

### Medic

Much like the engineer, this recruit is specialized in certain skills. They come with a high [Field Medic](/field-medic) skill as well as a decent [Science](/science) skill. Other than those they have identical [Stats](/statistics) to the basic Sword for hire.

-   [Holy Nation Medic](/holy-nation-medic)
-   [Medic Recruit](/medic-recruit)
-   [Outlaw Medic](/outlaw-medic)
-   [Shek Medic](/shek-medic)
