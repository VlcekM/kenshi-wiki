Idad was an author that wrote the books [Mass Extinction IV](/mass-extinction-series), [Cultural Groupings And Evolution I](/cultural-groupings-and-evolution-series), [The Mystery Of Skeletech II](/the-mystery-of-skeletech-series), [Legend of the Grievewraith](/legends-series), [Legend of the Headless Ancient I](/legends-series), and [Legend of the Mist Ghoul](/legends-series). Little is known about Idad since Idad doesn't exist as an NPC that players can interact with, however, it's likely that Idad was a [Machinist](/machinists) and that Idad is possibly dead (hence the lack of an NPC).
