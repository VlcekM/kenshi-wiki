**Empire General Shop** is a [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) vendor found in the [United Cities](/united-cities). This shop is a slightly bigger version of the [General Shop](/general-shop). This location is controlled by the Traders Guild, so it will never stock hemp or riceweed.

### Possible Locations

-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Heng](/heng)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

Depending on World States, there may be a Independent Bar found in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
