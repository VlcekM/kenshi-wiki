**Ravaged Hamlets** are the remains of abandoned villages dotted around [Bast (Zone)](/bast-zone). Ostensibly, these small settlements used to belong to the [United Cities](/united-cities) but were destroyed or abandoned after [Holy Nation's](/holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> offensive into the region.

It is likely that they are the remains of the farms which used to populate [Bast](/bast-zone), which fits with its former purpose as a farming region.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

There are in total 9 Ravaged Hamlets scattered around [Bast](/bast-zone).

## Trivia

-   The large number of hamlets in Bast could explain why the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) are able to sustain their population, along with the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants)'s presence in cities and [Vagrant Camps](/vagrant-camp); due to them both being a displaced population.
-   Another closely related building, the [Farm Ruins](/farm-ruins) located in the northernmost tip of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), is similarly a small settlement that was presumably sacked by [United Cities](/united-cities) during their conflict against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), whose offensive has since stalled and been pushed back to [Bast](/bast-zone).
-   It is possible that the destruction of this area caused further reliance on slave labour <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, in addition to [The Red Rebellion](/the-red-rebellion).
-   Since the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) have abandoned this region and made no effort to reclaim the area,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> combined with regional hazards<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> it is possible that this place lost its value after [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s attack. Especially since there are other examples of the [United Cities](/united-cities) abandoning settlements located on their frontier.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
