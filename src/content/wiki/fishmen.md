**Fishmen**, also known as **Gurglers**, are fishlike abominations who live on an island south of [Catun](/catun) called [Fishman Island](/fishman-island). Their leader, [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler), can be found in the south-westernmost corner of the island in the [Island Lab](/island-lab). **Fishmen** control and infest the ruins of the city [Fish Isle](/fish-isle) which is located in the center of the island.

Despite being relatively weak on their own, they should not be underestimated when in large numbers (much akin to [Fogmen](/fogmen)). Just like the Fogmen, they will attempt to carry injured organic characters and bring them back to their nests, where they will be tied to a pole and eaten alive by them.

Gurglers presumably hatch from [Gurgler Eggs](/gurgler-egg) and sleep in Fish Beds. They stop spawning in the Fishman Island once the King Gurgler is killed.

## Trivia

-   In the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set), aside from the racial groups Fishman and Alpha Fishman, there is an unused racial group called King Fishman. Most likely this was intended to be used for the King Gurgler but was discarded in favor of making the character an Alpha Fishman.
-   When fighting Fishmen if you were to severe one of their limbs, it would immediately become invisible. This is because they do not have a limb assigned to drop as an item.
    -   The following mod, **[Fishsticks - Gurgler Limb Mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1752014748)**, adds Gurgler limbs to the game.
