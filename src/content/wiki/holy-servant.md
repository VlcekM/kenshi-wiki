**Holy Servants** are characters in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), often seen acting as the companions of [Paladins](/paladin) and [Priests](/holy-priest).

Instead of working in the mines, they patrol and perform shopkeeping duties; [Holy Bakeries](/holy-bakery) in particular are managed by Holy Servants.

Among the Holy Nation's population, only Greenlander men are given the opportunity to become **Holy Servants** - possibly on the path to eventual [citizenship](/holy-citizen), although many Holy Servants are drafted as [Holy Chosen](/holy-chosen), and die fighting on the frontlines against the [United Cities](/united-cities), or potentially, [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

Although they have more privileges than the Lower Servants, it is possible that a relatively few Holy Servants have [escaped](/escaped-servant) to join the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
