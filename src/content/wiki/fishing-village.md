There are two locations called **Fishing Village** in Kenshi.

There is one Fishing Village North of [World's End](/worlds-end) in [Northern Coast](/northern-coast) which is a quaint town with piers and shacks by the sea side. The town is owned by the [Deadcat](/deadcat) faction. Small herds of [goats](/goat) can usually be found nearby. Residents complain of nearby [Cannibals](/cannibals). [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) can often be found in the nearby area. The town contains a bar as well as a general item trader shop and a fish salesmen.

The other Fishing Village is walled village in [The Outlands](/the-outlands) East of [Black Scratch](/black-scratch). It is also owned by the [Deadcat](/deadcat) faction. This town is threatened by [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates) and [Reavers](/reavers). The only service this town contains is a [bar](/bar) and two fish shops.

[Green Finger](/green-finger), [Hobbs](/hobbs), [Horse](/horse) and [Soman](/soman) could be recruited in a fishing village.

## Town overrides

Both towns will be [taken over](/town-overrides) by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) if [Tinfist](/tinfist) is killed ([Fishing Village, Slave Traders](/fishing-village-slave-traders)).

## Trivia

-   There appears to be an override bug with the The Outlands Fishing Village, as technically it is supposed to belong to the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction according to its FCS data. The mod, **[Fixed Fishing Village](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2348787545)**, resolves this issue.
