A **Sake Still** creates [Sake](/sake) from [Riceweed](/riceweed). It can be constructed after researching [Sake Distillation](/sake-distillation-tech) and requires a worker to operate.

Processing [Riceweed](/riceweed) into [Sake](/sake) trains a party member's [Cooking](/cooking) stat, and as such brewing time is affected by the Cooking multiplier.
