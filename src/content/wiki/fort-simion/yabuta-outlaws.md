**Fort Yabuta** is a [Town Override](/town-overrides) which can occur at [Fort Simion](/fort-simion). Fort Yabuta is controlled by the [Yabuta Outlaws](/yabuta-outlaws). The [Yabuta Chief](/yabuta-chief) can be found here. For this location to be created, players must ensure the following [World States](/world-states):

-   [Yabuta of the Sands](/yabuta-of-the-sands) is not dead or imprisoned
-   Player is not allied with [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)
