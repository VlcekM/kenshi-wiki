_This article is a [stub](/category/article-stubs). You can help the Kenshi Wiki by [expanding it](/fort-simion/rebel-swordsmen)._

[Fort Simion](/fort-simion) descends into [ruins](/town-overrides) controlled by [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) if [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states).

This override may imply that the rebels no longer have the need to stay in their hideout and abandoned it.

[Boss Simion](/boss-simion) will also disappear from this location, unless he relocates to [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) which is only possible if [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Longen](/longen) and [Tinfist](/tinfist) are dead.

Roaming [Rebel Farmer](/rebel-farmers) squads will also cease patrolling around the hideout, though the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) will still continue to patrol the region of [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).
