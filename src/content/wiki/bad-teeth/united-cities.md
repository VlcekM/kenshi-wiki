**Bad Teeth, United Cities** is a city controlled by [United Cities](/united-cities).

It belonged to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) until [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) were kidnapped or killed while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) were alive and free. It could have also belonged to the [Shek Kingdom](/bad-teeth/shek-kingdom) or [Kral's Chosen](/bad-teeth/krals-chosen), a previous override, until High Inquisitor Valtena and Esata the Stone Golem were kidnapped or killed while Emperor Tengu and Longen were alive and free.

This town can switch to the destroyed override if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is killed as well.

The town itself is guarding a mountain pass between the fertile valley of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and hostile wilderness of the [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

[Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) applies here.

## Trivia

-   Once this override is achieved, the status of [Longen](/longen) and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) cease to matter for futher overrides, conversely the status and death of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) will switch this place into destroyed variant. As a result, it could be presumed that [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) still have significant influence or potential influence over this territory.
-   This override is the only one that could directly switch from [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) or [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) to [United Cities](/united-cities) control, even if [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is still alive for the latter case.
