[Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) will fall into ruins under [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) have all been killed or kidnapped.

It appears that this place is (depending on previous overrides) nominally reclaimed by The Holy Nation<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> after Shek Kingdom/Kral's Chosen have been defeated or vacated from this area, or otherwise evacuated and abandoned by the military after the high command governing this settlement is incapacitated.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

All buildings are abandoned and destroyed in this override.
