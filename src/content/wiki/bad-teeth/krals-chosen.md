The [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) will overtake [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) if [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) have been killed/kidnapped, as long as [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is still alive and free.

This town was slaughtered and conquered by the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) after the combined pressure of [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) and [Flying Bull's](/flying-bull) forces overwhelmed The Holy Nation's southern defense after their fracture in the chain of command after Seta was defeated.

Alternatively, the town may have also already been conquered by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) prior and merely changed its ownership to Kral's Chosen after Esata was defeated.

Some of the squads found in this town are still affiliated with Shek Kingdom.

Much like the [Shek Kingdom override](/bad-teeth/shek-kingdom), it appears to be a town where most buildings and original inhabitants are destroyed and massacred in the midst of the conflict, with a few of the buildings rebuilt and Shek's HQ relocated in this area.

This override can switch to [Shek Kingdom](/bad-teeth/shek-kingdom), [United Cities](/bad-teeth/united-cities), or [Destroyed](/bad-teeth/destroyed) overrides.
