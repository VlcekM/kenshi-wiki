The **Old Control Tower** is located in Venge, east of the [Ruined Control Tower](/ruined-control-tower) and the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse). It is the home of the [Scavengers](/scavengers) and their leader [Gutterhead](/gutterhead). The entire place has been ransacked except for some small container, one of which is a secured wooden chest. This chest can contain loot just as an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) as per the [Artifacts](/artifacts) System.
