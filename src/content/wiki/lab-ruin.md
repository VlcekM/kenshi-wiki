**Lab Ruin** is a ruined lab located in [The Crater](/the-crater). This location was likely destroyed and/or looted before the player could get to it. Sometimes they still contain loot though not the best quality. [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) have [mapped](/maps) these locations with great precision.
