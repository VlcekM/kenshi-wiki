The **Stationhouse** is a big structure that requires [Large Building Shells](/large-building-shells-tech) tech and 40 [Building Materials](/building-materials) to build, same as the [Snailhouse](/snailhouse). It provides the biggest amount of usable floor space out of the buildings shells which can be built by the player.

Stationhouses can usually be found in larger cities and commonly functions as HQ, for example for [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

## Defenses

While it may seem that the ramp leading to the entrance of the building could be useful as a defensible choke point, the Stationhouse has the same measly 16 door durability as a [Storm House](/storm-house), severely reducing its use as a bunker. In fact, it is slightly less defensible than the Storm House, because the latter's stairs somewhat restrict the number of enemies who simultaneously can bash at the door, while the Stationhouse provides a large platform for everyone to fit by the door.

If the player is looking for a bunker-style building, [Outpost s-IV](/outpost-s-iv) is the only significant improvement over the regular buildings with its 50 door condition and turret points right over the door ([Outpost s-III](/outpost-s-iii) has 40 door condition but doesn't allow shooting turrets from the inside).

## Comparison to Snailhouse

_Main article: [Snailhouse](/snailhouse)_

Asides from being simply bigger, the Stationhouse is a much more standard and utilitarian building than the Snailhouse, but this has more to do with the limitations of the Snailhouse than Stationhouse having specific advantages.
