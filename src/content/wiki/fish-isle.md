**Fish Isle** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) in [Fishman Island](/fishman-island). Like the rest of the island, it is inhabited by [Fishmen](/fishmen).

It is completely in ruins, but prior to being overrun it contained several [medium sized buildings](/medium-building-shells-tech). The fact that part of the town is surrounded by walls suggests that it was a smaller outpost that was finally found to be worth investing into. It was probably expanding rapidly before being abandoned quite abruptly.

This location spawns a lot of random loot.
