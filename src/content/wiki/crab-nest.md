**Crab Nests** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), [Howler Maze](/howler-maze), and [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone).

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### crab nest

This squad can contain 5 to 30 [Crabs](/crab). They spawn with age multipliers between 0.15x and 0.4x.

## Item Spawns

-   [Crab Egg](/crab-egg)

## Trivia

-   Nests have a habit of (For whatever reason) spawning in the minimum population a decent percent of the time.
