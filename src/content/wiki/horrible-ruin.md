The **Horrible Ruin** is a dilapidated village found in the eastern part of [Cheaters Run](/cheaters-run).

It's labelled as a location that belongs to the [Deadcat](/deadcat) faction despite being inhabited solely by [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).

Initially, when the player sees this location in the distance, the name for it is "Nice Ruin." Upon closer inspection, they realize this is not the case.

## Trivia

-   If you are seen by the inhabitants of this ruin one of the Skin Bandits will begin to speak to you.
    -   After the Skin Bandit finishes its dialogue all nearby Skin Bandits are drawn to your location. They will not be drawn to your location if you are already hostile to Skin Bandits or attack before the dialogue is over. This is the only location where this happens outside of the nearby [Police Barracks](/police-barracks).
-   This location is technically still controlled by Deadcat, meaning that Skin Harvests will never originate from this location.
-   This ruin is the only location that Skin Bandits own where they do not use the AI goal to "kill random prisoner" and will not attempt to put a robotic prisoner who escaped back into a peeler.
    -   In other words, this ruin can be used to lose your limbs "safely" and consistently as a solo robotic character.
