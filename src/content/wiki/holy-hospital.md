**Holy Hospital** is a vendor found in [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) various military settlements. Holy Hospitals sell [Medical Items](/medical-items). By talking to the surgeon, players can open the [Character Customization](/character-customization) screen for their selected character for a small sum of cats.

### Possible Locations

-   [Holy Military Base](/holy-military-base)
-   [Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) (& half-destroyed)
-   [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield)

### Residents

-   [Surgeon](/surgeon)
-   [Doctor](/doctor) (4)

  
[Trade culture](/traders#trade-cultures) can prevent some items from spawning in this list.
