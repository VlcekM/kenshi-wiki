**Flat Shelters** are [camping](/camping) [buildings](/buildings-list) which the player can construct anywhere after learning the blueprint. They require very few materials in order to construct and do not result in the creation of a [player town](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). Camps can be used as a quick shelter from acid rain. If you so wish, this tent can fit inside most [buildings](/buildings-list) and also be constructed on top of them.

The blueprint can be purchased from any of these locations:

-   [Adventurer Bar](/adventurer-bar)
-   [Flats Lagoon Bar](/flats-lagoon-bar)
-   [Shark Travel Shop](/shark-travel-shop)
-   [Tech Hunter Shop](/tech-hunter-shop)
-   [Travel and Repairs Shop](/travel-and-repairs-shop)
-   [Travel Gear](/travel-gear)
