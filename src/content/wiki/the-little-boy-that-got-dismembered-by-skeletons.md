**The Little Boy That Got Dismembered By Skeletons** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item can be found in many Holy Nation vendors.

From this item, players learn how deeply entrenched the hatred of [skeletons](/skeleton) is in The Holy Nation.

Due to there being a lack of children in Kenshi, this book does give some insight as to the way that children are indoctrinated in The Holy Nation.
