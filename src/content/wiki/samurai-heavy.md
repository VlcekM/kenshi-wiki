**Samurai Heavies** are heavy foot-soldiers of the [United Cities](/united-cities). They wield the larger Nodachi compared to their non-heavy counterparts and have a chance to spawn with the better chain shirt. They are also the lowest-ranked patrolling units to have a [Catun](/catun-scrapmaster-manufacturer)\-grade weapon.

There are in total 2 **Samurai Heavies** that guard [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
