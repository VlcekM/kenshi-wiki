**Copper Alloy Bench** is a [building](/category/buildings) which converts [Copper](/copper) and [Iron Plates](/iron-plates) into [Copper Alloy Plates](/copper-alloy-plates). It can be constructed after researching [Copper Alloys](/copper-alloys-tech). This building uses and trains the [Engineer](/engineer) stat.
