**Shun** is an abandoned region at the southwestern corner of Kenshi. There are many ruins and lost ancient locations. Aside from scavenging, there is not much to do in Shun.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in Shun. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

### Ancient Locations

_These are places which do not have competent inhabitants. Ancient Locations are usually lost outposts or ruins. Beware: sometimes these locations contain territorial protectors such as [Security Spiders](/security-spider)._

-   [Ancient Tech Labs](/ancient-tech-lab)
-   [Armoury Ruin](/armoury-ruin)
-   [Library Ruins](/library-ruins)
-   [Lost Armoury](/lost-armoury)
-   [Lost Library](/lost-library) x6

## Tips

-   Unlike in most of the other regions where they can be found, Tech Hunters may start unprovoked fights with the player.
-   Roaming Garrus might be baited to attacking enemies garrisoning the Lost Armoury and the coastal Lost Library, distracting them enough to loot both places.
-   This region is a good place to find some of the early research artifacts. Looting all of the six Lost Libraries can net around 8 [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) and 25 [Books](/book). Other paper loot includes around 75 [Parchments](/parchment), 40 [Old Maps](/old-map) and 500-600 Damaged books that can be sold for over c.23,000.
