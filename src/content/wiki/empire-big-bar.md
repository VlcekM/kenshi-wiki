**Empire Big Bar** is a vendor found in [United Cities](/united-cities) settlements. Renting a bed in this bar costs c.150.

This bar is larger than [Empire Bar](/empire-bar) and [Small Empire Bar](/small-empire-bar), making it the largest variant of Empire Bars.

### Possible Locations

-   [Heft](/heft)
-   [Heng](/heng)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last) half destroyed
-   Heng half destroyed
-   Sho-Battai half destroyed
-   Sho-Battai (Empire Peasant)
-   [Stoat](/stoat) half destroyed

-   [Barman](/barman)
