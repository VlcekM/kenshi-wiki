**Luxury Goods** are a Trade Good that can be bought and sold to make a profit. They are sold by [Trade Goods Traders](/trade-goods-trader) at the 'Trade' building in towns.

These are commonly found in [Noble Houses](/noble-house), where they can be stolen to earn a minor profit if strapped for cash. They look like a casket (with expensive items probably inside).
