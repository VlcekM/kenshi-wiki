The **Manhunter Base** is an outpost controlled by the [Manhunters](/manhunters). It acts as their headquarters and only controlled location.

Its proximity to [Slave Markets](/slave-markets) and [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) suggests that it responsible for suppling both locations with labor, and preventing any would-be [escapees](/escaped-slave) from succeeding.

This location exclusively contains [Manhunters](/manhunter), and has no unique characters.

The **Manhunter Base, Mercenary Guild** is an outpost controlled by the [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild). It belonged to [Manhunters](/manhunters) until [Slave Market Master](/slave-market-master) and [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) were both killed.

Although the [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture) disappeared from this location when the override occurred it still retains it, meaning you can sell stuff like Hashish at for the increased price.
