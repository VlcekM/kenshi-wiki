**Soldierbot Guards** are members of the [Skeletons](/skeletons) faction. They can be found guarding the [Scraphouse](/scraphouse). As this character does not have a dialogue package, they will not react to witnessing a theft.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
