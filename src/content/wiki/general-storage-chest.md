The **General Storage Chest** is a container for storing any item. Players can construct this building after researching [Item Storage](/item-storage-tech).

##### NOTE: Characters will pick up food from the container when they are hungry but wont leave the chest afterwards. \[V 0.93.5\]

### Details

This chest has a storage space of 18 x 18 squares and a stacking multiplier of 5.
