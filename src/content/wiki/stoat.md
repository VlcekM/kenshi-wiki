**Stoat** is a [United Cities](/united-cities) town located in the south-west of [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) region. The economy of this town relies on the supplies from both the [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) and the [Slave Farm](/slave-farm). It is the last major town when heading east through the desert. It can be discovered by travelling or by acquiring and reading the [**Map**](/maps) **of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

At one of the corner of the town houses wind generators and a bunch of camp beds that are most likely slums for the homeless to sleep constructed by the government or [peasants](/empire-peasants) themselves.

[Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) governs from the Noble House in this town.

The [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) pacifier can be found in Stoat.

The town contains four [gates](/defensive-gates) and thus four sets of powerful [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard), so beware.
