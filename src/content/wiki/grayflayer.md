**Grayflayers** are members of the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers). They can be found in the [Grayflayers Village](/swamp-village) and in [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar).

They have an Honorable [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive Prince
