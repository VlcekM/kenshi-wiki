**Small Time** was a small HN town found on the cliffside of a valley in the mountains north of [Blister Hill](/blister-hill). The town had little in terms of notable residents, only possessing a couple bars, a trade shop, and a few small shacks.

Its most notable detail is that it and the lands around it were overhauled and transformed into the slave camp of [Rebirth](/rebirth) shortly before the 0.92.0 update.
