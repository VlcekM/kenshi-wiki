**Iron Valleys** is an area located north of the [Deadlands](/deadlands). Much like the [Deadlands](/deadlands), Iron Valleys acts like an environmental barrier because of constant Acid Rain plaguing the area. The area is made harder to traverse because of the rocky valleys it's named for, but there are some bridges connecting the top portions making it a little easier. The area is also patrolled by [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in the Iron Valleys. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

The water here is acidic so [Human](/human), [Shek](/shek), and most animal characters will be harmed if they attempt to swim. The ground here is 50% acidic, meaning that characters which are not immune to acid and are not wearing shoes will be hurt, but it will not be as severe as in other locations with acidic ground.
