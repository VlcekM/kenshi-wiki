**Pacifiers** are characters found in various locations that can repair [relations](/guide-to-faction-relations) with their parent [faction](/factions) if the player has gotten on their bad side, for a price. They have a variable fee, demanding more for their services the worse the player's relations are, ranging from c.15,000 to c.100,000. Some particularly bad instances may require multiple payments, as they have a limit to how much they can fix at a time.

There are also sometimes restrictions in place that will prevent the player from fixing their relations with certain factions. These generally involve things like being allied with their enemies, or the [killing/imprisonment](/world-states) of important characters from their faction. The Pacifier will explain why he is refusing to provide service to the player if he does though, providing a clue as to what might be done to rectify the situation.

## Locations

The list of the various pacifiers and their locations. If a chance is not specified at a location, it is a guaranteed spawn.

-   [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) - [Mud Town](/mud-town)
-   [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) - [Squin](/squin)
-   [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) - [Shark](/shark), [Waystations](/waystation) (25% chance)
-   [Hounds](/hounds) - [The Hub](/the-hub)
-   [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) - [World's End](/worlds-end), [Waystations](/waystation) (25% chance)
-   [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) - [Stoat](/stoat)
-   [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) - [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Waystations](/waystation) (25% chance)
-   [United Cities](/united-cities) - [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Waystations](/waystation) (25% chance)

## Restrictions

These are the various restrictions that will prevent the use of the respective pacifier's services.

### [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)

-   Be allied with the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild)
-   Be allied with the [United Cities](/united-cities)
-   Be allied with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)
-   Kill or imprison [Big Bo](/bo)
-   Kill or imprison [Jaegar](/jaegar)
-   Kill or imprison [Old Grey](/grey)
-   Kill or imprison [Tinfist](/tinfist)

### [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas)

-   Be allied with the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation)
-   Kill or imprison [Moll](/moll)

### [Holy Nation](/holy-nation)

-   Be allied with the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas)
-   Kill or imprison [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta)
-   Kill or imprison [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena)
-   Kill or imprison [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

-   Kill or imprison [Bayan](/bayan)
-   Kill or imprison [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem)
-   Kill or imprison [Seto](/seto)
