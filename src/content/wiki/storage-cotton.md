**Storage: Cotton** is a [storage](/storage) container which exclusively contains [Cotton](/cotton). Players can construct this building after researching [Cotton Farming](/cotton-farming-tech).

This container can hold a total of 100 [Cotton](/cotton).

<table class="wikitable"><tbody><tr><td><b>Note: Auto-Haul <a href="/jobs">Jobs</a></b><br>&nbsp;The characters will auto-haul into the storage when they assigned to care for a farm. Make sure when you set multiple tasks for a character, hauling into the storage is the last thing on their list, or at least hauling into storage is after they put the material into machines. Otherwise, they will go back-and-forth between the two buildings.</td></tr></tbody></table>
