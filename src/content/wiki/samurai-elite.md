**Samurai Elites** are fearsome soldiers serving in the [United Cities](/united-cities) armies. These Samurai can exclusively be found guarding [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) in the Faction Throne in [Heft](/heft) (and notably, exclusively [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander)). They have extremely high combat stats, making them deadlier than even the [Elite Hunters](/elite-hunter).

There are in total 5 of them guarding **Emperor Tengu** under default [Heft](/heft), or with only 3 of them if Heft is malnourished.

They wear \[High\] armour (10% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths) (75%) or [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers) (25%).

## Trivia

-   Samurai Elites have some of the highest combat stats in Kenshi. Their stats equal that of Esata (Without the racial multipliers) the Stone Golem as well as the Holy Lord Phoenix.
-   If enough Samurai Elites are killed and Tengu is knocked unconscious (and gets back up still in his throne room) he can spawn new Samurai Elites in.
