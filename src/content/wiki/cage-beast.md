Cage Beasts are animals which exist in the Kenshi game files. They can be added to Kenshi through [Modding](/category/modding).

They appear somewhat similar to [Garrus](/garru) with [Garru Backpack](/garru-backpack), [Large Backpack](/large-backpack) and [Prisoner Cages](/prisoner-cage) attached unto them.
