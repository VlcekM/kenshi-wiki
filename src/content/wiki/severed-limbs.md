**Severed Limbs** are items which are created when a character loses a limb. Characters must be of a [Human](/human), [Shek](/shek), [Hive](/hive), or [Cannibal](/cannibal)/[Cannibal Skav](/cannibal-skav) race in order for a limb item to be dropped for the corresponding limb. Severed limbs may be grabbed by [Bonedogs](/bonedog) or [Boneyard Wolves](/boneyard-wolf) who will eventually eat them. [Animals](/fauna) never lose limbs.

Characters with lost limbs can replace said limbs with [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs).
