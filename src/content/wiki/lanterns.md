**Lanterns** can be bought in various shops, most commonly in the [Western Hive](/western-hive) territory. Lanterns serve as a light source when it's dark and are equipped on the Belt slot.

Occasionally, while passing a [Hive](/hive) merchant they will chase after you and pressure you into purchasing a lantern. Merchants will highly recommend that you buy a lantern before entering the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands).

Lanterns are very useful in crafting, labouring or operating crossbows for reducing the penalties associated with lack of light during the night.
