This [Waystation](/waystation) is a small [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) outpost in the easternmost edge of the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). Its residents include [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild) members and [Drifters](/drifters).

This location is a good place to rest and hide after earning a small [bounty](/bounty) from the nearby [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

As with all other non-ruined Waystations, this place has a 25% chance to spawn pacifiers from [United Cities](/united-cities), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

Just north of the gate there is a campfire with 3 [Dried Meat](/dried-meat). This appears to be a left-over from development of the game and is a mini-easter egg.
