**Nests** are locations that can spawn in the wilds of Kenshi. They are home to a variety of the world's [Fauna](/fauna) (with the exception of [Fogmen](/fogmen) and [Fishmen](/fishmen)), such as [Bonedogs](/bonedog), [River Raptors](/river-raptor) & [Beak Things](/beak-thing) among others. Nests are recognizable on the map by a paw print mark. Nests have many bones and clutter. They can occasionally have loot such as [Animal Skins](/animal-skin), [Weapons](/weapons), or [Beak Thing Eggs](/beak-thing-egg) (found in [Beak Thing](/beak-thing) nests). It is possible to wipe out the population of a nest. When this happens, a notification will appear and the nest will de-spawn after a certain time, before spawning new nests in replacement.
