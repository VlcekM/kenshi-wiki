42068-rebirth.mod **Mongrel HQ** is the headquarters of the [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) faction. [Arc](/arc), the leader of Mongrel, resides here.

It houses three [Undertakers](/undertaker) and is heavily guarded by the [Shinobi Guards](/shinobi-guard).

Plenty of mid quality Shinobi gear can be found here.
