The Sonorous Junk Reef is an area of shallow water south of the [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark) and west of the [Ashlands](/ashlands) filled with twisted scrap and inactive [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider). There are no homeless spawns or hostiles as it is outside the usual zones of the world in a similar manner to the [None Coast](/none-coast).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in Sonorous Junk Reef. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
