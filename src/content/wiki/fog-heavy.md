**Fog Heavies** are members the [Fogmen](/fogmen) faction. They are the soldier variant of the [Hive Soldier Drone](/hive-soldier-drone) for the Deadhive. Fog Heavies are often found escorting a [Fog Prince](/fog-prince) in groups of 3 to 5.

Fog Heavies are corrupted Hive Soldier Drones. They lurk in the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) in groups and are hostile towards anyone they come across. Like all Fogmen, they will kidnap nearby unconscious characters except skeletons.

Fog Heavies can also be found in the [Tower of Fog](/tower-of-fog) and [Deadhive Overruns](/deadhive-overrun).

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
