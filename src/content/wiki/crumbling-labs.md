The **Crumbling Labs** is an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) which is located in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) to the northeast of [Spring](/spring). This location is surrounded by acid water, so you have to swim through acid for a bit to get there. The lab consists of two buildings, one is called "Nest" and contains the [King Gorillo](/king-gorillo). The other one contains [Security Spiders](/security-spider).
