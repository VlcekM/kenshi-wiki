**Kral's Chosen** are a large faction of [Sheks](/shek) with close ties to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) comprised of loyalists to [Flying Bull](/flying-bull). Based mostly within [New Kralia](/new-kralia), they often randomly harrass and assault outsiders in the wilderness. They are led by [Ida the Flying Bull](/flying-bull), who was a former [Invincible](/the-five-invincibles) to Shager until the latter's [overthrow](/shagers-overthrow). Their leader has large popularity, and they have significant amount of members<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They are devout followers of [Kral](/way-of-the-warrior-the-book-of-kral) and they do not like weaker races, an outsider must earn their respect by proving their strength in combat. They will often harass and assault wanderers that are passing by. If a player settles near their territory, they will launch a campaign of [Shek Proving](/shek-proving) which the player must defeat.

They worship the [Shek](/shek) hero Kral, a legendary warrior rumored to have faced 100 swordsmen in an ambush during his final battle and was stabbed in the back. After the first hit he kept fighting and was dealt 100 cuts before finally going down. The book [Kral's Last Stand](/krals-last-stand) tells of this tale.

-   [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem)
-   [Seto](/seto)
-   [Bayan](/bayan)

## Relations

###  Faction Relations

_This faction has no special relations towards any other faction. For all factions, it will use this faction's default (-100)._

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Harming [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) will seriously damage your relations with this faction.
-   The player could potentially turn in bounties into towns that are under **Kral's Chosen** rule after certain World States are achieved and get positive relations.

## Trivia

-   The town overrides of Kral's Chosen have some weird instances.
    -   When Kral's Chosen take over towns, they uncharacteristically do not attack outsiders unprovoked unlike the roaming squads.
        -   They will nonetheless still attack you if you are found defending yourself against the assaults of their roaming squads.
    -   [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) in their towns are incapable of assigning bounties when seeing crime.
    -   The town guards of Kral's Chosen wouldn't mind if their leader [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) being carried by you and could even trigger the same dialogue when he is imprisoned/killed.
    -   Because the Kral's Chosen have a different [trade culture to the Shek](/shek-kingdom-trade-culture), they would actually have [Hashish](/hashish) being sold in their [General Shops](/general-shop). But their gate guards will still check your bags for contrabands nonetheless, even if there is technically no possible illegal substances under the Kral's Chosen.
-   Kral's Chosen are occasionally referred to as "Skull Collectors" in their dialogue within the FCS, this might be removed content that wasn't completely removed, causing some instances where mentions of Skull Collectors still pop out (like [Green's recruiting dialogue](/green)).
-   When compared to the homeless spawns of Shek Kingdom where they overlap, Shek Kingdom is 0.75x ([Border Zone](/border-zone)), 1.17x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 1.31x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf)), 1.31x ([Spider Plains](/spider-plains)), 3.5x ([Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)) as much as Kral's Chosen in individuals, suggesting Kral's Chosen are a significant minority inside the Shek society.
    -   Accounting for Seto's world state as well, Shek Kingdom is around 1.5x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 2.25x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf)) as much in individuals.
    -   If also accounting for [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) too (even though it's mutually exclusive with the above), Shek Kingdom is around 2x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 3.2x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf)) as much.
    -   Kral's Chosen additionally also patrol in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Skimsands](/skimsands).
    -   This can be contrasted with [Berserkers](/berserkers) or [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) which are outnumbered by Kral's Chosen in all zones that they overlap, suggesting they are smaller groups.
