[Bark](/bark) is taken over by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) if either:

-   [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Sanda](/lady-sanda) are both killed.
-   Sanda, [Wada](/slave-master-wada) and [Kana](/lady-kana) are killed (and Bark _isn't_ [half-destroyed](/bark/half-destroyed))

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

[Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) squads, and those inside the barracks and [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) are malnourished in this area.

In [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) there is two kinds of prosperous Bark, however it's the same Bark only the conditions to reach the world states are different. However, there is still a slight difference since more [Generic Recruits](/generic-recruits) could be found if the override occurs with the Tengu's death.

[Izumi](/izumi) and [Jewel](/jewel) still hang in bars and [Hobbs](/hobbs) may hang there too.

Empire Peasants run some shops along [Free Traders](/free-traders) and [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) protect the town. [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) are settled in the noble house if [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive and free.

[Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) still applies here since Empire Peasants and United Cities share the same trade culture.
