[Bark](/bark) can become malnourished if [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda) is alive, but [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) and [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) are killed or imprisoned.

The town is plagued by rampant famine and poverty after all the slave camps of [Port North](/port-north) and [Port South](/port-south) supplying this town have been destroyed and supply lines cut off, as a result the ensuing chaos has destroyed and killed a part of the town and its inhabitants.
