[Bark](/bark) becomes half-destroyed if [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda) is killed or imprisoned.

Of all 21 buildings inside the town:

-   10 are destroyed
-   8 are Residencies
-   2 are Slave Shops
-   1 is a Barrack (notably, the previous [Noble House](/noble-house)).
