**Southern** **Hive** is a settlement of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) where the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south), her [Elite Droneguards](/elite-droneguard), and the unique enemy [King](/king) can be found. Its found south in the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley).

## Buildings

-   Hive Throne
-   Hive Commune Hut x5

## Town Overrides

If **Queen of the South** is killed/imprisoned, **Southern Hive** will become [Deadhive Overrun](/deadhive-overrun).
