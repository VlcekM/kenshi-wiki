A **Grog Machine** converts [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) and [Water](/water) into [Grog](/grog). It can be constructed after researching [Grog Distillation](/grog-distillation-tech) at Tech Level 2. It requires a worker to operate.

It uses 20 [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) and 1 [Water](/water) per unit of [Grog](/grog). Using the Grog Machine trains the [Cooking](/cooking) stat and is affected by the cooking time reduction multiplier that a higher Cooking level brings.

Be aware, for most settlements assigning your chef to make Grog as an automatic job will swiftly drain your supplies of Wheatstraw.
