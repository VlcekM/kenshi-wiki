The **Preacher Cult** is the faction of [The Preacher](/the-preacher) and its followers living in [Cult Village](/cult-village) in the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast). They appear to be worshipping the "Moons", believing they will give them a good harvest by making blood rain on their farmlands. They also exclusively use [Moon cleavers](/moon-cleaver).

Preacher Cultists attack all outsiders on sight and will tie their prisoners to metal poles. They will randomly decide to hack one of their prisoners to death, before disposing of any bodies in a [Corpse Furnace](/corpse-furnace). Members of this faction do not carry medical supplies, so many of them will slowly become crippled or die after engaging in a fight.

###  Faction Relations

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Healing [The Preacher](/the-preacher) with at least one [Moon Guardian](/moon-guardian) or [Farmer](/farmer-preacher-cult) to witness can result in up to +95 relations.
-   If you attack [The Preacher](/the-preacher) this can result in -200 relations.
-   If you have captured and/or killed [The Preacher](/the-preacher), this act can result in a relatively low -25 relations.
-   If you have placed [The Preacher](/the-preacher) into a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine) this will also result in -25 relations. Additionally, death by means of a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine) will not result in any further negative relations.
