**Rebel Base** is a one-house-bar town. Despite technically being a [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) settlement, the only occupants are [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas), the same people who operate the Bar in [The Hub](/the-hub) close to it. Guards frequently engage the various bandits that roam the area.

The vendor which spawns here is a [Black Market Bar](/black-market-bar) vendor who sells a wide variety of goods, notably crossbows, weapon blueprints, building materials, and food (including [Greenfruit](/greenfruit)). It's also noteworthy for accepting stolen goods from any faction that isn't their own.

There are two [copper](/copper) nodes next to the site, which makes it a promising location for an early mining career.

## Tips

-   Although the Trade Ninjas are the sole occupants of Rebel Base, the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) are listed in the FCS as the ones actually owning the settlement.
-   Take care when kiting enemies here to be taken care of by the [Ninja Guards](/ninja-guard). For one the guards will side with law enforcement like Holy Nation paladins against the player, and secondly, although the guards are capable of defeating wandering patrols of [Starving](/starving-bandits) or [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), bringing larger or multiple groups, or alternatively trying to get them to take on [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas) from an outpost raid will often result in the group losing and the barman dying without player aid.
