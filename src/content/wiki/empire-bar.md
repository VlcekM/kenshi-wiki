**Empire Bar** is a vendor found in [United Cities](/united-cities) settlements. Using beds at this vendor costs c.150.

This bar is larger than the [Small Empire Bar](/small-empire-bar) and smaller than the [Empire Big Bar](/empire-big-bar), making it the intermediate version of bars in the series of Empire Bars.

### Possible Locations

-   [Bark](/bark)
-   [Brink](/brink)
-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)
-   [Drin](/drin)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   Bark (Empire Peasant)

### Residents

-   [Barman](/barman)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (2 to 3)
