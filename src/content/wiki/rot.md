**Rot** is village run by the [Hounds](/hounds) faction within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). It becomes allied to the player once you join the [Hounds](/hounds) by talking to [Ears](/ears) and [Big Grim](/big-grim). It has no notable characters, except for a [Fish and Drugs Seller](/fish-and-drugs-seller). The village can be discovered by trudging through the swamp or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Swamps**.

The [Swamper Gate Guards](/swamper-gate-guard) here are owned by the Hounds.
