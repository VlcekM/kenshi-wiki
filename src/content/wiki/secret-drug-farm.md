**Secret Drug Farm** is owned by the [Blackshifters](/blackshifters) and located in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). It contains an abundant amount of [hashish](/hashish) and [hemp](/hemp), resources which can be sold for massive profits at the nearby city [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon).

Five of these houses are locked, while the sixth house is destroyed. It also houses multiple [Security Spiders II](/security-spider-ii) which will attack you on sight.

## Buildings

-   5 [Storm Houses](/storm-house)
-   1 destroyed [Storm House](/storm-house)
