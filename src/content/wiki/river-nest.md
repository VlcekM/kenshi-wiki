**River Nests** are randomly spawning animal [nests](/nest). They can spawn in the [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride).

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

This squad can contain 3 to 12 [River Raptors](/river-raptor). They spawn with age multipliers between 0.5x and 0.7x.

## Trivia

-   Despite River Nests being part of the [Wildlife](/wildlife) faction, all River Raptors spawned by it are part of the [Raptors](/raptors) faction.
