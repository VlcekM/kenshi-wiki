A medium weight [backpack](/backpacks) that is unable to stack [Trade Goods](/trade-goods) and has an adequate amount of space with 10x14 tiles but hinders you more in combat than a [Small Backpack](/small-backpack). It has an inventory size of 5x4.

Can be bought commonly from [Item Traders](/item-trader) for c.1,500.
