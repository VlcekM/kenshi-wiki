_This article is about a character. For Soldierbot subrace, see [Soldierbot](/soldierbot)._

**Soldierbots** are members of the [Ancient Ruins](/ancient-ruins) faction. They can be found in [Dead Workshops](/dead-workshop). Although called a "Soldierbot", these characters are [Screamer MkI](/screamer-mki) Skeletons.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
