**Hydroponics** are [farming](/farming) buildings which can be constructed after [farming tech](/farming-tech) research in tech level 5. Initial research into [Hydroponics](/hydroponics-tech) concepts costs an [AI Core](/ai-core) and research into crop-specific hydroponics require 4 [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) each.

These buildings do not rely on [environment](/environment) for crop yields, allowing them to be constructed in all [zones](/category/zones) as well as indoors. Unlike [Rain Collectors](/rain-collector) plants can be watered by acid rain meaning that even in normally "DEAD" zones you can still grow crops on the top of your buildings. Hydroponic farms can grow the following crops: [Greenfruit](/greenfruit), [Hemp](/hemp), [Riceweed](/riceweed), and [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw).

-   [Hydroponic Hemp](/hydroponic-hemp)
-   [Hydroponic Riceweed](/hydroponic-riceweed)
-   [Hydroponic Vegetables](/hydroponic-vegetables)
-   [Hydroponic Wheatstraw](/hydroponic-wheatstraw)

Hydroponic farms require [Water](/water) and [Power](/power) to grow. If there is not enough power, farms can pause growth or grow at a decreased rate.

After completing all the research, characters can build Hydroponics with 4 [Iron Plates](/iron-plates), 2 [Electrical Components](/electrical-components), and 10 of their related crop.
