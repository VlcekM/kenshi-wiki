_**This article is a Work in Progress. It may contain out of date or incomplete information.**_

**The Hook** is part of the south coast. It is controlled mainly by the United Cities, Traders Guild and Slave Traders, it is one of the two main Southern regions of UC, with the other one being [Bonefields](/bonefields). Many of the patrolling squads resemble the Northern UC.

## Inhabitants

This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest).
