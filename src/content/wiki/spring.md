**Spring** is the base of the [Anti Slavers](/anti-slavers). It is located on the top of a mountain in the south-west part of [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). Inside the Faction HQ, the legendary [Tinfist](/tinfist) can be found. The base features a mix of [Storm Houses](/storm-house), [Metal Wallhouses](/metal-wallhouse) and Swamp houses in its architecture.

Having characters wearing armor from enemy factions may trigger a hostile response, as well as being allied with their enemies.

Having highly positive faction reputation with some factions (e.g United Cities) can cause guards in the city to attack the player.
