The **Spider Plains** are a large hilly field filled mostly with nothing but [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider), which shares a similar theme with the neighboring [Arach](/arach).

This place appears to have been once the stronghold as part of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) territory<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but their hold over this region has since been significantly diminished.

This place also has (to a lesser degree) several Shek bandits such as [Berserkers](/berserkers), [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) and [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen).

### Tips

-   The main enemy, [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider), are slow, and any character who is not encumbered can easily outrun them.
-   A weapon with special bonuses on animals and/or spiders, such as the [Desert Saber](/desert-sabre) or a [Polearm](/polearms) can be extremely useful for dealing with the Spiders.
-   Skin Spiders eat KOed organic characters, do not hesitate to run away if you believe your characters are at risk.
-   Skin Spiders are vulnerable to being kited (hitting an enemy and running away to avoid retaliation), use a long reach or [Ranged](/crossbows) weapon to capitalize on this weakness.
    -   Ranged weapons have EXTREMELY low accuracy against Skin/Blood Spiders so only use a Crossbow if you have a lot of extra ammunition.

## Trivia

-   Being a zone that directly neighbors [Arach](/arach), it is likely that several of the Skin Spiders in this region might have migrated from that region.
