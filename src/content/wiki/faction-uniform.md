A **Faction Uniform** is specific [Equipment](/equipment) which members of certain [Factions](/factions) wear. Not every faction has a uniform.

An [armour](/armour) is considered a Faction Uniform if the clothing is _tagge_d as being a uniform of a set faction. Player Characters wearing Faction Uniforms will be considered "[in Disguise](/disguise)" and may be arrested by police for [Uniform Theft](/bounty).

Your characters may be allowed to wear a Faction Uniform if you are allied with that particular faction.

Faction Uniforms have a 70% price reduction - for example, a regular [Crab Helmet's](/crab-helmet) base price is c.3,956, but as a faction uniform it is worth only c.1,186.
