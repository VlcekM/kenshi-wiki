The **Trade Ninjas** are a small faction which owns bars in [The Hub](/the-hub), [Mourn](/mourn) and the [Rebel Base](/rebel-base).

They act as the guards of each location, often attacking any [Dust Bandit](/dust-bandits), [Starving Bandit](/starving-bandits) or [Bonedog](/bonedog) that wanders near their property, as well as anybody who causes a disturbance in their bars.

One can consider them as a faction label for shady blackmarkets of sorts.

## Trivia

-   Although the Trade Ninjas are the sole occupants of Rebel Base, the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) are listed in the FCS as the ones actually owning the settlement.
-   The Trade Ninjas could potentially be interpreted as the "shadowy figures" mentioned in [Ludin's Reports](/ludins-reports), who dissuaded the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) from reoccupying the Hub. Despite using violent means to scare the Holy Nation away, they aren't hostile to (or enemies of) the Holy Nation in gameplay.
