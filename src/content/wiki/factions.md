In [Kenshi](/kenshi), there are several different factions you can join and fight against. Some factions will be hostile to any person they see by default, such as: Cannibals, Berserkers and Swamp Ninjas.

You can potentially achieve perks and various bonuses by getting in the good graces of certain factions (such as handing in [common bandit bounties](/bounty)).

While many factions have rivals, it should be noted that an alliance with one signals cooperation with the faction in question, not hostility towards their rivals. Unless your alliance involves actions or pledges<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that explicitly antagonizes said faction's rivals<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, your relationship with said faction's rivals remains unchanged/neutral<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as support for one group doesn’t inherently translate to aggression towards the rest, although your relations would likely sour as a result<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

A faction would be considered "Allied" if their relation towards you is +50 and above, and "Enemy" if -30 and below.

By attacking a faction, you will damage your relations with them and may eventually become an enemy. Some factions have [special methods to repair relations](/guide-to-faction-relations) (such as [Pacifiers](/pacifiers)), but most do not. So, pick your battles wisely.

The player's faction. All your characters belong to it.

## Major Factions

The main factions of the world, they tend to have several cities and outposts across the world and will likely be the primary factions you interact with during gameplay.

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

This is a very populated [Human](/human) [Greenlander](/greenlander) faction made up of religious and xenophobic fanatics who worship a deity named [Okran](/okran). They treat [Shek](/shek) and [Hivers](/hive) as corrupted and subhuman, enforce women's submission to men, have a great fear of [Skeletons](/skeleton) or those with robotic limbs and will attack them on the spot. Their primary rebel faction is the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) which is made up of a few defected women and escaped slaves. They are also at conflict with the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [United Cities](/united-cities).

### [United Cities](/united-cities)

Otherwise known as the Empire, is the largest faction by land area in Kenshi. The Empire is ruled by a cruel cabal of nobles, chaired by an [Emperor](/emperor-tengu). The United Cities are closely allied with many factions such as [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunters), [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), and the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). The Empire values wealth above all else. They are opposed by the destitutes of their region, the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers). Its territory is populated by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). They are also at conflict with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

### [Western Hive](/western-hive)

This is a faction of the insect-like [Hive](/hive) race. The Western Hive proper is located on an island off the western coast, but a majority of the Hive lives on the continent, in series of [Hive Villages](/hive-village) stretched along the river in [Vain](/vain), as well as the neighboring region [Dreg](/dreg). A few other, however, live rather isolated from their main civilization at [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).

[Lost Drones](/lost-drone) and much of the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) originated from this faction.

## Moderate Factions

These factions hold considerably less influence and population than the main 3 factions but are big enough to not be considered minor.

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

They are a supremacist and aggressive warrior culture made up solely of [Shek](/shek) with very low population, mostly residing within [Admag](/admag). Their kingdom is at the brink of complete destruction after a long war with The Holy Nation (and United Cities), but their [new leader](/esata-the-stone-golem) is trying to prevent their own extinction by preventing them from all going [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) (on the promise that they could eventually destroy [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)). They are surrounded by many rogue Shek factions such as the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), [Berserkers](/berserkers) and [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones).

### [Traders Guild](/traders-guild)

An entrenched organization that makes up the economical backbone of [United Cities](/united-cities), residing in their Capital of [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) (& [Heng](/heng)). They engage in various trading and supply activities that feeds the vast majority of townsfolk. They are the managers of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), who also have close ties with the likes of [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) that engage in the practice of [slavery](/slavery) to capture the various individuals (mostly the [extreme](/rebel-farmers) [destitutes](/starving-vagrant)) to keep the industrial cogs running.

### [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)

Composed of the destitute farmers and peasants of the [United Cities](/united-cities) who are evicted and have their farms confiscated for being unable to pay taxes. They engage in widespread banditry across much of the Empire's territory.

### [Swampers](/swampers)

Denizens of [The Swamp](/the-swamp) composed of various gangs and criminals with settlements spread out across the area, mostly concentrated in their 'capital' [Shark](/shark). They are run by the top dog criminal syndicates of [Hounds](/hounds), with [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), [Grayflayers](/grayflayers), [Stone Rats](/stone-rats), [Twinblades](/twinblades) below them making up most of the organization inside the swamp.

### [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)

A popular splinter group from the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) led by [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) that broke off since [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) came to power. They hold a significant amount of [Shek](/shek) population, rivalling that of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

## Minor Factions

The minor players in the world. Usually only have a couple of outposts or cities at most and have very low population. There are quite a few across the world, so you'll likely meet several of them as you explore, though you'd be forgiven for not noticing some of them.

### Machinists and Tech Hunters

Based out of [World's End](/worlds-end), the [Machinists](/machinists) work to determine the history of Kenshi and theorize about different factions. Machinists take their findings and distill some information into [Books](/books) which are distributed across the world. [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) venture out into the wilderness in search of [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs) and Technologies. Tech Hunters have built Waystations to serve as bases for them to rest between journeys. In addition to [World's End](/worlds-end), they control several [Major Towns](/major-towns) in the southeastern part of the continent.

### Border Zone factions

The [Border Zone](/border-zone) is the region between the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) territories. Aside from those factions, the Border Zone is home to three minor factions. [The Hub](/the-hub), a city previously under Holy Nation control, is now owned by [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws). At The Hub and the nearby [Rebel Base](/rebel-base), [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas) can be found. The majority of the Border Zone is terrorized by [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) and [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits). The Border Zone is also home to the [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas) and a [Waystation](/waystation-border-zone).

### Flotsam Ninjas

The [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) are a faction mostly made up of women who escaped the oppression of The Holy Nation and former slaves from Rebirth. They live in a [hidden village](/flotsam-village) near the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), as the painted tribes provide a good deterrent from their pursuers.

### Deadcat

In the northwestern corner of Kenshi, the [Plains](/cannibal-plains) have been overrun by Cannibals. The [Deadcat](/deadcat) civilization has dwindled to their current state of existing only in the corners of their former lands. [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) work in the area to decrease the Painted Tribes' power.

### Mongrel

In the middle of the infamous [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), a few outlaws and survivors have built the city of [Mongrel](/mongrel). The Shinobi ninja guards of [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) keep the [Fogmen](/fogmen) at bay while the artisan locals perfect their craft inside the city walls.

### Skeletons

A few remnants of an ancient civilization living isolated with in the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city).
