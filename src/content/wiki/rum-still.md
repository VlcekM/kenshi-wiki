A **Rum Still** converts [Cacti](/cactus) into [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum).

Using the Rum Still trains the [Cooking](/cooking) stat, and is affected by the cooking time reduction multiplier that a higher Cooking level brings.

NB This building does not require [Empty Rum Bottles](/empty-rum-bottle) to store the Rum.
