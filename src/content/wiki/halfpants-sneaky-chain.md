**Halfpants \[Sneaky Chain\]** are Light Armour equipped in the Legwear slot. It can be bought from Clothing Shops in [UC](/united-cities) and [HN](/the-holy-nation) towns. Other variants of Halfpants include: [colored](/halfpants-colored), [padded](/halfpants-padded), [ragged](/halfpants-ragged) and [reinforced](/halfpants-reinforced).

It can be randomly equipped by [Hounds](/hound), [Moon Guardians](/moon-guardian), [Farmers](/farmer-preacher-cult), and [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x4**.

It can be crafted in the [Chain Armour Crafting Bench](/chain-armour-crafting-bench) and [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench) after researching the blueprint.

## Appearance

Halfpants \[Sneaky Chain\] are depicted as dark pants that are torn at the shin. A cargo pocket is located at each side of the pants.
