The **Burning Forest** is a swampy forest filled with [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider). It has its namesake from the [Acid Rain](/weather-effects) which commonly occurs here.

An [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) can be found here containing both loot and [Security Spiders](/security-spider), as well as a ruined farm affiliated with [United Cities](/united-cities).

Players passing through this area without adequate Acid protection should consider learning the [Blueprints](/blueprints) for a camp tent. Tents can be used as a temporary shelter from Acid Rain and are much easier to construct than a full building. However, tents will not provide safety from [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) which are a danger in this Zone no matter the weather.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in the Burning Forest. This zone does not spawn Camps.

## Tips

-   Having a member of the party that can spot spiders, like [Miu](/miu), is advisable in this area.

## Trivia

-   The Burning Forest is the only zone in Kenshi that has [acidic rain](/weather-effects) and is not connected with any other adjacent zones that also have acidic properties (the closest zone with acid rain is [Deadlands](/deadlands)).
    -   It could be speculated that the [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs) located this zone may have been the cause of acidic properties, given that it doesn't appear to have been spread from other areas.
