**Expedition 5** is a location similar to [Expedition 4](/expedition-4). It is a partially ruined town with lots of components on ground around buildings. It can be found east of [Spring](/spring) and south of [Reaver Camp](/reaver-camp).

This location was created by the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) during their fifth expedition south. This location is referenced in [Tech Hunter's Notes: The Ashlands](/tech-hunters-notes-series). Although the number implicates that there were 4 expeditions before it, only the 4th one has an observable abandoned settlement, it's not explicitly stated in the game what the results of the other expeditions were.
