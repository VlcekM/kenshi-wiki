**Purple Sands** is a large desert north of [The Shrieking Forest](/the-shrieking-forest) and south of [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail). The region is immediately recognizable for the purple hue of its desert sand and satellite-like structures partially buried everywhere in the desert.

The region itself is barren and sparsely patrolled, but occasionally [Cannibals](/cannibals), [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits) and [Beak Things](/beak-thing) wander into the area from other regions.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in Purple Sands. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

Sandstorms constantly plague the region allowing it to have high wind speed. Due to absence of water, the area is considered an area of extreme climate and one of the most inhospitable regions in the game. Also, prospecting reveals no raw resources within the region further reinforcing that the region cannot be colonized. Occasionally individual [Beak Things](/beak-thing) may be found lost in the desert.
