**The Hub** is a town of [Holy Nation Outlaws](/Holy_Nation_Outlaws) located near the center of The [Border Zone](/Border_Zone), it is a mostly ruined outlaw town formerly belonging to [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation), until it was taken and razed by [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom), and later retaken by The Holy Nation, before eventually getting abandoned by both sides for not serving much purpose, where the ruins have since been partially inhabited by shady outlaws who call this place home.

The [bar](/Barman) in The Hub is owned by the [Trade Ninjas](/Trade_Ninjas), who also occupy a nearby location known as the [Rebel Base](/Rebel_Base). The [Shinobi Thieves](/Shinobi_Thieves) occupy a watchtower at a far end of the settlement.

Many characters can potentially be found at the bar here. Most notably, (Hobbs will be in the Hub if spawn settings are 2.0 when starting out) [Hobbs](/Hobbs) might ask the player to come have a drink with him. Other possible bar squads include [Bar Thug Drunk](/Bar_Thug_Drunk). You start in The Hub when using [The Wanderer](/Game_Starts#wanderer), The Holy Sword and [Son of a Captain](/Game_Starts#son-of-a-captain) game starts, and a chance to start there with the Nobodies start. If you are not starting out in The Hub, it is possible to place it on your map by reading the [Map of the Border Zone](/Maps) or just running to it yourself.

The [Hounds](/Hounds) pacifier can be found here.

## Lore

The Hub is in the [Border Zone](/Border_Zone), a generally uninhabited frontier region between the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) and the [Holy Nation](/Holy_Nation). The history of the Hub is written in a series of notes that can be found in the Small Shack that is not in ruins. That building contains 5 letters in total:

-   [Ludin's Reports](/Ludin's_Reports) (3 total notes)
-   [Priest Varloo's Note](/Priest_Varloo's_Note)
-   [Priest Tyluig's Note](/Priest_Tyluig's_Note)

The notes reveal that the Hub was once part of the [Holy Nation](/Holy_Nation) but the [Shek Kingdom's](/Shek_Kingdom) forces takenover this region, disposed the corpses of previous inhabitants, and occupied the area for sometime before eventually sacking the city and set it ablaze before retreating.

After that, a Holy Nation squad was sent to the ruins to investigate. They initially found no enemy presence as the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) has deserted the region, remarking that "Retaking the Hub would be effortless but resettling it will prove most difficult.", presumably as a result of the level of devastation and death of this city.

In the 2nd report sometime after the 1st one, they spotted "shadowy figures" in the city, a bonedog escaped the leash of their owner and went chasing after the shadowy figures but went missing during the chase.

In the 3rd report, they went to explore The Hub during the cover of night, hearing the voice of both a man and a woman both of which vanished once they entered the gates. After losing one of the Sentinel to some unseen force picking them off, the Holy Nation soldiers retreated, claiming the ruins are haunted by "wraiths". The High Paladin sent to investigate claimed that the "wraiths" have driven the Shek soldiers out by using the same tactics.

### Interpretation:

(This is the interpretation likely course of events happened transpired over the course of The Hub)

The Hub was likely a frontier settlement<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> used to aide the rudimentary development in the region<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the name of this town would suggest that this place was perhaps a "Supply Hub" for the various basic development projects here.

Being close to [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom), this place and the region as a whole had suffered from chronic raids and constant harrassments from their warlike neighbors throughout centuries<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This all changed when [The Holy Nation's](/The_Holy_Nation) [current Phoenix](/Holy_Lord_Phoenix) decided to launch [The Holy Crusade](/The_Holy_Crusade) to "bring peace to all believers" by eradicating their raiders once and for all<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Shager, the then leader of [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom) reacted with rage to this development and escalated the existing conflict from raids into total war [under his reign](/King_Shager's_Reign) by mobilizing large portions of their own populace as warriors in an attempt to "bring blood and doom" and destroy [The Holy Nation](/The_Holy_Nation). It was very likely in the midst of this conflict between the two powers that The Hub was takenover by the [Shek Kingdom](/Shek_Kingdom), who have occupied the settlement for some time<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and may have used it as a staging ground for assaults further into The Holy Nation territory<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

However, as the war raged on, it became clear that the [Sheks](/Shek) were losing terribly, as even their own accounts detail that their forces are being decimated by those of the Okranites, who far outnumbered them<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the final stages of the conflict, after the Sheks had crippled their population and pushed themselves to the brink of extinction<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Shager intended to make a final strike with whatever remaining warriors they have left in a [suicidal-assault](/Kamikaze) that would've completely destroyed themselves. However, [Bayan](/Bayan) protested to this action, and [Esata](/Esata_the_Stone_Golem) helped Bayan by killing Shager and becoming the new leader of Shek Kingdom in his place. Under Esata now, they have withdrew the remaining bulk of their forces from the frontlines<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and this may have been the point where Shek Kingdom abandoned this settlement and deserted this region.

Sometime after, a [Holy Nation scout patrol led by Ludin](/Ludin's_Reports) went to The Hub, who remarked the emptiness and devastation of the region, it was likely at this point where they retook this area and built the shack that stored the [various](/Priest_Varloo's_Note) [notes](/Priest_Tyluig's_Note). The effort they took into retaking and controlling this settlement was minimal, as they have stationed no permanent garrison inside this area<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, repeatedly abandoned it<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, likely allowing the various shady figures to move in such as the outlaws sometime later<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. After suffering an earthquake<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the loss of a hound<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and one of their [sentinels](/Holy_Sentinel) to an unknown force (likely the outlaws) picking them off<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> over the course of several months, they decided that this region is cursed and abandoned all current efforts at settling here, leaving the place to become an outlaw haven where [various](/Holy_Nation_Outlaws) [shady](/Shinobi_Thieves) [groups](/Trade_Ninjas) have made this place their home.

The Hub can be considered to be a very loosely controlled and sparsely populated town of The Holy Nation with little value compared to their [other](/Stack) [larger](/Bad_Teeth) [towns](/Blister_Hill) in their core regions<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, given its frontier nature, lack of effort for either side to establish a permanent presence, and the fact that The Holy Nation vacated/abandoned this settlement despite having largely won the conflict against Shek Kingdom, and also despite their capability of stationing presence in this region<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Tips

-   You can make an outpost just outside the walls (for example in a valley to the West). With careful management, production lines can be established from resource-gathering operations outside the Hub and to the resource-processing production chains inside the [player-owned buildings](/Player-Owned_Buildings_in_Town) in the Hub.
-   Several copper and iron nodes within walking distance from the gates make it possible to run a fairly safe mining operation inside of the Hub. (Purchase a Small Shack, research ore storage and build a storage box for the material you wish to mine. Then hold Shift + Click on an ore vein close enough to The Hub to assign mining it as a job. The character should haul the ores to the storage container without needing to assign a new job)
-   The small shack that is not ruined and contains two [bed](/Bed)[s](/Bed) that can help speed up the healing rate of your characters.
    -   Be careful when entering this building as if certain NPCs see you they may attack.
-   There are many buildings for sale in The Hub, but they are collapsed and in need of repair. This leads to cheaper prices for the real estate but requires extra effort to collect [Building Materials](/Building_Materials) to repair them. Once the houses are functional, buildings such as a [Research Bench](/Research_Bench) can be built inside, allowing for the player to reach a much higher tech level before heading out and building their own outpost.
-   Even if purchased you'll still want to repair the buildings in order to fit more stuff inside of them. It'd be wise to repair the building closest to the Shinobi Thieves, so they will run out to protect you if attacked (Assuming they are allied).
-   [Dust Bandit Camps](/Dust_Bandit_Camp) can spawn near The Hub - you can bait them to follow you to the Shinobi Thieves' den and, if you are allied with them, they will be killed swiftly and can be looted for some early-game armour.
-   If you run to the Shinobi Den, you will Ninja-step across the ledge of the ruined building just outside their base. If you path to the space between the buildings instead, you can step through in 2 clicks, and any following bandits will run through the ledge instead. This buys you time to enter the Tower, from which Ninjas will then defend you.
