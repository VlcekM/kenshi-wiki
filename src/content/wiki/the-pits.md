**The Pits** is a zone in the Southeast quarter of the map, making it within the "Forbidden Zone". It is frequently home to fights between the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) and the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders).

[Fishmen](/fishmen) from [Fishman Island](/fishman-island) will appear in this zone and can sometimes be seen kidnaping organic characters from the frequent fighting in the area. If [Elder](/elder) is imprisoned or killed, the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) will also take part in the kidnapping of organic characters.

[Drifters](/drifters) from [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) will also appear here but won't always be friendly, as some of these groups will attack strangers on sight.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in The Pits. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
