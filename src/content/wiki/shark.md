**Shark** is a city run by outlaws, smugglers, gangs, assassins, criminals and anyone else just trying to make a living. It is located in the center of [the Swamp](/the-swamp) and serves as the center of trade within the marshy land, and apparently holds influence over [Rot](/rot)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The city is inhabited by the [Swampers](/swampers), but you quickly learn the [Hounds](/hounds) set up base here and actually call the shots. In the town you can gamble in a "[bar](/blackshifter-casino-bar)" run by one of the gangs, the [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), or trade and gather information in [the Dancing Skeleton](/the-dancing-skeleton). There are various merchants that deal anything from weapons to hashish and many residential buildings for sale to call home.

Don't get too comfortable though. There's always something going down from [Blood Spider](/blood-spider) attacks, to drunk [Bar Thugs](/bar-thugs), and [Red Sabre](/red-sabres) and [Swamp Ninja](/swamp-ninjas) assaults.

[The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) pacifier can be found here. [Doctor Chung](/doctor-chung), [Green](/green), [Stubs Momuso](/stubs-momuso) could also be recruited here unless they are in [Mud Town](/mud-town). [Hamut](/hamut) has a chance to spawn in Shark as well. Uniquely named [Skeleton Survivor](/skeleton-survivor) will always be found in Shark. [Disheartened Skeleton](/disheartened-skeleton) may be found there as well.

## [Town Overrides](/town-overrides)

This location is affected by [world states](/world-states). And will go by the priority from [Big Grim](/big-grim) -> [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) -> [Big Gray](/big-gray) -> everyone dies; depending on who is killed/imprisoned first.

If [Big Grim](/big-grim) is killed or kidnapped while [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) is alive and free, it will cause the Shark to be taken over by the [Blackshifters](/blackshifters).

If [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) (together with Big Grim) is also dead or kidnapped while [Big Gray](/big-gray) is alive and free, then this location will be taken over by the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers).

If all three gang bosses are killed/imprisoned, then Shark will be destroyed.
