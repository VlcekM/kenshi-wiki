**Smugglers Bar** is an [Outpost](/outpost-s-iv) [Bar](/bar) belonging to the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers). It is located in [Shem](/shem). [Smugglers](/smuggler) can be found in this location, as well as some of the randomly named [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) and [Generic Recruits](/generic-recruits).

### Residents

-   Smuggler Barman
-   [Grayflayer](/grayflayer) (6)

This location is likely to spawn a Smuggler Caravan or a squad from the [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild).

The upstairs section provides a wide array of free [beds](/bed), suitable for larger parties.

This location can spawn any of the following characters: [Recruit](/recruit), [Adventurer Recruit](/adventurer-recruit), [Fighter Recruit](/fighter-recruit), [Amateur Recruit](/amateur-recruit), [Bard](/bard), [Ex-Slave Recruit](/ex-slave-recruit), [Outlaw Recruit](/outlaw-recruit), [Tenacious Recruit](/tenacious-recruit), [Recruit Seeking Revenge](/recruit-seeking-revenge), [Silvershade](/silvershade), [Outlaw Medic](/outlaw-medic), [Engineer Recruit](/engineer-recruit), [Wall Man](/wall-man), [Medic Recruit](/medic-recruit), [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja), [Ninja](/ninja), [Sand Ninja](/sand-ninja), [Dust Bandit](/dust-bandit), and [Hungry Bandit](/hungry-bandit).

Smugglers Bar is a location which buys illegal items. The vendor here has a refresh time of 24 hours and a fill total of 50. Like all bars, this shop does not close for nighttime.

The Smugglers Bar is a part of the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) faction, which means it is governed by the [Swamper Trade Culture](/swamper-trade-culture), despite not being in the swamp. This restricts the items which are available at this location.

The Smugglers Bar also sells Gohan for 25% off.
