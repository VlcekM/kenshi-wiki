### Details

-   Power Consumption - 10
-   Efficiency - 100%
-   Produces - [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs)
-   Requires - Electrical Components, Iron Plate, Steel Bar, Robotic Component
-   Workers - 1

##  Skill Levels

The quality outcome for any crafted limbs is based on crafter's [robotics](/robotics) level.

Just like [armour and](/armour-smith) [weapon smithing](/weapon-smith), it is possible to crit a higher grade level. Although the chance does not display in the queue window.
