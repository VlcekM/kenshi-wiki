**Spine Canyon** is a long rocky plateau range that separates [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert). The [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders) and [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) call this place home and can be found roaming this small region.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or Ancient Locations in Spine Canyon. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest).

### Minor Outposts

_These locations are typically small towns, villages, or faction bases._

-   [Cactus Den](/cactus-den)

### Camps

_These are temporary locations which can spawn randomly. They usually spawn with [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) which are free to use. This zone has a max number nest of 4._

-   [Blood Bandit Camp](/blood-bandit-camp)

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. (1x multiplier) This list is in order of likelihood._

-   [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders)
    -   Blood Bandits Nest {4} (3 ~ 11 [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders))
-   [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas)
    -   Sand Ninja Cell (3 ~ 5 [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas)) {2}
    -   Sand Ninja Jounin (1 [Sand Ninja Jounin](/sand-ninja-jounin)) {1}
-   [Wolves](/wolves)
    -   Wolf Pack Nocturnal (1 ~ 6 [Bonedogs](/bonedog) aged 70) {1}

### Weather

_This zone has the 'great desert small' season. Seasons may cause harmful [Weather Effects](/weather-effects) or be purely aesthetic._

-   Extreme sandstorms are almost unending in this region, making wind turbines extremely useful. However, these storms also give stat debuffs to any unprotected characters.
