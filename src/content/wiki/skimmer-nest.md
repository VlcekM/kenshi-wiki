**Skimmer Nests** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in the [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert). Non-patrolling Skimmers of these nests will sleep under the sand during the day and will only emerge from beneath the sand at night, or to attack any who get too close.

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### skimmer hunter solo 24hr

This squad will contain 1 [Skimmer](/skimmer). They spawn with age multipliers between 0.9x and 1.0x.

### skimmer nest

This squad can contain from 1 to 3 [Skimmers](/skimmer). They spawn with age multipliers between 0.5x and 0.9x.

## Items

-   No items spawn at Skimmer Nests.
