**Flotsam Safehouse Note** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) which can only be found in [Flotsam Safehouse](/flotsam-safehouse). This item is a letter written to escaped [Rebirth Slaves](/rebirth-slaves) from the leader of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), [Moll](/moll).

This item is not unique though, as Flotsam Safehouses might also spawn in an [overwritten](/town-overrides) version of [Rebirth](/rebirth).
