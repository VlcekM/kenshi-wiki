**Slave Master Wada** is a slave master of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). He can be found inside his home in [Port South](/port-south).

As with the other TG Nobles, Wada lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house) and is guarded by 4 [Empire Noble Guard Hooded](/empire-noble-guard-hooded) characters and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

He has Smart [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmasters](/catun-scrapmasters) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Wada is killed, then [Port South](/port-south) will become destroyed. Furthermore, if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are also killed then Port South will fall under [Empire Peasants](/port-south/empire-peasants) Rule.

If Wada and [Longen](/longen)/[Ruben](/slave-master-ruben)/[Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) are killed, then [Heft](/heft) will Fall into a malnourished state if under UC rule.

If Wada and [Kana](/lady-kana) are killed, [Bark](/bark) will fall into a malnourished state if it's under UC rule. Furthermore, [Sanda](/lady-sanda) is also killed then [Bark will come under Empire Peasants rule](/bark/empire-peasants).

Slave Master Wada is considered part of the Northern Slave Masters along with [Longen](/longen), [Lady Kana](/lady-kana), [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) and [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren). The death of all these characters will also stop [Manhunter](/manhunter) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng), [Skimsands](/skimsands), and [The Eye](/the-eye), as well as [Slave Hunter](/slave-hunter) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng).

## Trivia

Out of all TG Nobles, **Slave Master Wada** is the only one that has absolutely no influence on the patrols of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).
