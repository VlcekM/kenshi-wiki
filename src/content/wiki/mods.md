# Mods

**Mods** are user-created modifications for Kenshi that change, expand, or enhance the game. They can range from simple tweaks, like adjusting stats or adding new items, to large-scale content additions, such as new factions, locations, or gameplay mechanics.

## Overview

Mods allow players to customize their Kenshi experience beyond the base game. Because Kenshi supports [modding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_modding) via its [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/app/233860/workshop/) and community files, players can share their creations or combine multiple mods to create unique gameplay experiences.

Mods can include, but are not limited to:

-   New equipment, weapons, and armor
-   Factions, characters, and NPCs
-   Gameplay mechanics and balance adjustments
-   Visual or audio enhancements
-   New locations, buildings, or quests

## [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop)

The Steam Workshop is a platform for sharing community-created mods through Steam. It provides:

-   A large library of Kenshi mods, from small tweaks to major content additions.
-   Ratings, comments, and Steam Awards that recognize the mod author’s work.
-   Automatic updates for subscribed mods, ensuring users have the latest versions.
-   A way for mod creators to upload updates, respond to feedback, and provide guidance for users.

## [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com)

Nexus Mods is one of the primary hubs for community-created mods. It provides:

-   A large library of mods of varying scope, from small tweaks to full content expansions.
-   Ratings, endorsements, and comments to help gauge the quality and reliability of a mod.
-   Download options for manual installation or via the Nexus's own mod manager [(Vortex)](https://www.nexusmods.com/about/vortex/).
-   Patches and compatibility guides for using multiple mods together.
-   A built-in bug reporting tab for each mod, allowing users to report issues directly to the mod author.

The Nexus Mods page also allows mod creators to upload updates, respond to issues, and provide detailed instructions for installation and usage.

## Installing Mods

Mods for Kenshi can be installed using several methods, depending on where the mod is sourced and how you prefer to manage them.

### Steam Workshop

Mods can be installed directly through the Steam Workshop:

-   Subscribe to a mod on its Steam Workshop page.
-   Steam will automatically download the mod files and apply them.
-   Open the Kenshi launcher and enable the mod under the "Mods" tab.
-   Steam Workshop does not include a dedicated mod manager, so load order will need to be managed through the game launcher

### Vortex Mod Manager

Mods can be installed and managed using Vortex, a third-party mod manager:

-   Download and install [Vortex](https://www.nexusmods.com/about/vortex/).
-   Use Vortex to download or import Kenshi mods from Nexus Mods or local files.
-   Enable the mods within Vortex to apply them to the game.
-   Vortex provides tools to manage load order, resolve conflicts, and ensure files are placed correctly.
-   Using Vortex can simplify installing multiple mods and reduce the chance of errors compared to manual installation.

### Manual Installation

-   Download the mod files from Nexus Mods or another trusted source.
-   Place the mod folder into the game's "Mods" directory. Folder structure goes like: Kenshi/mods/(Folder of the mod itself)
-   Enable the mod in the Kenshi launcher under the "Mods" tab.
-   Some mods may require additional patches or compatibility adjustments if used alongside other mods.

## Load Order

When using multiple mods, the order in which they are loaded—known as the **load order**—can be very important. Some mods may override or conflict with others and having the wrong load order can cause issues such as missing content, crashes, or unexpected behavior in-game.

-   Users are generally responsible for determining the correct load order, either by trial and error or by following recommendations found online.
-   Some mod authors provide suggested load orders in their mod descriptions or as part of a [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/app/233860/workshop/) collection.
-   When possible, look for community guides or collections that have already resolved load order conflicts to save time and prevent issues.
-   Load order can also be edited manually by modifying the \`mod.cfg\` file located in Kenshi’s game folder:

 \* Open the \`mod.cfg\` file with a text editor.
 \* Move mod entries up or down to change their load priority.
 \* Save the file and restart the game to apply changes.
 \* More detailed guidance can be found [here](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1850250979).

-   Proper load order management helps ensure that mods work together as intended and reduces the chance of errors in your game.
