**Major Towns** are [locations](/category/locations) where many different types of Vendors can be found. Often, Major Towns will contain more than one Bar. Major Towns tend to be large settlements with many buildings and residents, although not all of them provide the same services, and their size and population can vary wildly.

They often have a large selection of stores and multiple buildings for sale. They are also the only items on the map that can be seen at any zoom level, besides your own settlements.

## The Holy Nation

The zealous fanatics of [the Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) inhabit these towns. They are not safe for non-human [Races](/races) without [Human](/human) escort. Even then you may encounter trouble with the residents and guards. [Skeletons](/skeleton) should stay away at all times, as they are killed on sight regardless of any escort.

### [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)

-   A fortress city guarding a mountain pass between the fertile valley of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and hostile wilderness of the [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam). Dust storms and hungry bandits laid waste to the land east of Bad Teeth, but the city remains a place for respite and safety.

### [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)

-   The glorious capital of the Holy Nation and the seat of [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). The areas around here are patrolled heavily by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), and as such are relatively free of any bandit presence. If you are a full [Human](/human) squad, the very fertile areas around here are great for setting up a base.

### [Stack](/stack)

-   A large town just southeast of the center of the map, on the edge of [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) territory. It has a decent variety of shops, and its surrounding areas often have [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits), Wild [Dogs](/bonedog), and [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits). With all of the relatively weak enemies it makes for a decent training area for newer characters. Its proximity to the [Western Hive](/western-hive) and [The Hub](/the-hub) makes it a good location for aspiring traders and thieves alike.

## Shek Kingdom

### [Admag](/admag)

-   The Shek capital and home of the [Stone Golem](/stone-golem). It is located on top a hill in the northern part of the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). It features almost all types of shops and bars. There also is a [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) tower. Many 10 percent quality Copper mines dotted outside town.

### [Squin](/squin)

-   A town nestled in a narrow canyon. It is located in the western part of the [Border Zone](/border-zone). There are two [bars](/bars) alongside most basic shop types. The town has relatively safe access to high quality Iron and Copper resource nodes.

## United Cities

### [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last)

-   A large town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction, located in the region of the map known as [The Hook](/the-hook). The town has a number of farms in the surrounding area, though the area is frequently attacked by [Outlaw Farmers](/rebel-farmers). The town is frequently visited by traveling merchants, as well as [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) and [Manhunters](/manhunters).

### [Catun](/catun)

-   City in [Bonefields](/bonefields). Contains a weapons shop controlled by the [Catun Scrapmasters](/catun-scrapmaster-manufacturer).

### [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)

-   A compact town controlled by the United Cities faction, located in the Southwest region of the map known as The Hook.

### [Bark](/bark)

-   A coastal city controlled by the United Cities in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

### [Brink](/brink)

-   City on the western side of [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

### [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)

-   A city controlled by the United Cities located in the central part of The Great Desert.

### [Stoat](/stoat)

-   A city located in the south-western part of The Great Desert region. It is controlled by the United Cities.

### [Heft](/heft)

-   The current capital of the United Cities, Heft is located in the southern part of The Great Desert. It is home to [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu).

### [Heng](/heng)

-   Heng is located in the [Heng](/heng-zone) region, south of the Great Desert. Built upon a rocky plateau, it is well fortified and an important trading city for the United Cities.

## Tech Hunters

### [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)

-   City in [The Outlands](/the-outlands), built at the bottom of a high ancient tower that is visible from a distance. Black Scratch is the home to the renowned [Great Library](/the-great-library), the largest collection of [Blueprints](/blueprints) and [Lore Books](/lore-books) in all of the world.

### [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon)

-   City in [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon-zone). The Tech Hunters turned an abandoned offshore platform into a well-defended fortress with multiple shops.

### [Mourn](/mourn)

-   Once a thriving [United City](/united-cities) mining town in [Bonefields](/bonefields), built in the shadow of a looming ancient structure, Mourn was left in shambles after a slave rebellion. The ruined building in the middle of the city contains a [Great White Gorillo](/great-white-gorillo).

### [World's End](/worlds-end)

-   Located in the North of Holy Nation territories, high in the mountains, it belongs to Tech Hunters. The University of Machinists can be found here, with a fair number of lore objects in barrels.

## Other Factions

These towns belong to smaller more minor factions. They often only have one town, and in some cases may not even exist outside of these towns. They are safe for all [Races](/races) unless otherwise specified.

### [The Hub](/the-hub)

-   The sole town of the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws), and the least town-like town in the game. It's located in the Border Zone and consists mostly of ruined buildings with various criminals living in them. There is a large bar on the end of the town which functions as multiple types of traders at once. This is the starting location of the Retired Soldier and Wanderer [Game Start](/game-starts).

### [Mongrel](/mongrel)

-   The sole town of the [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) faction, located in the middle of the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). Due to its location, it is extremely dangerous to attempt to get to, many players can lose multiple characters to the [Fogmen](/fogmen) on the journey. However, once you reach the town you will be safe as it is protected by many very powerful guards. The town itself has a good variety of shops, many having goods not available in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

### [Shark](/shark)

-   Major town of [The Swamp](/the-swamp), Shark is home to 5 gangs. The [Hounds](/hounds) are the leaders of The Swamp. The other gangs found nearby are the [Stone Rats](/stone-rats), [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), [Twinblades](/twinblades), and [Grayflayers](/grayflayers).

### [Spring](/spring)

-   Major town in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). Home of the [Anti Slavers](/anti-slavers).

### [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city)

-   A town surrounded by acid rain (as well as acidic ground and lakes) and run by [Skeletons](/skeletons) in the middle of the [Deadlands](/deadlands) region. As there are no human residents, the town lacks a food supply. The [Robotics Shop](/robotics-shop) and nearby [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) sell high quality and rare items.

## Hostile Factions

### [Cannibal Capital](/cannibal-capital)

-   The largest location owned by the [Cannibals](/cannibals). The fallen home of the Deadcat faction.

### [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile)

-   Less a town and more a massive ruin, Cat-Lon's Exile is the last holdout of the Second Empire and its emperor, [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon), who bides his time to get his revenge on humanity.

### [Fish Isle](/fish-isle)

-   An abandoned ruin that's home to a massive shoal of [Fishmen](/fishmen).

### [Southern Hive (Location)](/southern-hive-location)

-   A large Hiver settlement built around a ruined building. It's the home of the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south).

## Potential Towns

### [The Free City](/the-free-city)

-   Not really a town, it's more similar to a [waystation](/waystation). It's founded by [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) after [Longen](/longen) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are dead.

### [Slave Farm South (Free Traders)](/slave-farm-south/free-traders)

-   Not really a town, but rather a slave camp converted into farm village. It is taken by [Free Traders](/free-traders) after [Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) and [Market Master](/slave-market-master) are dead.

### [Okran's Shield (Reavers)](/okrans-shield/reavers)

-   Not really a town, but rather a military base taken over by Reavers. It happens if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are dead while [Valamon](/valamon) is alive.
