**Blister Hill** is a large town and the capital of the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation), located towards the north of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride). It is home to the current [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) where he has a throne room with an entourage that includes several [High Paladins](/high-paladin) and a special bodyguard known as [Protectors of the Flame](/protector-of-the-flame). It can be discovered by travelling near or by acquiring and reading the **Glorious Holy [Map](/maps)**.

There are two sections in this town, with the section that includes throne room and military buildings separated by walls to the outer buildings of the general populace.

Blister Hill has a large variety of buildings that include Shops, [Bars](/bars), and [Temples](/temple).

The [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) pacifier can be found here. [Bard](/bard) and [Griffin](/griffin) have a chance to be recruited here as well.

## Trivia

The towns name "Blister Hill" is likely to be a reference to the hill behind them that is the [zone called Rebirth](/rebirth-zone), with the rough rocks making it look like a giant blister.
