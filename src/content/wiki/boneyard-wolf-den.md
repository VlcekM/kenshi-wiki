**Boneyard Wolf Dens** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in the [Bonefields](/bonefields) and the [High Bonefields](/high-bonefields). Despite what the name implies, these Dens contain [Bonedogs](/bonedog), not [Boneyard Wolves](/boneyard-wolf).

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### Wolf Pack nocturnal

This squad can contain 1 to 6 Bonedogs. They spawn with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.7x.

### Wolf Pack Den

This squad can contain from 1 to 7 Bonedogs. Between one to two of these will be spawned with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.8x, with the other zero to five spawning with age multipliers between 0.1x and 0.3x.
