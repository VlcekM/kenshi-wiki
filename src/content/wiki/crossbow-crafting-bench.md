**Crossbow Crafting Bench** is a [crafting](/crafting) [building](/buildings-list) which players can craft [Crossbows](/crossbows) using [Steel Bars](/steel-bars), [Spring Steel](/spring-steel), [Hinges](/hinge) and [Crossbow Parts](/crossbow-parts). It can be constructed after researching [Crossbow Crafting](/crossbow-crafting-tech). The [Crossbow Smith](/crossbow-smith) skill determines the speed and quality of the final products at this bench. Only [Junkbows](/junkbow) can be crafted without using [Blueprints](/blueprints).
