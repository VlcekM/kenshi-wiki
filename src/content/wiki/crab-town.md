**Crab Town** is a [Minor Outpost](/minor-outposts), serving as the capital of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) located in the northeast of [The Pits East](/the-pits-east). The [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) can be found here as well as the [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) and Crab-lover [Lumi](/lumi).

The [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) in the town are non-hostile, so anyone can enter unless you are allied to the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) or the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).

This means that Crab Town can be a surprisingly useful [regional settlement](/minor-outposts), containing a [bar](/crab-bar) and a [Crab Smithy](/crab-smithy) which allow players to sell goods despite its remote location.

Neutral players can also purchase [Crab](/crab), however you must be allied with the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) in order to purchase the [Crab Armour](/crab-armour) set and its blueprints.

However, the gate guards will attack you if you are being pursued by a wandering [Crab Raider](/crab-raider) patrol, so players should ensure they are not being followed before approaching the [settlement](/minor-outposts).
