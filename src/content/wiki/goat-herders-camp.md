**Goat Herder's Camps** are randomly spawning camps. They can spawn in the [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), [Dreg](/dreg), [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Shem](/shem), and [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).

This location spawns [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) which the player can use for free.

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this camp. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### Goat Herder

This squad can contain from 4 to 16 Goats. One or two of these Goats spawn with age multipliers between 0.0x and 0.1x, with the other potential four to fourteen Goats spawning with age multipliers between 0.9x and 1.0x. This squad is led by a [Goat Herder](/goat-herder).

### Goat Herder Camp

This squad is led by another Goat Herder with between four to ten Goats which spawn with age multipliers between 0.4x and 0.9x.

## Item Spawns

This location spawns Dung.
