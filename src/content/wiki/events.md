Anticipated or ongoing **Events** can be found on the right side of the [Factions](/factions) window. Events include scheduled visits to a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) by another faction and ongoing Bodyguard Jobs. The timer will show how much time is left until an event starts or the time left on a Bodyguard job.

Information regarding only _one_ bodyguard job will be presented at a time, although the player can have multiple bodyguards hired at once. In contrast, several _different_ visits can be scheduled and listed in the Events list.

## Bodyguards

Bodyguards are characters that are set to guard members of your faction or your outpost for a limited amount of time. They can either be hired or will join you as allies out of their own free will. Once you have acquired a bodyguard, an Event will be shown under the Faction tab stating the number of hours before they leave. Some bodyguards, depending on personality, may stay longer if all members of the player's squad are KOed upon expiry of contract. For a full list of characters capable of being bodyguards, see [Bodyguards](/category/bodyguards).

### Mercenaries

Mercenaries are bodyguards that are usually hired at bars using cats. They usually give a choice between bodyguarding the player or their nearest outpost.

-   [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild) - after talking to a [Mercenary Captain](/mercenary-captain) or a [Human Mercenary](/human-mercenary) squad leader. The Human Mercenary variant is only found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They cost c.1,000 per day up to two days to guard the player, and c.2,000 every two days up to eight days to guard the player's outpost.
-   [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) - after talking to a [Drifter](/drifter) squad leader of this faction that are found in bars. They cost the same as normal mercenaries.
-   [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) - after talking to a [Cannibal Hunter](/cannibal-hunter) squad leader. They cost c.3,000 per day to guard the player, and c.3,000 every two days up to eight days to guard the player's outpost.
-   [Black Dogs](/black-dog) - after talking to a [Black Dog Bodyguard](/black-dog-bodyguard) trying to extort the player. They cost c.2,500 per day to guard the player, and upon completion of the contract will demand an exit fee of c.700 or can be re-hired. Note that refusing to pay the exit fee or not hiring them in the first place will cause them to attack. They also have a unique bodyguard event where they show up at a player's outpost to extort protection. This will cost c.4,000 for two days but can be lowered down to c.3,500 or c.3,000.

### Allies

If a player has allied with these factions, some of their patrols can be asked to follow the player or may randomly follow the player on their own.

-   [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) - if [Big Bo](/bo) is in the squad, she will sometimes hail Anti-Slaver squads for three days (interestingly may include Tinfist's squad //confirm). Alternatively, if the player has taken down any high-ranking UC leader or noble groups, random Anti-Slaver squads will also bodyguard the player for three days.
-   [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) - random protection from [Crab Raider](/crab-raider) patrols. They will guard the crabbed for 6 hours.
-   [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) - after showing [Note From Moll](/note-from-moll) to [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja) squad leaders consisting of more than 4 individuals in a patrol.

-   [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) - after speaking to a patrolling Rebel Farmer squad leader. If the option implying importance they will bodyguard the player for three days, else they may refuse or only bodyguard the player for one day.
-   [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) - after speaking to a [Rebel Captain](/rebel-captain) squad leader that is not guarding a town. They will bodyguard the player for one day.

-   [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) - after speaking to a [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian) squad leader that is not guarding a town. They will bodyguard the player for 3 days or defend the player's outpost for two days.
-   [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) - after giving food to the leader of patrol (don't expect them to last long).

-   [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) - showing the [Holy Seal](/holy-seal) to a [Paladin](/paladin) patrol leader. They will bodyguard the player for 36 hours.
-   [United Cities](/united-cities) - random protection from patrols, usually led by a [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant) ([Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) can technically bodyguard the player, if they are somehow allied to UC while the conditions for Rogues to spawn are met). They will bodyguard the player for three days.

### Other

Unique bodyguard events with special "contract" conditions.

-   [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins) - if the player finds a lone tech hunter near ancient ruins, they may be able to talk to them. Accepting their company will cause them to bodyguard the player for 24 hours.
-   [Error\_Code\_0xFFFFFF](/error-code-0xffffff) - after being seen by them. They ally themselves to the player character that saw them with a bodyguard "contract" that lasts over a hundred in-game years.

## Types of Outpost Visits/Assaults

Major factions visit the [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) for a multitude of reasons. The most common reason for a visit is when a major faction establishes that another settlement has sprung up in previously barren land and that it has to be included in **their** sphere of influence. Each major faction will send representatives of their government to demand a token of loyalty of a new settlement to their authority. Should this gesture of obedience be refused, the major powers will then visit player's outpost for a second reason - to destroy and pillage the upstart criminal. Some of these assaults can only be stopped by destroying the [key figures](/world-states) of the major power that sends them. Destroying key figures will bring about their own special "visit" events in retaliation.

Typically, the assaults compose of a faction sending a small fraction of their army force as a platoon to the player base, which tend to increase in size (with a few exceptions<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>) and difficulty for each previous one defeated until the largest assaults that commonly happen as a result of defeating their key leaders. Sometimes important leaders (Such as [Eyegore](/eyegore) and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem)) may also personally lead the platoon into assault, the size and scale of which the leader leads can also differ drastically between the trigger used. Many of these assaults can be repetitive, potentially infinitely, and tend to be re-launched every few days or weeks.

### How visits trigger

-   Each faction checks for a new event every 6 in-game hours.
-   A faction runs one event at a time. While one is running or scheduled, it won't roll another.
-   Your base must be within the event's range of one of the faction's towns. The closer, the more likely. Past the max range, never.
-   One of your characters must be at the base. Events don't target empty bases. A scheduled event whose base is empty waits or switches to another of your bases, and is eventually cancelled.
-   Some events need a minimum number of characters, counted across all your squads (listed below).
-   Each event has a cooldown before it can happen again. Many first visits (Tax Man Visit, Traders Guild Visit, Prayer Day, Tribute Round, Hound's Welcome) only roll once per game. Later visits are follow-ups.
-   Follow-ups (return visits, assaults after you refuse) are scheduled by your dialogue choice or by how the event ended. They show in the Events list with a timer.
-   Your base's level doesn't matter, except for the Shek [Tribute Round](/tribute-round).

Two options affect these events:

-   **Town raid events: frequency** (0.25x to 3x) scales how likely events are. Caravan events ignore it.
-   **Town raid events: size** (0.25x to 4x) scales how big events are. Size is counted in characters: whole squads are added until the size is reached. Your base's level or wealth doesn't change it.

If more than one allied reinforcement event is valid for a specific hostile event, all valid allied reinforcements will be sent.

## Types of Outpost Visits/Assaults: Base Attacks

Base Attacks are not technically part of the events system, but have similar interactions with player bases. They are wandering squads that spawn in the wilderness, and actively seek out and assault player owned bases if they are nearby. Sometimes these can also be affected by [world states](/world-states), generally the killing or capturing of said faction's leader or leaders will stop these types of squads from spawning, though rarely it is the status of other faction's characters.

A base attack only starts if:

-   the base has a gate. Bases without a gate never get base attacks;
-   one of your characters is within 2,500 units of the base;
-   the squad isn't allied with you;
-   that faction's cooldown has passed.

The **Town attacks frequency** option sets the cooldown: how long each faction waits after its last base attack ends before it can attack again.
