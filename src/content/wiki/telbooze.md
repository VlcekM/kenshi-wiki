**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Telbooze is a small mountain town located southeast of [Brink](/brink-alpha). The area is apparently patrolled by the [Imperial Lords](/imperial-lords), however Telbooze itself is owned by [Dead Cat](/dead-cat). Nearby towns include [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge-alpha) and [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha). Telbooze itself is balanced precariously on the side of a mountain.

Telbooze does not appear in the final version of the game, however its assets were repurposed to be used in the [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south).
