**Reaver Neophyte** is one of the weaker members of the [Reavers](/reavers) faction. They can be found in [Ark](/ark) and in [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).

These characters have a 10% chance of spawning with a crossbow. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (20% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient), [Unknown](/unknown), or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Shek
