**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Brink is the starting town in which you spawn, and is owned by [Imperial Lords](/imperial-lords). It is located on a large hill, in the northern half of the map. Nearby towns include [Catun](/catun-alpha), [Shark](/shark-alpha), and [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge-alpha) as well as an outpost to the North east.

You spawn near the front gates and a Bar. This is where most players will pick up their first party member.

[Brink](/brink) made it into the final release of the game as a settlement of the [United Cities](/united-cities).

## Important Buildings in Brink

-   2 Bars
-   2 Clothing Traders
-   A Weapon Trader
-   An Armour Trader
-   A Footwear Trader
-   A Hat Trader
-   A Trade Building
-   An Item Trader
-   Police Station
