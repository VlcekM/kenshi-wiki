**Fancy Rug** is a [trade good](/trade-goods) found on the floors of the luxurious [Noble Houses](/noble-house). It can be picked up from the floor with an "Alt+left click". Since Fancy Rugs are only found in possession of the nobles, such an act is always [theft](/thievery), at least at first. A Fancy Rug can be placed back on the floor by simply dragging it out of the inventory of a character. [Simple Rugs](/simple-rug) work much in the same way and are also available for purchase in some places.
