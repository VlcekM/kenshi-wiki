**Leather Shirt** is [armour](/armour) that is equipped in the [Shirt](/shirts) slot. It is sold in most armour shops.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

There are [no-sleeve](/leather-vest), [long-sleeve](/leather-turtleneck) and [dark](/dark-leather-shirt) versions of this clothing. Additionally, there is also a [Hiver-equivalent](/leather-hive-vest) of this clothing.

It is most commonly found on members of the [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters), [Swampers](/swampers) and [Slavers](/slavers), as well as worn by some of the special [Generic Recruits](/generic-recruits).

It can be crafted using the [Leather Armour Crafting Bench](/leather-armour-crafting-bench) after researching the appropriate blueprint.

## Appearance

The Leather Shirt is similar in appearance to a T-shirt. It is made of a light brown leather that covers the chest and ends where the shoulder meets the bicep. The arms and neck have a dark green almost scaly trim.
