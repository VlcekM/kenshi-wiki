The Kenshi world map is vast and multicolored. Kenshi has many zones with natural, earthy designs which are at odds with more outlandish areas like [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [Gut](/gut), and [Venge](/venge).

Kenshi has many non-player locations, of sizes ranging from a single building to large cities. The game includes a [world states](/world-states) system which creates reactions to deaths of notable people. These reactions to power vacuums can result in new locations spawning in or towns being [taken over](/town-overrides) by new [factions](/factions).

Zones in Kenshi are not just aesthetic, as they have specific [soil types](/environment), resource availability, and [seasons](/seasons-and-weather). These factors come into play when players are [constructing outposts](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). Even without building anything, weather can give players pause, as harmful [weather effects](/weather-effects) can occur all across the map.

  

  
Throughout development, the world of Kenshi went through many changes. For information on Old World locations prior to the map overhaul, see [Old World](/old-world).

## Trivia

-   Kenshi takes place on a tidally locked moon, similar to Earth's moon, meaning the same side of the moon is always the visible side.
