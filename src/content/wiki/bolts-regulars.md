**Bolts \[Regulars\]** are used with [Crossbows](/crossbow-class). For long expeditions, crossbows that use regular bolts are the most efficient.

After researching [Crossbow Bolts](/crossbow-bolts-tech) and learning the [Blueprint](/blueprints), these bolts can be made on an [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench). Then can later be stored in a [Boltbox](/boltbox).

## Used with

-   [Oldworld Bow MkI](/oldworld-bow-mki)
-   [Oldworld Bow MkII](/oldworld-bow-mkii)
-   [Ranger](/ranger)
