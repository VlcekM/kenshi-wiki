**Hydroponic Vegetables** is a [Hydroponics](/hydroponics) bay which grows [Greenfruit](/greenfruit). It can be constructed after researching [Hydroponics Vegetables](/hydroponics-tech). This building uses and levels characters' [Farming](/farming) stat. It requires inputs of [Power](/power) (10 used) and [Water](/water) to grow.

Despite being an "interior" building, Hydroponics can be placed on the roofs of buildings.

### Details

-   Condition: 16
-   Power: 10
-   Plants Produced: 18 (2 plants per harvest at 100% harvest chance, for a total of 36 [Greenfruit](/greenfruit) per cycle)
-   Maturation Time: ~17.5 Hours
-   Workers: 1
