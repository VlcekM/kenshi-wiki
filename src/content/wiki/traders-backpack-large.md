The **Large Trader's Backpack** is a heavy weight [backpack](/backpacks) that is able to stack [Trade Goods](/trade-goods) by at least two items per spot. It makes usually non stackable items stack by atleast 2, then multiplies the maximum stack for any stackable item by 3 leading to stack sizes of 6 in most cases.

Can be found equipped (not sold) by traders at the [Holy Armour Shop](/holy-armour-shop), Clothing Shop - Holy Nation, [Holy Headgear Shop](/holy-headgear-shop), [Holy Boot Shop](/holy-boot-shop), and [Boot Shops](/boot-shop). 

They cannot be bought, so to obtain one they must be taken from a Shopkeepers. As it looks similar to the other Traders Backpack in game an easy way to tell if a Shopkeeper is wearing a Traders Backpack Large and not a Shopkeepers good bag is to check their movement speed. If they are not slowed down a ton by encumbrance, then they have a Traders Backpack Large.
