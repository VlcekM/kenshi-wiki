[Kenshi](/kenshi) takes place on an unnamed tidally locked moon, with a history spanning several thousand years. Much of it is shrouded in mystery, with legends, lies, and myths mixed in with the historical records.

This is a vague timeline of events we know to have happened, sorted very loosely categorized to chronological order as close as possible with our limited knowledge. Much of the information on this page is speculative and should not be considered 100% accurate.

**SPOILER ALERT!**

## Timeline

### The Ancients (Likely Many Thousands of Years Ago):

-   **The First Empire** \- A powerful and technologically advanced [civilization](/the-lost-ancients-series), possessing automated factories, satellites, genetic engineering, giant robots, and space elevators. Unknown how long they existed, what came before them, how far their reach extended, or if they were even from Kenshi's world or not; regardless, they were advanced to the point of godlike levels compared to the modern times. Possible rusted remains of spacecraft can be found around the continent.
    -   It is possible that the large industrial remains in [Floodlands](/floodlands) and [Deadlands](/deadlands) are the result of this civilization.
-   **The War of Behemoths** \- At some point, a war broke out between the First Empire and an unclear enemy either internal or external. The war used weapons so powerful that their owners began to fear the consequences of using them, such as the Behemoths, giant Skeletons that tower several times the height over usual individuals.
    -   **[Obedience](/obedience)** \- Due to their fear of the Behemoths, the Ancients decided to dispose of them after the war. Utilizing their unquestioning loyalty, the Ancients ordered the Behemoths down into a [massive pit](/obedience) with molten metal that encased them permanently.
-   **The Chaos Age and the fall of First Empire** \- A time of many calamities, rumored to be from dangerous celestial activity, to natural disasters, to plagues, to even a skeleton rebellion. It's unclear exactly what happened, or if this happened before or after the events of Obedience, possibly even spanning across the entirety of the event. But the First Empire had ceased to exist in a form recognizable as before and fallen.

### Second Empire (Approximately 1000+ Years Ago)

-   **The Second Empire is Founded.** Sometime after the Chaos Age, to redeem themselves in the eyes of humanity for their actions of the past (though the humans had long since forgotten)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) and his Skeleton followers founded an [empire](/second-empire). They attempted to rebuild civilization and study the remains of the Ancient Empire.
-   **The formation of Hydraulic Knights.** At some point, and for reasons unknown (even Skeleton characters themselves claim that they do not know), the [humans in the northwest](/cannibals) developed a taste for human flesh. Cat-Lon's empire spent a fair number of resources combating them, including the formation of a militant group known as the [Hydraulic Knights](/hydraulic-knight). These knights would combat the external enemies of the empire, such as pirates and cannibals. The cannibal tribes have legends that speak of a skeleton warrior they know as "The Inedible One." It is highly likely that this warrior was a member of the Hydraulic Knights, possibly their leader [General Jang](/general-jang).
-   **The Empire starts to Crumble.** While initially founded with good intentions, to keep the peace, the empire became more and more dictatorial<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. [Child prisons](/rhinobot), crackdowns on piracy and religious cults, and the killing of many of their own innocent citizens in the process much-inflamed tensions amongst the human populace<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The Second Empire's [military](/skeleton-legion) began decapitating dissident skeletons, [enthralling](/skeleton-no-head-mkii) all traitors who sided with the humans<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. When a great famine hit, the empire neared its breaking point<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
-   **(+5000 years ago) The Founding of the Holy Nation.** While details from this time are sparse (and rarely trustworthy), legends say it was around this time that a great leader of humanity stepped forward and gathered his followers in rebellion. Legends call him the Phoenix, champion of the god Okran and possessor of the everlasting Holy Flame. He united the humans in a movement that eventually went on to found one of the oldest living civilizations to this day, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
-   **The Fall of the Second Empire.** It is unclear what actually caused the fall of the empire, although it is suggested to be a multitude of factors, ranging from famine<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, human rebellion and Skeleton treason<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Regardless, the empire gradually decayed and collapsed. Their leaders retreated into the dangerous [Ashlands](/ashlands), where they reside to this day.

### Intermediate Period (Approximately 100-1000 Years Ago)

-   **(1000 years ago) setting of [Kenshi 2](/kenshi-2)**
-   **(937 years ago) [Armour King](/armour-king) founds his shop.**
-   **Appearance of the Hivers.** It is unclear when or how the [Hivers](/hive) appeared, or if they were present during the time of First or Second Empire. Whether they originated/mutated from humans or came from a land across the seas, they appeared and have been a resident of our lands ever since.
-   **The Rise and Fall of Kral.** For as long as anyone can remember, the Shek were simple tribes living in the wilderness, divided and warring regularly. At one point, a great Shek warrior named [Kral](/way-of-the-warrior-the-book-of-kral) unified the tribes into a powerful kingdom, taught his followers a code of honor and strength, and led them into a march of conquest. Eventually, however, he was [slain](/krals-last-stand) in battle against an enemy force that legends say outnumbered him a hundredfold. After his death, he attained near godlike levels of worship from his followers, who want nothing more than to die in a great battle like him. As such, they have continued their raiding and warlike ways with their neighbours ever since.
-   **Appearance of the** **Modern Shek.** While it is likely the [Shek](/shek) existed during Cat-Lon's empire, and possibly even back to the days of Ancients or First Empire, they did not have the appearance they currently sport. Their previous appearance was much more similar to a standard human, as they lacked the horns they are known for, and were known as Enforcers. It is unknown what role they played in the empires of old, though given the name it is possible they were a soldier or law enforcement caste, dedicated to fighting their master's enemies.
-   **The Birth of the United Cities and the Traders Guild.** It is unclear when or how [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) came into being, but it is possible they were formed in the ashes of a former empire. Whether this was the Second Empire itself or another one is unknown, but the United Cities and the Traders Guild seized power and ruled as powerful entities ever since.
    -   During the formative years of the United Cities, anti-government rebels invented [Spring Bat](/spring-bat) crossbows to counter the Empire's heavy [Samurai](/samurai).

### Recent History (0-100 Years Ago)

-   **As is Holy Nation tradition, the [62nd Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)** was taken from his birth parents shortly after his birth and raised in isolation by the High Priest. There he was indoctrinated into the Okranite religion and taught all the skills he needed in order to rule the empire. The 62nd Phoenix is said to be one of the harshest Phoenixes in the Holy Nation's history, having judged his own family at the age of 16, sentencing them to be purged in Holy Fire. Ever since, Holy Lord Phoenix has championed the unrelenting will of [Okran](/okran).
-   **The Red Rebellion and the ascendance of Tengu.** A few decades ago, the southern plains suffered a significant drought, which in turn caused a severe famine that nearly brought the United Cities to its collective knees. Due to the famine, the southern cities were forced to rely on the Traders' Guild to bring them the supplies they needed to survive. Unfortunately, bandits and Shek raiders blockaded the routes and took these supplies for themselves. What little did get through was instantly set upon by the nobles in a bidding war, and the lower classes were driven to starvation and [rebellion](/the-red-rebellion). The resultant fighting claimed many lives, including many nobles and even [Emperor Anzai](/emperor-anzai) himself, but the rebels were eventually put down and survivors enslaved. This marked the selection of a new Emperor from the [Noble's Circle](/noble-circle), [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), a cruel and impulsive man known for his lack of common sense. As a result, the poor and downtrodden are now even more heavily oppressed, even being arrested and sold into slavery for merely being poor as they are seen as a threat to the stability of the empire itself.
-   **The destruction of Bast.** [Bast](/bast-zone) was once a growing area of the United Cities, filled with many hamlets and towns for trading. However, everything changed when the Holy Nation attacked, who proceeded to burn the namesake city to the ground and haul the youngsters off to [Rebirth](/rebirth). The land has been a warzone where both the Holy Nation and United cities fight endlessly in the region, trampling underfoot any life that may have once thrived here.
-   **Revenge of Luquin.** [Luquin](/luquin) and his family were sold into slavery at a young age, where they were subjected to beatings, starvation, and exhaustion from working in the mines. Eventually, his father would succumb to the horrid conditions, devastating both Luquin and his mother, who, with nothing left to lose, devised an escape plan to save her son. She succeeded in freeing him, but at the cost of her own life. Starving and alone in the desert at the age of 15, he was found and taken in by a clan of ninjas who trained him in the arts of stealth and assassination. Seven years later, he returned to his hometown for blood, using his skills to stalk and assassinate noble after noble. Eventually, Luquin was arrested and paraded through the streets as a criminal before being sent off to [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault), where he was subjected to many tortures, including being forced to write a [book](/beautiful-tengu-a-tribute) in tribute to Emperor Tengu.
-   **Overthrow of King Shager.** [Shager](/king-shagers-reign) was the latest in the line of Kings from the Shek Kingdom. Like most Shek, he saw battle as a point of honor, with death in battle being the highest of honors. Under his rule, the Shek Kingdom was crumbling under the loss of life sustained in their wars with the Holy nation and the United Cities. King Shager proposed a desperate, suicidal last stand with whatever weakened warriors were left. To this, the warrior [Bayan](/bayan) spoke up, protesting against throwing away their lives needlessly. Shager was furious, but one of the [Invincible Five](/the-five-invincibles), [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem), stepped between the two and shouted, "the man speaks the truth. You want Bayan silenced; you'll have to cut me down first". Their swords crossed, and that day [marked the beginning](/shagers-overthrow) of the Stone Golem's rule, with Bayan at her side. She pulled her warriors off the front lines, made peace with the United Cities, and opened her borders to trade from the other races. Despite protests from some of her warriors, she is determined to ensure her people survive into the future, even if it means tearing down some of their long-held ideals and traditions.

### Unclear Chronological Order

-   **[Stobe](/stobe) dies:** [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) and [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) are named after him; the latter is where he lays dead.
-   **Fogmen Infestation:** For whatever reason, a cousin Hive civilization next to the Western Hive lost its mind and became roaming Fogmen inside the Fog Islands. Causing The Holy Nation to be wary of their existence<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
-   **Waterlevels Change:** It is heavily hinted that the water levels were higher by hundreds of meters long time ago, during the time of Old Empire<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
-   **The Eyes** were large, destructive satellite-like structures that brought death and destruction whereever they went. At least one of them has become defunct and lays mostly inactive in [The Eye](/the-eye), whereas at least one other is still running in [Venge](/venge).
-   **Mass Suicide of Skeletons**: The vast majority of Skeleton remains found by [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) suggest that they all died from suicide<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, exact cause unknown.
