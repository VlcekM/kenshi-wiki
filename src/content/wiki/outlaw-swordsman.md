**Outlaw Swordsmen** are typical [sabre](/horse-chopper)\-wielding bandits fighting for the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers).

In certain [town overrides](/town-overrides), some of the swordsmen abandon banditry and become guards for farming towns controlled by the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). Others presumably joined the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen).

They can be found patrolling several biomes, usually in the UC territories: [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Hook](/the-hook). They will patrol these areas in groups of 1-6, tagged along by a group of [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer), so long as [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) is alive. They can also spawn in the same number from [Raider Camps](/raider-camp) that dot Heng or The Great Desert.

A small squad of Outlaw Farmers led by a single Outlaw Swordsman can spawn in [Bast](/bast), Grey Desert, Heng, Sinkuun, Stormgap Coast, and The Great Desert.

They can also be found in heavy squads roaming Bast, Grey Desert, Heng, and Sinkuun. 1-3 may accompany the [Outlaw Heavy](/outlaw-heavy) patrol, led by an [Outlaw Swordsman Captain](/outlaw-swordsman-captain).

With [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) dead, an extra heavy squad will spawn in Bast, Heng, and Stormgap Coast.

Heavies can also be found in groups of 4-6 in Barracks in Fort Simion, as well 0-2 serving as bodyguards for Boss Simion himself.

They will also populate the Barracks in peasant-controlled Port North, Port South, and Slave Farm South.

A heavy squad will also help repel attacks by the [United Cities](/united-cities) on the player base should the player be an ally of the Rebel Farmers.

All these squads stop spawning when [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed, having presumably either laying down the sword, or joining the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen).

They have a 10% chance of spawning with a bounty ranging from c.300 to c.1,300. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
