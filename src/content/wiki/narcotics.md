**Narcotics** were once a Trade Good in older versions of Kenshi that could be bought and sold to make a profit. They were sold by traders in towns. The item was introduced in the game at the same time as Hashish and took up the same space and had the same function as [Hashish](/hashish), which is likely the reason why it was removed.
