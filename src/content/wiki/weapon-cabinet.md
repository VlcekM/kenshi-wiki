**Weapon Cabinet** is a [Storage](/storage) building which contains [Weapons](/weapons). It can be constructed after researching [Gear Storage (Tech)](/gear-storage-tech).

## Details

-   This chest has a storage space of **18x18** squares.
-   Mechanically, this building is identical to [Weapon Stand](/weapon-stand), the only difference being cosmetics.
