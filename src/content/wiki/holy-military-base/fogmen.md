If [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), both [Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base) will [become ruins](/town-overrides) overtaken by [Fogmen](/fogmen).

It appears that the military has evacuated from these settlements after Phoenix was killed or imprisoned, causing Fogmen to come here and inhabit this area.

This override coincides with the drastically increased [Fogmen Nest Infestation](/fog-deathyard) throughout [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), indicating that the defenses broke down after the death of Phoenix which caused rampant Fogmen infiltration.
