_This article is a [stub](/category/article-stubs). You can help the Kenshi Wiki by [expanding it](/holy-military-base/destroyed)._

If the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is [dead or imprisoned](/world-states), at least one of the Holy Military Bases should fall into ruins. However, due the way how the [Town overrides](/town-overrides) mechanic works, this override will always have a lower priority than [Holy Military Base/Fogmen](/holy-military-base/fogmen) — meaning that, without mods, the player will never see this override in their game.
