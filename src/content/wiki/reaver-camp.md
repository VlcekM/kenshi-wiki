### Level Data

zone.49.42.zone

**Reaver Camps** are locations controlled by the [Reavers](/reavers). There are three locations called **Reaver Camp** in Kenshi: One is in [The Outlands](/the-outlands) while the other two are located in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), one of them just bordering [The Crags](/the-crags).

Players enslaved by the Reavers will likely be taken to one of these camps.

These locations can contain 4 to 6 [Reaver Whelp](/reaver-whelp) characters, 2 to 12 [Reaver Slave](/reaver-slave) characters, 4 [Reaver Ironclad](/reaver-ironclad), 11 to 17 [Reaver Blooded](/reaver-blooded), and 2 to 4 [Reaver Neophyte](/reaver-neophyte) characters.

### Buildings

-   [Corpse Furnace](/corpse-furnace) x1 (not in The Outlands camp)
-   [Swamp Dome](/swamp-dome) x1 (only in the Stobe's Garden camp)
-   [Wallhouses](/metal-wallhouse)

### Loot

All items have the same relative chance of spawning in this location except for [Trader's Wooden Backpack](/traders-wooden-backpack) and [Medium Backpack](/medium-backpack), which spawn about half as often.
