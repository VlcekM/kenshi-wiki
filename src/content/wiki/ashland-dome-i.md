**Ashland Dome I** is a large, multi-floored, dome-like structure located in the [Ashlands](/ashlands). The dome has two entrances and one winding staircase in the middle, leading to the higher floors.

It is located southeast of the [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq). It houses the [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture) and several [Legion Thralls](/legion-thrall). The location can be marked by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Ashlands** or an **Old Treasure Map**.

## Guaranteed Loot (Artifacts)

-   3x [Engineering Research](/engineering-research)
-   3x [AI Cores](/ai-core)

## Possible Loot

Total item count: 25

Refresh time: 24 hours
