**Exile Camp** is a small encampment located Southwest of [Squin](/squin) and directly south of the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) [Waystation](/waystation-stenn-desert). It's the only location controlled by the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones). Notable residents include [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless).

If the camp is approached, some exiles will run out to chase the player (or any other faction's) party away.

The camp features [Camp Beds](/camp-bed), [Tents](/tents) and MKIII [Training Dummies](/training-dummy).
