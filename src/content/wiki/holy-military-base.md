**Holy Military Bases** are military outposts controlled by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). There are two bases, both located on the western edges of [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf). Both of them can be noted on your world map by reading the **Glorious Holy [Map](/maps)**.

Judging by how close these **Holy Military Bases** are with the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), and how [World States](/world-states) show that when these military bases collapses they become overrun by [Fogmen](/fogmen) alongside with [Fogman Nest Invasion](/fog-deathyard) starting at Okran's Gulf, it is highly likely that these military bases were built to fortify the defensive position against the Fogmen who would've overrun the region from the western part<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> otherwise.

Some structures such as Holy Bakery, Hospital and Holy Armory can be traded with.
