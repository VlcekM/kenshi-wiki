**Shinobi Citizens** are characters that can be found squatting or living in several areas such as [The Hub](/the-hub), [Mongrel](/mongrel), and [Mourn](/mourn). When living in The Hub, they are members of the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) faction.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
