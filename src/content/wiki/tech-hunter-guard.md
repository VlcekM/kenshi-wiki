**Tech Hunter Guards** are unnamed members of the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) faction. They can be found patrolling tech hunter-controlled settlements like [World's End](/worlds-end), [Mourn](/mourn) and [Black Scratch](/black-scratch). They also serve as shop guards for [Tech Hunter Shops](/tech-hunter-shop) located in [Spring](/spring), [The Free City](/the-free-city), and tech-hunter controlled [Stoat](/stoat).

They have a 2% chance of having a bounty ranging from c.1 to c.4000. They wear \[Standard\] armour (12% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
