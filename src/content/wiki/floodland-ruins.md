**Floodland Ruins** is a faction label for ruins in the [Floodlands](/floodlands). There are no roaming squads attributed to this faction. All characters found in the Floodlands are likely to be members of the [Old Machines](/old-machines) faction.

Strangely a single ruin belonging to this faction can be found in the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).
