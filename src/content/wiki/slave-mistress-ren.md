**Slave Mistress Ren** is a noble of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) faction. She can be found in the Noble House at [Slave Farm](/slave-farm) in [Heng](/heng-zone).

As with other Traders Guild nobles, she is guarded by 4 [Empire Noble Guard Hooded](/empire-noble-guard-hooded) and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

She has a 90% chance of carrying money items and can be holding between c.1,000 and c.5,000. They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

She also has a Dumb [Personality](/personality).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Slave Mistress Ren** is killed/imprisoned the Slave Farm will fall into ruins.

**Slave Mistress Ren's** death/imprisonment alongside with [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga) is required for [Stoat](/stoat) to fall into a malnourished state.

**Slave Mistress** Ren's death/imprisonment alongside with [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) will cause [Heft](/heft) to fall into a malnourished state

**Slave Mistress Ren's** death/imprisonment alongside with [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga) while [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive and free will cause [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) ranging from 4 to 13 individuals starting to spawn in [Bast](/bast) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

**Slave Mistress Ren's** death/imprisonment alongside with [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) while [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive and free will cause [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) patrols ranging from 4 to 13 individuals starting to spawn in [Heng](/heng), [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Eye](/the-eye).

**Slave Mistress Ren's** death/imprisonment alongside with [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) while [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive and free, patrols of [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin) ranging from 1-4 individuals will start spawning around [Heng](/heng-zone).

If **Slave Mistress Ren**, [Longen](/longen) and [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) are all killed/imprisoned, **Slave Traders** patrols will cease to spawn in [Heng](/heng-zone).

**Slave Mistress Ren** is considered to be one of the Northern Slave Masters/Mistresses alongside with [Longen](/longen), [Lady Kana](/lady-kana), [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada) and [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben). Killing/Imprisoning any one of them will cause [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) patrols start spawning in [Heng](/heng-zone). Killing or imprisoning them will also further decrease [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert). They are reduced to none when all of the Northern Slave Masters/Mistresses are killed or imprisoned.
