**Cat-Lon's Exile** is the Throne of the maddened and former Emperor of the Second Empire, [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon). It is deep in the [Ashlands](/ashlands). The location can be marked by reading the [Bugmaster's Map](/maps) or a **[Map](/maps) of the Ashlands**. This location is controlled by the [Second Empire Exile](/second-empire-exile) faction, unlike the Ashland Domes which are controlled by the [Second Empire](/second-empire). [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) provides lore during your stay at the **Cat-Lon's Exile**.

The first dome houses up to 120 [Stormthralls](/stormthrall). The second dome houses Cat-Lon himself.

The place has only one entrance and exit, via the large land ramp at the west of it.

A large piece of debris is suspended over **Cat-Lon's Exile**. When viewed from far, the object would appear to be the remnant of a large space-elevator<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

It's also of note that the sink-hole is likely created by the above structure which may be a variant of the satellites observed in [The Eye](/the-eye) and [Venge](/venge).
