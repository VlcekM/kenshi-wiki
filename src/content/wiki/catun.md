**Catun** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), It can be found by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Extended Territories)**.

It is a relatively isolated and small town surrounded by frequent hostile wildlife located on top of an elevated plateau in the middle of a valley in the [Bonefields](/bonefields).

[Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) is the residing member of the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) for Catun. The town resembles a medieval fortress with a large number of Outpost s-IIIs acting as wall towers. It is home to the [Catun Scrapmasters](/catun-scrapmasters), most sophisticated weapon smiths living in the world of Kenshi ([self-described](/catun-scrapmaster-character)). Catun is subject to a change under the [world states](/world-states).

Catun is a well-defended settlement. United Cities samurai, armed with polearms, frequently engage trespassing [skin spiders](/skin-spider), [boneyard wolves](/boneyard-wolf), [garrus](/garru) and [beak things](/beak-thing). Humanoid troublemakers are only represented by one or two occasional [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones-character) characters. None stand a chance against the skill and numbers of the UC samurai in this town.
