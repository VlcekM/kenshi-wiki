Raptor Island is an ancient citadel-turned-nest with a variety of mid-range [weapons](/weapons) and [armour](/armour). Since the nest is partially sunk, speed is severely reduced, but a sufficiently high athletics character can grab one of the weapons and escape before the [Megaraptor](/megaraptor) (whose speed is unaffected) hits.

It is located in the middle of the eponymously named [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone) zone.
