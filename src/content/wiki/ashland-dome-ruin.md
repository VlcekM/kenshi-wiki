The **Ashland Dome Ruin** is an [Ancient Location](/ancient-locations) controlled by [Ancient Ruins](/ancient-ruins). This location does not have any inhabitants or spawn any loot. The location can be marked by reading the **Old Treasure [Map](/maps)**, indicating that not long ago this place was worthy of a visit. There are two Ashland Dome Ruins in the Ashlands.

Given their placement in Ashlands relative to other [Ashland Domes](/ashland-dome-i), it is possible that there were other 2 high-ranking officers of the defunct Second Empire in these domes, but they were destroyed as a result of the building being ruined. Either that, or the placement of these buildings are purely aesthetic.
