**Puppet** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in the [United Cities](/united-cities). This item can only be found in residential buildings inside of [United Cities](/united-cities) locations.

This letter was likely written soon after [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) came to power and references the previous Emperor of the UC - [Emperor Anzai](/emperor-anzai).
