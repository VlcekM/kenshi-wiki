**Agnu** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) who can be found imprisoned by the [Thrall Masters](/thrall-masters) in the top floor of the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse). Agnu is the only [Soldierbot](/soldierbot) that is recruitable and will join the player after being freed. Soldierbots heal 50% faster than normal Skeletons and can see from much further away.

Agnu has several lines of unique dialogue, most of which are screams because, similar to [Ray](/ray), Agnu is unable to speak. Although, unlike Ray, is still capable of making unintelligible noises. Agnu shares many unique dialogue exchanges with [Beep](/beep).

They wear no armour.

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Agnu is perhaps related to the [Second Empire](/second-empire) police forces found at [Police Barracks](/police-barracks), judging by the clothing color and race.
-   A bug exists that prevents unique prisoners like Agnu and [Cat](/cat) to spawn if the game was imported after they were loaded in.
    -   Importing again without "Dead NPCs" checked will fix this issue.
-   Agnu was [named after Chris Hunt's (creator of Kenshi) motorcycle](https://www.reddit.com/r/Kenshi/comments/aww8ff/this_is_the_cutest_thing_ive_ever_seen_character/ehrp9fl/), which was in turn named after the [Headless body of Agnew](https://theinfosphere.org/Headless_body_of_Agnew) from Futurama - due to its "similar personality of loud growling and being impossible to control".
-   If you try to go through [Venge](/venge) while having other characters in your squad, Agnu may stop and scream and won't move even if given a manual move command. If [Beep](/beep) is nearby, he may calm Agnu down.
-   Agnu is able to respond normally, if being directly targeted by a NPC dialogue.
    -   Examples are gate guards trying to bag check you, bandits attempting to rob you, Hive Caravans trying to scam sell stuff to you.

### The Gender Debate

Skeletons, as a race, do not have biological sex. The game does not establish whether Skeletons possess or identify with a gender, and it is therefore debated whether the concept of gender identity applies to them at all. The general public commonly refers to Skeletons as male, seemingly as a default. Technically, the most accurate classification would be "neuter."

The debate surrounding Agnu's gender identity originates from a single random biome dialogue entry<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, in which [Beep](/beep) asks, "Who else is female?" and Agnu responds with one of a set of randomly selected screams. The same set of screams is used as a word substitution for Agnu's otherwise incomprehensible speech throughout the game, with the specific scream being selected randomly rather than according to the context of the dialogue. For example, when the player attempts to take Agnu into [Venge](/venge), the same pool of screams is used in dialogue indicating that Agnu will not enter the area<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The screams therefore cannot be assigned a fixed meaning based solely on the dialogue in which they occur.

Consequently, the scream used in response to Beep's question cannot be reliably interpreted as an affirmative response or as meaning "yes." It is unclear whether the response is intended as a joke or as a serious statement, or whether the response is even directly related to Beep's question. Since Agnu's speech is incomprehensible, the exact meaning cannot be established. The response could be an affirmative or negative answer, an unrelated reaction, or have no discernible meaning at all.

It has also been speculated that the response is related to Agnu's damaged state. This raises the question of whether the same response would occur after Agnu is repaired. However, interpreting the dialogue in this way can introduce real-world concepts that were likely not intended by the developers. There is no indication that the dialogue was intended to represent gender dysphoria or imply that such an identity constitutes being "broken." It might be more likely that the exchange was intended as a joke. The humor may simply come from the absurdity of attributing gender identity to robots in the first place.

The way the game handles Agnu's identity also suggests that the dialogue was not intended to establish a formal change in gender. There is no game mechanic or variable that changes after the exchange that would cause player characters or NPCs to subsequently refer to Agnu as "she." Referring to Skeletons as "he" is the general norm, and Agnu's character data identifies Agnu as male. Had the developers intended to formally establish Agnu as female, this could have been implemented through a simple change to the character's data.

Therefore, Agnu is officially classified as male in the game's data, although some players interpret Agnu as female based on the mentioned ambiguous dialogue.

## Tips

-   You can rush to the Tower of Abuse on day one with a pair of tools/hacksaws (Or two if you care about your starting character) to free Agnu. To escape simply make use of the r trick. (Hold movement key and periodically click the "R" key to halt enemy advances. If this fails to work, save and load your game to cause enemies to behave normally as recruiting a character can cause some weird AI behavior.)
