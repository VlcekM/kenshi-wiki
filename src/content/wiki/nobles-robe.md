These are worn by [Noble Hunters](/noble-hunter) and all other [Nobles](/noble-circle) of the [United Cities](/united-cities). It is part of a costume set with [Noble's Trousers](/nobles-trousers). Despite the matching pants designated as clothing, this robe is qualified as armour with no class assigned.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

The Noble's Robe appears to be a puffy, long-sleeved, collared, green and orange robe of a vibrant design, tied at the bottom by a light green sash.

## Crafting

The blueprint for this item might be found in the Great Library in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch). After researching, it can be crafted at the [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench).
