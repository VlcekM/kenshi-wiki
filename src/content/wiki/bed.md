**Beds** are used for restoring the health of characters. Beds help organic characters heal their stun and bandaged health faster. Beds can be found in [towns](/towns) or [constructed](/construction) in player buildings.

## Key Points

-   Only one person can sleep in a bed at a time.
-   If you are carrying an injured person or NPC, you can lay them in a bed to stop wounds from degenerating.
-   Placing incapacitated NPCs in a bed may result in a relation gain with their [faction](/factions).
-   A bed heals the organic health of a unit at 8x the normal [healing rate](/guide-to-health), twice as much as a [Camp Bed](/camp-bed).
-   Sleeping reduces [hunger](/food) rate to 1/3.
-   Beds can be rented in friendly towns or outposts from as little as c.50. Beds are rented for 24 hours so a healed character can always leave the bed to give place to their wounded comrade as long as the clock has not run out.
-   Typically, barracks and other private buildings have beds that are forbidden to sleep in. This can be bypassed if the player is "Allied" with the faction that owns the building.
