In Kenshi, weather is dependent on what seasons occur in each Zone. Most [Zones](/category/zones) only have one season, but some have multiple. Zones with multiple seasons typically have the year evenly divided between those seasons but not always. In Kenshi, a year lasts 100 days.

Some seasons include weather which have [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). These effects can be harmful or detrimental to combat.
