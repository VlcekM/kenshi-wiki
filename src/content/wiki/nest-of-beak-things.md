Run.

**Nests of Beak Things** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest). They can spawn in the [Bonefields](/bonefields), [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Gut](/gut), [High Bonefields](/high-bonefields), [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [The Crater](/the-crater), [The Unwanted Zone](/the-unwanted-zone), and [Vain](/vain).

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### Beak Squad Small

This squad can contain 1 to 3 [Beak Things](/beak-thing). They spawn with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.85x.

### Beak Squad -Nest

This squad can contain from 1 to 5 [Beak Things](/beak-thing). One to three of the Beak Things spawn with age multipliers between 0.6x and 0.85x., the rest will be spawn with age multipliers between 0.15x to 0.4x.
