**Cannibal Camps** are randomly spawning locations of the [Cannibals](/cannibals) faction. They usually contain 5 [Camp Beds](/camp-bed), 5 Prisoner Poles, 3 [Torch Posts](/torch-post) and 1 central [Cooking Pit](/cooking-pit). [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal) **w**ould take unconscious characters to their camps in order to do what they do best. It is possible wipe out this location along with its inhabitants. They can spawn in the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), [Dreg](/dreg).
