**Fallen towers** are watchtowers fallen into disrepair and often lying on the ground. It is more of a landmark ruin since it is not accessible and there are no lootable items around. The place doesn't seem to serve any purpose except for lore aesthetics. There is one in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [Floodlands](/floodlands), [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), and in [Arach](/arach).
