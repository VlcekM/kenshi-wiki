Junk is a term used to describe an item with no useful function to serve other than being a miscellaneous or [trade item](/trade-goods). A majority of junk items can be found in the upper floors of [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs), in locked or open crates. These items usually weigh a large amount, like the [Generator Core](/generator-core), or a little, like [Gears](/gears). Junk items can be extremely valuable if your character(s) have a high enough [Strength](/strength) to carry them, as well as a [backpack](/backpacks) for surplus.
