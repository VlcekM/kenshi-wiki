**As of the Beta launch in 0.93.0, this town is no longer available as it was in the [Old World](/old-world). Please refer to the [World of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) page for a list of locations currently available.**

Port South is a town located in the southwest of the map and is controlled by the United Cities faction. It is located at the base of a hill, while nearby towns include [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha), [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge-alpha), and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam-alpha).

The location made it into the final release of the game as a Slave Trader [town](/port-south).

## Important Buildings in Port South

-   A Bar
-   A Police Station
-   [A Flophouse](/flophouse)
-   A Trade Building
-   Two Item Traders
-   A Clothing Trader
-   An Armour Trader
-   A Weapon Trader
-   A Hat Trader
