**Hydroponic Vegetables** is a [Hydroponics](/hydroponics) bay which grows [Riceweed](/riceweed). It can be constructed after researching [Hydroponic Riceweed](/hydroponics-tech). This building uses and levels characters' [Farming](/farming) stat. It requires inputs of [Power](/power) (10 used) and [Water](/water) to grow.

Despite being an "interior" building, Hydroponics can be placed on the roofs of buildings.

Each growth cycle it produces 18 plants, but each plant harvests for 3 [Riceweed](/riceweed), for a total of 54 [Riceweed](/riceweed) (assuming 100% harvest chance)as of v1.0.68
