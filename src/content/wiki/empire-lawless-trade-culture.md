**Empire Lawless Trade Culture** is a [trade culture](/traders) which removes cultural items from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and several food items. It's similar to the [Empire Trade Culture](/empire-trade-culture) with the notable exception there is no illegal buildings and goods.

Since there is no illegal goods in a territory ruled by this trade culture, [Hashish](/hashish) and [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) are sold without particular bonuses, except if a complementary trade culture applies locally like the [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture). It's the case at the [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) : [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) are tied to **Empire Lawless Trade Culture** but [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture) is applied locally. That's why [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) buy [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish) at better prices at [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) than [Black Scratch](/black-scratch) despite the fact those two cities are ruled by the same faction.
