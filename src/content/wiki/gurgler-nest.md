**Gurgler Nests** are randomly spawning animal [Nests](/nest) that contain Gurglers. They can spawn in [Fishman Island](/fishman-island).

## Squads

These are the standard squads which have a chance to spawn at this nest. More information about these squads can be found in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).

### Gurgler nest residents

This squad can contain from 6 to 10 [Gurglers](/gurgler).
