**Pack Beasts** are tamed [Garrus](/garru) used in Trade Caravans for [Nomads](/nomads), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) and the [Western Hive](/western-hive) to haul supplies and materials in their [Garru Backpacks](/garru-backpack). They also make sturdy guards as they will happily participate in combat to protect their caravan. Despite their herbivorous nature in the wild, when tamed they are able to eat any kind of edible [food](/food), including [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat). They also have a voracious appetite, eating enough for two humans. Pack Beasts can be purchased from [Nomad Animal Traders](/nomad-animal-trader).

The Garru Backpack can carry goods in stacks of 6, which is one more than a Bull Backpack; they are otherwise identical in size, meaning an optimally-loaded Pack Beast can carry more stacked goods than its [Bull](/pack-bull) equivalent. However, the Garru Backpack has a built-in lantern that cannot be turned off except by removing the pack, so Pack Beasts make your party less sneaky at night. The light may potentially provide a boon to nocturnal crossbow users. Both Domesticated Bulls and Pack Beasts make sturdy combatants, though Garrus lack the powerful charge attack of the Bull. Overall, both species are great for hauling tons of materials even if they aren't the best fighters.
