The **Skeletons** faction consists of [Skeletons](/skeleton) from the ancient times who have founded their own civilization of sorts. They have their own city in [Deadlands](/deadlands), known as the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city). It is a city where many skeletons retreated to hide in isolation, as most life forms cannot survive the journey to the city. They also have a few other outposts throughout the world with a few solitary Skeletons living in them.
