**Big Grim** is the leader of the [Hounds](/hounds) faction. She can be found in the Hounds Faction HQ in [Shark](/shark). In order to join the Hounds, players need to find Ears to "smooth things over." Player characters can talk to Big Grim before speaking to Ears, but she will not be friendly.

She has the [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status) tag and will critically decrease relations with the Hounds if you attack her.

When she just appeared in the Swamp as the leader of the Hounds, she wrote a [letter](/letter-to-darkbrow) to [Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) with an offer an alliance. It is indicative of her low level of literacy, but also of her diplomatic qualities.

She has the Brave [Personality](/personality).

## Interactions

After talking to [Ears](/ears), players can speak to **Big Grim** about joining the Hounds. Big Grim wants the player to "drop" (smuggle) some [hash](/hashish) into the UC ([United Cities](/united-cities)) and return the profit to her, this is the first assignment she gives to the player.

There is dialogue where she asks the player to drop one of the Swamp gang leaders that they are carrying. She will not attack if the player is allied with the [Hounds](/hounds) but will express her disapproval either way. Being allied ends with nearby [Hounds](/hound) expressing their embarrassment.

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Big Grim** is killed or kidnapped, this will cause [Shark](/shark) to be taken over by [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), or [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) if [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) is also killed. [The Hound's Welcome](/hounds-welcome), [Hound Check In](/hound-check-in) and [Revenge of the Hounds](/revenge-of-the-hounds) events will cease to trigger.

If both Big Gray and Big Grim are not alive while [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless) is, then [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) take over [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village) and may make that their new headquarters.

If Grim, [Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) and [Big Gray](/big-gray) are all dead, [Rot](/rot) is taken over by spiders.
