**The Old Empire** is an unused faction found in the game files. [Empty Ruin](/empty-ruin), [Old Empire Watchtower](/old-empire-watchtower) and [The Forgotten Throne](/the-forgotten-throne) used to be owned by this faction for a brief period. Since two of these locations now belong to the [Second Empire](/second-empire) (Forgotten Throne being unused in the released game), it is possible that **The Old Empire** was a precursor to the Second Empire faction that was later scrapped.
