**Shem** is a sandy oasis area. It is home to [Nomads](/nomads) as well as Smugglers from the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) gang.

This is a rather isolated and underpopulated area is mostly filled with [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) and [Nomads](/nomads), as well as [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Bloodspiders](/blood-spider), likely wandered in from the neighboring [Venge](/venge) or [The Burning Forest](/burning-forest).

Smugglers Bar is a location worth remembering, especially for players travelling through nearby [Venge](/venge). There are numerous Beds and, short of kidnapping [Big Gray](/big-gray), there isn't any reason for the Grayflayers to be hostile to the player.
