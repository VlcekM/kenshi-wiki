A **Storm House** a relatively big and cheap early-game building players can [construct](/construction). It is slightly more than 3x expensive compared to the [Small Shack](/small-shack), yet features a very open first and second floor, of which more than doubles the floor-space of the structure.

If one is concerned if the materials spent is worth the extra space, rest assured the Storm House yields 4-5 times the useable floor-space of The Small Shack. It also has an open-top second floor which allows the use of some external constructs as well, such as [Small Wind Generators](/small-wind-generator), [Rain Collectors](/rain-collector) and is defensible with a rather absurd number of [Mounted Crossbow](/mounted-crossbow) emplacement points, of which have limitless ammunition.

It is a great starting structure for newer players and can be made relatively cheaply so long as a meager supply chain for [Raw Stone](/raw-stone) and [Building Material](/building-materials) is established. As for more seasoned players, it makes a good, cheap and quickly build general purpose structure.

The storm house requires the research [Small House](/small-house-tech) to build.

### Old Storm House

In some towns such as [The Hub](/the-hub), Old Storm Houses may be available for purchase. There is little difference between them, aside from a higher cost for the older buildings and their slightly worn look.

### Lagoon Storm House

In the central southeast, there are some instances of Storm Houses which have external metal beams. These can be found specifically in [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) and [Secret Drug Farm](/secret-drug-farm).
