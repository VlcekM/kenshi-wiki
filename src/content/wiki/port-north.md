**Port North** is a town controlled by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). Despite being called a "port," this town is essentially a [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) because it contains many [stone mines](/stone-mine) and raw materials processing industries. It likely produces [iron plates](/iron-plates) and [building materials](/building-materials) for export. [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) lives here in a [Noble House](/noble-house), surrounded by her hooded [Empire Noble Guards](/empire-noble-guard-hooded). You can find this town by venturing to "middle" of the northern seashore of [the Great Desert](/the-great-desert) or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

Although all the industrial machineries her only includes [Stone](/raw-stone) and [Iron](/raw-iron)\-related products, it is said by **Lady Kana** that this slave camp is the biggest 'Slave Farm' and also a producer of [clothing](/clothing). Therefore, it is likely that the industrial buildings resource output should not be taken literally as their export of commodities.

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Bark](/bark) and [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai).

Despite featuring buildings labeled "[Slaver Bar](/slaver-bar)" there are no barman in this town to trade with.

There is also another "port" in **The Great Desert,** called [Port South](/port-south) and it is controlled by [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada).

## Trivia

-   This labor camp can be considered to be the most important/influential camp out of all 6 camps in northern UC, as its destruction is tied directly with the most [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) presence, as well as [Slave Traders](/slave-traders)/[Manhunters](/manhunters) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and causes the direct [malnourishment of Sho-Battai](/sho-battai/malnourished) and is necessary for [Bark](/bark) to become one so too.

Designation of this town as a "port" (despite lacking a usable harbor due to defensive walls blocking off the sea) is yet another indication that the world of [Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi) currently uses maritime transport, especially to trade goods between the non-bordering [Northern United Cities](/the-great-desert) and [the](/catun) [Southern](/clownsteady) [United Cities](/drifters-last) despite engine limitations meaning boats do not exist in game. This is also supported by the existence of the [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates), and NPC dialogue that mentions boats/ships in passing. Shipment of raw materials produced on site would be a safer and more convenient method of transportation compared than using desert caravans to cross the notoriously bandit-controlled "forbidden zone".

Information revealed about [Kenshi 2](/kenshi-2) confirms that the [Second Empire](/second-empire) had significant maritime power that allowed them to use ships around the continent to traverse it, due to the raised sea levels turning the game map's continental mass into an archipelago of smaller islands. Confirmation that the map will also be bigger also strongly implies that other landmasses exist outside of the continent of Kenshi that are not featured in the current game, implying that Port North among other port cities may hold much greater significance.
