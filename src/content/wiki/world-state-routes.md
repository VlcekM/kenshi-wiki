World State Routes is a term used to describe certain [World States](/world-states) achievements that can further enhance a factions power/influence. World State Routes may also be complimentary to the routes of other factions, making both of these routes capable of co-existing with one another or further enhancing the effect.

This section will compose of all the factions that have World States which could increase their influence inside Kenshi's factions, though it should be noted that not all of them have quests or can be reasoned with (such as the [Reavers](/reavers)), and even those with quests may not necessarily target all the individuals that might be of interest to them. This article will serve as the hub for all the factions that have potential to increase their influence in the world, as long as they are shown in the faction tab.

The following factions have their routes:
