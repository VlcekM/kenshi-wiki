**Tech Hunters** are unnamed members of the [Machinists](/machinists). There are a total of four of them that can be found in the University in [World's End](/worlds-end).

For the mercenaries of the faction [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), see [Drifters](/drifter).

They have a 4% chance of having a bounty ranging from c.1 to c.4,000.

They are among the few characters in the game to be carrying [splint kits](/splint-kit), a tribute to their preparedness and knowledge of their craft.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (8% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
