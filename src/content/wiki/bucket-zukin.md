The **Bucket Zukin** is Heavy Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It is the standard headgear for regular soldiers of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). The helmet can be [dyed](/colour-scheme).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

The **Bucket Zukin** appears to be a pot helmet with a flat chainmail sheet attached as to wrap around the sides and back of the head. Two plates are riveted on the each side of the sheet, with a decorative chain link attaching opposing plates through the back of the head.
