**Martial Artist Bindings** is a [shirt](/shirts) which provides no armour protection and is therefore considered [clothing](/clothing).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

## Appearance

Martial Artist Bindings are white cloth hand, abdominal, and foot wrappings on the character. The appearance is similar to what professional fighters wear to prevent injuries sustained from striking and being struck.

Female characters will have the bindings wrapped around their breasts instead of their abdomen, covering the original breasts wrappings in cloth of a whiter and cleaner material. As with males, the bindings also cover hands and feet.

If the character that is wearing this clothing has prosthetics or is limbless, the MA bindings will not cover the limbless/prosthetic part (oddly, the effects of MA bindings will still apply nonetheless).

## Crafting

It can be crafted at a [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench) after researching [Clothing Manufacture](/clothing-manufacture-tech).

## Trivia

-   The attack damage effect impacts Martial Arts and melee weapon damage.
    -   It has no impact on ranged weapons or turrets.
-   As the attack damage effect is a multiplier it will stack with other bonuses like Sleeveless Dustcoat.
    -   Combined they grant 1.1025x damage. (1.05x1.05).
