**The Shrieking Forest** is a zone of dense vegetation located to the North of the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) and [Obedience](/obedience), West of the [Floodlands](/floodlands), and south of the [Purple Sands](/purple-sands).

It is home to large groups of [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits) who gave it the name.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or Ancient Locations in The Shrieking Forest. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.

### Homeless Spawns

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. (**2.5x** multiplier) This list is in order of likelihood._

-   [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits)
    -   Shrieking Squad (9 ~ 26 [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits)) {10} (Malnourished)

The natives here are quite territorial and their endless horde will never allow you take their fertile land that pretty much allows most types of crops at 100% fertility. [Prospecting](/prospecting) reveals little mineral resources but there are spots that allows for stone production at 100% quality. The place is really beautiful and bountiful but all that screaming day and night really turns any sane man off.
