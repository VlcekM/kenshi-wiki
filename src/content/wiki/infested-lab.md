**Infested Lab** is found within [the Swamp](/the-swamp) southeast of the city [Shark](/shark). This is an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs) which has become mostly empty ruin. [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) can be found here.

This location can have anywhere from 5 to 20 adult [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider).
