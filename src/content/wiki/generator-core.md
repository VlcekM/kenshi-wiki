**Generator Core** is an [item](/items) which has no designated function. It is the heaviest stackable (In a backpack) object, making it useful for [strength](/strength) training. Aside from that, its only use is trading.

In lore-wise terms, it's most likely used as an essential component for setting up [Wind](/wind-generator) and [Fuel Generators](/small-generator), as well as a component to repair those generators in case they break down.
