**Twinblades** are members of the [Twinblades](/twinblades) faction within [the Swamp](/the-swamp). They can be found roaming the city [Shark](/shark) or within the village [Mud Town](/mud-town).

They have a Maniacal [Personality](/personality). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
