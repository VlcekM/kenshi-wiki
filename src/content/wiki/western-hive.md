**Western Hive** is a faction of the [Hive](/hive) race which dwell in a series of [Hive Villages](/hive-village) and [Distant Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village).

## Overview

Most Hive [Playable Characters](/playable-characters) in the game originate from the Western Hive (instead of [Southern Hive](/southern-hive)) and will often mention their reluctance to return to their home when they enter [Vain](/vain).

Because of their xenophobia, their neighbors, the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), will arrest Hivers that trespass into their borders; however, they are not diplomatically hostile to them as a faction. As such, most wandering Hive traders will usually not be found venturing too far inside Holy Nation territory and instead stay within the relative safety of their lands, with the Holy Nation doing the same.

For low-level players that have their starts near the [Border Zone](/border-zone), the Western Hive is an excellent place for non-Hivers to do trade with. In particular, they are one of the closest traders which sell cheap prosthetics for characters that have lost a limb.

## Population

The overwhelming vast majority of Western Hivers live in their heartlands of [Vain](/vain) and [Dreg](/dreg), constituting around 96% of all Hiver population. The rest live in a much more isolated area at [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

The Hivers outnumber [Beak Things](/beak-thing) by at least 10 times inside [Vain](/vain)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their economical capacity is much smaller than that of [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), being roughly around 1/5th as big<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their population is similar or more compared to the [Fogmen](/fogmen) of [Fog Islands](/fog-islands)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

The population and presence of the wandering [Princes](/lost-prince) would suggest that that the Hive civilization as a whole are around 3x as populated as all the [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, likely making them at least anywhere between 100 ~ 120x smaller than [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
