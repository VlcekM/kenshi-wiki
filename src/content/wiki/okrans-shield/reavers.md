_This article is a [stub](/category/article-stubs). You can help the Kenshi Wiki by [expanding it](/okrans-shield/reavers)._

If [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) are dead while [Valamon](/valamon) is alive and free, [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) will [fall under the control](/town-overrides) of the [Reavers](/reavers).

Assuming the conditions for achieving this override remains the same, this settlement will immediately switch to [Destroyed override upon 2nd entry](/okrans-shield/destroyed), unless either [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) or [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is released from prison and kept alive. [Valamon](/valamon) is needed for this override to be achieved to trigger, but is not needed to be alive for this override to remain.

The icon of the area would be a Town icon if the player hasn't visited the place before.

The override can flip to become the [Destroyed variant](/okrans-shield/destroyed).
