**Grass Pirates** are members of the [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates) faction. They are normally led by a [Straw Lieutenant](/straw-lieutenant) in groups of 5 - 12, patrolling the areas of [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

They have a 10% chance of having a bounty ranging from c.1,000 to c.3,000 with the [United Cities](/united-cities). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown) and [Ancient](/ancient).
