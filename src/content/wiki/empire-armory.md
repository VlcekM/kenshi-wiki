**Empire Armory** is an Armory found in the [United Cities](/united-cities). Armour-wise, this location has the same stock as [Empire Armour Shop](/empire-armour-shop). This location cannot be purchased from.

### Possible Locations

-   [Drin](/drin)

Depending on [World States](/world-states), this vendor might be available at the following location:

-   [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield)

### Residents

-   [Samurai](/samurai)
-   [Samurai Conscript](/samurai-conscript) (3 to 5)
