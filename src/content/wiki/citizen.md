**Citizens** are random NPCs found in the towns in Kenshi. They will normally roam around the town they are in, buying stuff from shops and generally minding their own business and if found near combat may flee to a safer area (i.e, Police Station).

## Statistics

-   Humans
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive
    
-   Skeletons
    

-   Greenlander
