**Swamper Gate Guard** is a member of the [Swampers](/swampers) faction. They can be commonly found protecting the outlying villages in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) as well as [Shark](/shark).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (4% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander
    
-   Scorchlander
    
-   Shek
    
-   Hive Prince
