**Ancient Locations** are [Locations](/category/locations) which were most likely created before the fall of the previous civilization. [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) search for these locations to find ancient technologies. Theorizing about the creators of some of these locations can be found in [Lore Books](/lore-books) written by [Machinists](/machinists).
