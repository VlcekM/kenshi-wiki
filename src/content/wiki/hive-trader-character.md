Hive Traders are [vendors](/hive-trader) of the Western Hive, selling to the player in any of the [Hive Villages](/hive-village). They will, however, refuse to trade with the [Hiver](/hive) characters calling them "Hiveless ones". This only seems to occur with the exiles of the [Western Hive](/western-hive).

They have a "Smart" [personality](/personality).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (22% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by Ancient.
