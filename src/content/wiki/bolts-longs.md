**Bolts \[Longs\]** are ammunition used with [Crossbows](/crossbow-class).

After researching [Crossbow Bolts](/crossbow-bolts-tech) and learning the [Blueprint](/blueprints) (that can be bought occasionally in [World's End](/worlds-end)), these bolts can be made on an [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench) for 0.5 steel bars per Bolts \[Longs\]. They can later be stored in a [Boltbox](/boltbox).

These bolts are the rarest bolts in Kenshi, as most of the ranger shops on the map do not sell them. Available places to buy large quantities of these bolts are the ranger shops in [World's End](/worlds-end) and [Mongrel](/mongrel). They can also be found as rare loot in several ruins.

## Used with

-   [Eagle's Cross](/eagles-cross)
