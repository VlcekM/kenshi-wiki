**Settled Nomads** is a village located in the [Dreg](/dreg) region, south of [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone). The village is controlled by the [Nomads](/nomads) faction. It contains four buildings: three residential buildings and a Nomad Animal Shop. None of the buildings can be purchased by the [player faction](/nameless).

Players can purchase animals from the Nomad Animal Shop. They have [Bonedogs](/bonedog), [Bulls](/wild-bull), [Garrus](/garru), [Goats](/goat) for sale. They also sell [Garru Backpacks](/garru-backpack), and [Bull Backpacks](/bull-backpack).

[Cornelius](/cornelius) the Goat, a [unique recruit](/unique-recruits), may be purchasable here.
