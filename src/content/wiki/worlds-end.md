**World's End** is a settlement resembling a very large [Waystation](/waystation) found on a mountain top in the [Arm of Okran](/arm-of-okran).

It is currently controlled by the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), while also serving as the home of the [Machinists](/machinists) faction. The latter have their University based in the town, which happens to contract most of the local Tech Hunters. The [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) pacifier can also be found here.

Two statues resembling [Okran](/okran) or [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is found at the southern gates, the official reason given by [Finch](/finch) is that they are used to appease and convince [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) that they are faithful despite the large technological cache inside the towns. It is unclear if The Holy Nation actually believes this pretense of faith given their zeal in inquisition<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, or deliberately turns a blind eye to this location for unknown reasons.

[Holy Construction Traders](/holy-construction-trader) in particular are aware of World's End presence, as well as their technological cache<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Hired ninjas act as the local guards. Although there is no prison in town, some form of [Empire](/united-cities) law loosely applies, as UC bounties are issued to troublemakers. These bounties are not actually enforced in town; many of the Tech Hunters themselves also have UC bounties.

A [Map to World's End](/map-to-worlds-end) can help find this location.

## Lore

World's End, as the home base of the Tech Hunters, offers one of the largest stores of machine and science items within Holy Nation territory.

Finch, the head of the University of Machinist, details that despite being within Holy Nation territory, and the prevalence of Okran statues at the town, the town is totally independent from the Holy Nation. He argues that the statues were sufficient enough to trick the gullible Holy Nation authorities into leaving the town alone. Because of this, one can find a large number of Skeletons within its walls, whereas in any part of the Holy Nation, just about all Skeletons are killed on-sight.
