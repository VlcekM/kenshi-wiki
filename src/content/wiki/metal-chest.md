**Metal Chest** is a [Storage](/storage) container for storing any item. Players can construct this building after researching [Item Storage (Tech)](/item-storage-tech).

## Details

This chest has a storage space of **12x12** squares. Unlike [General Storage Chest](/general-storage-chest), it does not have stacking capabilities.
