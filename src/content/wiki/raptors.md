**Raptors** are one of the animal [factions](/factions) in Kenshi. This faction does not appear on the [factions list](/factions) as it is "not real".

## Characters

Only the [River Raptor](/river-raptor) is a member of this faction, all other raptors are part of the [Swamp Creatures](/swamp-creatures) faction.

## Relations

-   [Herbivore](/herbivore) (0) (coexistence)
-   [Spiders](/spiders) (100)
