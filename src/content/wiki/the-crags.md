**The Crags** is the home of the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits), alongside their leader, [Elder](/elder). [Reavers](/reavers) and [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) will also appear here and can often be seen skirmishing with one another around these regions. If Elder is imprisoned or killed, groups of [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) and the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) will appear in the zone as well.

Groups of [Drifters](/drifters) from [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) can be seen here but won't always be friendly, as some of these groups attack strangers on sight.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in The Crags. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
