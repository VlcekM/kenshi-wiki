**Drifter's Last** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) faction, located in the Southwest region of the map known as [the Hook](/the-hook). The town has a number of farms just outside the town walls. This location is home to [Lady Merin](/lady-merin) of the Noble Circle. It can be found by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Extended Territories)**.

The area is frequently attacked by [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer).
