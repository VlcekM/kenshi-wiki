**Cannibal Chiefs** are minor leaders of the [Cannibals](/cannibals) faction. They can be found in [Cannibal Capital](/cannibal-capital), [Cannibal Village](/cannibal-village), [First Village](/first-village), [Suspicious Lab](/suspicious-lab).

This character has a 50% chance to spawn with a c.10,000 [bounty](/bounty) which can be turned in at either [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) or the [United Cities](/united-cities).
