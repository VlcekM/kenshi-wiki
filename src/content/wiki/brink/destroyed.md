If [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) and [Valamon](/valamon) are both killed or imprisoned, [Brink](/brink) falls into ruin. The location will still however belong to the United Cities.

It appears that since the head noble governing this region was taken out, increased [unrest](/rebel-farmers) and banditry may have caused death, devastation and abandonment of this city.
